# P28 — World audio: region and place music and ambience, the sounds of the new peoples, creatures, engines and formations

Wave 17, lane B (beside P27). Branch `pkg/P28`. Plan: `docs/plans/M6_M9_PLAN.md` §3.11, with §4.1
(the M7-M9 rows of the cue map), §4.2 (synthesis, the world column), §4.3 (the score and the
ambience map), §4.4 (the mix), §4.5 (formats and budget) and decisions 2-8. The goal: the five
regions and their main places sound different by data; the world reacts to the giant's size;
formations, engines and water are audible as what they are; and every enemy, engine, ability and UI
event that M7-M9 add has its cues before it exists, named by §2.5's conventions so the router finds
them. P29-P37's own coverage tests (their records resolving to these cues) close the loop.

## What was built

    in the Valley's zone frame, Aldmere's field-drum snare, the three bosses' drums, themes and
    phase layers in their own frames - 30 loops, 2.80 MB of Ogg - and fourteen stingers (0.32 MB).
  - `gen_world_ambience.py` (281001-281120): five 20 s stereo region beds, six 15 s mono place beds,
    five 10 s loops (the formations' march, Hollowmere's pond, the lake shore, the falls, the town

  - `gen_world_sfx.py` (282001-282601): 98 mono WAV masters (69.0 s; 0.62 MB imported as QOA) in

  Every file is in `assets/audio/manifest.json` with `"scope": "world"` and a calibrated `est_kbps`
  (the build's estimate before encoding is the built size within 1 %), and in
  `assets/audio/PROVENANCE.md` (three rows and a note); `build_audio.py --verify` regenerates all

## The world's sound, for the owner's listening pass

Nobody has listened to any of it (the container has no speakers): every check is a measurement
(levels, peaks, loudness, seams, frames, onsets), as for P21 and P23.

### The score

The zone sets share P23's zone frame, `zone_96` (D dorian, 96 BPM, 4/4, twelve bars = 30.0 s =
661,500 frames) and its chords, so they share the Valley's danger and combat stems (`pulse`,
`combat_drums`, `combat_brass`), and a region crossing - or the walk from the Kings' Door into the
Crownmarch - lands on a bar line with the harmony carried on (P23's aligned set changes). Each region
has a drone and three melody variants written by P23's phrase writer from one motif, in four-bar
phrases that never cross a segment boundary (the scheduler may switch variant or rest at any
segment). Layers as in §4.3: explore drone 0 / melody −2; danger drone 0 / melody −9 / pulse −3;
combat drone −4 / melody −10 / pulse −5 / drums 0 / brass −2.

## The rules (hotspot edits)

The router (P19) already implemented every §4.1 row with fallback chains; P21 and P23 added cues.
P28, the W17 owner of the three files, added the M7-M9 cues and changed or added these rules (each
also documented at the top of its file):

**`cue_router.gd`**
1. **Voices for records the mix does not name** (`fallback_voice`): by the record's archetype
   (`ARCHETYPE_VOICES`: troll, troll_matriarch/matriarch, rival_giant -> giant, cavalry -> horse,
   dire_wolf), else by its short id (`SHORT_ID_VOICES`: troll_matriarch, harrok, ulfra, rival_giant,
   cavalry; or a voice's own name such as `dire_wolf`), else `human`. A giant that is not the player
   **bellows** where the player roars (`voice_call`): `sfx.voice.giant.roar` stays the player's own.
2. **A formation's brace** (P19's rule: one `sfx.threat.windup.wall` per formation per brace cycle,
   its band telegraphs merged within `wall_merge_s` 0.5 s, at its striker, priority 100) is kept and
   now tested with ten pikes. **Members' own footfalls are silent while their formation holds its
   slots** (`Formation.is_intact()`: formed, advancing, braced): the march loop is their sound;
   broken or routed, each steps on its own again.
3. **Formation cues sound at the live formation's anchor** (`Formation.live()`, `anchor()`: the front
   rank's centre at chest height), else at the members' mean position as before.
4. **The incoming whistle** of an indirect shot (an action with `indirect`, or a projectile visual
   in `INDIRECT_VISUALS`: `catapult_stone`): the shot's telegraph circle is remembered as its landing
   point (`LANDING_MEMORY_S` 15 s, per shooter; remembered even without a giant); at the launch
   (`projectile_spawned`) `sfx.threat.incoming` is scheduled at the landing circle (1 m up) to start
   `flight - whistle length` later, so it ends as the stone lands (flight from the horizontal distance
   over the launch's horizontal speed, or, with no circle, the arc back to the launch height under
   the projectile's gravity), and it is dropped if the shot lands early (`projectile_impact`). P19
   played the whistle at the telegraph, before the launch.
5. **Size gates answer with their own cue** (`size_gate_cue`): a `SizeGate` (group `size_gates`) by
   its `cue` at `last_contact` (where the giant touched it), else at the gate; a stage-gated prop
   by its material (`GATE_KINDS`: wood, foliage, cloth -> timber; stone, iron -> stone) at the prop; a
   ledge (an interactable) as stone; `sfx.gate.default` behind them.
6. **The friendly-settlement arrival**: when the giant's audio zone becomes a record with `arrival`
   and the giant stands at its `min_stage` (default 4) or above, `sfx.voice.crowd.gasp` sounds 25 m
   from the giant toward the settlement and `sfx.world.town_bell` at the record's first POI, **once
   a level visit** (cleared on `level_entered` and `level_leaving`), never while the record's faction
   is warned or hostile (checked by name: the factions arrive with P29 and P35).
7. **The campaign's completion**: `flag_changed` is now a state signal of the router (it stays
   deliberately silent); `campaign.completed` turning truthy calls `music.on_campaign_completed()`.
8. **A world event's alarm horn sounds at its record's `centre_m`** (the `world_event` schema's
   spelling; an encounter-style `center_m` is read too). P19's handler read `center_m` only, so the
   horn of every world event would have sounded at the giant.

The M7-M9 rows P19 had coded and P28 now gives cues and tests: the cavalry's gallop swell
(`sfx.cavalry.charge` on an action tagged `Charge` from a `cavalry` archetype), splashes from
`water_at` (the giant or a heavy body entering water deeper than 0.5 m) and `wade` footfalls, the
flush reaction, the rout stinger on `formation_changed(routed)`, the brace, broken-horn and
banner-fall cues, `poi_discovered` (`world_reveal` for a vista, else `discovery`), `travel_requested`
(fast travel), the siege engines' fire and abandon, `trampled`, `boss_yielded` and
`boss_disengaged`, `ancestry_chosen`, `world_event_ended` (`quest_complete` on the defended outcome).

**`music_director.gd`**
1. **A place that keeps its region's set applies its melody rules**: when the zone record changes
   and the set stays (Brannock inside the Crownmarch), `refresh_melody_rules()` sets the scheduler's
   rules again from the set's melody block and the record's `melody` override, keeping the
   scheduler's state (the layer override already followed the record every poll).
2. **`on_campaign_completed()`** plays `mus.stinger.crowned` (priority 95) before the campaign's
   end context.

**`ambience_director.gd`**
1. **Shared beds and emitters play on through a zone change**: a bed the new set shares with the old
   (same cue) keeps playing at the new set's level instead of cross-fading against itself; an emitter
   is the same when its cue and position are (`emitter_key`, to the decimetre).
2. **The formations' march** (§2.5's "up to two formation march emitters"): of the formations
   advancing (`Formation.live()`, state `advancing`, living members) within `MARCH_REACH_M` 120 m ×
   the distance scale of the listener, the nearest `MAX_MARCHES` 2 each carry `amb.march_loop` (a 3D
   stream on the Ambience bus, class big, priority 75 in its bank) at their anchor, following it
   every frame and fading in and out over 1 s; a formation that stops advancing, dissolves or is
   freed fades out. Nothing in a sandbox, a level that is silent by decision, or while the level's
   samples register.

## Public APIs

### CueRouter (`scripts/audio/cue_router.gd`, P28's additions)
- `voice_of(definition_id) -> String` (the mix, then the fallback); `static fallback_voice(
  definition_id) -> String`; `static voice_call(voice, call, is_player) -> String` (`roar` ->
  `bellow` for a giant that is not the player).
- `size_gate_cue(gate_id) -> Dictionary` (`{"cue", "at"}`).
- `static is_indirect(def: ActionDef) -> bool`; `static flight_seconds(from, velocity, landing,
  gravity) -> float`; `cue_seconds(cue_id) -> float` (the first file's length); `scheduled() -> Array`
  (requests waiting for their time: tests, F3).
- `arrivals() -> PackedStringArray` (the settlements announced this level visit).
- Constants: `ARCHETYPE_VOICES`, `SHORT_ID_VOICES`, `INDIRECT_VISUALS`, `LANDING_MEMORY_S`,
  `INCOMING_HEIGHT_M`, `GATE_KINDS`, `ARRIVAL_MIN_STAGE`, `ARRIVAL_GASP_M`; `STATE_SIGNALS` gains
  `flag_changed`.

### MusicDirector, AmbienceDirector
- `MusicDirector.refresh_melody_rules()`; `on_campaign_completed()` (now with the crowned stinger).
- `AmbienceDirector.MARCH_CUE`, `MAX_MARCHES`, `MARCH_REACH_M`; `marches` (formation id ->
  `{"player", "formation" (weak), "gain", "target", "volume_db"}`); `march_ids() ->
  PackedStringArray`; `static emitter_key(cue_id, pos) -> String`. The F3 line counts the marches.

### Data
- `audio_region.arrival`: `{"faction": "faction.<f>" (optional, by name), "min_stage": 1..10
  (default 4)}`.

### The pipeline (Python)
- `gen_world_music.py`: `REGIONS`, `melody_notes(region, variant)`, the boss frames' constants
  (`REGIMENT_*`, `MATRIARCH_*`, `HARROK_*`); `gen_world_ambience.py`, `gen_world_sfx.py`: `FAMILY`
  and the cue lists `build_audio.py` collects (`--only=world_music,world_ambience,world_sfx`).
- `test_synth.py WorldTests`: the regions' melodies fit the scheduler (every phrase inside its
  segment, three different variants), the bosses' frames are whole bars of their tempo, every world
  cue is the world's (scope, folder, one seed a file, seeds 280000-282999), the effects are samples
  of at most 2 s, and the budgets hold by the build's arithmetic (the estimate within 1 %).

## Decisions as built

- **The zone frame and P23's phrase writer for every region**, so the world's sets share the
  Valley's danger and combat stems, and the score stays one kingdom's; a region's colour is its
  drone and its melody's instrument and motif. Aldmere alone adds a stem (the snare) to combat.
- **Loudness balances the world's files** as it does P23's (drones −24 LUFS, melodies −19, the snare
  −21, boss stems −16 to −19, stingers −16; beds by LUFS; spots by peak and their banks' volumes).
- **Aliases instead of new samples** wherever a new file would add nothing (the evolutions, the
  engines' projectiles, the axe and fists, three spots): the world's registered seconds and bytes
  are its budget's.
- **The whistle starts at the launch, not at the telegraph**, timed to end at the landing: the
  plan's "a rising incoming cue there for the flight time" is about the flight, and a whistle during
  the windup would tell the giant the stone is already in the air.
- **A formation is heard as one body**: one wall per brace, one march loop while it advances, its
  members' footfalls silent while it holds its slots (thirty footsteps would be noise and would cost
  the pool's footfall voices).
- **The arrival is once per level visit**, not per entry: a giant pacing at a town's edge hears it
  once.
