# P54 — The wardrobe: garments and armour by slot, accessories

Plan: `docs/plans/M11_PLAN.md` §3.7 (and §1.1, §1.5-§1.7, §2.2, §2.4, §2.5, §2.6, §2.12, §2.13, §6.3). Wave W32, lane A
(the wave's asset writer), beside P55 (the creator II). Built on 19 November 2026 on `pkg/P54` from the tag `wave-31`
(0.11.0-m11 and P53); the version stays the release's (the wardrobe ships in 0.12). Every number here was taken on
the shared container (4 CPUs, no GPU: llvmpipe), the other lane's checks running beside it; the loads are given where
they matter.

The goal: the 25 new garments and 8 accessories of §2.6, each a part on demand, stacking without intersection, dyed
by material, within the giant's share. **All 25 garments and all 8 accessories were built; nothing on §6.3's cut list
was cut.** Twelve outfits, fourteen dyes.

## What was built

### 1. Slots and shells (`tools/giant/kit/giant_garment.py`, `Wardrobe`)

Every garment belongs to a slot; each slot has the plan's least standoff from the skin at stage 1
(`SLOT_SHELL_M`: legs 0.012, chest 0.014, shins 0.020, arms 0.022, feet 0.024, waist 0.030, armour 0.034, shoulders
0.046, head 0.020; the cape hangs free). The wardrobe is built slot by slot in `LAYER_ORDER` (legs, chest, shins, arms,
feet, waist, armour, cape, shoulders, head). After each slot the **envelope** grows: per body vertex, the farthest any
garment built so far stands off the skin along the skin's normal (a shell's own offset; for a band, the braies and a
cloak, its vertices measured over the skin vertex each stands over, plus `MAPPED_MARGIN_M`). Every garment of a later
slot keeps at least `LAYER_GAP_M` (0.012 m at stage 1) outside that envelope wherever its footprint covers it, the
clearance eased off beyond the inner garment's edge in a ramp (`RAMP_SLOPE`) across its own footprint only. So an
outer shell clears the loosest inner garment of every inner slot, and any two garments of different slots can be worn
together without sharing a surface.

Order decisions the plan's table leaves open: the cape lies over the armour and under the shoulders (a mantle or a
pauldron goes over a cloak's top; the cloak lies on the back down to `hang_below` 0.29 m under the shoulders and hangs
from there, so the shoulders' garments never have to clear its hanging cloth), and the head garments come last (their
footprint begins above the collars).

Kept true in every pose: a shell's vertex carries the weights **and now the normal** of the skin vertex it stands on
(`export_game.py`), so under the skin's linear blend two shells over one skin vertex stay on one line, and the build
sliders' push (`VERTEX += NORMAL * ...`, P53) moves every layer exactly as the skin under it. The cut garments' caps
keep the cloth's own normal at their rim and take no push at their centre (`split_part(..., skin_rims=True)`).

The cuts are cleaner: a hem is snapped onto its cut level along the skin's tangent plane (`snaps`), the stair steps of
the body's quads are eased along the boundary (`ease_boundary`), single teeth of a cut are removed (`clean_faces`), and
the ragged hems move down the skin's tangent plane (they keep their height). The tunic's sleeve is cut across the arm
(`t_up` 0.6) instead of at a plane of x (P53's follow-up: the sleeve edge stepped at build -1).

### 2. The garments (25 new, 35 in all)

Built in the plan's order (the cut list's reverse): loincloth, trousers, kilt (legs: the kilt's pleats run round the
hips, the loincloth has deep flaps before and behind); hood (head, hides all hair); mail hauberk (armour: short sleeves
and a skirt to mid-thigh, the mail detail map); leather cuirass; iron pauldrons (both shoulders, soft lames); iron
vambraces; greaves (shin and knee front, a keel); long cloak (the cape carried on 0.80 m below the body's cut in ten rows,
flaring back and out); shirt (long sleeves, split); sleeveless vest (leather, a V opening); plated war belt (a band of
14 raised plates); fur mantle; leather cap; iron helm (a crest, a brow band); fur cloak (fur, 0.48 m below the cut);
arm wraps; chest wrap (a band and a strap from the left shoulder); sash (a band with a knotted end at the left hip);
wrapped sandals (a sole, a lattice of straps up the ankle, instep straps); bronze breastplate (a keel); scale coat (the
scale detail map, a skirt); horned helm (the iron helm's shape with two curved horns, 100 % on `head`); bone
spaulders (both shoulders, bone plates: the cloth shader's bone kind, in their own colour). **Mail and scale are shells
with a detail map** (`cloth_mail.png`, `cloth_scale.png`, 256 px, drawn by `pack.py`), not modelled rings.

### 3. Head garments and hair (`hides_hair`, the index's `hair_cut`)

The hood hides every hair style ("all"). The leather cap, the iron helm and the horned helm cut the hair ("top"): the
export measures, for each style, the first point along its cards' length (rounded down to 0.05) where a card stands
within the gap of the helm's shell or beyond it over the helm's footprint; the style is drawn cut there by the hair
shader's `length`, or not at all when less than 0.3 would remain (`export_game.hair_cut`). Measured: the crop (a shell
style) is drawn whole; the long back and the long braided hair keep 0.3 of their length; the warrior's tail and the wild
mane are not drawn under a helm. `GiantLook.hair_cut()` reads it; `apply` hides or shortens the style.

### 4. Accessories (8)

Rigid meshes on one bone each, at most 400 triangles: the iron torc and the gilded torc (open at the front, knobbed
terminals) and a tooth necklace on `spine_03`; an iron arm ring, a bronze arm spiral and a gilded arm ring on
`upperarm_l`; a drinking horn and a beast's jaw on `pelvis`. Each is placed clear of the whole wardrobe's envelope
(the neck pieces clear the neck's own skin and what is worn on the neck: a fur collar's rim further out is soft, and a
torc sits on it). The rings take the skin's build weights and normal (they move with the neck or the arm the build
sliders swell or thin); the trophies hang rigid, placed clear of the fullest build. Each draws in its own colour (the
index's `color`) with the cloth shader's metal or bone kind.

### 5. Coverage

Each garment's `covers` (the design regions it hides) is generated from the index's measured coverage (a region is
covered when every body region of it is a mesh's `covers` or has 0.75 of its coverage), so the data and the index
agree for every garment and every outfit (`test_giant_look_real`).

### 6. Outfits, dyes, metals (`data/appearance/`)

Twelve outfits (M10's six unchanged, and huscarl's mail, hooded traveller, bronze lord, bone reaver, scale guard,
highland herdsman, each dressing most of the ten slots; the huscarl's dresses all ten: the fullest slot look); fourteen
dyes (soot black, ochre, heather, slate grey, rust, pine green added); the five metals of P53. The presets are
unchanged (each is its M10 record migrated: `test_appearance_migration` checks it).

### 7. The index v2, the share, the draw calls, the mesh buffers

`meshes[<id>]` gains `slot`, `shell` (the slot's offset), `material`, `hides_hair`, `hair_cut`; an accessory `slot`,
`bone`, `color`; the index gains `accessories` and `slots` (`shell_m`, `layers`). The export records each garment's own
least and greatest standoff (`export.slots`) and the shells' distances (`export.shell_distances`, `shell_poses`).

## The cloth shader's kinds and GiantLook (`assets/shaders/giant_cloth.gdshader`, `scripts/presentation/giant_look.gd`)

- `kind` 1 (metal) now keeps a part of its colour as diffuse (metallic 0.55, specular 0.65) and its detail map's dark
  gaps dull: with no reflections to mirror (the world's sky is a colour, the creator's backdrop is dark) the plate drew
  black in the first renders. Mail and scale use it with their own maps.
- `kind` 2 (new): bone - a pale aged bone under the dye's faint stain, a soft sheen, no metal.
- `GiantLook.DETAIL_STRENGTH`, `CLOTH_RIM`, `CLOTH_DETAIL`, `DEFAULT_TEXTURES` know mail, scale and bone;
  `DETAIL_KIND` gives the shader's kind per detail map; an accessory draws in its index `color`; `hair_cut()` and the
  hair's visibility and `length` under a head garment.

## Deviations

- **M10's garments moved by more than a few millimetres** where an inner slot has a looser garment (the table above).
- **The hair under a "top" helm**: the plan's `length` cut, measured, leaves the long styles 0.3 of their length and
  hides the tail and the wild mane; a cut by a plane in the hair shader would keep the hair below the helm's rim
  (FOLLOWUPS, for P56, the hair shader's owner).
- **The cape slot's order and the cloak's hang**: the cape lies on the back down to 0.29 m under the shoulders (M10's
  hung from the shoulder blades), so the shoulders' garments lie over it; the cape covers the upper back too now.
- **The shells' distance of a cloak** counts the cloth lying on the body; its hanging cloth (on the cape chains, posed
  by the springs in play) is kept `HANG_MARGIN_M` (0.05 m) clear at rest instead.
- **The draw-call budget is asserted on meshes** (the plan's own count); the renderer's count adds the hair's and
  the beard's pre-passes and is reported and kept at most 28.
- **The giant's estimate is judged by the -m12 budget** (`build_giant.ASSET_MILESTONE`): the wardrobe ships in 0.12 and
  the version string still reads -m11 (P58 sets it). The Web build was run with `--version=0.12.0-m12` for the same reason.
- **Owned paths stretched**: `export_game.py`, `giant_parts.py` (the chains' let-go height) and
  `scripts/presentation/giant_look.gd` (the detail kinds, the accessories' colour, the helms' cut) are not in §3.7's
  owned list; the first two are the wave's asset writer's (`tools/giant/**`), the third holds the cloth kinds the plan
  gives P54 and the hair cut its item 3 needs.
