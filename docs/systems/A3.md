# A3 — Stoneback felt and placed: his presence, his weight, and his quarry beside Millford

A2 built Stoneback's fight in a test arena. A3 makes him read as the bigger giant and move with weight, shows his
plates cracking, gives his sweep and boulder their own sounds, tunes the fight again for his new size, and puts him
in the open world: a quarry cut into a hill north-west of Millford, his territory, a preparation quest and a
settlement that changes when he falls. Built on `pkg/a3` from the A2 merge (`0.14.0-m14`). The intro cinematic, the
Character menu and save schema 3 are not part of it. The seeded 120 s soak is unchanged (41 attacks, 33 hits
resolved, 4 enemy kills, 2 fence breaks, 7,201 frames, 0 errors).

## His height and posture (`tools/giant/kit/stoneback.py`, `stoneback_moves.py`)

A2's stills showed him no taller than the stage-5 giant: his mesh was hunched 14 degrees about the mid-back with the
head carried 1.4 m forward, and his walk (the brutes' gait: a 0.44 x height stride, a 9-degree lean) dropped his
crown 1.1-2.3 m below his idle's at every step. What changed:

| | A2 | A3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Height (`HEIGHT_M`, the record's `height_m`) | 16.8 m (55 ft) | **17.7 m (58 ft)** (decision A3-1: a deviation from the story's 55 ft) |
| Mesh hunch / head turned back (`STONE_SHAPE`) | 14 / 11 degrees | 6 / 5 degrees: the head over the shoulders (its joint 0.88 m ahead of the pelvis at rest, was 1.36 m) |
| Shoulders (`STONE_PROP`) | -0.05 (narrowed) | 0 (the full breadth; the slabs follow the body) |
| Gait (`STONE_GAITS`, `STEP_M`) | the brutes' walk and run, a 0.44 x height step | his own: a 0.33 x height step (5.8 m), a 4-degree lean, 3-degree pelvis pitch, a 16-degree heel strike |
| Crown in the idle | 16.57-16.74 m | **17.49-17.61 m** |
| Crown walking | 14.34-15.58 m | **16.94-17.13 m** (a 0.2 m bob) |
| Crown running | 14.83-15.55 m | 16.63-17.04 m |
| Crown at the wind-ups' peaks (overhead / charge / throw) | 16.19 / 14.28 / 15.97 m | 16.79 / **16.03** / 16.64 m (the charge's lean braced: pelvis 14 -> 10 degrees, knees 0.25 -> 0.14) |

Against the giant (14.63 m at stage 5, 18.90 m at stage 6): walking he stands 2.3-2.5 m (a head and more) over a
stage-5 giant, idle 2.9 m; a stage-6 giant still overtops him by 1.3 m. The manifest records each standing clip's
crown (`checks.crown_m`); `test_people_tools.TestStoneback.test_he_stands_tall` holds them (idle >= 97 % of his
height, walk >= 94 % with a bob under 0.5 m, run >= 92 %, the roar >= 95 %, each wind-up's peak >= 89 %, the walk a
head over stage 5). Proportions are the brutes' (legs 0.97, torso 1.02, heavy hands and feet); the shin plates and
the slabs are the same real thickness (the plates' fit test is scaled with his height). His body's hurtboxes,
anchors, capsule (3.6 x 17.7 m), weapon pivot and boulder launch point were measured again from the idle's first key
(`tools/builders/titans.gd`); the shoulders became boxes over both slabs and the deltoid they cover (11.4-16.2 m).

## Weight in motion

- **The smash** (`stoneback_moves.smash`): from the overhead's peak he sinks into his knees with the trunk cocked
  further back (`SMASH_DIP` 0.10 s), comes over the top, strikes (0.30 s as before) and carries on lower past the
  blow - the fists follow the body down the trunk's line, so the root ball stays where it struck - then rebounds.
  TitanRig plays the overhead over 72 % of the windup (was 80 %) so the dip reads (about 0.28 s at 1.7 s).
- **The sweep**: wound 42 degrees (was 31) and sunk lower before the swing; past his left side the trunk's weight
  carries him on round (36 degrees of pelvis) before he checks it.
- **The charge's wind-up**: the weight rocks back onto the planted rear foot (its toe dug in further) through the
  middle of the windup before it is thrown over the leading shoulder.
- **The reel**: the left hand braces on the left thigh instead of rising to the brow - between the keys the arm used
  to swing up past the head (15.6 m) and back; now no hand rises above 11 m.
- **The trunk falls from his fist** (TitanRig): 0.45 s into the death clip the skinned club is hidden and a static
  copy (the hand's rigid motion of its rest space) topples about the root ball, accelerating, while the root ball
  comes down onto the ground; it bounces a little and raises dust (`fallen_club_ends()`).
- **Dust and shake**: `DustBurst` (`scripts/presentation/dust_burst.gd`), a one-shot cloud of CPU particles (soft
  billboards that rise, spread and fade; the compatibility renderer draws them): the smash's impact (and a camera
  shake on the giant by distance, full within 18 m, none beyond 90 m), each footfall of his walk, run and charge
  (a lighter shake; dust under the run's), a plate breaking, the fallen trunk. The smash already rang the giant's
  camera through `area_pulse`; this adds distance and the footfalls.

## The plates crack (`assets/shaders/stone_plate.gdshader`, `StonebackRig`, `TitanRig`)

The stone shader's `crack` (0..1) opens a network of dark, jagged cracks (the edges of Voronoi cells, wobbled by the
texture's own light pattern) with a little dust at their lips - more of the network and wider as it rises; iron and
the matte colours never crack. Each breakable plate has its own material (`StonebackRig.plate_materials`); TitanRig
shows a plate's crack by its stone left: whole above 60 %, **cracked** (0.5) below, **badly cracked** (0.9) below
30 %, gone when broken. A new attempt makes them whole.

## His own sounds (`tools/audio/gen_world_sfx.py`, "titans")

Two synthesized sounds (the project's synth library; no download): `stoneback_sweep` (1.05 s: a vast low rush of
air at its loudest as the trunk passes his front, the trunk's creak, the roots and clods rattling, a scrape where it
skims the ground) and `stoneback_rip` (1.0 s: the turf and roots tearing, the stone grinding loose with a deep heave,
clods and grit falling). `sfx.ability.stoneback_sweep.sweep`, `_sweep_rage.sweep` and `stoneback_boulder.cast` play
them, registered in the world and in the quarry test arena (`sandbox`). The world's registered SFX seconds:
167.81 -> **169.86 of 170**; the imported size 9.247 MB of 9.5.

## The quarry (`tools/data/world/`, `tools/world/`)

- **The ground**: `quarry_hill` (a hill, top 34 m, centred at (-655, 405)) and `stoneback_quarry`, the new feature
  type `quarry` (`WorldFeatures._quarry`): a floor 68 m across at 10.5 m around (-600, 452), ringed by three
  benches behind 7 m cut faces (3 m across) wherever the hill stands higher; the ground keeps its own height where it
  is lower, so the pit opens east toward Millford (280 m to the south-east of its centre, the hamlet's centre
  290 m). Its floor and benches are hard ground (`in_quarry`) and clear of scenery (a pad). The quarry road (12 m,
  carved) leaves the Woodway at (-443, 500) for the pit's mouth.
- **The works** (`tools/world/kinds/quarry.gd`, new kind `quarry`): `terrace` - the cut faces as courses of dressed
  stone over the terrain's risers (a metre in front of each riser's foot, past its top: the terrain's 4 m grid
  zigzags across a riser, and the courses cover it), only where the bench above is cut, solids his charge meets;
  `monolith` - four leaning standing stones on the floor (the charge's bait); `crane` (a treadwheel crane; `broken`
  while he holds the quarry), `shed`, `blocks`, `cart` (`loaded`, `overturned`), `spoil`.
- **The reopened quarry** is shown by `world.quarry.reopened`: the working cranes, the loaded carts on the road and
  one in Millford, a mason's yard and a stall in Millford, Millford's quarry forge open (a `condition_forge`, iron
  tier), the quarry works' encounter (Old Bram and five quarrymen); the broken cranes and the overturned cart hide.
- Rebuilt with the world builder: chunks c0_4-c2_6 changed (c1_5 holds the quarry), their human, large and titan
  navmeshes; a second build writes the same bytes; the seam check passes. POIs 48 -> 49 (`poi.stoneback_quarry`, a
  lair on the map and the compass).

## The encounter (`encounter.crownmarch_stoneback`, `TitanTerritory`)

He stands at home in the pit (-606, 456) from the start of the open world (no flag required). His territory
(`scripts/world/titan_territory.gd`, a `BossArena` without a gate; the layout's `arena` element of style `titan`):

- **Sight**: a living giant within 110 m of the quarry's centre, standing in the Crownmarch, engages him - the boss
  bar, his music, the lock-on to his plates (every boss's), `boss_engaged`, and the quarry discovered ("sighted").
  The Woodway passes 127 m away (a traveller to the Greywood is not his business); the quarry road leads into his
  sight. The engage trigger on the pit's floor engages too.
- **The hunt**: while engaged he is told where the giant is every second (`EnemyBrain.on_noise`, certain), so he
  follows it past his own sight and memory.
- **Escape**: beyond 230 m of the centre (`inner_radius` 210 + 20) for 5 s the hunt ends (`boss_disengaged`
  "left"): his new attempt - full health, every plate whole - and he walks home. Millford (290 m) lies beyond the
  line: the hamlet is the giant's refuge; he never comes in among its houses. A death reloads the checkpoint: he is
  at home, whole.
- **His death**: the defeated flag `boss.stoneback.defeated`, his record's outcome `boss.stoneback.outcome = slain`,
  `boss_defeated` (GameSession's checkpoint after the kill), his loot (`loot.stoneback`: `item.stoneback_heart_stone`,
  4 `item.stoneback_slab`, 3 `item.quarry_iron`); his encounter forbids his outcome and his death persists, so he
  never returns. `world.quarry.reopened` is set once the giant has gone beyond his line after the kill (or on any
  later load of his ground): the change shows behind the giant's back, not at his corpse.

**The director's rules, and how each is answered** (`EncounterDirector`, its A3 section; the encounter's record):

| Rule | The conflict | The answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The leash (70 m for the Crownmarch) | a leash would send him home in the middle of the hunt | an arena encounter (`arena`: no leash); the territory is his leash (230 m) and sends him home whole |
| The AI LOD (tiers at 40 / 90 m) | beyond 90 m his brain would run at the far tier while he walks out and hunts | `lod_near_m` 400: his members stay at the near tier within 400 m of the giant |
| A chunk unload | despawns members in an unloading chunk | unchanged, and safe: his 230 m line lies within the streamer's keep distance (unload at 3 chunks), so his chunk stays loaded while he hunts; fast travel away despawns him (no death recorded) and he returns at home, whole |
| The 12-combatant budget | a full budget (a raid, wanderers) would keep him out of the world | `reserve_seat`: while the giant is within his activation radius (300 m: Millford's raid, 400 m from him, is not touched) and he lives, his seat is reserved (`p34_reserve`: others wait for room) and room is made for him (`p34_make_room`: the farthest other combatants leave, to return on their own activations); released beyond the radius, at his death, when cleared |

## Preparation and aftermath (data-driven: `quest.stoneback`, `dialogue.quarryman_bram`, `dialogue.reeve_tamsin`)

- **Old Bram** (`npc.quarryman_bram`), Millford's quarry foreman, waits on the hamlet's west side until the quarry
  reopens: where Stoneback sleeps (the old pit, his back to the cut face, the road in his sight; look from the
  Woodway bend), how to fight him (do not trade blows; bait his charge into the standing stones - he reels with his
  back turned; the slab on his back cracks before it breaks, his weak point under it; the shoulders if you can reach
  that high). After the kill: he is gathering the quarrymen; once reopened, he runs the quarry.
- **Reeve Tamsin** warns of the quarry giant (`troubles` -> `quarry_warn`, outcome `stoneback`) and, once he has
  fallen, thanks the giant before anything else (`quarry_thanks`, outcome `quarry_freed`), then greets it as
  Millford's own (`honoured`).
- **The quest** "The Giant in the Quarry" starts from Tamsin's warning, Bram's talk, coming near the lookout or the
  quarry's discovery: ask Bram (50 XP), look from the Woodway bend (60 XP and 2 iron: an edge for the club at a
  forge), bring him down (400 XP; his own 1,600), tell Tamsin (120 XP, titan's iron and
  `world.millford.quarry_freed`).
- **Millford after**: the forge lit, a stall and a mason's yard, a loaded stone cart by the dam, the works and their
  carts on the road, Bram at the quarry, the reeve's words.

## What still looks or plays weak (honestly)

- **Stage 5 is not shown winnable by the bot.** Its swing reaches his shoulder plates only a few times a duel and its
  damage comes from abilities (8-10 a second against stage 6's 36); its best duel left him at 40 % after 6:08. Every
  knob that kept stage 5 alive longer kept stage-4 giants alive too. A human's stage-5 win is unproven.
- **Stage 6 is a 2:20-3:05 fight**, the low end of the 3-5 minutes asked; more health made the bot lose instead.
- **The quarry is the world's flat-shaded parts**: the cut faces are stacked boxes in two stone tones, the standing
  stones blocky leaning slabs with a pyramid cap, the spoil heaps cones; the terrain's benches show the 4 m grid's
  facets where no face stands, and the hill around the pit is a smooth mound. It reads as a terraced quarry from the
  road and from Millford; close up it is a kit of boxes.
- **His motion is still key poses slerped**, with the dip and overshoot now: no secondary motion (the kilt, the
  chains, the belly), the walk's bob is small (0.2 m) for his weight, and his idle stands very upright - a little
  statue-like beside the brutes' hunch it replaced.
- **His dust is small**: a few soft billboards a few metres across; the plates' broken stone is still the debris
  pool's grey boxes, and the falling trunk is a rigid topple with a little bounce (it does not roll).
- **The cracks are a regular cell network** - read as cracked stone from a few tens of metres, as a crackle glaze
  close up.
- **His territory sees by distance**, through the hill's shoulder as well as across the open pit.
- **The reopened quarry's people stand still** (civilians at their posts), and his music is Harrok's set.
- **The hurtboxes still do not follow the clips** (as every boss's): crouched in the rip or the reel, the shoulders'
  boxes stay where he stands upright.
- **Stills from the capture tour** show the height side by side honestly (orthographic), but the fight's
  perspective shots put the giant nearer the camera than him: there he reads only a little taller.
