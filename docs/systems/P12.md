# P12 — Progression, attributes, loadout, inventory, loot and the forge upgrade

Wave 5, lane B (beside P10b). Branch `pkg/P12`. Plan: `docs/plans/M2_M5_PLAN.md` §3.14, with the
contracts in §2.4 (`xp_award_requested`, `xp_gained`, `level_changed`, `ability_learned`,
`attributes_changed`, `upgrade_applied`, `loot_awarded`), §2.6.1 (`level`, `level_exempt`,
`recovery_scale`), §2.7.1 (the `progression` component), §2.7.4 (`Forge`), §2.8.2 (item fields and
`loot_table` refs), §2.8.3 (`loot_table`, `progression.default`), §2.9 (the character's progression
keys), §2.10 (the XP bar, the ability notice, the inventory, attributes and forge screens, the
Loadout and Attributes shrine tabs), §2.12 (`forge.tscn`), §4.7 (XP, abilities, weapons, loot and the
upgrade) and decisions 6, 7 and 20.

## What was built

- **Progression rules** (`scripts/progression/progression.gd`, `class_name Progression`, pure): the
  XP table of `progression.default` (levels 2–6 at 100 / 250 / 450 / 700 / 1,000 XP in total, cap
  20), `add_xp` (3 attribute points and 1 talent point per level), `allocate` (the six attributes,
  whole non-negative points, no more than unspent), `derived_preview` (the maxima, poise, recovery,
  resistance and damage scale an attribute set gives, through a scratch `Combatant`), the abilities
  a level and stage meet, where a learned ability is auto-slotted, and the progression post-pass.
- **Loot** (`scripts/progression/loot.gd`, `class_name Loot`): `roll(table_id)` (guaranteed items,
  then `rolls` weighted picks with `Sim.rng_for("loot")`), `award_once(id, table, recipient)` through
  `WorldState.try_award_loot`, so an enemy or a prop drops at most once even across reloads.
- **PlayerProgression** (`scripts/actors/player/player_progression.gd`, the `progression` component):
  level, XP, points, attributes, ancestry and `level_exempt` in the character state; XP from
  `xp_award_requested`, from the player's kills of ENEMY actors (`xp_reward`) and from F8; level-ups
  that push the level into the controller (once gated), announce and learn the abilities the new
  level meets; `learn_ability(id, source)` for rewards; kill loot (the actor's per-spawn
  `loot_table`, else its record's); allocation; the shrine-only loadout.
- **Forge** (`scripts/world/forge.gd`, `class_name Forge extends Interactable`) and its scene
  (`tools/builders/forge.gd` → `scenes/world/forge.tscn`: a stone hearth with a chimney and glowing
  coals, an iron anvil on a block, sized for a giant): the one upgrade interaction. It reforges the
  equipped weapon into its reinforced variant for its cost (2 iron), equips it, drops the old one
  from the owned list and emits `upgrade_applied`; the village forge needs a help outcome.
- **Screens**: inventory (`ui/screens/inventory_screen.gd`), attributes
  (`ui/screens/attributes_screen.gd`) and forge (`ui/screens/forge_screen.gd`), the shrine's Loadout
  and Attributes tabs (`ui/screens/shrine/{loadout_tab,attributes_tab}.gd`) and the shared
  `AttributePanel` (`ui/screens/attribute_panel.gd`).
- **HUD widgets**: the XP bar (`ui/hud/xp_bar.gd`) and the new-ability notice
  (`ui/hud/ability_notice.gd`), which waits until the giant is out of combat.
- **BreakableProp loot tables**: a prop rolls its loot table (its own `loot_table` export, else the
  record's) beside the inline `loot`, inside the one `try_award_loot(world_id)`.
- **Schemas and data**: the `loot_table` record type, item `category` / `quest_item`, `loot_table`
  refs on enemies and props, the forge-cost rule on weapons, the progression post-pass; four loot
  tables and three items; the crate rolls `loot.crate_quarry`.

- **Level gating** is on only after `apply_state` applied a character level below 99 (New Game,
  Continue, checkpoint reloads, tests that apply a character). Then `controller.level` follows
  `level`. A fresh giant (the sandbox, most tests) keeps `controller.level = 99`; it still earns XP,
  levels and points (spendable at the sandbox shrine), but nothing is ever locked. Its captured
  `level` is 99, and applying 99 keeps gating off; the level shown then comes from the XP.
- **XP sources.** `Events.xp_award_requested(amount, source)` (props with `xp_reward` — P08 sends
  `"prop:<world_id>"` — and later quests and dialogue); kills: `Events.entity_died` whose killer is
  the player's entity id (weapon blows, projectiles, throws and crushes all carry it) of a team
  ENEMY actor in group `enemies` gives its record's `xp_reward` (source `"kill:<entity_id>"`). A
  NEUTRAL actor (a dummy, a village guard before the village turns hostile) gives nothing, and a
  prop never pays twice (it pays through `xp_award_requested` only). F8 (`debug_xp`, debug builds):
  +100 (`"debug"`).
- **A level-up** (`add_xp`): `xp_gained(tick, amount, source, total)`, then `level_changed(tick, old,
  new)` once, the HUD line "Level 3! +3 attribute points, +1 talent point", the level into the
  controller (when gated), and every newly met library ability learned (source `"level"`). A growth
  (`growth_changed` for the player, upwards) also learns what the new stage meets (`"growth"`);
  P05's profile unlocks (Boulder Hurl) are learned by PlayerGrowth first.
- **Learning** (`learn_ability`): into `GiantPlayer.known_abilities` and the controller's library;
  into `controller.level_exempt` when its `unlock_level` is above the character level (a reward
  taught early is usable at once); auto-slotted (`Progression.auto_slot`); `ability_learned(tick, id,
  slot)` and, when slotted, `loadout_changed`.
- **Kill loot**: the killed actor's `loot_table` export (the director's per-spawn override), else its
  record's `loot_table`, through `Loot.award_once(entity_id, …)`. An enemy without a table leaves no
  delta.
- **Loadout** (`set_slot`): only with a shrine whose record has `loadout` true, outside combat
  (`pools.in_combat()`); a known ability of the right kind (ultimates only in `ultimate`); an
  ability already in another slot swaps places with this slot's; `loadout_changed`.
- **Attributes** (`allocate`): `Combatant.set_attributes` (the maxima keep their fractions;
  CombatEntity pushes Dexterity's `recovery_scale` to the controller), then `attributes_changed`.
- **Save state**: `level` (99 while ungated), `xp`, `unspent_attribute_points`, `talent_points`,
  `attributes` (six ints), `level_exempt` (ids), `ancestry` (`CharacterState.PROGRESSION_KEYS`).
  `known_abilities` and the loadout stay GiantPlayer's core keys (applied after the components); the
  component learns into the same list. A restore is silent (no events, no notices).

## Decisions and deviations

1. **Doc name.** `docs/systems/P12.md` (the task and §1.1 item 5); §3.14's owned paths name
   `P12-progression-inventory.md`.
2. **Auto-slot uses the ability's §4.7 slot first.** The plan says "first empty slot". At level 3 a
   stage-1 giant meets Runic Bolt before growing (Boulder Hurl needs stage 2), so "first empty"
   would put Runic Bolt into slot 5 and Boulder Hurl into slot 6, against the §4.7 slot table and
   the hurl tutorial's `{slot_5}`. A learned ability now goes into its preferred slot (its position
   among the library's non-ultimate abilities — the library is in §4.7 order) when that is free,
   else into the first free slot; an ultimate goes into the ultimate slot when it is free. Stone
   Skin still lands in slot 3 at level 2 (the planned test), and P05's growth unlock of Boulder Hurl
   (first empty) then finds slot 5 free.
3. **Gating off means level 99 in the save.** A giant that never had a character applied is
   ungated; its captured `level` is 99 (as before P12), so a test level played in the sandbox mode
   reloads ungated. Applying level 99 keeps gating off and derives the shown level from the XP.
4. **`xp` is the total.** The save's `xp` is the total earned (the §2.9 example, level 3 with 268,
   is consistent with that); the XP bar shows the part inside the current level.
5. **Kill XP needs team ENEMY and the group `enemies`.** The plan's rule ("never for NEUTRAL") plus
   P08's note: a prop's CombatEntity also emits `entity_died`, and props already pay through
   `xp_award_requested`; requiring an enemy actor keeps the gate and the barricade from paying twice.
   Kill XP is per death (a respawned non-persistent enemy pays again; every Valley spawn persists its
   death); loot is once per entity id.
6. **The old weapon leaves the owned list through `PlayerEquipment.apply_state`** (with only
   `weapons_owned`): `player_equipment.gd` is P08's file and has no remove call; `apply_state` with
   only that key replaces the owned list and keeps the equipped weapon.
7. **The forge checks LOCOMOTION before it takes the cost**, so equipping the reinforced weapon
   (which needs LOCOMOTION) cannot fail after paying.
8. **A closed forge does not open its screen**: E shows `closed_message` (an extra export with a
   default; the layout only needs the four §2.7.4 exports). An open forge opens its screen even when
   the weapon cannot be reforged, and the screen says why.
9. **Crates per placement.** `prop.crate` defaults to `loot.crate_quarry`; BreakableProp gained a
   `loot_table` export (like `EnemyActor.loot_table`), so the armory crate is
   `prop.crate` + `loot_table = "loot.crate_armory"`. Both give one iron either way.
10. **`AttributePanel` is a new file** (`ui/screens/attribute_panel.gd`) outside §3.14's owned list:
    the Attributes screen and the shrine's Attributes tab share it. No other package uses the path.
11. **The inventory's combat refusal lives in the screen** (`on_open` posts "Not during combat" and
    closes it): ScreenStack (P03) is not P12's file, and the check then covers every way of opening
    it.
12. **Raider loot is wood scrap**, never iron: §4.7 counts all five ingots (village 2, quarry chest,
    armory crate, Ogre King), so a raider table with iron would break the upgrade schedule.
13. **The progression post-pass calls `PlayerEquipment.upgrade_problems`** (as P08 suggested) and adds
    the one-step rule; GameData therefore depends on `Progression` (and through it on
    `PlayerEquipment`), like GameSession already depends on GiantPlayer.
14. **Learning on growth.** Besides level-ups, a growth (upwards, for the player) learns the library
    abilities the new stage meets, so an ability gated by stage and level is never missed; P05's
    profile `unlocks` run first.
15. **Existing tests changed.** `test_shrine.test_shrine_screen_rest_and_grow_tabs` expected the tabs
    `["Rest", "Grow"]` with the note "P12 adds Loadout and Attributes"; it now expects all four (the
    plan's intended change). Suites whose check counts changed because they iterate what exists:
    `test_anchors` 300 → 318, `test_scene_kit` 89 → 90 (the forge builder), `test_player_components`
    99 → 93 (the progression keys are owned now, so their passthrough checks are skipped) and
    `test_schemas_v2` 325 → 324 (`loot_table` is no longer a type without a module).
