and content rows, and the web world check `tools/web/world_check.mjs`). Branch `pkg/P39`, made from `wave-22`.

## Decisions and deviations

- **Iteration 0's plan said the bolt's poise 140 -> 100**; it stays 140, because decision 21 and P32's test want a
  bolt to stagger a stage-4 giant. Only its power fell.
- **The Iron Regiment's Charge threshold** (`tools/data/world/aldmere.json`, the arena's phase `below`, 0.6) is a
  layout number: this lane left it to the merge with P39f's whole-world rebuild. It must go above both
  compositions' bars after the Wall (desktop 0.622, web about 0.67): about 0.70, then a rebuild of the arena's
  chunk and `w.order_crown:novice:1` and the stage-4 battle run again. Until then the crown order is a miss.
- **The lone soldier's awe** (`tactics.awe_morale_loss` 35; 46 or more makes a lone soldier flee a stage-4 giant on
  sight) is left for the merged code, where P39f changed the raiders' rout; it is the confirmation phase's.
- **The Iron Regiment's web aggregate** (11,740) is kept by decision, not tuned: the battle's phases are fractions of
  its own aggregate, but the web's smaller Wall leaves the bar at about 0.67 after the Wall against the desktop's
  0.622, so the Charge's threshold above must clear both (FOLLOWUPS: the web aggregate's row is struck as kept, and
  the threshold stays open in the Iron Regiment's row).
- **The world walk's route**: plan §6.3's world walk is "a route through every region at stage 6 sprint, a settlement
  and a border walk"; the world's default route (`walk_route`, about 400 m, three regions) is the web checks' and
  stays as it is, so the capture passes its tour with `--route=` (in `docs/PERFORMANCE.md`), `--walk-stage=6` and
  `--walk-speed=16` (the stage-6 sprint, carried).
- **The two engines of the formation stress** are placed by the stress scenario at run time, not built into the
  formation test level: the formations builder runs before the siege builder (`tools/build_scenes.gd`), so it
  cannot instance the engines' scenes, and the other users of the level (P30's tests) keep it as it was.
- **The bot batches' per-region CPU** comes from the probe's sections, not from a second probe: one probe a run, as
  P17a set it up.
- **The pace target** (75-140 minutes of human estimate) is judged, as §6.4 writes it, on the unassisted novice's
  completed runs (the main path played straight); exploration (`--bot-explore`) was measured on one assisted seed:
  30 % of each oath region's places adds 3.5 minutes. The miss is the report's, not a data lever's.
- **The report's set** is the novice's nine runs made again on the final numbers (`p39_rep_world`, on `00a2bad`)
  with iteration 3's nine expert runs (`p39_i3_world`, on `cee2edd`), not "the latest run of each slot" of the tune
  part, which mixed iteration 2's five novice runs (the Matriarch at 15 / 25 hp/s) into it. The two codes play the
  same game: the four slots iteration 3 had run replayed to the tick on `00a2bad`, so the probe's region sections
  change nothing in the game. The experts were not run again (their nine runs are iteration 3's on the final data),
  so their CPU per tick carries no region sections. Since fix-up 1 the set is `p39_report_set_fx1`: the two
  conqueror novices come from `p39_fx1_world` (`331d3b4`, the novice without the lure); the other seven novice runs
  never meet the lure and the expert's den is the same code, so they stand.
- **The captures were taken twice**: on 12 November beside the other lane's work and two of this lane's bot runs
  (the draft's numbers), and on 13 November, after a container restart, with nothing else running (the numbers in
  `docs/PERFORMANCE.md` and `docs/HOW_IT_PLAYS.md`); both sets are in the evidence folder (`perf/`).
- **No fourth iteration; one bot change after the report (fix-up 1)**: the report's review showed that the conqueror
  novice's slay of the Matriarch on seed 1 was lost to iteration 3's brazier lure (with its walk clock's restart
  removed and nothing else, the run slew her and completed), so the lure - a rule of play this lane added, which made
  the novice play worse - is now the expert's alone. Made again without it, the novice still does not slay her
  (`docs/BALANCE_LOG.md`, "M9 (P39), fix-up 1"): no data changed, the plan allows three iterations.
