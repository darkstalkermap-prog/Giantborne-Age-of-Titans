# A2 — Stoneback's fight, in a test arena (not yet in the open world)

The open world's first signature titan fights: Stoneback, the 55 ft (16.8 m) quarry giant of A1, with breakable stone
plates over weak points, a trunk-club smash, a sweep, a boulder torn from the ground and a shoulder charge, a rage
phase, a death and loot. Built on `pkg/a2` from `0.14.0-m14` (A1's merge). He stands in the quarry test arena
(`scenes/test/quarry_test.tscn`); placing him in the world is a later package's. Nothing of the other bosses, the
player's numbers or the Valley changes: the seeded 120 s soak is identical (41 attacks, 33 hits resolved, 4 enemy kills,
2 fence breaks, 7,201 frames, 0 errors).

## The plates: a reusable system for every titan

- **The record** (`plates[]` in an enemy record; `PlateRules.parse` fills the defaults): `id`, `region` (the hurtbox
  region it covers), `piece` (the rig's mesh), `anchor` (a lock anchor under `Anchors/`), `breakable`, `health` (the
  stone's own, in raw damage), `reduction` (kept from the body while whole), `poise_reduction`, `weak_multiplier`
  (the region's once broken), `heavy_multiplier` (a Heavy cracks stone faster; default 1.5) and `break` {`stagger_s`,
  `debris`, `cue`}; `plate_default_region` for a hit with no hurtbox. Armour-only entries (`breakable` false) are his
  hide (torso 55 %, legs 50 %) and limb plates (forearms 60 %, shins 70 %). The schema checks unique ids and regions,
  health on breakable plates, a weak multiplier only where a plate breaks (`PlateRules.errors`).
- **The rules** (`scripts/combat/plate_rules.gd`, pure; `plate_set.gd`, the state on `CombatEntity.plates`): before the
  resolver, `prepare()` reads the plate of the hit's region and sets `plate_id`, `plate_state` ("armoured", "weak", "")
  and the region multipliers; `DamageResolver` applies them after armour (exactly 1.0 for every other hit, so no pinned
  number moves); after, `absorb()` takes the blow's raw damage off an intact breakable plate's stone and breaks it at 0
  (`HitResult.plate`, `CombatEntity.plate_struck`). A broken plate's region is a weak point with its own multiplier
  (it replaces the generic x1.25 weak point for a titan).
- **The struck region** (decision A2-1, `docs/DECISIONS.md` §2.7): hits stay de-duplicated per entity; of a titan's
  candidates in one pulse `HitFactory.pick_titan_regions` keeps one, where his first stood - the hurtbox nearest the
  attacker's lock point when the attacker holds him locked, else the earliest sub-step, the deepest contact, the
  distance and the region's name. A weapon swing aimed at a plate that first touches him elsewhere waits three ticks
  for the plate, then its first contact lands. Every key is a position, a tick or a name: a seeded fight breaks the
  same plates on the same ticks (`test_stoneback_seeded`).
- **A break** (`TitanEnemy`): `Events.plate_broken`; a forced stagger (2.2-2.8 s, whatever he was doing - a wound, not a
  blow his poise answers); the phase rule (`enter_on_plates_broken`); the rig hides the plate's mesh
  (`StonebackRig.set_plate_visible`), throws grey stone from it (`DebrisPool.spawn`'s new colour) and the router plays
  the large stone break at its anchor. A blow on an intact plate rings on stone (`sfx.hit.stone`) and shows
  "ARMOURED n (stone m %)"; a weak point "WEAK POINT n" (orange); the breaking blow "PLATE BROKEN" (`HitFeedback`).
- **Lock-on**: `TitanEnemy.titan_lock_point` (through `CombatEntity.lock_anchor_position`) frames the nearest plate
  anchor the giant can reach (its height + 0.5 m), a broken one first (8 m of preference); behind him that is the back,
  before him a shoulder or the brow. A giant too small to reach any (stage 3-4 against the 13.6 m shoulders) frames his
  knee as for every tall boss. A swing at a titan tilts toward that point (`PlayerAttack.a2_plate_offset`, up to 40
  degrees). It is a pure function of the bodies' positions (the camera and the HUD ask every frame).

## Stoneback (`enemy.stoneback`, archetype `titan`; `TitanEnemy`, `TitanBrain`, `TitanRig`)

`TitanEnemy` (`scripts/actors/titan_enemy.gd`) is a `RivalGiantEnemy` - the boss framework's phases and sequences,
chained strikes, the committed charge and its body sweep, the lobbed boulder committed at its windup, attempts and the
disengage rule - with the plates above and:

| Attack | What it does | Telegraph |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trunk smash (`stoneback_smash`) | A1's overhead wind-up, the trunk brought down two-handed: the root ball strikes 8 m ahead (4.5 m), a shock runs on to 28 m (`titan_shock`: line pulses sharing one pulse id - one hit a target for the whole line). Unblockable. 1.8 s to stand up: the punish window | a line along his facing |
| Sweep (`stoneback_sweep`) | drawn back low on his right, swung through 220 degrees at 13.5 m; blockable; the answer to a giant held on his flank | a 220-degree cone |
| Boulder (`stoneback_boulder`) | he crouches and tears a boulder out of the ground at his left foot (dirt flies), draws it back over his shoulder (A1's throw), lobs it; dodgeable by walking out of the circle | a 7 m circle where the giant stood |
| Shoulder charge (`stoneback_charge`) | A1's lean (the left shoulder), then 17 m/s in a straight line for up to 75 m; tramples the props a stage-6 giant tramples; against a rock or the quarry wall he reels 3.5 s (`stoneback_reel`) with his back to the giant | a 75 m line |
| Rage (`stoneback_roar`) | phase 2's roar: at half health or after two plates broke, when he is not staggered | a circle |

Phase 1 cycles smash, sweep, boulder, smash, charge; phase 2 the raging smash (1.4 s, chained into the raging sweep),
the charge, the boulder, the raging smash, the raging sweep, the charge, with his pauses x0.6 (`strike_pause_scale`).
`TitanBrain` (`scripts/ai/titan_brain.gd`) answers a giant held on his flank (55-115 degrees) inside the sweep's reach
for 1 s with the sweep (a cone of 180 degrees or more may start with the target anywhere in its arc), at most every
9 s; behind him only his 75 deg/s turn answers - his back is the opening. His blows scale with his size against the
target's (`versus_size_exponent` 1.5; decision A2-3). Never grabbable; ignores morale and Fear. Loot (`loot.stoneback`,
records only, no recipe yet): `item.stoneback_heart_stone` (his brow's helm-stone), 4 `item.stoneback_slab`, 3
`item.quarry_iron`. His outcome flag is `boss.stoneback.outcome`. The numbers and why: `docs/BALANCE_LOG.md`, "A2".

**His body** (`tools/builders/titans.gd` -> `scenes/enemies/stoneback.tscn`): a capsule of 3.4 m by 16.8 m; hurtboxes a
region each - brow (a sphere at 15.9 m), the shoulders (2.2 m spheres at 13.6 m), the back (behind the upper back),
the torso (a smaller box over the chest and belly, under the shoulders), the forearms, the legs and the shins; lock
anchors `Torso`, `KneeL`/`KneeR` and `PlateShoulderL`/`R`, `PlateBack`, `PlateBrow`. As every boss's, the hurtboxes do
not follow the clips.

## The strikes, animated (`tools/giant/kit/stoneback_moves.py`)

Seven new clips in `stoneback_clips.glb`, each a few key poses solved as A1's (the legs by their feet, the held trunk
and boulder by `Holder.hold`) and every bone's rotation slerped between them on an ease; through the two-handed
segments the left fist is solved back onto the trunk each frame. A strike starts at its wind-up's peak (A1's
`overhead_pose` / `throw_pose` at their last key) or the stance and ends on the idle's first key: `smash` (54 frames;
the blow at 0.30 s), `sweep` (66; the blow at 1.2 s), `charge_run` (a loop: the run's legs under the charge's lowered
shoulder), `reel` (72), `rip` (30; the grip at 0.75 s), `release` (36; let go at 0.25 s), `death` (75: struck, the
knees go, he kneels and falls on his face). `TitanRig` (`scripts/presentation/titan_rig.gd`) plays the wind-up over the
first share of each action's windup and the strike's swing over the rest, so the blow lands on the first active tick;
the follow-through and the recovery over the active and recovery phases; the charge's run on the distance run; a
stagger or the reel as `reel`. Sheet D's page d4 shows six keys of each (`tools/character_sheet.tscn -- --sheet=d
--pages=d4`). `build_people.py --stoneback` regenerates them bit-exact (`--people`); the other files of his are
unchanged.

## The quarry test arena (`scenes/test/quarry_test.tscn`)

A bowl 70 m across inside quarry walls (three stepped terraces, an entrance to the south), four great rocks and a cut
face (WORLD: his charge reels on them), three palisades his charge tramples, quarried blocks and spoil heaps as
scenery (no collision), a DebrisPool, a BossArena without a gate (its disengage rule as every boss's), the titan
navmesh. Its root (`TitanArenaTest`) readies the giant on its first tick on the floor for the stage the command line
asks for:

    tools/gd.sh --path . -- --level=res://scenes/test/quarry_test.tscn --titan-stage=4

(`TitanDuel.prepare`: the growth to that stage, the stage's required level by experience, the attribute points spent as
the bots spend them - strength and endurance in turn -, the campaign's weapon for that stage, full pools; default
stage 5.)

A stage-5 giant (healed every tick) against Stoneback with his AI off, each beat forcing his attack (`force_action`)
and the giant walking, dodging and swinging through its input router; the plates break under synthetic heavy blows
through the ordinary resolution. Nineteen stills (`a2_NN_<moment>.png`): the face-off, the smash's telegraph, blow and
shock, the sweep's telegraph and blow, the boulder's rip, throw and landing, the charge's telegraph, run and reel
against a rock, a plate breaking, the exposed weak point struck, the rage, the raging smash and sweep, his fall and
the fallen titan. Headless the tour runs every beat (`test_stoneback_battery`). The HUD is hidden in the stills.

excerpt of it. The frames are not committed.

## What still looks or plays weak (honestly)

- **Scale**: Stoneback is 16.8 m but stands hunched; beside the stage-5 giant (14.6 m, upright) in the stills he does
  not read as the bigger giant he is - the owner's "a bigger giant" wish needs his posture straighter or the camera
  lower; the stage-6 giant visibly overtops him.
- **The strikes are key poses slerped**: the smash and the sweep read as heavy and their blows land on the right tick,
  but the in-betweens are smooth rather than weighty (no anticipation dip, no overshoot beyond the keys); the reel's
  arms pass through a raised pose between keys; the trunk stays upright in his fist as he lies dead.
- **The boulder in his hand** is hard to see in the rip (the fist is low and the boulder sits on the curled fingers,
  A1's note); the rip's dirt is the debris pool's boxes.
- **The hurtboxes do not follow the clips** (as every boss's): his crouch in the rip or the reel leaves the shoulders'
  spheres where he stands upright.
- **The bot does not beat him at stage 5** (it breaks one shoulder and dies at 47-88 % of his health) and wins at
  stage 6 in under three minutes; the balance holds for the bot's play, not for a human's - the owner's playtest at
  stages 5-6 should set the health (6,200) and the strike pause.
- **The quarry is boxes**: ring walls, terraces and rocks are textured boxes; the cut face and the rocks read as blocks.
- **Plate stone is not shown cracking** before it breaks (only the number's stone %); a cracked stage per plate would
  read better.
