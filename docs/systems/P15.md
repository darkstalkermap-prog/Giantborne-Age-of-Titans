## Dressing and draw calls

`CosmeticField` (`scripts/world/cosmetic_field.gd`, `class_name CosmeticField extends Node3D`, group
`cosmetic_fields`): one per dressed district, `Districts/<id>/Dressing` (hollow, village, ruins,
kingsfall). The builder writes its items; the field makes three MultiMeshes at runtime.

```gdscript
const RECORDS := {"bush": "prop.bush", "banner": "prop.banner", "sheaf": "prop.barley_sheaf"}
static var TEMPLATES                       # kind -> [[mesh, local Transform3D, Color or "cloth"], ...]
@export var kinds: PackedStringArray       # per item
@export var transforms: Array[Transform3D] # per item: place, yaw, scale (field space = level space)
@export var tints: PackedColorArray        # per item: the banner cloth (alpha 0 = the default red)
var react_counts: PackedInt32Array; var react_count: int; var multimeshes: Dictionary
func item_count() -> int; func item_position(i) -> Vector3; func items_near(pos, radius) -> PackedInt32Array
func is_flattened(i) -> bool; func is_wobbling(i) -> bool; func item_transform(i) -> Transform3D
func react(i, source: Vector3, strength: float) -> void   # CosmeticProp.react for one item
func part_transform(mesh, k) -> Transform3D; func parts_of(i) -> Array   # tests (the CPU buffer copy)
static func react_radius(kind) -> float
```

It mirrors `CosmeticProp`: `area_pulse` within the radius (+0.4 m) wobbles (0.6), `ability_effect`
sector (inside the arc) and roar wobble (0.7), actors (player, enemies, NPCs) coming within an
item's `react_radius_m` + their radius, checked at 5 Hz, wobble it (0.5) or, a stage-2+ giant,
flatten it for 4 s; one reaction per item per tick; never saved. Animation rewrites only the
reacting items' instances (the whole buffer is uploaded while something moves). No shadows,
`visibility_range_end` 140 m.

| District | Items |
| --- | --- |
| Hollow | 10 bushes round the Cradle and the trail, 2 blue banners at the Hollow Shrine |
| Thornwick and its fields | 10 green banners (the four gates, the elder's door), 7 bushes, 10 barley sheaves |
| Ruins | 6 red banners (the courtyard's walls, before the sealed citadel) |
| Kingsfall | 2 red banners at the antechamber |

Barley rows (layout `barley_rows`): 0.8 x 0.9 m boxes across each field every 2.4 m (23 rows,
one MultiMesh, no shadow). Ridge trees (layout `ridge_trees`): 52 visual-only trees at seeded
places on the cliff tops (y 15-30 m), drawn by the district's existing tree MultiMeshes (no new
draw call, no collision, no navigation change).

## Decisions and deviations

1. **Doc name** `docs/systems/P15.md` (the task and §1.1 item 5); §3.17 names
   `P15-valley-content.md`.
2. **One record per file** (as P10a, P10b, P11a, P11b): `encounter_<name>.json`, `quest_<name>.json`,
   `data/tutorial/<name>.json`.
3. **"Entered" stages also accept the states that can only come after them.** Quest conditions read
   world state, except `entered`, which is level memory (P13: it resets on a reload). A giant that
   walked through an area while an earlier stage was current, then reloaded, would have had to
   walk in again; and one that went round Thornwick's palisade to the barricade never passes the
   west gate. So `leave_hollow` also completes on wolves A cleared or the forest ogre dead,
   `to_village` on the elder met or an outcome, `bridge` on the practice gate broken, `courtyard` and
   `causeway` on the king's defeat; `quest.village` starts on any of those too. None of them can
   hold earlier than the plan's own condition along the road, so the order and the rewards are
   unchanged (the walkthrough checks the stage order).
4. **`quest.tutorial` is abandoned at stage 2** (`abandon_when`); the plan only says it never blocks
   `quest.main`. An unfinished tutorial would otherwise sit in the tracker for the whole slice.
5. **Prompt fallbacks.** The tutorial director shows one prompt at a time and never pre-empts it, so a
   lesson the player ignores (no first-person look, no dodge through a blow) would hold back every
   later prompt, the boss charge's included. Every prompt that waits for an action has a
   `timeout_s` fallback (15-60 s; the lesson is then not marked done). The plan's own timeouts
   (soldier guard 10 s, boss charge 8 s) are the timeout type; the shrine's "or 15 s" and the
   attributes' "or 20 s" are fallbacks. Priorities: boss charge 50, grab 40, block and dodge 30,
   hurl 25, soldier guard 20, shrine 15, the Hollow's sequence 10, attributes 5. `tutorial.heavy`,
   `footfall` and `lock` are flag-triggered by the previous lesson's `.done` flag.
6. **The elder's conversation** adds nodes the plan's example leaves to P15: `thanks_cache` and
   `thanks_ogre` (where to go), `waiting_cache` and `waiting_ogre` (an offer made, nothing done yet;
   they keep the stage-2 intimidation open), `done_help`, `done_cowed` and `done`. Offers are
   first-write-wins (`quest.village.offer`); a giant that offered one help and does the other is
   still rewarded once (`reward.village.help`) and the unused branch quest never starts (or is
   abandoned).
7. **The village captain stands at (94, −23.5)**, beside the barricade's road, instead of §4.4's
   (92, −20) in its middle: every human path through the opened channel follows the barricade's nav
   link along z −20, and the return patrol's fleeing soldiers (and the stage-3 giant) ran into him
   (he holds his post; agents have no avoidance). He stands more than 6 m from `guard_post_3`
   (88, −26): nearer (a first try at (92, −24)), he could claim guard 3's post first and leave guard
   3 without one (P11b's posts are the nearest free formation marker within 6 m of home).
   P11b's data tests read the position from the record; P10b's planned-spawn check of (92, −20)
   still holds; the clearance suite checks every shipped spawn on its navigation layer.
8. **The ambush's archer** stands on the watchtower beside the guards' tower archer, (76.2, 6, −38.8)
   instead of the same spot, and the ambush carries faction `village` (it follows the village's
   standing; it only spawns while the village is hostile).
9. **The dressing is batched** (a new `CosmeticField`, a new script outside §3.17's owned paths, and a
   new cosmetic record `prop.barley_sheaf`) instead of ~50 `CosmeticProp` instances: P14 measured the
   Valley far over its draw-call budget and the wave notes forbid unbatched dressing; the plan's
   behaviour (react to steps and pulses, never saved) is kept. Ridge trees and barley rows are
   visual only.
10. **Not built: the Hollow cottage arch** (§4.2's optional 4.5 m stage-1 shortcut; P10b's decision
    15): it would add navigation polygons and a ceiling for an optional shortcut the village's
    alleys already demonstrate.
11. **`progression_default.json` is unchanged**: its library order, new-game slots and XP table already
    give §4.7's schedule (P12's auto-slot uses the library order; the abilities' `unlock_level` and
    `unlock_stage` are the plan's). The shrines, loot tables, enemies' XP and the props' XP and texts
    needed no change either (the barricade's `xp_reward` is 30, the gate's 20).
12. **The walkthrough's shortcuts** (plan §3.17: "direct calls"): teleports; kills and prop breaks by
    synthetic hits of the right category, tags and growth stage; the combat timer reset before a
    growth (the shrine refuses growth for 10 s after a blow); spawned enemies outside the village
    faction get their AI switched off as they spawn (the guards, the villagers and the sparring post
    keep theirs). The Hollow tutorial is played with the real controls (camera toggles, the light,
    heavy and Footfall on a dummy, a lock, a held block and a timed roll against the sparring post)
    through the shipped prompts.
13. **The boss checkpoint scenario runs inside the intimidate walkthrough** (its mid-run death is on
    the return leg after the king and the growth to stage 3) instead of a fifth Valley run.
14. **The flee test lets one soldier flee at a time** (the others wait with their AI paused and each
    leaves through the director once it has arrived): fleeing together, a soldier that reached its
    flee point on the barricade's nav link blocks the next one on the same line (no agent
    avoidance, P04's choice). Known gap below.
15. **XP budget scope**: the field ogre's kill counts on the help_ogre path (the branch needs it);
    the test checks inequalities against data-derived levels, not the plan's numbers.

## Known gaps

- Soldiers fleeing together through an opened gate or barricade can jam on its nav link (agents
  have no avoidance; the first to arrive stands on the link's line). Reachability of every flee
  point is tested; crowd behaviour is not.
- The walkthroughs drive the quest line, not the fights (enemy AI off, synthetic kills); the bot
  (P17a) plays the fights.
- A giant that ignores a lesson until its fallback loses it: the optional tutorial then stays
  unfinished until it is abandoned at the Grove.
- The Hollow cottage arch is not built (decision 10).
- Draw calls are still over the 250 budget (P14's finding, P17b's to reduce); the dressing adds a
  few per view (above).
