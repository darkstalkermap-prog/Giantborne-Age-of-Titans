## The launcher's notes (as shipped)

> **New in 0.12.0-m12.** You dress your giant piece by piece. Clothing has ten places to fill (head, shoulders, chest, armour, arms, waist, legs, shins, feet and cape) from 35 garments and pieces of armour in cloth, leather, fur and metal, with 12 outfits to start from; three more places hold a torc or necklace, an arm ring and a trophy on the belt. Cloth, leather and metal each take a colour, and every piece can have a colour of its own. Hair has 12 styles and beards 10, with beads for both; tattoos go on the legs too, and he can wear up to six scars. Two sliders shape his build (brawn and girth) and six his face (jaw, chin, nose, brow, cheeks and age). There are 14 presets. The creator opens each row as a grid you can search by name and filter by material. His body was reproportioned: longer legs, a shorter torso, a visible neck, narrower shoulders, a larger head and smaller hands, the same at every height from 12 to 62 ft (the heights themselves did not change). One change in the fighting: a soldier walking back to its post that ends up pressed against you stops and waits after a second instead of pushing on. Your saves and looks from 0.10.0-m10 and 0.11.0-m11 carry over: an old outfit is put on piece by piece the first time it loads, and nothing about it changes. A look made or changed in 0.12.0-m12 shows only in 0.12.0-m12 or later: an older build keeps it in the save but shows the default look until you change it there.
>
> **What it is not yet.** Only your giant changed: the soldiers, villagers, beasts and the world are still the game's simple shapes; they come in the next build. Some pieces still look rough: seen from behind, capes and cloaks show two lumps over the upper back; seen from the side, beards break into blocks, and the short beard reads greyish; the crop shows a thin strip of scalp at the front; the tunic and the shirt, and the jerkin and the cuirass, look too much alike; the fur cloak is boxy. In the browser the creator's grids show names instead of pictures. Hair is still made of shaped strips and layers, not single strands. In first person you do not see your legs or feet. The balance is a bot's: it walks from objective to objective, no person has played the world yet, and some fights are still harder for it than they should be (the Iron Regiment, if you take the crown's oath first at 36 ft). Siege trains do not wander the roads. The browser version runs on one thread, so expect slow frames and music that can stutter on weaker computers.
>
> **The next build.** 0.13.0-m13 makes the people and beasts around you realistic too: soldiers, villagers and riders, the named characters, the rival giants, horses, brutes and wolves, made the same way as your giant.

The masthead reads "more customization: clothing and armour piece by piece - build 0.12.0-m12"; the download note
"About 44 MB" (the figure the launcher's script writes from the BUILD line: the engine and the pck as shipped,
44,277,716 bytes).

## Decisions and deviations

- **The release's Web build is in the scratch folder, not `builds/web/`**: built with `--out=`, so
  `build_web.sh` did not write the BUILD line into `web/launcher.html` itself; the line was copied from the build's
  own `play.html` (the same numbers), as P52 did.
- **The Chromium checks ran on the second of the identical builds**, so that nothing they write lands beside the
  files the lead publishes from the fourth (built on the final check's commit).
- **The cross-version run used the older tags' code, not their exported binaries** (the plan names no binary; the
  code is the release's at its tag and the engine the same). Its driver stays in the scratch folder (FOLLOWUPS).
- **No `--strict-times`**: on this container the default tier reads over the script's 75 s (a warning), so the
  check was run as `--build --slow --gate --giant-full --web-tools --audio --require`.
- **Not run on the export**: the assisted Valley bot run and the tour under Xvfb (P52 ran both on 0.11's export);
  the tour's part B ran from source for the screenshots. The skin's native CPU was not re-measured.
- **No game file changed; two tests changed with the script** (`test_giant_web_presets`' expected branches,
  `test_giant_tools.BudgetTest`'s last default): each states the rule the release moves, none was loosened.
