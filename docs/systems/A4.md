# A4 — Milestone A finished: Stoneback toppled, the Character menu, save schema 3 and the intro

A3 put Stoneback in the world; a stage-5 giant still could not beat him (its swings rarely reached his shoulder
plates: 8-10 damage a second). A4 makes size-matched play physical - his shins break and drop him to a knee -,
adds the Character menu, moves the save to schema 3 with a migration, and gives the open world its intro cutscene
with a reusable cutscene player and a video export. Built on `pkg/a4` from A3's merge (`0.14.0-m14`). The seeded
120 s soak is unchanged (41 attacks, 33 hits resolved, 4 enemy kills, 2 fence breaks, 7,201 frames, 0 errors).

## 1. Down on a knee: the topple (a generic plate effect)

### The plate system's new fields (`PlateRules`, `enemy_schema`)

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `plates[].on_break` `{"effect", "down_s", "again_damage"}` | `"stagger"` (the default: the break's stagger only) or `"topple"`: broken, the titan goes down on a knee for `down_s`; once broken, every `again_damage` more health damage to its region (or the record's `topple.again_regions`) drops him again (0: never) |
| `plates[].lock_bias_m` | added to the plate's lock score (below 0: the lock frames it sooner than a nearer plate) |
| `topple` `{"kneel_s", "rise_s", "damage_mult", "heavy_mult", "again_regions", "again_immune_s", "recover_s"}` | the record's down: the kneel's and the rise's lengths (his clips'), every blow's multiplier while he is down and a Heavy's besides, the regions whose damage counts toward the next topple, the time after he rises before he can go down again and before his next strike (dazed) |

`PlateRules.parse_topple`, `down_total_s` (kneel + down_s + rise) and `down_weight` (0 standing .. 1 on the knee:
the kneel eased in, held, the rise eased out) are pure; `PlateRules.errors` refuses a topple on an unbreakable plate,
a down of 0 s and an unknown again region. Nine more titans can name any effect here; only `topple` is new.

### What `TitanEnemy` does with it

- A topple plate's break (or its `again_damage`, counted by `_count_toward_topple` from each blow's health damage
  to the broken region or `again_regions`, no sooner than `again_immune_s` after he rose) calls `_topple`: his
  action is cut short and he is staggered for the whole down with the reason `topple` (`CombatController`'s own
  stagger: no brain, no movement, no attack; poise damage is ignored as for every stagger). `Events.titan_toppled`
  and `Events.titan_rose` (new); the cue router plays `sfx.body.fall.huge` at him (no new sound).
- **His body follows the kneel** (decision A4-2): every hurtbox and lock anchor of his scene carries a
  `kneel_position` meta - its standing position carried by its bone's rigid motion from the idle's first key to the
  kneel's last frame (`tools/builders/titans.gd` `KNEEL_FOLLOW`, from the creatures' manifest's
  `checks.kneel.bones`, written by `kit/stoneback.py kneel_frames`) - and `_down_step` lerps them by
  `down_weight` every tick. Coarse (positions only; the boxes keep their size and turn), but the upper hurtboxes
  come down: the brow 16.6 -> 9.5 m, the shoulders 13.8 -> 7.7-9.1 m, the back 13.2 -> 8.7 m; the lock anchors with
  them (the back's 13.7 -> 9.2 m, the brow's 16.9 -> 10.9 m). A query at the lowered brow finds the brow's hurtbox
  (`test_stoneback_topple`).
- While he is down every blow's region multipliers take `topple.damage_mult` (x1.15) and a Heavy's
  `topple.heavy_mult` besides (x1.6) (`PlateSet.down_mult` / `down_heavy_mult`, set before `DamageResolver` as the
  plates' own multipliers are; exactly 1.0 standing, so no pinned number moves). Another plate breaking while he is
  down keeps him down (no reel). A new attempt stands him up whole.
- Rising, he is dazed: his next strike waits `recover_s` (3 s) (`brain.strike_ready_tick`).
- **The lock** (`titan_lock_point`): an anchor above `LOCK_COMFORT_SHARE` (0.9) of the giant's height counts
  `LOCK_HIGH_PENALTY_M` (12 m) farther (a swing at it passes under it), each plate's `lock_bias_m` is added (his
  shins -10 m: the legs first, at every stage), and a topple plate is never framed while he is down (a knee on the
  ground is no target): down, the lock frames the lowered brow, a shoulder or the back, a broken one first.
- **A swing may tilt down** at a titan's plate (`PlayerAttack.a2_plate_offset`): when no tilt from the stage's
  default up to +40 degrees reaches the lock's point, tilts down to 30 degrees below the default are tried
  (a strictly closer one wins, so every tilt that reached before still wins: the stage-6 swings are A3's).

### Stoneback's plates and down (`data/enemies/stoneback.json`)

| | A3 | A4 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shins | armour only (70 %) | **breakable**: 700 stone, 70 % kept while whole, x1.4 once broken, `lock_bias_m` -10, anchors `PlateShinL/R`; `on_break` topple, 6 s down, again after 180 more damage |
| Forearms | armour only (60 %) | breakable: 450 stone, 60 %, x1.6, a 1.6 s stagger (no anchor: the lock never frames them) |
| `topple` | - | kneel 0.8 s, rise 1.2 s (the clips'), x1.15 every blow and x1.6 a Heavy while down, again regions `legs`, again immune 6 s, dazed 3 s |
| His rage (phase 2) | after two plates | after **three** (the shins and forearms break now) |
| Health | 6,200 | **16,000** (stage 6 topples him too now) |
| Strike pause | 2.0-2.8 s | **2.4-3.2 s** |

The numbers and the runs: `docs/BALANCE_LOG.md`, "A4".

### The clips (`tools/giant/kit/stoneback_moves.py`)

`kneel` (24 frames): from the stance the right leg gives way (`KNEEL_BUCKLE` 0.3 s), he lurches forward and the knee
strikes the ground (`KNEEL_HIT` 0.6 s), a small rebound, and he settles - the right knee on the ground with the shin
laid back and the toes dug in, the left foot planted ahead with its knee up and the left hand braced on it, the
trunk planted at his right side with its root ball on the ground, the trunk pitched forward and the head hanging a
little. Its last frame is held while he is down. `rise` (36 frames): the weight pushed onto the left knee, the right
foot brought under him, up to the idle's first key. `kneel_key` sets the knee on the ground by the right leg's
lengths (the thigh near upright over it); the manifest records the kneel's crown (11.8 m of 17.7), the knee's
height (0.46 m) and the twelve bones above. `build_people.py --stoneback` regenerates them bit-exact; the clips
file grew 766 -> 851 KB. `TitanRig` plays `kneel` (dust and a shake as the knee strikes, `knee_impacts`), holds it,
and plays `rise` over the down's last `rise_s`.

### The duel bot (`TitanDuelBot`, `BotCombat`)

The balance runs measure what a skilled player does, so the bot learned the titan's new fight (decision A4-3): in
his recoveries it strikes the plate the lock frames where it stands when that is low (his shins) instead of going
round to his back; while he is down it walks (sprinting only beyond 16 m) to where its heavy reaches the lowered
plate the lock frames (`_strike_distance`: the swing's own geometry, `PlayerAttack.closest_approach`) and lands
heavies, waiting for a heavy's stamina while he stays down long enough for it; as he rises it backs out of his
sweep's reach, aside as well as back (straight back from his front is his smash's line). Its abilities keep two
heavies' stamina (`BotCombat.ability_stamina_floor`, 0 for every other bot).

## 2. The Character menu (`ui/screens/character_screen.gd`)

ScreenStack `SCREENS["character"]`, opened at any time in play - also in a fight - with the new action
`character` (C / pad Y; with LT held Y is still ability 4, which the input router consumes first), remappable in
Controls as every binding, or from the pause menu's new **Character** entry (it opens above the pause menu; closing
it comes back there). It pauses the game; its binding or Esc / B closes it. Five tabs - click, Enter on a focused
tab, pad LB / RB (wrapping); the focus rows are the tab row and Close, wrapping every way. Its footer names the
binding by the device in use ("Esc or C: close" / "B or Y: close"). It fits 1280 x 720 above the footer at level 20
with every row filled (`test_character_screen`).

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Giant | the level, the XP to the next ("XP 120 / 500 to level 10"), the stage and its height in feet and metres; the growth track of stages 1-10 (12, 18, 26, 36, 48, 62, 78, 96, 116, 140 ft at levels 1, 3 ... 20): reached, "you are here", **next** with what it needs (the level, the shrines whose `max_stage` is that stage and the first of their conditions: "next: level 11 (you are 9); grow at Shrine of the Standing Stones; win the oaths of two of the three powers first") and later; 7-10 "Beyond the rim (locked)", their heights only. Beside it the giant itself (the creator's `GiantPreview` in the session's look, at its stage's profile) where a display draws it |
| Attributes | the six attributes with what a point gives, the unspent points, the ancestry and the talents; "Change them at a shrine: its Attributes, Talents and Ancestry tabs." |
| Equipment | the weapon in hand: its damage multiplier, length (and its scale at this size), the stage it fits to, the light and heavy it swings and their power, its perk; the seven armour slots milestone B adds (head, shoulders, chest, arms, waist, legs, trinket) as "not yet available" rows (save schema 3 holds them, all empty). The look's cosmetic `armour` slot is untouched |
| Titans | the ten signature titans (the roster, below) as a trophy list: Stoneback by name with his state (not yet seen - "said to hold the old quarry beside Millford" -, sighted, slain), the most plates broken in one fight, his kills and what he dropped; every other titan "???" beside a dark silhouette block until met (none is named) |
| Materials | the titans' materials held (Stoneback's heart-stone, slabs, quarry iron) with their first sentence, then every other material item by name |

The content builders are static (`giant_info`, `growth_track`, `needs_text`, `attribute_lines`, `equipment_rows`,
`titan_rows`, `material_rows`), read by the tests. The HUD's controls overlay lists the binding.

**The roster** (`data/titans/titan_roster.json`, record type `titan_roster`: `scripts/core/schemas/titan_roster_schema.gd`):
the signature titans in the story's order with, for the ones in the game, their enemy, boss ids, outcome and
defeated flags, sighted POI and loot table. The story names nine; the tenth slot (`titan.tenth`) is held for the
owner's tenth (FOLLOWUPS).

## 3. Save schema 3 (`SaveSlots`, `ExpansionState`)

`SCHEMA_VERSION` 3. `scripts/core/expansion_state.gd` (pure) holds the expansion's own fields; `GameSession.expansion`
the live copy (written into every checkpoint document, read on Continue and on a checkpoint reload, reset by a New
Game):

| Key | Schema 3 | Filled by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `act` | 1 | (the throne ends act one in milestone C) |
| `titans` | `{titan id: {"state": unseen / sighted / slain, "plates_broken_best", "kills", "first_sighted_play_s", "first_sighted_unix", "drops": [{"item", "count"}]}}` | `boss_engaged` (sighted, the first time noted), `plate_broken` (the most this fight), `boss_defeated` (slain, a kill, his loot table's drops) through the roster's ids; every snapshot also brings it into line with the flags (`sync_from_flags`, never lowering) |

| `settlements` | `{settlement (its POI id): {"level": 0, "flags": {name: true}}}` | today the world flags it mirrors: Millford's `quarry_reopened`, `quarry_freed`, `defended`, `burned` |
| `cinematics_seen` | the cutscene ids played | the intro (section 4) |

- **`_migrate_2_to_3`** (decision A4-4) follows 1 -> 2: a pure step on a copy, in memory; the next checkpoint
  writes schema 3 and the schema 2 file becomes the backup (it loads, so `SaveIO` keeps it); a damaged main file
  falls back to the migrated backup. It adds the five keys from the flags A3 wrote - Stoneback slain by
  `boss.stoneback.outcome` = slain or `boss.stoneback.defeated` (one kill, his drops), sighted by
  `discovered.stoneback_quarry`, Millford's flags - and leaves the flags where they are (the game still reads them).
  A key already present is kept. Each key is optional in `validate` (as the progress block is): present, it must
  have its type (`ExpansionState.validate`); `sanitize` drops a titan the roster does not know and an armour item
  this build does not know, with the usual "Dropped ids" message.
- **A schema 4 file** is refused and kept as `valley_1.schema4.rejected.json` with the existing "newer version"
  message (`test_save_migration.test_a_newer_schema_save_is_refused_and_kept`: was the schema 3 test).

**The fixtures** (`tests/fixtures/saves/v2_a4_*.json`): real schema 2 saves the pre-A4 build wrote - from a clean
`git archive` of A3's merge (`d1fb8bf`) with its own user folder, `tools/run_bots.sh --tag=a4fx --jobs=1

`campaign.completed`), `--tag=a4fx_mid --autoplay=500 w.mixed:novice:1:assist` (`v2_a4_world_mid`: level 10,

`v2_a4_stoneback_slain`, is **derived**: the mid-world save with the flags and the loot Stoneback's death writes
(`boss.stoneback.outcome` slain, `boss.stoneback.defeated`, `world.quarry.reopened`, his heart-stone, four slabs,
three quarry iron) set by hand, since no bot run kills him.

## 4. The intro and the cutscene player

### The player (`ui/screens/cutscene_screen.gd`, `scripts/presentation/cutscene_rules.gd`)

A cutscene is data: a `cutscene` record (`data/cutscenes/`, `scripts/core/schemas/cutscene_schema.gd`) whose
shot list is in ticks (60 a second) - camera travels (`from`/`to`, `look_from`/`look_to`, `fov`, an ease), actors
(`stoneback`: his model through `StonebackRig.pose`; `giant`: the giant model in the session's own look at a
stage; `villager`: one of the people) with keys (place, turn, clip, the clip's time and rate, loop, on the ground),
subtitles, sound cues (cue ids: no new sound), camera shakes, dust clouds, fades and a title card, and a `focus`
the world streams round before it starts. `CutsceneRules` is the pure timeline (`camera_at`, `actor_at`, `line_at`,
`due`, `fade_at`, `title_at`, `shake_at`: plain values, deterministic sines for the shake). `CutsceneScreen` plays
it as a UiScreen (`SCREENS["cutscene"]`): the stack pauses the world, hides the HUD and shows the footer, which is
the **skip hint by device** ("Enter / Esc: skip" or "A / B: skip"); one tick a physics frame (so `--write-movie` at a
fixed fps films the same frames every time); its camera, actors and dust under the level (running while paused;
the level's own giant hidden meanwhile); letterbox bars; any confirm or cancel (Enter, Space, Esc, pad A, B, Start,
interact, jump, dodge) skips at once; at its end or skip the camera, the giant and the streaming focus come back
and it closes with `{"cutscene", "skipped", "ticks"}`. Later titans' intros are new records.

### When it plays (`GameSession`)

On the world's first entry of a New Game - its "Skip to the Old Kingdom", and the Valley's hand-over through the
Kings' Door (the first travel into the world): where a new game first meets the world and the story it tells (the
Valley's prologue is the giant's youth; the intro is the kingdom's) - once its level is entered (`level_entered`,
after the loading gate). Never on a Continue, never in the Valley, never twice for a game that has seen it
(`expansion.cinematics_seen`, saved). Off in tool runs (`--autoplay`, `--scenario`), headless, and with
`--no-cutscenes`. **Replayable** from the title's new **Watch the intro** (GameSession's mode `cinematic`: a New Game
in the world that saves nothing, `cinematic_only`, and the title again after it).

### The video export (`tools/export_cinematic.sh`)

    tools/export_cinematic.sh [--cutscene=intro] [--out=DIR] [--keep-avi]

runs `xvfb-run ... tools/gd.sh --path . --rendering-driver opengl3 --fixed-fps 30 --resolution 1280x720
--write-movie <out>/intro.avi -- --no-user-settings --cinematic=intro --quit-after-cinematic` (the intro alone in a
world that saves nothing, the run ending after it) and turns the AVI into `<out>/intro.mp4` (H.264, AAC) from the
cutscene's first frame (CutsceneScreen prints `CUTSCENE_START <id> frame <n>`; the loading screen before it is cut).
On llvmpipe it renders at about 5 frames a second (944 frames in 3:13). The mp4 is evidence, not committed.
