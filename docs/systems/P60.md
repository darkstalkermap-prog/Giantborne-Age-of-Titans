# P60 — PersonRig, the crowd and the soldiers

Plan: `docs/plans/M11_PLAN.md` §3.13 (and §0.3's Part C rows, §1.2-§1.7, §2.9, §2.11, §2.12, §2.13, probes 7 and 8).
Wave W35, lane A, beside P61 (the wave's asset writer). Built on 21 November 2026 on `pkg/P60` from `60c16b4` (main
after P59). Every number was taken on the shared container (4 CPUs, no GPU: llvmpipe natively, the editor binary),
with P61 running beside it.

The goal: the soldiers, archers, mages, pikemen, shieldmen, bannermen, the iron guard and the siege crews are P59's
realistic people in play, alone and in formations, with gameplay proven unchanged. They are.

## What was built

### 1. The data: `person_role` records and the `body` key

- `scripts/core/schemas/person_role_schema.gd` (the module P59 pre-declared): a role's record names P59's
  `asset_role`, the `variants` an id's hash picks from, the `palette` lists (skin, hair, cloth; `#rrggbb`) and the
  `factions` table (the faction band's colour by the actor's faction, `default` required). `body_field()` is the
  actor records' `body` key (`role`, `variant`, `palette` overrides, `gear`), merged into `enemy_schema.gd` and
  `npc_schema.gd`; `enemy_schema.gd` also takes the giants' `appearance` key (an `appearance_preset` id, P62's).
- `data/people/role_{soldier,archer,mage,crew}.json`: four roles, six skin tones, six hair colours, five cloth
  colours each, nine faction colours.
- `data/enemies/{soldier,archer,mage,pikeman,shieldman,bannerman,iron_guard,siege_crew}.json` gain `body`: the
  role and the gear (a sword; a pike; a sword and a shield; a bow and a quiver; a staff; a hammer); the iron guard
  names its variant (`iron_guard`) and stands at its record's 1.9 m (a height scale of 1.056). Nothing else in a
  record changed.

### 2. `PersonLook` (`scripts/presentation/person_look.gd`)

Pure functions of the record, the role and the entity id (decision 12: no random stream): the variant (the record's
named one, else the pool at `absi(hash(entity_id)) % size`), the palette row (16 rows combined from the role's lists
so that neighbouring rows differ in all three; a second hash of the id), the faction colour by the actor's faction,
the record's overrides, the height scale (the record's height over 1.8 m times P59's `natural_height_m`: 0.93 for
the women when P62's villagers come), the gear. `palette_image()` is the crowd's 4 x 16 palette texture (columns skin,
hair, cloth, faction), so a skinned body and its crowd instance show the same colours.

### 3. `PersonModel` (`scripts/presentation/person_model.gd`, `scenes/people/person_model.tscn`)

The pooled skinned body: `People/Skeleton3D` with P59's 23 bones, a `Mesh` node, an `AnimationPlayer` rooted at the
model (P59's clip tracks start `People/Skeleton3D:`), turned 180 degrees about Y. A model takes any role and
variant: `set_variant()` puts the variant's mesh, its skin and its own joints' rests on it. A role's data is read
once a process from P59's files (the glTF's variants, the five crowd-pose meshes, the far mesh, the atlases), the gear
from `gear.glb`, the clips from `people_clips.glb` (loops set on idle, walk, run, flee, held, tumble, cower). The
scene stores no mesh, material or clip: P59's files stay the only copy in the pack. `tools/builders/people_models.gd`
writes the scene (the bones read from the soldier's first variant) and `person_visual()`, the Visual every person's
scene now has.

### 4. The shaders (`assets/shaders/person.gdshader`, `person_crowd.gdshader`)

Culling disabled (single-shell garments). The albedo times the band's colour (the ORM map's B read texel by texel,
`int(B * 5)`; the map is sampled twice: filtered for AO and roughness, `texelFetch` for the band). Lighting: the
roughness from the map; a band-less texel the map calls smooth (under 0.35: mail, helms, buckles) is metal
(metallic 0.8, its roughness held to 0.46, specular 0.25), so mail under the sun reads as worn iron and not wet
plastic; skin's roughness is held to 0.58 with a low specular. `tint` and `flash` as every actor shader. The crowd
shader reads the palette row, the flash and the faction row from `INSTANCE_CUSTOM` and the tint's multiplier from the
instance colour (probe 8's layout), and skips the normal map. Both draw the gear in a `gear` mode (its vertex
colours, the faction colour where alpha is 1).

### 5. `PersonRig` (`scripts/presentation/person_rig.gd`) and the levels

A `PersonRig` is an `ActorRig`: the brain still poses its (now empty) pivots on the frame clock. The levels are decided
for every person at once every second frame (`ALLOC_FRAMES`): the held (always, past the cap), then the striking, the
staggered and the falling, then the nearest to the giant (in the giant's distance scale) within 40 m, up to
`MAX_SKINNED` 16 (`--people-cap=N`; a skinned person keeps its slot against one 15 % nearer); a person not skinned is a
crowd instance out to its far distance from the camera (the record's `render_lod_m` x the square root of the giant's
distance scale: 45 m at stage 1, 103 m at stage 6), a far instance beyond; `--people-far` forces the far level. AI
LOD tier 2 is unchanged: `Visual/LodProxy` (now a one-instance MultiMesh of the far mesh) shows.

- **Skinned**: a pooled `PersonModel` under `Visual/Body`, its `AnimationPlayer` in manual mode, `seek(t - dt,
  false)` then `advance(dt)` every frame, each change of clip cross-faded by `play(clip, 0.15)` (probe 7). The clip:
  `death` on its own clock; `held` / `tumble` (the thrown body: the holder no longer holds it); the walk on the
  brain's walk phase (`t = fposmod(phase + pi/2, 2 pi)`, P59's 1.8 m a cycle: no foot slides), the run and the flee
  above 2.6 m/s on their own phase at 2.6 m a cycle (the brain's phase would turn a run over at 240 steps a minute);
  `brace` for a block, a guard or a formation brace; else `idle`. Then the arms: the clips' arms while nothing
  happens; when the brain acts (an attack, a cast, a block, a brace, a pose override such as the archer's draw) each
  arm goes over at 10 a second to where the brain's (yaw, pitch) points, by a two-bone solve (the shortest arc on the
  clip's upper arm and forearm, so the twist stays the clip's, the elbow down, back and out), the torso's twist
  spread over the spine; in a weapon arc the weapon hand is put by tip matching (`TipMatching.from_landmarks` on the
  person's own joints) and is exact in ACTIVE. The gear: the weapon on `WeaponSocket` at the fist (its blade or haft
  along the carried line - a sword forward and down, a pike, staff or bow upright - or along the brain's or tip
  matching's direction), the pike fitted to the formation's 4.2 m with 3.5 m before the hand, a round shield on the
  left forearm facing out, a quiver on the back. The bannerman keeps the 7 m pole and the swaying flag on his body.
  Draw calls: the body and the weapon (2; 3 with a shield), the body's one shadow.
- **Corpses**: the death plays skinned under an upright Body (the brain's own fall is left out: the clip falls); two
  seconds after the clip ends the body becomes a `corpse` instance and frees its slot.
- **Crowd and far** (`CrowdRenderer`, rewritten): a formation's renderer draws its members and its banner's body; one
  shared renderer of the loners (made with the first person drawn) draws everyone no other renderer drew that frame.
  One MultiMesh per person role and pose (hold, brace, advance, flee, corpse, far) and one per piece of gear and pose
  (the gear's place in a pose from the manifest's pose joints), each instance with its palette row, its flash and
  its faction's row. A flash or a status tint reaches every instance.
- Headless nothing is made or instanced; `--skin` / `--no-skin` switch the people as they switch the giant;
  `PersonRig.enable_for_tests()` for the suites (`ResetHooks` resets it).
- `PEOPLE_STATS` (`--skin-stats`, the Autopilot): skinned frames, promotions, the variants drawn skinned, crowd
  frames, instances (the sum and the largest frame), corpses, the most skinned at once, the cap.

### 6. The hooks and the scenes

- `enemy_actor.gd`: **no edit was needed.** The rig reads the record, the id, the faction, the brain's state and the
  held state; `set_lod_tier` already hides `Visual/Body` and shows `Visual/LodProxy`; the corpse hand-off is the
  rig's own clock.
- The builders `soldier.gd`, `ranged.gd`, `formations.gd`, `siege.gd` (the crew) put `person_visual()` on the eight
  scenes; every gameplay node (capsule, hurtboxes, anchors, NavAgent) and the brain's pivots stay where they were;
  `formations.gd` keeps `_add_body` and the box gear for Corvane's stylized body (`big.gd`, P62's). Every scene was
  rebuilt; a full `build_scenes` leaves no diff.

**The CPU budget is not met on this machine.** A skinned person costs about 50 µs a frame in the editor binary's
GDScript (the brain about 20, the clip's seek and advance about 16, the arms and the gear about 12), an instanced one
about 5 µs and its share of the renderer about 15 µs (the per-instance transform, palette, flash and gear batches).
What was done: the levels every second frame, instanced people skip the brain's posing and read their state as often
as the levels are decided, the renderer's role sets and each person's gear per pose looked up once. What remains for
P64 (the people's Web bench owns this row): measure on a release export on the reference PC; if still over, move the
renderer's per-instance loop to typed arrays written once per set (`multimesh.buffer`), and skip the arms' bone reads
while the clips' arms show.

## Determinism

`tests/gate/test_people_determinism.gd`: the seeded 120 s soak with the people off and on - **byte-identical**
AUTOPILOT_RESULT, and the baseline's numbers (41 attacks, 33 hits resolved, 4 enemy kills, 2 fence breaks, 7,201
frames, 0 errors); PEOPLE_STATS in the soak: 2,608 skinned frames, 2 promotions. The formation stress (40 s, stage 4)
identical with the people on and off but for what draws; PEOPLE_STATS there: 64,689 crowd instances drawn (20 in the
largest frame), 38,336 skinned frames, four variants drawn (soldier_a, soldier_b, soldier_c, crew_b). The Valley's
seeded twin with `--skin` and `--no-skin` (`test_giant_determinism`) now runs the people too.

**Deviation from §3.13's design 6**: the comparison is the people on against off on this code and the recorded soak
baseline, not against a `0.10.0-m10` worktree: 0.12.0-m12 changed one rule of play (a guard walking home waits when
pressed against the giant), so 0.10's results are not this game's.

## What was cut, moved or deviated

- **The loners' shared renderer was built** (no split was taken): the loners have the crowd and far levels through
  one renderer.
- `enemy_actor.gd` needed no presentation hook (above).
- The walk: P59's 0.9 m step and 13.6 cm pelvis drop are played as P59 made them (no foot slides at the brain's
  1.4-1.6 m/s, a natural cadence of about 107 steps a minute); the run and the flee are played at 2.6 m a cycle. In
  the frames the walk reads well at the game camera's distances; the knee bend is visible only close up.
- Files touched outside the owned list: `scripts/debug/autopilot.gd` (one line: PEOPLE_STATS), `tests/unit/
  test_skin_rules.gd`, `tests/unit/test_scene_kit.gd`, `tests/integration/test_rig_merge.gd`,
  `tests/integration/test_render_lod.gd`, `tests/integration/test_formation_freed_members.gd`,
  `tests/slow/test_formation_perf.gd`, `tests/slow/test_untelegraphed_warning.gd`,
  `tests/support/formation_draw_probe.gd` (the rewritten suites), `tests/support/people_probe.gd` (new).

## For P61, P62, P64 and P65 (asset notes, not changed here)

- The boots read as feet with toes from close up (the boot shell follows the toes); the mail hauberk reads as a
  grey tunic at the game camera (its rings vanish at 512 px).
- P59's hands are half closed; a weapon is carried along a line the rig chooses in the hand's frame
  (`PersonRig.GRIP_AXIS`), so close up the handle does not pass through the fist's curl exactly. A grip landmark in
  the manifest (as the giant's `grip_local_r`) would let the rig close it.
- The held clip hangs the body from the chest anchor the simulation holds; at stage 1 the feet nearly touch the
  ground (the gameplay grab point, unchanged).
