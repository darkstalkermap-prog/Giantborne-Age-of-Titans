# P17b — Balance loop and the how-it-plays report

Wave 10, solo. Branch `pkg/P17b`. Plan: `docs/plans/M2_M5_PLAN.md` §3.20, with §5.6 (the balance
loop), §5.7 (the report) and the two items of `docs/plans/FOLLOWUPS.md` assigned to P17b (the
Valley's draw calls; the force branch's 16 hostiles). The results are in `docs/HOW_IT_PLAYS.md`
(the report), `docs/BALANCE_LOG.md` (every tuning change with before and after) and
`docs/PERFORMANCE.md` (the draw calls).

## What was built

- **Draw calls (FOLLOWUPS).** `SceneKit.merge_meshes` merges a prop's visual groups into one
  `ArrayMesh` per material: every visible, unscripted `MeshInstance3D` reached through plain
  `Node3D` groups and static bodies becomes part of one mesh per (shadow casting, render layers,
  visibility range), one surface per material, with each part's transform baked
  (`SurfaceTool.append_from`); the parts are freed and the emptied groups pruned. The prop builders
  (`world_props`, `props` (the fence's Intact and Broken separately; the practice dummy is left alone
  because it flashes its own materials), `shrine` (its Glow kept apart), `forge`) merge every scene,
  and the merged meshes join LevelRoot's `scenery` group so a level's preset fades them with the
  rest of the scenery. The Valley's arena gate bars are merged too. The Valley's sun uses two shadow
  splits instead of four, and the `low` preset of the Valley and of the stress arena has no sun
  shadows (`shadow_distance_m` 0). The static world (no actors) now draws at most 93 calls per view
  at `low` and 221 at `medium` (was 402 / 584); see `docs/PERFORMANCE.md`.
- **The force branch's budget (FOLLOWUPS).** An encounter member whose definition is an enemy and
  whose faction is hostile now counts toward the director's 12-active budget even when its encounter
  does not (`counts_toward_budget: false`, the village guards): it waits for room like any other
  member, and NPC members (villagers, the elder) never count. The force branch's return leg peaked at
  16 hostiles round the giant; with the change at most 12 are active (every matrix since: peak 12).
- **The swing's vertical aim** (a game change; the balance loop found it). Each growth stage tilts
  its weapon arcs down so a swing reaches the giant's feet; a fixed tilt cannot also reach a man
  standing farther off, so the light chain landed well short of `melee_reach_m` (P17a measured about
  3.3 / 4.0 / 3.5 m by stage) and a stage-2 giant could not strike a holding soldier from beyond its
  brace counter. Now a light or heavy swung at the hard lock (else the soft target in front) takes
  the smallest lift from the stage's tilt that reaches the target (at most +16 deg): the default when
  its sweep already passes through the target's core (half its radius), else the tilt whose sweep
  passes closest to the target's axis if that one reaches its body. The rig draws the aimed tilt for
  that swing; the next swing aims again. A soldier at the §4.8 reach is hit by every light: 3.5 m at
  stage 1, 5 m at stage 2 (the overhead third blow 4.6 m), 6.5 m at stage 3 with a reforged club; a
  wolf at a stage-2 giant's feet keeps the default tilt.
- **The Valley bot, measurement fixes** (`scripts/debug/bot_combat.gd`, `valley_bot.gd`; each
  justified by a matrix in `docs/BALANCE_LOG.md`):
  - only the expert keeps a dodge's stamina in reserve (the novice spends freely, as plan §5.1 has
    it; a reserve for both made the novice wait out whole forest-ogre fights);
  - no heavy starts when a dodge planned against a blow from within 10 m falls inside the heavy's
    commitment (60 ticks); a shot from farther off does not hold it back;
  - the reach band (where the light lands on the target) is measured over the aim's tilts through
    the target's core, and the bot stands at the plan's 0.8 x reach plus half the target's radius,
    inside that band (3.0 / 4.0 / 5.4 m by stage against a soldier);
  - an encounter whose living members are all out of reach (fleeing, elevated or unreachable) with
    the giant standing at it: after 8 s the bot walks on to the stage's other way out (the
    courtyard's causeway) or to its objective (the barrow's crown) instead of waiting out the
    stage's 240 s watchdog;
  - a fight that has taken no health off its target for 20 s is given up and that enemy left alone
    for 90 s (never the boss): a stage-3 giant chased harassing barrow wolves it never hit until the
    crown stage's watchdog.
- **The balance loop** (plan §5.6): the baseline matrix (`base`), the matrix after the fixes above
  (`fix`), two tuning iterations (`iter1`, `iter2`) and the confirmation matrix (`final`), each a
  full 22-run matrix; every data change is in `docs/BALANCE_LOG.md`.
- **Tools.** `tools/draw_call_probe.tscn` (the Valley's static world, no actors, seen from the 16
  stops of P14's walk in eight directions, with each group hidden in turn; needs Xvfb; prints
  `DRAW_CALLS` lines). `tools/bot_report.py` (Markdown tables of a `run_bots.sh` batch: summary, runs,
  segments, deaths, growth, abilities, perf, the stuck-event heatmap, the AI-state histogram per
  stage and the §5.6 targets; every table in the two reports comes from it; `run_bots.sh`'s header
  names it).
- **Docs.** `docs/HOW_IT_PLAYS.md`, `docs/BALANCE_LOG.md`, `docs/PERFORMANCE.md` (draw calls after
  P17b, the stress route after), this file, and `docs/plans/FOLLOWUPS.md` (both items).

## Public APIs

### SceneKit (`tools/scene_kit.gd`, builders only)

- `merge_meshes(container: Node3D, keep := PackedStringArray(), add_groups := PackedStringArray()) -> int`:
  merges `container`'s visual parts as above; nodes named in `keep` (and everything under them),
  hidden nodes and scripted nodes are left alone; each merged mesh joins `add_groups` (persistent).
  Returns how many meshes were merged (0 when fewer than two qualify: nothing changes).

### EncounterDirector (`scripts/world/encounter_director.gd`)

- `Member.enemy_def: bool`, `Member.faction: String` (from the spawn record).
- `static func counts(m: Member) -> bool`: true when the member takes a slot of the budget: its
  encounter counts toward it, or it is an enemy of a hostile faction. `active_combatant_count()`,
  the waiting spawns and `_can_activate` use it.

### PlayerAttack (`scripts/actors/player/player_attack.gd`)

- `const AIM_STEP_DEG := 2.0`, `AIM_MAX_OFFSET_DEG := 16.0`, `AIM_CORE := 0.5`, `AIM_SAMPLES := 25`.
- `arc_pitch_offset_for(action) -> float`: the aimed tilt while that swing runs, else the stage's.
- `aim_offset_range(action) -> Vector2`: (default tilt, highest tilt).
- `aim_target() -> CombatEntity`: the hard lock (alive), else the soft target, else null.
- `aimed_offset(action, distance, base_y, t_radius, t_height) -> float`, `sweep_reaches(...) -> bool`,
  `closest_approach(action, offset_deg, distance, base_y, t_height) -> float`: the geometry above
  (pure functions of the player's rig; the bot measures its reach band with them).

### PlayerTargeting (`scripts/actors/player/player_targeting.gd`)

- `soft_target(base_yaw: float) -> Array`: `[CombatEntity, yaw delta]` of the soft target in front,
  or `[]`; `soft_target_offset` is now built on it.

### BotCombat and ValleyBot (debug)

- `BotCombat.STANDOFF_REACH := 0.8`, `HEAVY_COMMIT_TICKS := 60`, `HEAVY_COMMIT_RANGE_M := 10.0`;
  `dodge_reserve() -> float`, `affordable(a: ActionDef) -> bool` (the expert's reserve),
  `dodge_planned_within(tick, ticks) -> bool`; `reach_band(action, t_radius, t_height)` now spans the
  aim's tilts.
- `ValleyBot.STALL_WALK_ON_S := 8.0`, `CHASE_GIVE_UP_S := 20.0`, `CHASE_IGNORE_S := 90.0`.
- M6 gate: `ValleyBot.STALL_START_M := 6.0`, `STALL_KEEP_M := 15.0` - the stall's 8 s wait starts
  within 6 m of the encounter's centre and lasts while the giant stays within 15 m (a wolf biting
  from behind pushed the dodging giant a metre or two past 6 m and the wait restarted every few
  seconds, so the expert's crown stage ran out its watchdog with the crown in reach;
  `tests/gate/test_barrow_crown.gd`).

## Decisions and deviations

- **A game-code change in a tuning package: the vertical aim.** Plan §5.6 asks for data-only
  adjustments. The short reach was not a number: every stage's arcs are tilted down to reach the
  giant's feet, and a fixed tilt cannot also reach a soldier at the stage's reach; tuning the tilts
  would have broken the hits at the feet (the M2 gate's wolf finding). The aim keeps the default
  tilt whenever it already reaches, so nothing changes up close. It is a player-facing change for
  the better (blows land where the reach says), tested by `test_vertical_aim`.
- **Bot changes in a tuning package.** The bot is the measuring instrument; each change fixes a
  measured mistake (a refused dodge for want of stamina, a heavy that ate the dodge, standing on the
  brace counter's edge, standing at an encounter with nothing to reach), not the difficulty. The
  novice's perception, latency, parry, block and dodge odds are P17a's, unchanged.
- **The courtyard regressed between the baseline and the fixed bot** (the standoff at 0.8 of the
  aimed band sat on the brace counter's 4.2 m, and every planned dodge held heavies back); iteration
  1 corrected the bot and toned down the brace and the archer's shot (`docs/BALANCE_LOG.md`).
- **Tests updated for deliberate changes**, none weakened: `test_graphics_quality` (the `low` preset
  of the Valley and the stress arena has no shadows), `test_boss` (the slam's 101 damage and 1.5 s
  windup, the combo's 67 / 77; one test renamed `test_the_slam_deals_101_to_a_stage_2_giant`),
  `test_awe_brace` (the brace's 28 damage), `test_npc_rules` (brace P24), `test_ranged_brains` (shot
  P18).
- **`test_obstacle_route` moved to `tests/slow/`** (8-9 s: the sandbox route in both cameras) so
  the default suite stays inside its 75 s budget (P17a left it at 75.8 s of wall time).
- **The navmesh pocket in the village yard** (x 62-72, z -50 to -35; P17a) is not fixed: closing it
  means moving a cottage or the watchtower (and the ambush archer's post), which the alley test and
  the watchtower's ambush depend on, for a pocket only the bot found (its breadcrumbs lead it out).
  Recorded under known gaps.
- **The human time estimate stays below 20 minutes** (about 15 for the novice's completed runs): the
  plan's 20-30 is a person's time; the bot neither explores nor learns, and the shortfall is in the
  Hollow, the forest and the Grove, where the estimate is a floor. No content was added to pad it
  (`docs/HOW_IT_PLAYS.md`).
- **The Ogre King keeps the spec's 1,500 health** although the novice rarely dies there (target 1-2
  deaths): the bot's boss fight is a race it wins narrowly at 1,500; at 1,800 the plain sword of the
  intimidate path loses every attempt and at 2,400 both builds tried do (replays in
  `docs/BALANCE_LOG.md`).
- **No third tuning iteration**: what stayed off target is a property of the bot or would need
  content (`docs/BALANCE_LOG.md`, iteration 2). The plan's confirmation matrix (`final`) followed a
  last bot fix (a given-up enemy is left alone for 90 s, not 30).
- **The report tool is Python** (`tools/bot_report.py`, like `run_bots.sh`'s own summary): it only
  reads the runs' JSON results.
- **The Valley's pillars** (Kingsfall) were left unmerged: `BossArena.pillar_positions()` and the
  smoke test count the pillar group's four children.
