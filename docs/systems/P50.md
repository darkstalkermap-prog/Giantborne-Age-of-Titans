## What was built (plan §3.3's design list)

### 1. The grip through the fist and the off hand (LC-5)

`PoseBrainModifier` places the weapon hand by its **fist**: the fist's centre (the index's `grip_local_r`, in the
hand bone's own space, which a test checks lands on the index's stage-1 `grip_r` at rest) goes where tip matching
or the generic mapping puts the grip, and `WeaponSocket` sits on that centre with the weapon along the grip's
direction, so the handle passes through the closed fist in every pose. Tip matching itself is built for the fist
(`TipMatching.for_fist`: the index's landmarks with each palm moved to its fist's centre and the reach measured to
it, 1.36 instead of the knuckle line's 1.385), so in a tested swing it still decides the hand's place and aim.
The hand is first set as M10 set it (its long axis toward the target, the knuckles across the handle, the wrist
bent at most 45 degrees), then turned toward laying the fist's own handle axis (`grip_axis_r`) along the weapon,
by as much of that turn as the wrist's limit allows (a bisection, `ALIGN_STEPS`); a tested swing keeps M10's
hand (tip matching decides it) and only the generic poses take the turn. The right fist closes by the weapon
family's grip clip (`grip_club`, `grip_sword`, `grip_hammer`, `grip_axe`, `grip_fist`, `grip_tree`; the tree's
node's clip is switched when the family changes). The off hand under the generic mapping keeps its wrist
straight (the hand along its forearm) and takes `open_l` with a 5 % share of `grip_l` (a relaxed hand, not the
bind pose); with the fists both hands close.

### 2. The legs' new sources

`SkinnedRig._drive_tree`, first match wins: dead; airborne; **landing** (`land` on its own clock for 0.25 s after
the floor returns from at least 0.2 s in the air); **stomp** (`stomp_r`: the windup seeked onto the clip's lift
and hold, [0, 0.72], so the foot comes down as the ACTIVE phase starts, the rest onto its settle); **roar** (a
cylinder pulse with morale damage: `roar` by the action's whole fraction; the plan's list has no roar source, the
clip had none to play without it); motion (the run, or **the sprint clip** above 0.8 of the sprint's speed; the
cadence capped at the sprint's); walking (idle, walk, run and **sprint**, the sprint by speed over the sprint's
speed, 0.8 to 0.95); **turning** (the yaw changing faster than 60 degrees a second while the brain does not walk,
held 0.12 s: `turn_l` or `turn_r` on the yaw travelled, a quarter turn a cycle; a snap over 30 degrees in a frame
is a teleport); **bracing** (blocking or guarding, standing: `brace`); the stance. The sources' weights are a chain
from the stance up to the death (`SOURCE_CHAIN`), each seeked only while it shows (since Gate A the tree places them
by a balanced, gated tree with the chain's weights: "Gate A" below).

**The cross-fades** (exponential easing; a fade reaches 95 % in 3 / rate seconds):

| Change | Rate (1/s) | Time to 95 % |
| --- | --- | --- |
| stance, turning, bracing <-> walking | 16 / 18 / 16 | 0.19 / 0.17 / 0.19 s |
| -> airborne | 24 | 0.125 s (M10: 45, 0.07 s; it turned a calf 30 degrees in one frame) |
| airborne -> landing; landing -> walking or stance | 20 | 0.15 s |
| -> stomp, -> roar and back | 20 | 0.15 s |
| -> motion (Shoulder Charge) and back | 12 | 0.25 s (the dash stops dead from 26 m/s with the legs at full stride: at 16 a calf turned 29 degrees in a frame) |
| -> dead | 20 | 0.15 s |
| first person's `fp_rest` in and out | 16 | 0.19 s |
| inside the gait: walk -> run -> sprint | 8 | 0.37 s (M10's speed blend) |
| the off hand's grips | 12 | 0.25 s |

The airborne pose now shows after one frame off the ground (`AIR_FRAMES` 1, M10's 2), so it is still past half by
the third frame (M10's contract) at the softer rate.

### 3. Flinches

`HitFeedback` hands the rig the hit's side (the attacker's place in the giant's own frame) with the flash's
strength on every hit, block and guard break it already flashes; `SkinnedRig.flinch()` plays `flinch_f`, `_b`,
`_l` or `_r` (named by the side the hit came from) through four Add2 nodes, each on its own clock, weighted by
the strength, and refuses one during an attack's ACTIVE phase (a flinch already running fades out at 40/s when
one starts). Nothing reads it back.

### 4. `SecondaryMotion` (`scripts/presentation/secondary_motion.gd`)

Probe 4 passed, so the engine's `SpringBoneSimulator3D` runs the leaf chains, one setting a chain, configured only
for the chains the shown meshes hang on (`SecondaryMotion.chains_for`: none for the crop, the short beard and no
cape). `SecondaryMotion` itself is a `SkeletonModifier3D` after the springs: it holds every joint within its
chain's `max_deg` of the pose its parent carries it in, a cape joint at most 6 degrees toward the front (off the
legs), and every joint at most 720 degrees a second against its parent (an instant change of speed - Shoulder
Charge's dash starts at 26 m/s and stops dead - otherwise whipped a braid from one side of its limit to the other
in one frame: 75 degrees). Colliders: a sphere on the head for the side locks, a capsule across the chest for the
beard (the back hair and the cape have none: probe 4's "only where a chain can reach them"; the cape's limits
keep it off the legs). Resets: a look change, growth, a teleport (a move of more than 3 m x k or a turn of more
than 30 degrees in one frame) and a respawn. **Tuning** (`TUNING`, over the index's starting values; FOLLOWUPS:
the next asset writer moves them into the index):

| Kind | Stiffness | Drag | Gravity | Index's guess |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hair | 3.0 | 0.5 | 0.8 | 1.6 / 0.45 / 0.6 |
| beard | 3.0 | 0.5 | 0.6 | 3.0 / 0.6 / 0.4 |
| cape | 1.2 | 0.5 | 2.5 | 0.9 / 0.35 / 1.0 |

The cape with the index's guesses lifted 50.5 degrees at its root on a sprint (a flag in wind, probe 4's
complaint); with these 40.6 and back at rest 0.25 s after the giant stands still (every joint within 2 degrees of
its resting mean; the idle's breathing still sways the cape's root about 1.5 degrees either way, which is the rest
it comes to). The braids reach their 38-degree limit on a sprint. The cape still reads as one board: its three
chains have no cross links (FOLLOWUPS).

### 5. The reach past a weapon's fit stage (`TipMatching.match_swing_reach`)

When the outer half of the visible weapon would lie farther than 0.95 of the capsule's radius from the tested
segment, the shoulder is carried toward the tip: the clavicle's protraction (at most 0.08 x k), then the spine's
lean about the pelvis (at most 12 degrees), then the pelvis's shift (at most 0.10 x k), each by the least that
brings the outer half within 0.75 of the radius - else within the radius - at most 15 degrees off, by bisection;
if nothing suffices, the nearest it came (the limit of honest reach). A weapon that fits never moves the body (a
test compares every fitted sample with M10's solve). On the bones (`PoseBrainModifier._extend`) the lean turns
spine_01-03 by a third each and the clavicle protracts; the pelvis then moves by what those joints fell short of
the shoulder the solve wanted (they turn about their own joints, the solve about the pelvis), within its 0.10,
and both feet are brought back where they stood by a two-bone solve of each leg.

### 6. First person

At rest in first person the arms take `fp_rest` (the weapon in the lower right of the view, in the fist's own
line); it fades out as the view turns down from -30 to -48 degrees, where the arm hangs as in M10 and the lower
corners stay clear (`test_first_person_shoulders`: at most 0.005 of a corner at -55 to -85 degrees, stage 1 and
6). A block raises both forearms into the view (the brain's block pose; frame `m11a_41_fp_block`). **Not done:
the skirt's waist fade** (see "Deviations").

### 7. The capture tour (`scripts/debug/capture_m11.gd`, `--scenario=capture_m11 --part=a`)

`CaptureM11` (a `CaptureM10` with its own step) puts the giant on the most open ground near the start (rays round
it) for the moving beats, dresses him in the young giant's look with the long braided hair, the braided beard and
the wayfarer's cape (every chain on screen), and at each moment pauses the tree and films it from the side, the
front and three-quarters with its own camera (k times its stage-1 distance; pulled in front of a wall as a spring
arm is), then gives the game's camera back a tick later; first-person moments through the game's camera. 43 beats,
56 moments, 163 frames: idle, walk, run, sprint, the stop, a turn each way, a jump and the landing, the club's,
sword's, hammer's and axe's light and heavy, the stomp (lifted, held, planted), the roar, Stone Skin, a block,
both dodges, Shoulder Charge, a grab, hold and throw, four flinches, stage 4 walking, the fists' light and heavy,

and its measures (the legs' source and the gait at each moment, the flinches by side, the grip over the generic
poses, the secondary motion's worst over its limits, the reach used).

### First person at rest (Gate A2)

The merge's tour showed the club upright over the right third of the view at rest in first person (0.10 drew none).
**Found by measuring the frame** (`tests/support/skinned_rig_probe.gd` mode "fp_rest": the weapon's pixels with and
without it, 480x270, pitch 0): stage 1, the club 0.183 of the frame with its box from the top edge to the bottom,
the sword 0.109, the hammer 0.083; stage 6's unreforged club and sword were small (0.015, 0.006) but its hammer

## Decisions as built

- The fist, not the knuckle line, is what tip matching places (`for_fist`); its contract holds unchanged.
- In a tested swing the hand keeps M10's orientation; only the generic poses turn the fist onto the handle.
- A roar source was added (the plan lists the clip, not the source).
- The springs' joints are rate-limited (720 degrees a second) in the limiter; the springs' own state is untouched.
- The pelvis also makes up for the clavicle's and the spine's shortfall against the solve's shoulder (bounded by
  its own 0.10 x k), so the reach holds in play and not only in the pure model.

## Deviations

- **The skirt's waist fade (design item 6) is not done.** A waist box in `giant_first_person.gdshaderinc` (tried:
  centre 2.08 m, 0.40 by 0.17 by 0.34 m) hid the caps but opened the limbs' and the skirt's tubes, so the view down
  showed their insides - worse than the caps. Closing the cut needs geometry (a dark inner cap on the limbs and the
  skirt part, an asset change) or a back-face pass in `giant_cloth`/`giant_skin` (P51's files in W30). FOLLOWUPS.
- **`carry_two_hand` is not wired**: the brain carries the tree one-handed over the shoulder (24, 30); the off hand
  on the trunk needs a carrying pose of its own. FOLLOWUPS.
- The creator's preview does not run `SecondaryMotion` (its posing is `giant_preview.gd`'s, not this package's).
  FOLLOWUPS.
- The skin's CPU on the Web was not measured (no Web export in this package). FOLLOWUPS.
