# P38 — The world bot

Wave 22, alone. Branch `pkg/P38`. Plan: `docs/plans/M6_M9_PLAN.md` §3.22 with §6.1 (the world bot:
commands, presets, structure, routing, goals, watchdogs, the human estimate, metrics, invariants),
§5.15 (the segments after the Valley) and decision 28. It depends on P27 (the map's data and fast
travel), P35 and P36 (every region) and P37 (the wander and event APIs).

The Valley bot (P17a) now plays the open world: from `--mode=world --new-game` (`world_start`) or, with
`--bot-campaign`, from the Valley's New Game through the Kings' Door, to the Stone Throne, under an
oath policy in any order. It routes across the streamed 2 km world on the road graph, answers the
yield prompts, breaks the den's braziers, fights the Iron Regiment's crews and the fort's ballistae,
keeps inside the boss arenas, measures how the world plays and checks the world's stability
invariants every tick.

## What was built

### `scripts/debug/bot_route.gd` (BotRoute)

The route planner (§6.1 "Routing", decision 13).

- **The road graph by stage.** `road_path(index, from, to, stage)`: A* over `WorldIndex.roads` from
  the node nearest the giant that its stage may leave (`nearest_leavable_node`: one of the node's
  edges allowed) to the node nearest the goal, using only the edges `WorldIndex.edge_allowed(e,
  stage)` admits (`min_stage` / `max_stage`: the Giant's Stair's three edges need stage 6, the
  Needle's slot is stage 4 at most). It returns the nodes, the edges and the length. The start skips
  a stage-closed spur's nodes because the giant stands where it walked (the Ballista Ridge's east end
  has two nodes whose only edges are stage-limited: at stage 5 no road left them); the goal's end
  keeps the nearest node, so a goal only a closed edge reaches has no road at that stage.
- **`plan(from, to)`**: the route's points are the road nodes then the goal, the first node dropped
  when the giant is already past it and the last when the goal lies before it. A goal within 250 m is
  walked on the navmesh without the road, unless the road there takes a `direct` edge (the Needle's
  slot has no titan navmesh: its steps are the only way through). A far goal with no road at the
  giant's stage plans false (the goal alone).
- **`steer(pos, tick, world)`**: one tick of the route. A `direct` step is steered at straight. Else
  the leg's target is the farthest route point at most `LEG_MAX_M` (250 m) along the route whose
  chunk is loaded and ready; a target in a chunk that is not ready is not asked for — the leg aims at
  the last point before it and names the chunk (`waiting_chunk`). The leg is a `NavQuery.path` on the
  stage's titan query mask (`PlayerGrowth.nav_query_mask`: 16 | 32 | 64 | 128 by stage), purpose
  `far`; a path that ends short of its target is asked again for nearer points (3 tries, the best
  kept). It is asked again every 2 s, 3 m off the path, or when the target changes — so a chunk that
  becomes ready moves the target at the next steer (replanning across chunk loads).
- **The giant's place along the route** (`_advance`): a point within 8 m is passed, and so are the
  points before a later segment (within six points, not past a direct step) that lies clearly nearer
  the giant than the one it is on. A navigation path does not keep to the road's line — it rounds the
  hill or the house the road crosses — and a point it never came within 8 m of held the route back:
  the leg window, measured from that point, shrank behind the giant, which turned round
  (found by the slow test: 2,555 m walked for 688 m planned, oscillating south of Brannock).
- **`fast_travel_point(index, from, to, stage, visited, flag_ok)`**: a goal over 600 m away with a
  visited waystone or shrine within 200 m of it, open to fast travel, whose flags hold and whose
  `max_stage` admits the giant: the nearest such to the goal.

### `scripts/debug/world_bot.gd` (WorldBot extends ValleyBot)

- **Options** (after `--`): `--scenario=world` with the Valley bot's (`--bot-skill`, `--bot-assist`,
  `--bot-seed`, `--bot-loop`, `--bot-trace`) and `--bot-preset=peaceful|conqueror|mixed`,
  `--bot-oaths=march:help,wood:drive,crown:parley` (also the order), `--bot-oath-count=2|3`,
  `--bot-champion=spare|slay|talk`, `--bot-corvane=spare|strike`, `--bot-ancestry=`,
  `--bot-explore=<share>`, `--bot-campaign` (with `--mode=valley --scenario=valley`), and
  `--bot-until=<flag>` (the run ends, `finish_reason` "until", once that world flag holds: the gate's
  smoke run stops at `oath.march.done`).
- **Presets** (§6.1): `peaceful` march help, wood drive, crown parley, Harrok by talk when the talk
  ending is open (stage 6, three oaths, Skarn's support) else spared, Corvane spared, Stoneblood;
  `conqueror` march force, wood slay, crown breach, Corvane struck, Harrok slain, Wildheart; `mixed`
  march intimidate, crown sabotage, wood slay, Harrok spared, Stormborn. Two oaths by default (the
  first two of the list). `tools/run_bots.sh`'s `world_orders` runs each oath first with a stage-4
  resolution: `order_march` (march help, crown parley), `order_wood` (wood drive, march force),
  `order_crown` (crown breach, wood slay).
- **Plan, every 6 ticks.** A yield waiting for its decision is decided through
  `EnemyActor.p34_decide` (Corvane and the battle by `--bot-corvane`, Harrok strike only for the slay,
  else spare) — never by the prompt's buttons. Then: the objective kinds that fight on their own terms
  keep their objective at their place even with threats near (the den, the battle, the duel: within
  120 m of their marker); else a fight when aware hostiles are near (an enemy above the giant's reach
  with no ranged blow ready is no fight in the world: an archer on Fort Aldmere's wall kept the giant
  standing under it; and a chase given up on a formation's member - 20 s without harm, the Valley's
  rule - gives the whole formation up for its 90 s: a braced shield wall takes nothing off from the
  front); else errands (rest below 45 % at a shrine within 150 m, reinforce and titanforge at an
  open forge within 150 m, the ancestry and the talent build at a shrine); else the main thread's
  stage: the beacon, grow4, the oaths in the policy's order (growth to stage 5 after the
  first oath once level 9 allows it), grow6 at the Standing Stones, the Moot, the champion, the throne.
- **The oaths.** March help: Edda's offer, then the toll gate, the toll fort and the Raiders' Hollow
  (nearest first), then Edda's "help"; intimidate: Edda; force: Brannock's south gate broken, then
  Edda's hall. Wood: Sedge's accept (Stormborn's bargain for a drive), the den, then Sedge's ending.
  Crown breach: the fort gate broken, then the battle; sabotage: the nearest standing camp or ridge
  engine until the army withdraws (while Aldmere is at peace its engines refuse a lock — P03's
  hostile-only targeting — so the first blow is a melee one, the offence that turns the army
  hostile; Crag Hurl follows once they can be targeted); parley: a token (Brannock's banner, the
  deserters' word — Sergeant Wynn spared at the burned mill — Stoneblood, stature), the herald, then
  Corvane's parley at the fort.
- **Encounters to clear** (the beacon's scouts, the toll fort, the Raiders' Hollow): the nearest
  living member on the giant's ground, else the encounter's centre - except, from stage 4, when a
  member above reach is all that is left: then the ground 16 m from it (`_elevated_shot_spot`), where
  ranged blows reach it (the toll fort's tower archers stand 13 m up behind their cabin's wall: from
  the fort's centre, 31 m off, every bolt struck the wall).
- **The den.** Slay: the Matriarch fought, a brazier broken while she stands within 7.5 m of it (8 s
  of Burning). Drive (P35's rules: her flight needs the three nests burned, the back wall open, and
  her health at or below 60 %): the three braziers first (each burns the nest beside it), then she is
  struck while above 35 % — below that only her whelps are fought and her blows defended — until the
  woodfolk open the wall (20 s after the last nest), then fought until she runs; a fleeing boss is no
  threat and is not struck.
- **The battle.** Its target: the nearest crew (distance halved: the engines deal most of the
  battle's damage), else the nearest unit of the Wall, the Charge or the Last Stand, or a fort
  ballista (distance × 0.7); nothing out of reach above. An engine is broken by blows or Crag Hurl.
  The two bastion ballistae stand 16 m up, out of reach: while one still shoots, the bot throws Crag
  Hurl at it from 14–38 m (stepping to 26 m on the bailey's side) and Runic Bolt at its crew
  (`_battle_ranged`); after 8 s of ranged blows at one target without hurting it, it is left alone
  for 2 minutes.
- **The arenas.** An engaged BossArena's disengage rule resets the attempt (the boss at full health)
  when the giant stays 20 m outside its floor for 5 s; a fight drifts. While an arena is engaged and
  the giant stands more than 8 m outside its floor it walks back toward the floor's centre, until
  within 5 m, instead of fighting on (`_arena_leash`; the conqueror's first run reset Harrok twice
  before it). Each walk back starts the stuck watch afresh: one that ends got back in, and a watch
  carried over from the last one read the fights between them as no progress.
- **A boss fight's respawn waystone first** (`_respawn_point_first`): before the den, the battle
  (from the fort gate's breach) and the duel, the waystone within 160 m of the fight's place is made
  the respawn point, as a player would (the first crown run died in the bailey and woke at the
  Kingsgate shelf, a kilometre away); a checkpoint is not taken with enemies aware near, so the bot
  waits there up to 15 s, then goes on. Never once the fight is engaged: no checkpoint commits then,
  and the walk out pulled against the leash (the Moot's duel engages as the champion's stage begins,
  with the Moot foot waystone 58 m outside the ring).
- **Exploration** (`--bot-explore=<share>`): before an oath, that share of the oath region's POIs not
  yet discovered (and within the giant's stage) is visited, nearest first, each given up after 120 s.
- **Fast travel** (`_try_fast_travel`): `BotRoute.fast_travel_point`, then
  `GameSession.travel_refusal` (mid-fight: "Not in the middle of a fight."; the refusal is recorded
  and the checkpoint not tried again for 30 s, the giant walks), then `GameSession.travel_to(level,
  checkpoint, "fast_travel")` as the map's Travel does (a map screen counted).
- **Movement and stuck recovery.** `steer_path` follows BotRoute; progress for the stuck check is the
  route's remaining length (a new plan's own length from the moment it is made: a replan measures
  along another line, and the ladder's level stays), and a leg waiting for a chunk is not stuck. The
  Valley's recovery ladder (sidestep and jump, a new route or the breadcrumbs back, a recorded
  teleport to the leg's next point on the terrain, a breach).
- **Watchdogs.** 300 s per main-thread stage (600 s for a boss stage: the den, the battle, the
  champion; 900 s for the oaths' other stages) plus a travel allowance (the stage's road route at
  4 m/s); the key is the main stage with the active oath quest's stage (and `/grow5` while growing),
  so each sub-goal has its own clock; 5 deaths per stage unassisted; stuck.
- **The campaign.** With `--bot-campaign` the Valley's own logic plays the prologue until
  `slice.completed`, then the world bot's (the Kings' Door, then the world). `--bot-loop` starts a new
  world game after each completed campaign, rotating the presets.

### `scripts/debug/world_metrics.gd` (WorldMetrics)

What only the world has, in `AUTOPILOT_RESULT.world_bot.world`: time per region and per stage,
regions entered, POIs discovered with their kind, sealed places opened, cairns read, the XP floors
applied, iron and titan iron over time, oath and boss outcomes, boss engagements, attempts (a
disengage or a reload starts a new one), phases and yields, formation stats (group, banner and single
lock samples at stages 4–6 with formations near, banners felled, formations broken and routed,
Formation Sweep uses), siege stats (engines destroyed and thrown, engine shots at the giant), wander
spawns by template, world events (starts and outcomes), fast travels and refusals, map screens,
streaming (loads, unloads, thrash — summed over level loads — the worst work frame, the smallest
margin of loaded ground ahead), path queries per tick (p50, p95, max), the music intensity per stage
(explore / danger / combat seconds while audio runs) and the largest save written. Its human-estimate
terms (6 s per map screen, 4 s per fast travel, 10 s per region entered first, 5 s per POI, 8 s per
sealed place) go into the current segment.

### Invariants and diagnosis

The Valley's invariants run in the world too, and `BotDiagnosis.check_world_invariants` adds §6.1's:
formation members ≤ the director's cap; no living member in an unloaded chunk; loaded chunks ≤ 25;
every layer map's regions ≤ 25 per layer and gate bit; no chunk reloaded within the thrash window; no
two world events at once; no event's fail timer running while its centre chunk is unloaded (after a
12-tick grace); the save ≤ 200 KB. The cheap checks run every tick, the member and region scans every
30 ticks. `diagnose_world` adds the index route (stage, road nodes, length, the goal's chunk and
whether it is loaded) and per-chunk path checks of its consecutive points where both are loaded and
ready: world_blocked when no road route exists at the stage for a goal beyond the loaded ground, when
a loaded leg of the route has no path (and none from where the giant was earlier), or when the
condition cannot hold; bot_failed otherwise.

## Decisions and deviations

- **The encounter budget in the world.** §6.1 keeps the Valley's "active combatants ≤ 12" among the
  world's invariants. The director's own rule (P10a, P17b) lets a faction's members already in the
  world stay in the fight when the faction turns hostile — they count from then on, and new spawns wait
  — so the count may pass the cap: the conqueror's march (Brannock's south gate broken beside the
  Raiders' Hollow shield wall) put 16 counted combatants in play for 0.15 s and the first conqueror run
  stopped there (exit 3). In the world the bot checks the director's rule itself instead: no counted
  member may spawn while the count is already at the cap (`_watch_budget`: the count re-read at every
  bot tick, flag change and spawn; a spawn that raises a count above the cap is a failure). The peak
  and the time over the cap are reported (`world_bot.budget`: `max_counted`, `over_cap_s`) for P39.
  The Valley bot keeps the strict check.
- **Objectives that fight.** §6.1's goal order puts a fight first. At the den, the battle and the duel
  the objective's own act fights instead (its order: the braziers before the Matriarch for a drive, the
  crews and the fort ballistae first in the battle, the ring kept in the duel); elsewhere the fight
  comes first as planned.
- **The arena leash** is the bot's: nothing in §6.1 asks for it, but a fight that drifts beyond the
  disengage line resets the boss to full health (the conqueror's first run: Harrok reset twice, then
  the giant died to the Moot battery's catapult).
- **A boss fight's respawn waystone** is the bot's too (§6.1 does not ask for it): the second crown
  run died in the bailey and woke at the Kingsgate shelf, a kilometre and the watchdog away. It is
  taken before the fight engages, never after (no checkpoint commits then).
- **The engage radius** is the Valley's table at stages 1–3 (the Valley's runs unchanged) and
  `max(the stage-3 value, 2.5 × the profile's melee reach)` from stage 4 (§6.1: 2.5 × the reach).
- **The watchdog's clock** adds a travel allowance (the stage's road route at 4 m/s) to §6.1's 300 /
  600 / 900 s and runs per sub-goal (the main stage with the active oath quest's stage): grow6 from
  the Greywood to the Standing Stones is over 1.5 km of road.
- **The drive-out** waits for the woodfolk (20 s after the last nest) rather than heaving the wall at
  stage 5: the heave is P35's shortcut; the bot's order (braziers, a held fight, then the fight to her
  flight) works at stages 4 and 5 alike.
- **Talents**: a fixed build per skill of passives only (the loadout the Valley taught stays); the
  ancestry per preset (Stoneblood: a parley token; Wildheart; Stormborn: the wood's bargain).
- **Exploration** (`--bot-explore`) visits POIs only; the bot opens no sealed place and reads no
  cairn. All the acceptance runs use the default 0.
- **Invariant cadence**: the cheap world checks run every tick, the member-in-unloaded-chunk scan and
  the per-layer region count every 30 ticks (the scans walk every member and every region).
- **`--bot-until=<flag>`** (not in §6.1) ends a run when a world flag holds: the gate's smoke run stops
  at Brannock's oath.
- **An enemy above reach** with no ranged blow ready is no fight in the world (an archer on Fort
  Aldmere's wall held the giant under it, beside the gate it was to break, until the watchdog).
- **`scripts/core/game_session.gd`** is not edited: `travel_to(level, checkpoint, "fast_travel")`
  needs no screen.
- **The default tier's budget** (+0.1 s): the package's own default suite takes 6 ms, but
  `test_anchors` compiles every script in the project, the package's new 3,500 lines with them
  (0.17–0.20 s), so the default tier grows by about 0.2 s; within decision 34's 75 s.
- **The acceptance runs ran three at a time** on the 4-CPU machine (the task allowed two bot runs at
  once): their simulated results are deterministic and unaffected; their work frames, CPU per tick and
  wall times are indicative.

## Known gaps

- **Fast travel** is taken only when a goal lies over 600 m away with a visited waystone or shrine
  within 200 m of it (§6.1's rule). The main thread's goals are new places the first time, and the
  walks back (Sedge after the den, Edda after the toll fort) are under 600 m: the acceptance runs
  travelled on foot throughout, so the travel path (`_try_fast_travel`, its refusal and retry) has run

## P39 (W23): what the balance pass changed in the bot

P39's measurement lane (plan §3.23: "bot fixes that correct measurement mistakes only") changed the bot
where a §6.4 target was missed because of how the bot measured or played, not because of the game.
`tests/slow/test_bot_measures_p39.gd` covers the lock sampling, the growth shares, the siege disruption,
the loop's rewards and the abilities' timing (each of those checks fails on 1c33b8e); the boss fights'
damage split and the waystones on the way are exercised by the balance matrix's runs.

- **The formation lock share** (§6.4: at least half of the locks at stages 4-6 are formation groups or
  banners "when formations are present"). `WorldMetrics` sampled a lock whenever any formation lived in
  the loaded world - a troll fight with the war camp 300 m off counted as a single lock with formations
  present. It now samples only while a formation is engaged with the giant: a living, aware, hostile
  member within `RunMetrics.HOSTILE_NEAR_M` (40 m). On P38's rule the slow test's lone-soldier lock with
  the only formation 100 m away gave 60 single-lock samples a second; now 0.
- **The growth and readability shares** (§6.4) are measured directly (`world.growth`, sampled every 6
  ticks at stages 4-6 among the enemies engaged with the giant): lone humans (a soldier, archer or mage
  in no intact formation, no crew, no boss) - engaged and fleeing-or-routed seconds; formations whose
  banner lives - engaged and holding (formed, advancing or braced) seconds; and the seconds with only
  lesser enemies engaged (none the music director counts: `MusicDirector.is_special`, the mix's
  `lesser_height_ratio`) with the music's intensity over them (`world.music_lesser`). P38's notes had
  these only through the AI-state histogram, where a formation's pikemen and a lone soldier share the
  `soldier` archetype.
- **The boss fights' damage by source**: while a boss fight is engaged, the damage the giant takes is
  added to that boss's record by the attacker's archetype (`world.bosses[id].damage_taken_by`; the M8
  release check's question: how much of the Iron Regiment's damage the fort ballistae deal).
- **The ability damage share** (§6.4: none over 40 %) leaves an engine's throw out as it left
  `throw_held` out (`PlayerGrab.is_throw`): a grab's throw is no slotted ability.
- **Siege disruption at stage 6** (§5.14; P38's runs threw 0-1 engines): a hostile engine on the giant's
  level within 45 m (`SIEGE_SEEK_M`) is seized before the fight goes on - struck until its poise breaks
  (a heavy) unless its crew is gone, locked and grabbed (interact), then thrown at the nearest enemy
  (BotCombat's throw locks it first); an engine tried for 25 s without being seized or hurt is left
  alone for 90 s. The smash task (sabotage, the Skarn line) and the battle seize an engine the same way
  at stage 6. A faction's engine at peace is no target (P03's hostile-only targeting).
- **Waystones on the way** (P38's runs never used fast travel: no far goal had a visited stone near
  it): out of combat, an unvisited waystone or shrine within 50 m (`WAYSTONE_DETOUR_M`) that the
  giant may use (flags, `max_stage`) is walked into, as a player lights the stones the compass shows;
  one not reached in 30 s is given up. Fast travel's own rule (a goal over 600 m away with a visited
  stone within 200 m of it) is unchanged.
- **A loop's next game** (`--bot-loop`, §6.2's 60-minute world run): the rewards already given were read
  before the new game emptied the world, so the new game's first reward - Kings' Door's, granted as the
  world starts - counted as given twice and the first world stability run stopped at its first loop
  (exit 3, "reward granted twice: reward.world_main.kings_door"). Both bots now read them after
  `GameSession.change_mode` (which empties the world at once). The Valley's loop had the same order; its
  first reward comes later, so it never tripped.
- **The abilities' timing at stages 4-6**: BotCombat's look at the abilities (the novice's once a second)
  that found the giant mid-blow was spent; from stage 4 it now waits for the giant's next free tick (P38's
  peaceful run: Titan Stomp used in 0 of its 9 ready windows, Sweeping Blow 0 of 13). The Valley (stages
  1-3) keeps P17a's rule, so its matrix stays comparable with the M6 gate's.
- **`--bot-skarn=5|6`** (a measurement option): on the way to the Standing Stones (stage 5) or the Moot
  (stage 6) the giant takes the siege of Skarn as a new player would - Ulfra's plea at stage 5 (a stage-6
  giant's arrival starts it), Skarn's green, then the line's five engines nearest first, seized at stage
  6, while defending - until the event ends; its watchdog is 900 s.
- **Shots at an enemy above reach go along a clear line** (the 60-minute world run's march breach): the

  giant 17 m east of the tower, where P38's shot spot (16 m from the nearest archer, toward the giant) put
