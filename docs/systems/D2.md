# D2 — The Stormcaller, on the Stormstair at the top of the Drowned Steps

Milestone D's second package, on `pkg/d2` from D1's merge. The version stays **0.16.0-m16** (development toward 0.17).
The sixth signature titan: the old kings' bell-warden, who rang the kingdom's great bell when they drowned the lowland
road to bar the Worldbreaker's way south, and has stood in the storm it called ever since. Decisions D2-1..D2-13 are in
`docs/DECISIONS.md` §2.7; the duels are BALANCE_LOG "D2"; what is left is FOLLOWUPS "From D2".

## 1. Where he is: the Stormstair

The top of the Drowned Steps under the Stonehold's wall, north of the Mere of Kings and west of the Giant's Stair, made
with the world builder (`tools/data/world/world.json` features, `drowned_steps.json` elements; chunks rebuilt):

- **Three pools on terraces** (`basin` features): the **Bellmere** (100 x 54 m, its bed at 18.2 m and its water at
  24.2 m: 6 m deep at its middle - to a stage-7 giant's hip, a stage-9's knee), the **Stepmere** a step down south-east
  (3.2 m) and the **Lowmere** a step down south-west (2 m). Each terrace is flattened into the slope (cut on its uphill
  side, an embankment blending down on its downhill side) and the basins shelve gently from a 10-12 m shore.
- **Rock humps** round them (`mounds`), four boulders, the **Bellframe** (the drowned kings' trilithons on the Bellmere's
  north-west shore: the landmark and the POI `poi.stormstair`), a warning post where the way up from the Mere reaches
  the lowest terrace (a bronze bell's broken lip and a blackened skull).
- **The storm's water** (`storm_water` element, `StormWater`): the pools as the lightning sees them - depth = the
  surface less the ground under a body's feet. Calm (only water) once he has fallen.
- **The storm** (`storm_weather` element, `StormWeather`, and the world's far `storms` clouds): from act two (the
  throne's fall woke him) the light over the Steps goes dark and grey-green, the fog closes in, rain is driven slant
  (one CPU particle system of additive streaks: no dark quads), far bolts walk in it with their thunder after them by the
  distance, and the Steps' ambience gains the rain loop. It clears over 14 s after his fall.

He stands at home on the Bellmere's north shore leaning on his rod (dry ground: 9 m north of the water). Sight does not
wake him (`TitanTerritory`, `wake_on_sight` off); a giant climbing into his pools, or a blow, does.

## 2. How the fight plays

**The storm's rule (D2-2).** The giant has a wet share: how much of him is under the storm's water (depth over his
height; `PlayerStorm`). The Stormcaller calls lightning; a bolt that strikes **water** (0.8 m deep or more) sends its
**shock** through it to every body standing in water within 32 m, by the **square** of its wet share (a quarter under:
the whole shock; half as deep: a quarter of it). **Size matters**: the same Bellmere is to a stage-7 giant's hip and a
stage-9's knee, so a bigger giant takes far less of the shock and wades faster (the water slows by 1.25 x the share).
**The place gives the opening**: he is the tallest thing in his own storm - a bolt of his striking water within reach
of him while he stands 3 m deep or more **grounds** him (down on a knee for 4.5 s, his blow cut short; not again for
16 s). **The ground telegraphs**: each called bolt is a red circle on the ground at once (and a ring on the water), then
the strike 2.0-2.6 s later; the HUD's water meter shows how deep he stands and turns to "LIGHTNING ON THE WATER - get
out!" while a bolt is coming down on water within its reach. **Counterplay**: step out of the water (a rim, the shore,
the terraces' dry ground) when the circles come; fight him from the shallows; lure him into the Bellmere and let his own
lightning put him on his knee, then go for his crown.

**His moves** (each telegraphed; damage against a smaller giant scaled by size, exponent 2.0): the **rod-slam** (46,
a shock line down the rod), the **rod-sweep** (36, the circling answer), the **gust** (10; it throws a smaller giant
far - into the water), the **call** (three bolts: the first on the giant's feet led 0.3 s, two round him 8-20 m out),
the **stamp** (46, a giant hugging his legs). **Phase 2** at 62 %: his roar breaks the storm open (a ring of six bolts
26 m round him), the **chain** (seven bolts walking down the ground toward the giant). **Phase 3** at 28 %: the eye of
the storm - a bolt falls on the giant by itself every 8 s (the phase's hazard) and the **ring** of ten bolts 20 m round
him.

**His plates**: the tined crown (head), two bronze pauldrons, the great bell on his back (broken, it unbalances him:
poise x1.4), the lodestone in his breast, the bracer on his rod arm, the greaves (broken, he goes down on a knee for
5 s). Health 10,000, poise 800, armour 75. 34.0 m tall: over a stage-8 giant (29.3 m), so his crown is in reach when he
kneels (grounded, a greave).

**His death** drops the Lodestone, seven drowned bronze and two storm tines (and each broken plate's own); the storm
clears, the pools are only water, and Maera's dialogue closes the quest.

## 3. Him as an asset

`tools/giant/kit/stormcaller.py` (body and gear) and `stormcaller_moves.py` (17 clips) build him in the people kit
(`build_people.py --stormcaller`, group `stormcaller`): a tall, upright, broad-shouldered giant 34.0 m (111.5 ft) with
slate-grey skin scarred by lightning (forked scars), three braided beard plaits, a crown of a copper band and seven
forked tines whose tips glow, two bronze pauldrons, the kingdom's great bell (10.2 m) strapped on his back, a lodestone
in his breast in a copper setting, a wire-bound bracer on his rod arm, bronze greaves, and the storm-rod (34 m: an iron
haft with a forked copper head). Silhouette: the rod standing over his head and the bell on his back, against the
Frostbound's stoop, the Bastion's wall and the Rootwarden's tree; his face and his voice are his own.

`StormcallerModel` / `StormcallerRig` (`scripts/presentation/`) drive the plates' cracks and charge (the crown's tips and
the lodestone glow as he calls), the bolts (`StormFx`: additive, unshaded quads and lines; no screen reads), the water's
warning rings and the shocks' rings, splashes and dust.

## 4. The reward

- **The Stormcaller set** (medium; seven pieces; `data/armour/armour_storm_*.json`, `set_stormcaller.json`): Tined
  Crown, Bell-bronze Pauldrons, Lodestone Cuirass, Wire-bound Bracers, the girdle, Bronze Greaves, the Lodestone
  (trinket). 2 pieces: the shock through water takes 30 % less of you; 4: armour +5 and elemental resistance +10 %;
  7: the shock a further 40 % less and the water slows you half as much. Made at Last Hearth's titan forges (the
  Hearth's forge, the Colossus Anvil; `works: storm_bronze`, forge tier `titan`) once he has fallen. Drawn on the giant:
  six modelled pieces (`giant_garment.py`'s cast bronze slabs and copper ribs).
- **The Bell-hammer** (a hammer, x1.62, fits to stage 9): **ring-through** - its heavies on a foe standing 0.6 m or more
  in water land half again as hard (damage and poise; the weapon perk `ring_through`).
- What he drops pays for the set and the hammer from one kill with two plates broken (a test holds it).

## 5. The quest and the world's answer

`quest.stormcaller`: from the look south from the Breach's top (D1's `lead.stormcaller.seen`), from finding the
Stormstair, or from his fall - climb into his pools, bring him down, tell Maera. Her dialogue (`dialogue_hearth_maera`):
before (`storm_watch`: how he is fought - out of the water when the circles come, lure him in), after his fall
(`storm_fallen`: the storm is gone from the Drowned Steps; sets `world.last_hearth.storm_told`, 240 XP) and after
(`after_storm`). **The world changes**: the storm clears over the Steps (light, fog, rain, far bolts, the rain's
ambience), the pools are only water, and the Lodestone stands on a plinth at Last Hearth beside the Heart-ice
(`world.hoarfell.hearth_lodestone`).

**A lead toward Harrok, only a lead**: Maera tells the pilgrims' rumour that Harrok's banner is on the road again
(`lead.harrok.hearth`); `quest.harrok_lead` sends the giant to look in at the Moot, where they last met
(`lead.harrok.seen`). Nothing of his return is built.

**No stage 9.** The growth track is unchanged. (The duels' "stage-9 numbers" are a test profile only, see BALANCE_LOG.)

## 9. What is weak

- **The duels are a bot's**, on the test arena's three pools; the bot steps out of the water on every warning and lures
  him to the deep pool on a rule. Stage 8 is two wins in three; the loss had him at 17 %.
- **The water is a flat plane**: the world's water over the basins (its shader's ripples, no waves); the storm's own
  water shader (ripples, the rain's rings) is used on the test arena and the tarn's open water.
- **His look is the kit's**: a MakeHuman-derived body, bronze as a shader on hard slabs. The bolts are additive quads
  and lines, not volumetric; the rain is streaks round the camera.
- **The set's pieces are slabs over the jerkin**, like the other titans' sets.
- **His voice and the thunder are synthesised** (`gen_world_sfx.py`): convincing at a distance, plain close up.
- **Last Hearth's stone ring is still not solid** (D1-11; not attempted).
