# P19 — Audio engine

Wave 12, lane A (beside P20). Branch `pkg/P19`. Plan: `docs/plans/M6_M9_PLAN.md` §3.1, with §2.5
(the engine, the record types, the router's conventions, the directors), §4.1 (the cue map), §4.3
(music), §4.4 (the mix), §4.5 (the size arithmetic), decisions 1-8 and 34, and §1.6 (the two W12
hunks shared with P20). The goal: everything the sound packages need so that they only add data
and generated files.

## What was built

- **The `Audio` autoload** (`scripts/audio/audio_director.gd`, `class_name AudioDirector`, last in
  the autoload order). Presentation only: it listens, reads state in `_process`, never emits a
  gameplay signal, never spends or changes anything and never touches `Sim.rng` or the global RNG
  (every child owns a private `RandomNumberGenerator`). Enabled, it makes the buses Music, Ambience,
  SFX and UI (sending to Master, no effects), builds the voice pool from `audio_mix.default`, loads
  the cue catalogue from every `audio_bank` record, connects the router to `Events` and wires rig
  footfalls and screens through `get_tree().node_added`. `--no-audio` leaves it off from the start:
  no buses, listener or players, nothing plays, `is_ready()` is true. The test runner switches it
  off for the whole run unless `--with-audio` (the buses, once made, stay).
  Children: `Pool` (VoicePool), `Music` (MusicDirector, `PROCESS_MODE_ALWAYS`), `Ambience`
  (AmbienceDirector, `PROCESS_MODE_PAUSABLE`), `Router` (CueRouter) and `Listener` (an
  `AudioListener3D` at the giant's chest, camera pivot height x 0.8, turned with the camera's yaw and
  pitch).
- **The loading gate.** When a level appears (a node of group `level_root`, else `sandbox`) the
  autoload registers that level's SFX samples (`AudioServer.register_stream_as_sample`; global cues
  first, then the cues whose `levels` name the level, in cue-id order) in slices of at most
  `register_ms_per_frame` (12 ms). `scripts/main.gd` builds the level with the tree paused, keeps it
  paused behind "Loading... N%" (the label is `PROCESS_MODE_ALWAYS`) until `Audio.is_ready()`, and
  then unpauses unless a pausing screen opened meanwhile. Paused frames run no tick, so the seeded
  timeline is the same with audio on or off; no `play()` ever registers a sample. (M6 gate: the
  gate also waits for the level's navigation map to sync, the autopilot - and with it the Valley
  bot - does nothing while it holds, and headless runs sync navigation on the main thread; before,
  seeded Valley bot runs diverged with and without audio. `tests/gate/test_valley_audio_determinism.gd`.) Samples a new level
  no longer needs are dropped with their streams (Godot has no unregister call: a web sample goes
  with its last stream).
- **Playing** (`Audio.play` / `play_request`): the cue after its fallback chain; a Music-bus cue
  plays as a stinger; a threat sting under the suppression thresholds is dropped; then the
  retrigger guard, the per-cue `max_voices`, `starts_per_frame`, the `far` variant beyond half the
  class's scaled range, the `min_level_db` audibility check at the listener, the variant (never the
  same twice in a row), pitch (cue x jitter x size pitch) and a voice. Every start is logged (the
  last 512: frame, tick, cue, requested cue, bus, position, voice, priority, reason, file).
- **VoicePool** (`voice_pool.gd`): 24 `AudioStreamPlayer3D` (16 on the web), two spot voices (3D,
  `PLAYBACK_TYPE_STREAM`, Ambience bus) and eight `AudioStreamPlayer` (UI cues `PROCESS_MODE_ALWAYS`;
  other 2D cues pausable for their play). Every 3D voice: `ATTENUATION_LOGARITHMIC`, no distance
  filter, no doppler, no emission angle, no area reverb, `max_db` 3; `unit_size` and
  `max_distance` per play from the cue's class. Stealing: a request with every voice of its kind
  busy takes the lowest-priority, oldest voice of priority at most its own; otherwise it is dropped.
  At most 16 starts a frame (8 on the web).
- **CueRouter** (`cue_router.gd`): one handler per `Events` signal of the cue map (§4.1) and per
  footfall source, each queueing a request (the cue's candidates, most specific first; the
  position; the source's height; priority; reason; for stings the expected damage and poise
  ratio) that the autoload drains in `_process`. `ROUTED_SIGNALS` and `SILENT_SIGNALS` list every
  signal (P21's coverage test reads them). Conventions of §2.5: the record's voice
  (`audio_mix.voices`, default `human`, `none` silent), the prop's material (`audio_mix.materials`,
  default `wood`), the footfall's size class (`giant_s<stage>` for the player; `audio_mix.steps` or
  the archetype for the others), the surface (`Audio.surface_at`: the level's `surface_at`, else the
  region record's `surface`, else `audio_mix.surfaces`, else `soft`) and `wade` in water above the
  foot (`water_at`, when the level has it); the cruise rule (after 10 s of continuous walking the
  player's footfall uses `.cruise` at -5 dB, back to the full cue in combat, on stopping, jumping and
  stomping). Polled, not signalled, so no actor code changed: awareness barks (the unaware -> aware
  edge of enemies within 70 m x scale, every 0.25 s, one per encounter per 4 s), ranged aim locks (a
  projectile windup passing its tracking fraction: `sfx.threat.aim_lock`), water entries. Threat
  cues: `telegraph_started` aimed at the giant plays the windup at the attacker and
  `sfx.threat.sting` when the attacker is off camera (horizontal angle > fov x 0.55) or farther than
  20 m x scale, unless suppressed (expected damage under 3 % of the giant's maximum health, or the
  poise ratio at its 0.25 floor); a formation's brace plays one `sfx.threat.windup.wall` per
  formation per brace cycle (merged within 0.5 s). Screens: a `UiScreen` joining the tree plays
  `sfx.ui.open` (the map `sfx.ui.map_open`), its `closed` signal `sfx.ui.close`; focus moves,
  `ui_accept` and `ui_cancel` while a screen is open play move, select and back. The M7-M9 signals
  P20 declares (`poi_discovered`, `region_changed`, `level_leaving`, `level_entered`,
  `travel_requested`, `size_gate_blocked`, `trampled`, `formation_changed`, `siege_engine_*`,
  `boss_yielded`, `boss_disengaged`, `ability_evolved`, `talent_learned`, `ancestry_chosen`,
  `world_event_*`) are connected only when `Events.has_signal(name)`, with handlers that take their
  arguments as one array, so a refined signature cannot break the connection.
- **MusicDirector** (`music_director.gd`): a set plays as one deck (a `PLAYBACK_TYPE_STREAM` player
  per stem on the Music bus, `loop` set on the Ogg resource from the set, all started in one frame);
  bars and segments come from the lead stem's playback position. States: silent (start,
  `level_leaving`, after a death's fade, while a level registers), context (title, end,
  campaign end, sandbox), zone (the winning `audio_region`'s set with intensity layers, the melody
  scheduler and the silence window) and boss (`boss_engaged` for a boss with an `audio_region`: the
  intro stinger, then the boss set cross-faded in 0.5 s; phase layers at the next bar on
  `boss_phase_changed`; victory on `boss_defeated`, the yield stinger on `boss_yielded` and
  `boss_disengaged(fled)`, straight back on `boss_disengaged(left)`). A set change waits for the next
  bar and cross-fades over `crossfade_bars`; layer gains ease over `layer_fade_beats`; melody
  variants switch by volume at segment boundaries; stingers are one-shot streams that duck the stems
  for their length plus 0.5 s; entering combat starts on the next beat with the set's `combat_intro`
  stinger once per combat; death fades out over 1.5 s. Intensity every `poll_s` (decision 6:
  threats weighed against the giant's height; bosses, rival giants, formation members and engine
  crews always count), rising at once and falling after `combat_hold_s` / `danger_hold_s`.
- **MelodyScheduler** (`melody_scheduler.gd`, pure): played loops (`play_loops`) and rested loops
  (`rest_loops`); per segment a variant or a rest (weight 0.15), never a variant more than
  `max_repeat` times in a row, never the same phrase (variant, segment) within `min_phrase_gap_s`;
  after `after_explore_s` of quiet exploration the silence window (fade over `fade_s`, silence for
  `length_s`), re-entering at the next bar (drone first, the melody a loop later) on a discovery, a
  zone change or danger.
- **AmbienceDirector** (`ambience_director.gd`): beds as looping streams cross-faded over 3 s on a
  zone change; weighted spots every `spot_interval_s` at a random bearing and distance (x the
  distance scale) through the spot voices; flush reactions (a giant's footfall or stomp within
  `flush_radius_m` x scale silences the `flush` spots for `flush_s` and plays `flush_cue` once); the
  nearest two emitters within their radius; ducks (dialogue, boss, combat).
- **AudioMath** (`audio_math.gd`, static): the distance scale and class curves, the logarithmic
  level, size pitch, fallback chains, variant picking, the steal index, beat / bar / loop and
  boundary math, ducks, sting suppression, the off-camera test, the expected damage of an attack,
  threat weighting and the intensity target, and the cached manifest.
- **Focus and pause.** `NOTIFICATION_APPLICATION_FOCUS_OUT` / `NOTIFICATION_WM_WINDOW_FOCUS_OUT`
  with `mute_when_unfocused` ramp Master to -80 dB in 0.2 s (back on focus in); on the web a
  `visibilitychange` callback (`JavaScriptBridge.create_callback`) mutes Master while the page is
  hidden. SFX and ambience players pause with the tree; the music ducks (`duck.pause`) while a
  pausing screen is open.
- **Settings.** `volume_master` 1.0, `volume_music` 0.8, `volume_ambience` 0.8, `volume_sfx` 1.0,
  `volume_ui` 0.8 (0..1, on top of the mix's bus levels, 0 mutes) and `mute_when_unfocused` true,
  in `Settings.DEFAULTS` and `RANGES`. The settings page opens with an **Audio** section (Master
  volume, Music, Ambience, Effects and Interface sliders in 5 % steps showing "Off" or "N %", applied
  live through `Settings.changed`, written when the drag ends; "Mute when the window loses focus"),
  pad-navigable like every row, then a "Camera, controls and display" header over the existing rows.
- **Five record types** (schema modules registered in `DataValidator.SCHEMA_PATHS` after
  `"progression"`): `audio_bank`, `audio_mix`, `music_set`, `ambience_set`, `audio_region` (below).
- **Data**: `audio_mix.default` and `audio_bank.placeholder` (below).
- **The generation pipeline** (`tools/audio/`): `synth/core.py` (oscillators with polyBLEP, white,
  pink and brown noise, envelopes, RBJ biquads, a TPT state-variable filter with per-sample cutoff,
  FFT filtering and convolution, seamless-loop wrap and cross-fade loops, normalisation, the signal
  checks, 16-bit and float WAV writers, a WAV reader, FLAC export through ffmpeg; float64 and
  single-threaded, a seeded PCG64 per cue); `build_audio.py`; `encode.py` (libvorbis with the
  bit-exact flags; refuses the native `vorbis` encoder); `manifest.py`; `test_synth.py` (24 tests);
  `gen_placeholder.py`.
- **Placeholder sounds** (`assets/audio/placeholder/`, 111 KB): four test tones, a three-variant
  test set, a 1 s test stinger and an 8 s 120 BPM test bed (4 bars of 4/4, frame `test_120`), with
  `assets/audio/manifest.json` and `assets/audio/PROVENANCE.md`.
- **Test tiers and the check.** `tests/gate/` and the runner's `--gate`; the runner is silent by
  default; `tools/check.sh --audio` and `--gate`; the suite times printed as a `TIMES` line and kept
  in `.userdata/check/suite_times.txt`.

### The pipeline

```
python3 tools/audio/build_audio.py [--only=<family>] [--verify] [--budget] [--masters-out=<dir>] [--out=<dir>]
python3 tools/audio/test_synth.py
```

Each `tools/audio/gen_<family>.py` exposes `FAMILY` and `CUES`: a list of
`{"name", "path", "seed", "func", "params", "kind" ("sfx" | "loop" | "oneshot" | "spot"), ...}`
(loops add `bpm`, `beats`, `frame`; Oggs `quality`, `rate`, `channels`; optional `scope`
"m6" | "world"). `build_audio.py` imports every module present, renders and checks each master
(clipping, silence under -60 dBFS, DC, SFX over 2.0 s, a loop seam step), writes the WAVs, encodes
the Oggs and rewrites `assets/audio/manifest.json` (per file: family, generator, function, seed,
params hash, frames, rate, channels, kind, scope, seconds, loop data, master hash, file hash, size;
the Python, numpy, ffmpeg and libvorbis versions). `--verify` regenerates everything into a temporary
folder and compares every master and file hash, reporting a toolchain difference separately from a
content change. `--budget` prints the §4.5 arithmetic (the exact QOA formula for WAVs, the Ogg size
x 1.03) and the registered SFX seconds per level, and fails over decision 8.

## Data

- **`audio_bank`**: `cues: {cue id: {files (1-4), bus, spatial, class, volume_db, pitch,
  pitch_jitter, size_pitch, max_voices, retrigger_ms, priority, far, levels}}`. Cue ids match
  `^(sfx|amb|mus)(\.[a-z0-9_]+)+$`; `sfx.` cues are on SFX or UI, `amb.` on Ambience, `mus.` on Music;
  SFX and UI cues name WAVs of at most 2.0 s (by the manifest), Music and Ambience cues Oggs; every
  file exists; `class` is a mix class; `far` is a cue id; `levels` name level records or `sandbox`.
- **`audio_mix`** (`audio_mix.default`, required): `bus_db`, `pool` (24 / 16 web 3D voices, 2 spot,
  8 2D, 16 / 8 web starts a frame, `min_level_db` -45, `register_ms_per_frame` 12), `classes` (player,
  human_step, human, impact, wolf, big, giant, boss, threat, spot: `unit_m`, `max_m`, `scaled` true,
  false, "sqrt" or "own"), `duck` (stinger, dialogue, pause, boss, combat), `materials` (all 13 prop
  records), `voices` and `steps` (all 7 enemy and 2 NPC records), `surfaces` (the Valley's
  districts, the stress arena, the director test level and the sandbox), `intensity`, `sting`,
  `footfall` (`cruise_after_s` 10, `cruise_duck_db` -5, `stop_gap_s` 0.9) and `threat` (the router's
  thresholds: off-camera fov factor 0.55, far 20 m, windup near 15 m, alert radius 70 m, alert gap
  4 s, flee gap 2 s, wall merge 0.5 s, denied gap 0.5 s).
- **`music_set`**: `bpm`, `beats_per_bar`, `bars`, `frame`, `stems`, `layers` (per intensity, stem
  -> dB; the key `melody` is the scheduler's choice), `melody`, `silence`, `combat_intro`, `intro`,
  `phase_layers`, `crossfade_bars`, `layer_fade_beats`. Every stem is an Ogg whose manifest entry
  lasts `bars x beats_per_bar x 60 / bpm` s to the frame and records the set's `frame` (so a stem
  shared by two sets of different frames fails); layers name stems or `melody`; the melody's
  variants are stems.
- **`ambience_set`**: `beds`, `spots` (weight, flush), `spot_interval_s`, `spot_distance_m`,
  `emitters` (cue, level, position, radius), `reactions` (`flush_radius_m`, `flush_s`, `flush_cue`).
- **`audio_region`**: `level`, `districts`, `regions`, `pois`, `context` ("", title, end,
  campaign_end, sandbox), `boss`, `music`, `ambience`, `priority`, optional `layers` / `melody`
  overrides and `surface`.
- **`audio_bank.placeholder`** (`data/audio/bank_placeholder.json`): one default per family of the
  cue map (`sfx.<family>.default` for hit, block, parry, guard_break, clank, swing, dodge, jump,
  step, ability, impact, projectile, status, grab, voice, player, body, threat, crack, break, world,
  cavalry, troll, gate, trample, formation, siege and ui), `sfx.threat.sting` and
  `mus.stinger.default`, all on the placeholder tones, set and stinger. Every router row resolves to
  one of them (the gate soak reports no missing cue); P21 and P23 replace them and P23 deletes the
  bank and the folder.
- No `music_set`, `ambience_set` or `audio_region` record ships: P23 (the Valley's score, beds and
  spots, the contexts) and P28 (the world) add them.

## Decisions as built

1. **Presentation only** (decision 1): own RNGs, no `Sim.rng` or global RNG (the policy test scans
   `scripts/audio/`), no gameplay signal; the gate test proves the seeded soak byte-identical with
   and without audio (and, since the M6 gate, seeded Valley bot runs: a New Game and a Continue at
   the Barrow, compared but for the PerfProbe's timings and the engine's node samples). Audio never reads `finished` into game logic.
2. **Playback policies** (decision 2): SFX are WAVs imported as QOA and played with
   `PLAYBACK_TYPE_DEFAULT` (Web Audio samples on the web), registered behind the loading gate; Music
   and Ambience players, spot voices included, set `PLAYBACK_TYPE_STREAM`; no randomizer,
   polyphonic, playlist, interactive or synchronized streams anywhere in `scripts/` or `ui/`.
3. **Buses** made at run time, no effects (decision 3); `audio/driver/output_latency.web=200`.
4. **Masters** (decision 4): SFX WAVs are both master and import source; music and ambience ship
   as Ogg only, their masters regenerated on demand (`--masters-out`), their hashes kept.
5. **Music** (decision 5): layered stems with the melody scheduler, rests and silence windows.
6. **Threat-weighted intensity** (decision 6) and 7. **sting suppression** (decision 7) as above.
8. **Budgets** (decision 8): `--budget` and the asset test hold 4.0 MB (M6) / 9.5 MB (all) and
   110 s / 170 s of registered SFX for the Valley / the world (now 0.057 MB and 1.65 s).
34. **Test tiers**: the default suite runs silent; `tests/gate/` holds the determinism soak.

## Deviations

- **The expected damage of a sting** uses `DamageResolver.mitigate` on a crit-free `HitEvent` built
  like `HitFactory.build` (`AudioMath.expected_damage`): the plan's `DamageResolver.preview` does not
  exist. It reads no random number.
- **A test stinger** (`placeholder/stinger.ogg`, 1 s, seed 190131) beyond the plan's four tones,
  test set and bed, so the Music-bus stinger path (`mus.stinger.default`) has a file; the
  placeholder folder is 111 KB, not about 80 KB.
- **`sfx.threat.sting` has its own placeholder cue.** Falling back to `sfx.threat.default`, the
  windup's cue, it was dropped by the retrigger guard of the windup started in the same frame.
- **Extra mix blocks**: `steps` (record -> step class; the plan derives it from the archetype only),
  `threat` (the router's thresholds as data) and `footfall.stop_gap_s` (the gap that ends a walk for
  the cruise rule); `voices` accepts `none` (the sparring post is silent).
- **The shared-stem rule is checked per record through the manifest**: each stem's manifest entry
  records its `frame`, and every set naming the stem must have that frame, which forbids a stem
  shared across frames without a GameData post-pass.
- **Cross-record catalogue problems** (a cue id in two banks, a `far` cue or a set's cue that
  resolves to nothing) are reported by `Audio.catalog_problems()` (tested empty), not as GameData
  load errors.
- **The runner is silent by default** (`Audio.set_process_default(false)`, also without
  `--no-audio`); `--with-audio` keeps the engine on for every suite. `reset_for_tests()` returns to
  that default before every test.
- **Test seams**: `music.clock_positions` with `Audio.advance_for_tests()` (bars from the clock, so
  bar-timing tests need no real-time mixing), `registration_files_per_frame_cap`,
  `router.set_walk_started()`.
- **Desktop registration**: `register_stream_as_sample` is a no-op on desktop, but the slices still
  load the level's streams, so `is_ready()` turns true one or two paused frames after a level
  appears rather than at once (the timeline is unaffected: paused frames run no tick).
- **`check.sh --audio`** runs the pipeline's own tests, `--verify` and `--budget`; the asset and
  policy suites need no Python and run in every default suite rather than only under `--audio`.
- **Size**: about 4,570 lines of GDScript (engine 3,923, schemas 643), 1,562 of Python and 1,732 of
  Godot tests, over the plan's estimate of about 3,500 in all; the router alone implements every row
  of §4.1, the M7-M9 rows included.

## Known gaps and notes for the next packages

- **No music or ambience plays in the shipped game yet**: no sets or regions exist until P23 (the
  title, end and sandbox contexts included). Every router row plays a placeholder tone.
- **The web path is untested in a browser here**: sample registration, the `visibilitychange` mute,
  the 16-voice and 8-start limits and the 200 ms latency are exercised by P23's web audio check.
- **Exit**: Godot frees a stopped playback on the audio thread's next mix steps, so a process that
  quit while sounds played (the soak's `quit()`) printed "ObjectDB instances leaked at exit" and

  now stops its players in `_exit_tree` and waits, at most 250 ms, until the mixing thread has
  dropped their playbacks; the gate test checks the audio soak's output for both messages. (The
  default suite's own "ObjectDB instances leaked" line at exit predates P19: the base commit prints
  it too.)

## M8 gate

- **F11: a yielded boss who rises to fight is a boss fight again.** A yield decided as a fight (Harrok
  struck rises and fights to the death) now emits `boss_engaged` again (P34's `EnemyActor.p34_decide`),
  so the boss state comes back after the yield stinger. `CueRouter._on_boss_engaged` passes the phase
  the boss fights in now (`_boss_phase_now`: its actor's `boss_phase()`, or its battle's; 1 when
  unknown) and `MusicDirector.on_boss_engaged(id, phase := 1)` starts the set there (a resumed fight is
  in phase 2, not 1). The arena gate's cue plays only on the arena's own engagement tick
  (`BossArena.engaged_tick`), so a resumed fight does not slam a gate that is long shut. Test:
  `test_yield_protocol` (`test_struck_he_rises_and_the_bar_and_the_music_follow_the_fight`: the music's
  boss id, its pending boss state and phase 2 after Strike; 4 failures before the fix). See P34.md,
  "M8 gate".
