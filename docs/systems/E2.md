# E2 — The Cinder Titan and the burning road

Milestone E's second package, on `pkg/e2` from E1's merge (`a1f464f`). The version stays **0.17.0-m17** (prepared, not
shipped). The seventh signature titan of the expansion's story ("Stage 9: the Cinder Titan burns a road toward the
kingdom"): the rumour E1 left (`quest.cinder_lead`: a road burned black east of the Stonehold) now pays off. Decisions
E2-1..E2-12 are in `docs/DECISIONS.md` §2.7; the duels are BALANCE_LOG "E2"; what is left is FOLLOWUPS "From E2".

## 1. The story

The Cinder Titan was the old kings' forge-giant: he kept the fires the Worldbreaker's chains were forged in and was
walled into the eastern mountains with his furnace when the forging was done. The throne's fall and the chains'
breaking woke the fire in him; he has come down out of the east over the rim into **the Ashfell** and burns a road west,
toward the Breach and the kingdom. Behind him the heath is black and the herders' steadings are shells.

- **The lead** (`lead.cinder.heard`, E1's last word at the Moot) starts `quest.cinder`. **The Ash Road** runs east from
  the Hoarfell's Chain Road into the Ashfell; at the land's edge a warning post (a herder's crook, a scorched skull) and
  the **refugees' camp**: the folk of Thornstead and Ewesholm and **Ysolde**, Thornstead's reeve. She tells what walks at
  the road's head - and what the herders saw from the ridge: where he waded the beck, the water screamed off him in steam
  and the fire in him went grey.
- **The preparation** (optional; `quest.cinder_dam`, 250 XP): the Ash-pit, the herders' tarn at the hollow's west end,
  drained through the **Cut** when his heat came. Over the Cut leans **the Grey Spur**, a 46 m fang of rock no men could
  move. A stage-9 giant heaves it over (a smaller one cannot): it falls across the Cut, dams it, and the springs fill the
  pit again, 6.5 m deep (`world.ashfell.dammed`).
- **The fight** in **the Ember Hollow** (§2).
- **The aftermath**: his fall puts the fires out - the road's flames sink and its embers go dark (the char stays),
  Thornstead stops burning, his smoke over the Ashfell clears. Once the giant has gone beyond his line (or on any later
  load: `world.ashfell.cooled`, the territory's reopened flag) the camp is struck and the folk are **home at Thornstead**,
  living in tents among the burned shells, Ysolde with them. Told he has fallen, Ysolde closes the quest and tells her
  herder's tale: on the ridge north of the Breach a giant king in armour, still as stone, the sunrise through him and
  nothing inside - the last giant king who went against the Worldbreaker (`lead.hollow.heard`, `quest.hollow_lead`:
  a lead only, 0 XP; nothing of the Hollow Titan is built).

## 2. How the fight plays

**The arena** is the Ember Hollow, a shallow bowl of heath 160 m across (flattened at 180 m) in the Ashfell: three of the
**Ashbeck's pools** run north to south through its middle (about 3 m deep at their middles - a stage-9 giant's ankle);
the dry **Ash-pit** at its west end (filled with the preparation); the burned road comes in from the east. He stands at
the hollow's east side at the head of his road, leaning on his brand; a giant he sees wakes him (`TitanTerritory`,
`wake_on_sight`): he raises the brand and roars (3.4 s, no blow). The boss bar, the music, no checkpoint and no fast
travel while engaged.

**The mechanic: his fire** (decision E2-2).

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **The burning ground** | Where his brand strikes the ground and his embers fall the ground burns: patches 5-7 m round (their flames and char drawn), burning 30-40 s, at most 40 (the oldest go out); from his last phase they never go out - the hollow shrinks as the fight goes on. Nothing burns on water. |
| **Heat** (the HUD's heat meter, in the frost meter's place) | Standing in his fire heats a giant 16 a second; his blows add their own (the blast 38, the slam 14, an ember 16); in his later phases his furnace heats a giant near him (4-6 a second within 26-30 m); striking his molten crust bare-handed burns (3 a blow). **A bigger giant takes less**: the flames stand a few metres off the ground - stage 8 the rule, stage 9 0.7 x (and the Cinder set less again). Full: **ABLAZE** - he burns (his scorch every second) until it is down to 45. |
| **Water** | Any of the hollow's water cools a giant fast (42 a second 2 m deep); dry, it falls slowly a while after the last gain. |
| **His molten crust** | While he is hot every blow does **0.45** of its damage to him and his plates' stone. |
| **The quench** | Water 1.4 m deep or more at his feet **quenches** him: steam roars off him, he reels (2.5 s) and his crust goes grey and brittle for 20 s - blows land whole, his plates crack. In the dammed pit (5 m or more) the steam takes him off his feet: **down on a knee** for 5 s. Then the crust melts again (not again for 10 s); his second phase's roar melts it at once. |

**His blows** (each telegraphed; scaled against a smaller giant by size, exponent 2.6: x1.47 on stage 9, x2.40 on stage 8):

- **Phase 1**: the **brand-slam** (the brand raised two-handed over his head, 1.9 s; a 56 m line on the ground; the ground
  burns along it), the **brand-sweep** (a 220-degree cone; the circling answer), the **furnace-blast** (his left hand
  flung open: a 70-degree cone of fire 50 m long - little damage, much heat), the **ember-rain** (four red circles round the
  giant 2.3 s before slag falls on them, burning where it lands), the **stamp** (a circle at his feet; the hug answer).
- **Phase 2** (62 %): the **furnace-roar** (his crust melts, his heat reaches out round him, six bursts in a ring), the
  **fire-run** (eight strikes walking out along the ground toward the giant, each a circle 1.2 s before).
- **Phase 3** (28 %): the **ring of fire** (twelve bursts 22 m round him: a giant at his feet is fenced in with him), his
  ash-cloud's embers falling on the giant by themselves, the ground burning until he falls.
- **His plates**: the **horns** of his slag crown, the slag **pauldrons**, the **heart-coal** in his breast (broken, it
  unbalances him), the bracer on his brand arm, the **greaves** (broken, he goes down on a knee, as the Stormcaller did).
  Health 7,800, armour 75, poise 900; 41.0 m (134.5 ft).

**Being a giant**: the stage decides the fire (a stage-9 giant stands above more of the flames than a stage-8 one); the
Grey Spur moves only for a stage-9 giant; the beck is ankle-deep to the giant and knee-deep to him; Colossus Stride
carries a stage-9 giant over a burning line.

## 3. Him as an asset (decision E2-1)

No new modelled body (the people share had 0.4 MiB left and the pack 1.1 MB): **the Stormcaller's body, skeleton and
17 clips**, burned into a giant of his own at load (`CinderModel`):

- **His skin** (`cinder_skin.gdshader`): a charred basalt crust with ash in its hollows; the Stormcaller's pale lightning
  scars are the furnace showing through, a net of finer cracks glows in patches; `heat` 1 hot .. 0 quenched (the fire
  sinks to a dull red, the crust greys with the steam).
- **His slag** (`cinder_plate.gdshader`): the Stormcaller's slabs re-cast as black glassy slag in plates whose seams run
  with molten iron; cracks open hotter as a plate breaks.
- **His gear** (`CinderGear`, made at load from a seed: no file): a crown of two great slag horns and seven spikes, a ridge
  of slag spines down his back and three over each shoulder, and **the Cinder-brand** - an iron-shod haft the length of a
  tower with a burning head of fused slag - in place of the rod; the bell, the tined crown and the beard are not drawn.
- **His carriage** (`CinderHunch`, a SkeletonModifier3D): the clips' pose bent forward, shoulders drawn up, head thrust out.
- **His fire** (`CinderFx`): upright flame billboards (`cinder_flame.gdshader`, added light, noise tongues) and burnt-ground
  discs, each in one MultiMesh - every flame of the fight one draw call, every disc another; the brand's head burns, a
  light flickers at it; sparks, smoke and steam are short CPU particle bursts.
- **His fall** is the Stormcaller's death clip (down on a knee, then full length on the ground); the fire goes out of him
  over 9 s.

## 4. The world (decision E2-3)

A new land, `ashfell` (`tools/data/world/world.json`): 360 x 320 m at [500..860] x [-1400..-1080], the Hoarfell's ground,
opening onto the Hoarfell along its east edge (the Ash Road comes in there), its other edges its own rim. Features: the
hollow (flatten), the Ashbeck's three pools (basins), the Ash-pit (a flatten 6.5 m down); south of it, the saddle between its rim and the Old Kingdom's is held at 222 m
(`ashfell_south_saddle`: chunk row 0 keeps its heights in the fine step, to about 230 m, and the rim would have topped it
at that row's north edge, so the seams there did not meet). The new **Ash Road** from the
Chain Road. On it (`tools/data/world/hoarfell.json`): the arena (`world.ashfell.cinder_territory`), the pools' and the
pit's water (`storm_water`; the pit's only with `world.ashfell.dammed`), **the burned road** (`cinder_road`: a 42 m
ribbon of char and ash draped over the terrain from the eastern mountains through Ewesholm and Thornstead to him, burnt
ground round the villages and the hollow, 48 fires along it, the six burned houses' charred shells - out at his fall),
Thornstead's fire and the smoke columns, his **ash haze** (`storm_weather` style `ash`: the light dimmed warm brown-grey,
the haze closing in near him, clearing at his fall), the refugees' camp (two tents, Ysolde, five villagers, until his
fire is out), stragglers on the road who run for the camp when the fight comes, the Grey Spur (a seal, `prop.ash_spur`),
the warning post, and the aftermath (Ysolde, tents and villagers at Thornstead). POI `poi.ember_hollow` (80 XP).
