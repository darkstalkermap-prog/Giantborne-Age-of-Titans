# C2 — The Bastion Titan, the Broken Warden, at the toll fort's old keep

Milestone C's second package, on `pkg/c2` from C1's candidate. The version stays **0.16.0-m16**. The fourth signature
titan: a giant warden of the old kings' toll road who wears the walls of the toll-gate he kept, asleep in the breach of
his own keep above the King's Road, a short walk from the toll fort. Decisions C2-1..C2-8 are in `docs/DECISIONS.md`
§2.7; the duels are BALANCE_LOG "C2"; what is left is FOLLOWUPS "From C2".

## 1. How the fight plays

From the King's Road's bend past the toll fort a warning post (a split merlon and an old giant's skull) and Hode the
toll-keeper's fire; above, on the shelf, the keep's broken north curtain and, in its breach, a giant standing asleep
behind a wall. Walking into his yard (or striking him) wakes him with a roar.

He is slow and very hard to hurt from the front. His **wall-shield** (a 5.8 x 13.4 m section of the gatehouse on his
left arm, turned 30 degrees to his left) **guards** him: any blow from an attacker in its 150-degree arc, no higher than
17 m, lands on the shield whatever it touched - 92 % of it kept from him, stone chips flying, the clank of stone. Each
caught blow fills his **guard meter** (a heavy 2.2 x its poise, a light 0.35 x, an ability 1.2 x); full, **the guard
breaks**: he reels, the shield knocked wide (his `guardbreak` clip), staggered 3.2 s and open for 5. The meter drains
after 10 s without a blow. The ways in:

- **round him**: his back (the portcullis: weak x2.6) and his broken right side (the mend's iron: weak x2.4; broken,
  his old wound unbalances him, every blow's poise x1.5) are outside the arc; he turns at 70 degrees a second under
  the stone, and his **bar sweep** (220 degrees) answers a giant circling him;
- **his bar's blow** (the hammer): the gate-bar brought down over the shield onto the ground, a shock line 26 m
  forward; his guard is down through its slow recovery;
- **his wall charge** (60 m, telegraphed by a line): dodge it and he runs into his own walls and **reels** (4 s, his
  guard down);
- **the fort fights for the giant**: two **crumbling towers** stand inside the curtain either side of his breach;
  bring one down (a heavy, 260 stone) while he is within 18 m of it and it falls on him - a 3.6 s stagger, his guard
  open, every blow's poise x1.4 for 12 s;
- **his greaves**: break one and he goes down on a knee (5 s), his helm (weak x2.2) and pauldron in reach;
- **the shield itself**: 5,200 stone. Broken, it falls from his arm and lies where it fell (**disarm**): his second
  phase at once - he takes the bar in both hands (a 2.4 s rearm, super armour) and fights faster (strike pauses x0.8)
  with the two-handed smash (a 30 m shock line) and sweep (240 degrees). At 35 % health he throws the shield down
  himself, so every fight reaches the second phase.

A giant who hugs his legs is stomped. Phase 1's sequence: hammer, bash, sweep, hammer, charge, bash (the bash: the
shield shoved forward, 12.5 m, 100 degrees). Phase 2: smash, sweep, charge, smash, sweep.

## 4. His keep (`tools/data/world/aldmere.json`; decision C2-5)

The old kings' toll-keep on the shelf above the King's Road, 240 m north-east of the toll fort (which is untouched: its
garrison, toll gate, tower archers and the crown's quests are as they were): a flatten feature (`world.json`
`warden_keep`, y 24.6) and a road (`warden_way`) off the King's Road; the north curtain (14 m, a 20 m breach where he
stands), two ruined walls, the old arch gate he kept, rubble, the two crumbling towers, his territory (`TitanTerritory`,
80 m, wake on the yard or a blow, never on sight; his hunt ends 115 m from the yard, well short of the fort and the war
camp), the warning post at the road's bend (`tutorial.bastion_warning`), the keep's mason-forge (works
`warden_stone`, open once he has fallen) and, after his fall, the masons' crane and blocks. Chunks c6_4, c6_5, c7_4 and
c7_5 rebuilt (their nav layers, the index, the map and the skyline with its landmark).

## 5. Hode's quest (`quest.bastion`, `dialogue.toll_keeper_hode`)

Hode the toll-keeper keeps a fire on the King's Road below the keep (`encounter.aldmere_toll_keeper`). He tells who the
warden is and how he is beaten (outcome `told`: the quest's lead); after the fall he thanks the giant (150 XP, 2
ashlar, `world.bastion.road_opened`) and brings the masons up (`encounter.aldmere_warden_masons`: Hode and two masons in
the opened keep). The quest's stages (`hode`, `slay`, `road`) all complete by his fall: a giant that goes straight to
him loses nothing. The tutorial `tutorial.bastion_guard` explains the guard the first time it breaks.

## 6. The aftermath, his parts and the Bastion set (decision C2-6)

His fall sets `boss.bastion.defeated` (and his outcome); once the giant has gone on the keep is opened
(`world.warden_keep.opened`): the crane in his breach, the masons, the forge. The toll fort's settlement record learns
`warden_fallen` and `keep_opened` (`ExpansionState.SETTLEMENT_FLAGS`); his roster entry turns slain with his drops
(the Character menu's Titans tab: "The Bastion Titan, the Broken Warden of the toll road").

**Loot**: the Warden's Keystone (a trophy), 4 Warden's Ashlar, 4 Gate-iron, and each broken plate's own (the shield's
always). One fall with two of his stone plates broken, and Hode's thanks, pays for the set.

**The Bastion set** (heavy; `works: warden_stone`): Bastion Helm, Battlement Pauldrons, the Portcullis cuirass,
bracers, the Gate-chain Girdle (the war belt and iron tassets), greaves and the Keystone; defence 4 / 7 / 8 / 5 / 3 / 6
/ 2. Bonuses: 2 pieces poise +12; 4 pieces `block_stamina_mult` 0.6 (a new passive key: a block's stamina cost) and
armour +4; 7 pieces poise damage taken x0.75. Six pieces are modelled on the giant (`giant_garment.py`
`_rigid_bastion_*`: merlons, a portcullis plate, stone greaves). **The Gate-bar** (`weapon.gate_bar`, a club in the
weapon's slot): his bar's iron on oak, formation poise x1.4.

## 8. The duel bot (decision C2-8)

`TitanDuelBot` learned two rules and keeps the expert's: **the guard** - while his guard is up and the giant stands
before it, press heavies into it when the meter is nearly full (hysteresis on stamina), else back off out of the bash's
reach and edge round off his shield side; **the tower** - a crumbling tower within his rubble's reach is smashed.
`BotCombat.prefer_heavy`. The duels: BALANCE_LOG "C2" (stage 4 dies 3 of 3; stage 5 wins 2 of 3 in 4.3-5.0 min;
stage 6 wins 3 of 3 in 3.0-3.5 min).

## 9. What is weak

- **The bot hardly ever breaks his guard at stages 5 and 6.** It wins by going round him, his greaves and the towers;
  the guard breaks it gets are at stage 4. The guard's numbers are tuned for a player who stands and hammers the shield with
  heavies (the slow test breaks it with a few full-poise heavies) - unmeasured for a human. If players never go round, the fight may read as
  "hit the wall until it breaks"; if they always do, the guard is scenery.
- **The duels are on the yard test arena's flat floor**, not at the keep in the world (its slope, ruins and road). The
  world fight was filmed by the capture tour, not measured.
- **The shield's hurtbox is a box fixed in his idle pose** (it follows the kneel only): when he bashes or sweeps, the
  drawn shield moves and the box does not. The guard's catching is by the arc, so blocking is right; but a blow aimed at
  the drawn shield mid-bash can land on his body.
- **The fallen shield is a drawn copy**: it lies where it fell but nothing collides with it; a reload or a new attempt
  removes it.
- **The towers fall by a swap to a heap**, not a collapse; a tower felled in a failed attempt stays down (one fewer next
  time).
- **His face is the brute kit's at his size**; the "broken" side reads by its iron and painted seam, not by the body.
  His left arm is long and the shield big; in a tight camera at stage 5 the shield fills the frame.
- **No intro cutscene** and no theme of his own; his sounds are Stoneback's re-pitched.
- **Stage 5 lost one of three** (seed 2) with him at 6 %: close, but a loss; stage 6's wins are quick (3-3.5 min).
- **The Web world check failed two runs of three** on one music hole each that the main thread did not explain
  (C1's tree passed twice the same afternoon); the walk never comes near him. Recorded as found (`docs/PERFORMANCE.md`
  "C2"), not explained.
- **The filmed duel is not the headless one**: rendered at 30 frames a second the stage-5 seed-3 duel is won at 299 s
  (headless: 260 s) - the physics steps two ticks a frame there; the film is evidence of how it looks, the table of
  how it plays.
- **Human playtest not run.**
