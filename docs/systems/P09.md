## What was built

- **Two ranged roles** (M4's five roles: soldier, wolf, **archer**, **mage**, ogre/boss).
  - **Archer** (`enemy.archer`, `ArcherEnemy` + `ArcherBrain`): keeps 12–20 m from the giant (15.6–26
    m and a 10 m spread once outmatched), seeks authored elevation, then cover markers that see the
    giant, retreats when the giant closes in and kicks at its shins under ~2.5 m. It shoots only
    when settled at its firing point, in the band, with line of sight, holding one of the giant's
    two ranged tokens and a launch slot. Counterplay: pressure it (it retreats and cannot shoot
    while moving) or throw something at it (poise 30: heavies, throws, Boulder Hurl and Runic Bolt
    stagger it).
  - **Mage** (`enemy.mage`, `MageEnemy` + `MageBrain`): keeps 10–16 m (kites at the far edge of
    13–20.8 m once outmatched); a slowing arcane bolt (30 % for 3 s) behind a 1.0 s line telegraph;
    a barrier (×0.5 damage taken for 5 s) on the most hurt ally within 12 m or itself, behind a
    1.2 s circle telegraph; a staff strike up close. Its casts are kind `ability` (CASTING) paid
    from the essence in its record (100, +6/s). Poise 25: heavies, Footfall, throws, Boulder Hurl
    and Runic Bolt interrupt its casts with no hit and no refund. Counterplay: interrupt or evade.
- **Committed aim** (decision 24): at `tracking_fraction × windup` (60 % for the shot and the slow
  bolt) a ranged actor stores its target's anchor in `runner.aim_point` and stops turning; the
  projectile flies to the stored point (P03's aim `target`; the arrow's arc passes through it). A
  sidestep after the lock dodges the shot.
- **The readability layer** (decision 25):
  - `TelegraphVisuals` (`scripts/presentation/telegraph_visuals.gd`, added by `main.gd` beside
    HitFeedback): a decal for every `telegraph_started` — a disc (circle), a strip (line) or a fan
    (cone) at the actor's feet — that fills from 0 to 100 % over the windup and flashes while the
    strike is active. Line telegraphs of projectile actions are **aim lines**: a beam from the bow
    or staff to `current_aim_point()`, following the giant until the aim lock, then frozen. A
    decal goes when its actor leaves the WINDUP/ACTIVE phase of that action instance.
  - The threat-arrow HUD widget (`ui/hud/threat_arrows.gd`): for a live telegraph aimed at the
    player from behind the camera or outside the view, an arrow 24 px inside the screen edge points
    at the attacker and brightens as the windup completes.
  - The ranged token pool (at most two ranged attackers per target, separate from the two melee
    tokens) and staggered launch slots (at least 1.0 s between launches at one target).
  - The GameData post-pass now makes an untelegraphed enemy attack with a windup of 0.8 s or more
    a data **error** (the switch P01 left at the P09 anchor).
- **Status tints for every rig** (FOLLOWUPS, M2 gate): `QuadrupedRig` gains `set_status_tint()` /
  `status_tint()`, and HitFeedback tints whichever rig an actor has (`HitFeedback.tint_target`), so
  a slowed wolf turns blue; the barrier has its own tint.
- **Scenes** (builder `tools/builders/ranged.gd`): `scenes/enemies/{archer,mage}.tscn` and the
  playground `scenes/test/ranged_test.tscn` (a platform with a railed ramp and an elevation
  marker, two low cover walls with cover markers, a sight wall, a navmesh baked offline, the giant,
  two archers, a mage and a soldier for its barrier). Run it with
  `--level=res://scenes/test/ranged_test.tscn`.

- **Firing points** (`brain.firing_point_resolver`): the nearest free elevation marker, else cover
  marker, within `tactics.cover_search_radius_m` of the unit whose flat distance to the target lies
  in the band, that is at least the spread from every other ranged ally and the point it holds,
  and from which the shot's launch height sees the target's anchor (`SIGHT_MASK`). The marker is
  claimed (meta `firing_point_holder`) until the unit gives it up, dies or leaves the tree. Without
  a marker: a ring point around the target in the unit's own direction, at its own distance
  clamped 1 m inside the band (the band's far edge when kiting), turned in 20° steps (±80°) until
  it is reachable (the navmesh's closest point with a path to it, the flee rules of P04; a ray
  without a navmesh), keeps the spread and sees the target. Markers may carry `faction` (P10a's
  `LevelMarker`); plain `Marker3D`s in the groups work too.
- **Ranged tokens and slots.** `_request_ranged_token()` takes `Sim.request_token(target +
  ":ranged", self, 2)` and remembers the key; `_request_launch_slot(action_id)` calls
  `Sim.request_launch_slot(target, self, launch_gap_ticks(action_id))`.
- **`apply_ally_cast(a)`**: the action's `on_hit_statuses` land on `brain.ally_target_id` when that
  ally is alive, of this team and within 1.5 × `tactics.barrier_range_m`, otherwise on the caster;
  `ability_effect` "self_buff" shows it at the receiver.

### EnemyBrain (below `## @anchor P09`)

```gdscript

func ranged_band(outmatched := false) -> Vector2      # tactics preferred_min_m..max_m (x1.3 outmatched)
func ranged_spread_m(outmatched := false) -> float   # tactics.spread_m (at least 10 outmatched)
func ranged_attacks() / close_attacks() / ally_casts() -> Array[String]   # projectile / other hit / targets_ally
func is_ranged_state() -> bool                        # APPROACH, ATTACK, RECOVER, REPOSITION, HOLD
func is_settled(my_position) -> bool                  # within FIRING_SETTLE_M of the firing point
static func ring_point(my_pos, target_pos, distance, band, far) -> Vector3   # the pure fallback
func _decide_ranged(..., outmatched: bool, far: bool) # the shared engaged rules (below)
func _ranged_special_attack(...) -> String            # hook (MageBrain: the barrier)
func _ranged_shot() -> String                         # hook (default: the first ranged attack)
func _ranged_path_failed(my_position) -> bool         # a path failure re-picks the firing point
```

**`_decide_ranged`** (ArcherBrain and MageBrain run it from `_decide_engaged` and from their
outmatched hooks `_decide_spread` / `_decide_kite`):
- target dead → PATROL; memory lost → ALERT (both release everything and drop the point);
- ATTACK: stand, facing the target, until the attack is over (`_attack_finished()`, i.e. after the
  recovery); then a strike pause (`ai.strike_pause_*`) in RECOVER, giving back the melee token
  and the ranged token;
- RECOVER: keep the band until the pause is over, then APPROACH;
- APPROACH, in this order: a close attack when the target's capsule is within its range band
  (`ai_min_range_m`..`ai_max_range_m`), the target is within 35° of the facing and a melee token
  is granted; the archetype's special (the barrier when ready); keep the band (hold or pick a
  firing point and go there: run when farther than 4 m or when the target is closer than the
  band); and shoot when the target is seen, the unit is settled, the distance is within the band
  (±0.75 m), the target within 45° of the facing, the shot is ready, a ranged token is granted
  and a launch slot is granted (a refused slot gives the token straight back).
- Any state but ATTACK releases the ranged token (ArcherBrain/MageBrain `decide()`); FLEE, ROUTED,
  DEAD, PATROL and IDLE drop the firing point. States other packages own (RETURN, STAND_DOWN) go to
  `EnemyBrain._decide_engaged`.

## Decisions and deviations

1. **Doc name.** `docs/systems/P09.md` (the task and §1.1 item 5); §3.9 names
   `P09-ranged-enemies.md`.
2. **Launch slots are padded by the windup difference.** A slot is taken when the draw starts and
   the launch follows one windup later, so with the plan's `launch_gap_s × 60` an archer (0.9 s)
   taking a slot 1.0 s after a mage (1.0 s) would launch only 0.9 s after it. Each request asks for
   `launch_gap_s × 60 + (longest ranged windup − own windup)` ticks: archers 66, mages 60. Launches
   at one target are then always ≥ 1.0 s apart (tested with two archers and a mage); two archers'
   launches are 1.1 s apart.
3. **Shots wait until the unit is settled** at its firing point (within 1.5 m). Without it an
   archer drew its bow on the way and rarely reached the elevation or cover marker the plan
   wants it on. Pressure works through it too: a retreating archer cannot shoot.
4. **Firing points.** Elevation markers come before cover markers (then the nearest). Line of
   sight is checked from the shot's launch height (1.45 m for the bow), not the eye, so a unit
   behind cover can shoot over it; cover walls must be lower than that (the playground's are
   1.1 m).
5. **The barrier is a `targets_ally` cast whose `on_hit_statuses` land on the ally** when it enters
   ACTIVE (no hit). `self_statuses` would always reach the caster (AttackRunner, P03), and the
   plan's single new field is `targets_ally`. The pick includes the mage itself (the most hurt of
   them; ties go to the mage). It is cast whenever it is ready (cooldown, essence) while the mage
   is engaged; the plan does not say when. The circle telegraph is drawn at the mage.
6. **Numbers the plan leaves open:** the arrow's arc (`arc_to_target`, so it passes through the
   stored point despite gravity 4), radius 0.15 and launch offset; the slow bolt's 12 essence and
   no cooldown (launch slots and the strike pause pace it); the mage's sight 28 m and essence regen
   delay 1 s; `stagger_vs_larger_s` 1.5 for both (a grab after a heavy lands as on soldiers);
   `outmatched_ratio` 2.5 for both (a stage-2 giant outmatches them, stage 1 does not).
7. **The kick is a short sector pulse** (100°, 2.4 m, at leg height). The ActorRig has no leg
   pose, so the rig sweeps the bow arm. Its band (0–1.5 m from the giant's capsule) is "under
   2.5 m" from a stage-1 giant's centre (2.35 m) and grows with the giant; the 2.4 m radius is sized
   to reach the leg hurtboxes from that band at stages 1-3 (computed from the scaled hurtboxes;
   the integration test covers stage 1).
8. **Decals follow their actor through the windup** (its feet and facing; the soldier and wolf
   turn during the first half of theirs) and stay where the strike began; aim lines start at the
   launch point. A decal's fill is read from the controller (elapsed / windup ticks), so it is
   exact per tick and identical in tests.
9. **Threat arrows** skip `targets_ally` casts (a barrier threatens nobody even though its event
   names the brain's target). Their direction is the attacker's position in camera space
   (x, −y), so something straight behind points down; the tip sits 24 px inside the edge.
10. **The barrier tint** ranks third in `STATUS_TINTS`, after Titan Surge and Stone Skin and before
    slow and mend, so a warded enemy reads as warded.
11. **The playground's navmesh is baked by the builder** from the same boxes as the collision
    (procedural faces, never GPU meshes) and saved in the scene; its ramp has rails, because the
    navmesh otherwise stepped onto the ramp's low sides, a ledge a capsule cannot climb.
12. **The playground climb is a slow suite** (`tests/slow/test_ranged_level.gd`, ~0.9 s): it is not
    one of §3.9's tests, and the default suite is near its 45 s budget.
13. **Existing tests.** None was edited. `test_schemas_v2.test_untelegraphed_enemy_attack_warns`
    (P01) already accepts the finding as a warning or an error; the new error is pinned in
    `test_ranged_brains`. Counts grew where suites iterate what now exists: `test_anchors` 278 →
    290 (12 new scripts compile), `test_schemas_v2` 322 → 327 (new records validate),
    `test_scene_kit` 87 → 88 (the `ranged` builder file exists).

## Cross-lane notes

- **P10a (same wave).** P09 uses no P10a symbol. Elevation and cover markers are read through the
  groups `ai_elevation_points` / `ai_cover_points` (plain `Marker3D`s in tests and the playground;
  `LevelMarker`s with an optional `faction` in levels). Flee and ring points reuse P04's
  `nav_region_for_layers()`, which asks a `level_root` node defensively. A leash return (P10a)
  pauses the AI, so the ranged brains never decide in RETURN; they hand any state they do not own
  to `EnemyBrain._decide_engaged`. Hunks sit below `## @anchor P09` in `enemy_actor.gd`,
  `enemy_brain.gd` and `game_data.gd`, apart from P10a's.
- **P10b / P15 (the Valley).** Spawn `res://scenes/enemies/{archer,mage}.tscn` (records
  `enemy.archer`, `enemy.mage`). Author `ai_elevation_points` on wall walks and towers and
  `ai_cover_points` behind cover lower than 1.45 m, inside 12–20 m (archers) / 10–16 m (mages) of
  where the giant fights, within 25 m of the spawn. The first mage of the critical path (road
  patrol) needs no other ranged unit (§4.4).
- **P11a / P11b.** The `kite`, `spread` hooks are filled; `retreat` is still unused. Archers and mages
  have no awe fields yet (P11b adds `awe_ratio` / `awe_morale_loss`). A `targets_ally` cast and
  `apply_ally_cast` are available to any enemy (connect `phase_entered` as `MageEnemy` does).
- **P12.** `xp_reward` 25 / 30 is already in the records (§4.7); `loot_table` is P12's.
- **P13.** `telegraph_started` carries the actor id; `actor_archetype` filters can read the actor's
  record. Barrier casts emit it too (their `target_id` is the brain's target).
- **P14.** `TelegraphVisuals` keeps two mesh instances per live telegraph (pooled); the stress
  route's two archers and mage exercise it.
- **P16.** Threat-arrow colour and size are constants in the widget; the decals' colours are
  constants in `TelegraphVisuals`.
- **P17a (bot).** `current_aim_point()` / `aim_locked()` say where a shot goes; the novice can dodge
  after `aim_locked()`, the expert reads the telegraph from the start. `TelegraphVisuals.is_live`,
  `progress` and `origin_of` read a `telegraph_started` record without the node.

## Known gaps

- No archer or mage in the sandbox (`sandbox.gd` is not P09's this wave); `ranged_test.tscn` is
  the playground.
- The archer's kick has no leg animation (the bow arm sweeps), and the draw pose is a simple
  arm aim.
- A held (P07) or leash-returning (P10a) ranged unit keeps its claimed marker until its brain
  decides again; nobody else can take that marker meanwhile.
- Decals lie flat at the actor's feet height (no projection onto slopes or steps).
- The barrier's circle is drawn at the mage, not at the ally it will ward.
- Threat arrows follow only telegraphs aimed at the player; an arrow's colour does not tell the
  attack apart.
