# D3 — Stage 9, the Elder Colossus; the growth track's requirements made real

Milestone D's third package, on `pkg/d3` from D2's merge. The version stays **0.16.0-m16** (development toward 0.17).
Stage 9 (116 ft, 35.36 m, level 17) on the growth track, the requirements of stages 8 and 9 asked of the titans the
story names, where and how the giant grows, what only a stage-9 giant can do in the lands that exist, the titans
re-fought at stage 9, and four fixes from D2's review. Decisions D3-1..D3-12 are in `docs/DECISIONS.md` §2.7; the
numbers are BALANCE_LOG "D3"; what is left is FOLLOWUPS "From D3".

## 3. Where he grows (decisions D3-3, D3-5, D3-8)

The **Rimstone** grows every colossus (max stage 9); no stone of its own for stage 8 or 9 (left with stage 10's). Its
shrine moved 8 m west (C1's spot left a stage-9 capsule touching the ring's lesser stone to the east). A shrine's pad and
spawn point stand 6.5 m before its stone - narrower than his 6.92 m capsule - so `PlayerGrowth.shrine_spot` steps a
stage-9 giant straight out from the stone, for the growth's pad and for a respawn at any shrine (smaller giants stand
where they did). A stage-9 giant fits at every checkpoint of the world (`test_d3_world`: 28 of 28).

**The growth as an event**: `GrowthMoment` swells the giant from his old size to the new over 3.4 s at stage 9 (1.6 s at
stage 2) with a little give at the end, the camera's pivot rides the body as it rises (the world drops away beneath),
the camera trembles as he swells and shakes as he settles, dust rises in a ring round his feet; a colossus's growth
sounds lower (`sfx.world.growth.colossus`) over the ground's quake (`sfx.world.growth.quake`) - both re-pitched files.
The growth itself is still one simulation tick (presentation only); the debug keys and a save's restore play none.

## 4. What only stage 9 does (decision D3-4)

- **Steps over what was a wall**: Colossus Stride clears 17.7 m - one of Fort Aldmere's 16 m corner towers or gate
  bastions - where a stage-8 stride is refused "too high" (14.6 m).
- **Scatters what fought him**: awe tiers that `break` - a troll (6.2 x), an ogre or a troll whelp (10 x), a rimkin
  giant or warleader (2.1 x), a dire wolf (19 x), a wolf (38 x), Corvane's iron guard (18 x) flee at the sight of a
  stage-9 giant even when they never flee, and rout when the flight ends; before a stage-8 giant they fight as before.
  Humans already break before a colossus (C1's 13 x).
- **Reaches what was out of reach**: the Fallen King's crown-iron on the top of his standing left shin, 35 m up (a ledge
  pickup, `requires_stage` 9: "...out of every lesser giant's reach. Grow to stage 9."): four titan iron, the Anvil's
  greatforging.
- **Outgrows the titans**: the Stormcaller is won in his real pools in under 2.2 min (never under 59 % health), the
  Frostbound in 2.3-4.6 min (never under a third) (§5).

## 6. Fixes folded in

- **The Stormcaller in his real pools** (§5): measured over three seeds a stage, the real arena already holds the
  intended curve; no tuning.
- **The gate tier's time** (decision D3-10): the determinism suites' compared runs and the Barrow's two seeds run side by
  side (`ChildRuns`, `tests/support/child_runs.gd`): the same child runs, arguments and checks. Measured in BALANCE_LOG /
  §8; no suite moved, skipped or weakened.
- **The Stormstair's pools** are drawn with the storm's water (ripples, the rain's rings fading after his fall): StormWater
  draws its pools in the world too, 4 cm over the chunk's water and 1.3 x the basin.
- **The Rimeglass tarn's ice** has more bare blue ice, darker where clearest, drifts laid along the wind and shaded as
  low mounds, and sastrugi (the shader only).
