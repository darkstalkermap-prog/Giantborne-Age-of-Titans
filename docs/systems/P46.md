## What was built

### 1. The hair's second pass (`tools/giant/kit/giant_hair.py`, `kit/pack.py`, `assets/shaders/giant_hair.gdshader`)

The W25 hair bar found the long hair and the full beard no better than the research's best variant: shingle
steps on the outline, scalp between the crop's cards. Before and after frames were drawn at the creator's Face
and Hair framings (and a side view), in the creator's light and in the world's, in chestnut and in blond with
half grey, and each step was kept only when its frames were better than the step before. The steps, in the
order they were kept:

| Step | Cause it removes | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The cards' sides feather, the darker root is slight and short, the tip's lighter colour starts later and is weaker | Every card showed its outline: a dark root over the lighter tips of the cards under it, a shingle a card | the hair shader (`side_feather`, `root_dark`) |
| The atlas's strands end anywhere in a card's lower two thirds and gather towards its middle | A card ended in one edge; now its tip thins out strand by strand | `pack.make_atlas` |
| Narrower cards, more of them, fewer points along the short ones | A card wider than it is long reads as a leaf (the crop: 400 cards 0.04 wide and 0.05 long became 1,100 cards 0.026 wide and 0.065 long) | `giant_hair.STYLES` |
| Empty atlas texels take the nearest strand's brightness and id | The filtered and mipmapped id blended towards zero at a strand's edge (grey fringes) | `pack.make_atlas` |
| The cards take 30 % of each other's shadow (`shadow_soft` 0.7) | The cards shadowed each other in patches, a dark shingle each: the largest single gain on the long hair from behind | the hair shader's `light()` |
| A card's shading normal leans half-way from the skin's normal to the direction from the head's centre, and keeps it on both faces | Neighbouring cards over the jaw, the ears and the temples were lit apart (grey leaves on the shadow side of the beard): the largest gain on the beards | `giant_hair.ribbons` (`NORMAL_ROUND`), the shader's `FRONT_FACING` |
| The grey share blends in where the atlas is minified | A minified card switched whole between its colour and grey: confetti on the short styles in fair colours | the hair shader |
| The scalp and the skin under the beard take 0.8 of the hair's colour (0.55 before); the short beard's `beard_tint` 0.85 (0.5) | The skin between short cards was darker than the cards on fair hair and redder on dark | `giant_skin.gdshader`, `set_beard.json` |
| The skin's baked relief is eased (`normal_detail` 0.6) | The fine horizontal ridges on the forehead under the world's low sun (P44's follow-up) | `giant_skin.gdshader` |

Tried and not kept: a per-strand cut in the shader by the strand's id (pixel noise where the id is filtered: the
atlas's own strand lengths do it better); a lower hair specular and a higher roughness (no change: the shader's
own `light()` draws the highlight).

The frames are the review's evidence (not in the repository): `before_long_full.png`, the steps `v1` to `v11`,
the styles `s1` and `s2` and the lengths `l1`, in `<scratchpad>/m10/w27/P46/hair/`. Against the approved
close-up: the full beard reads as one mass with strand tips and no shingle; the long hair is smooth from behind
with a strand fringe; the hairline is the cards' own (the approved render's flyaway strands are not there);
the crop and the short beard cover the skin in every colour, the short beard's edge still shows single cards at
the Face framing.

**The cut** (`length`): the hair shader draws the first `length` of every card's root-to-tip coordinate and thins
the cut end like a tip, so a style can ship a shorter cut of its mesh at no pack cost. A hair or beard option may
carry `length` (0.3-1, absent = 1); `Appearance.to_look` passes it as `hair_length` and `beard_length`
(`GiantLook.DEFAULT_LOOK`: 1.0 each) and `GiantLook.apply` sets the uniform. Two cuts ship because the option
check shows them apart: `hair.shoulder` (the long hair at 0.6: 0.0561 of the Hair tile against the long hair)
and `beard.trimmed` (the full beard at 0.55: 0.0283 of the Face tile against the full beard).

**The sway** (`head_motion`): the hair shader moves a card's vertices against `head_motion` by the square of the
root-to-tip coordinate, so the roots stay on the head and the tips lag. `GiantLook.sway(root, index, skeleton)`
reads the head bone's place each drawn frame, eases its motion (`sway_step`: the motion times `SWAY_LAG_S`
0.035 s, eased at `SWAY_EASE` 10 a second) and writes the uniform on the shown hair and beard in the mesh's own
space, at most `SWAY_MAX_M` 0.09 m at the tips at stage 1 (the growth scale divides out). `SkinnedRig` calls it
in `_on_skeleton_updated`, so it runs only where the skinned layers run (never headless) and writes only a
shader uniform under `Visual/Body`.

**Braids are cards, not an atlas.** The plan's braided-strand atlas (about 0.2 MiB shipped) is replaced by
geometry: a braid is a path hung from the head and kept off the body, with a herringbone of short lobes crossing
from each edge down to the middle on two planes at right angles, over a core card (`braid_guides`; the atlas's
clumped column). A braid costs about 300-400 triangles and nothing in the pack but its vertices. The floor's
styles after the second pass: `Hair_long_back` 5,920 triangles (430 cards), `Hair_crop` 4,400 (1,100),
`Beard_full` 3,960 (330), `Beard_short` 2,240 (560); `test_giant_assets`' caps (6,000 a hair, 4,000 a beard)
hold for all nine.

- **Braids from cards instead of a braided-strand atlas** (above): the same read at the creator's framings,
  about 0.2 MiB less in the pack.
- **`length` needed two files outside the package's list**: `scripts/core/schemas/appearance_set_schema.gd`

  the plan says SkinnedRig writes `head_motion` when the uniform exists, and P43 left it as a follow-up. The

  scars' note no longer says they are the floor's weakest from behind.
- **Three lengths were not made**: one cut a mesh (the long hair, the full beard); the other styles' cuts were
  not looked at.
- **No bake input changed** (the body, its UVs, the skin code, the bake settings), so `--giant` (the quick
  verify) is the pipeline's check and `--giant-full` was not needed.
- **The owner's answers** (§0.6): no World style (item 6 not built), the low-poly weapons kept (no material
  pass).

## After the W27 merge

**The sway out of the tree** (the merge's fix-forward). The gate tier, which this package's check did not run
(its gate cap is 0), failed in `test_giant_determinism`: the 120 s sandbox soak with `--skin` counted three engine
errors where the run without the skin has none. The sandbox's reset re-parents the player, the skeleton updates
once while the giant is out of the tree, and `GiantLook.sway` read two world transforms there. `sway` now returns
at once while the model or its skeleton is out of the tree and forgets its last frame, so the next frame in the
tree starts afresh instead of reading the reset as a jump of the head.
`tests/unit/test_giant_look.gd::test_the_sway_waits_while_the_giant_is_out_of_the_tree` fails without the fix (the
runner counts the engine's errors); the three soaks of the gate's test are byte-identical again with 0 errors.
