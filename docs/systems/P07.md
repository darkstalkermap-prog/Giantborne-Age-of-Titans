## What was built

- **Grab and hold** (spec §8, decision 16). `PlayerGrab`
  (`scripts/actors/player/player_grab.gd`, the `grab` entry of `GiantPlayer.COMPONENTS`):
  - E on a staggered target in reach requests `ability.grab_hold`. It runs in EXECUTING with a
    0.25 s windup; the windup lunges at most `grab_lunge_m` and stops 0.8 × `grab_reach_m` from the
    target. The hand closes on the first active tick (`enter_grabbed`, `holding_id`,
    `grab_started`).
  - Every refusal is a controller rejection with a reason code: nothing is spent, and the HUD
    shows why.
  - While holding:
    - the body sits in the off (left) hand every tick, low and to the left of the view in first
      person;
    - the giant walks at 0.6 ×;
    - light or heavy throws the body, E puts it down, and Crushing Grip crushes it;
    - every other ability waits for an empty hand;
    - the hold ends by itself after 5 s.
  - It lets go when the holder is staggered, grabbed or killed, the target dies, the scene
    unloads, a growth is refused because of the hold, or a pending checkpoint waits for nothing
    but the hold.
- **Throw** (`ability.throw_held`). A projectile action (visual `thrown_body`, category
  `projectile`, aimed like Boulder Hurl) launched from the held body's centre. The projectile
  system carries the body along the arc. The throw's hits on whatever it meets are ordinary
  HitFactory hits, and the body itself takes a landing hit where it comes down. It is released
  at a validated spot.
- **Crushing Grip** (`ability.crushing_grip`, slot 4). It crushes the held body, or grabs and
  crushes in one move: P40 × 1.5, tag `Grab`, `grab.max_mass: small`. Then it lets go.
- **Boulder Hurl** (`ability.boulder_hurl`, slot 5). The §2.8.4 data as written: stage 2, level 3,
  category `projectile`, a 28 m/s arc, a 1.5 m splash. Its precheck needs `throw_boulder` in the
  stage's `interaction_permissions`. During its windup the landing ring (`ability_effect`
  "aim_preview") shows where it will come down.
- **EnemyActor grab hooks** (below `## @anchor P07`): `can_be_grabbed`, `on_grabbed` (out of the
  simulation: no AI, no movement, no collision, no hurtboxes), `carry` (the holder places it) and
  `on_released` (a validated spot, back into the simulation, STAGGERED 0.6 s).
- **Thrown bodies on the projectile system** (below `## @anchor P07`): `carry_body`, the body
  riding the arc, the landing release and the landing hit.
- **Rig poses** (below `## @anchor P07` in `actor_rig.gd`): reach, hold (per attachment profile;
  both hands for a two-handed hold in third person), crush and throw. The hand meets the socket
  where the body is placed.
- **Data and scenes:**
  - the action schema's `grab` block and the enemy schema's `grab_attach`;
  - soldiers are `torso_fist` and wolves `scruff`, each with an `Anchors/Grab` marker (chest,
    scruff);
  - a giant's poise break staggers a soldier 1.5 s (`stagger_vs_larger_s`);
  - slots 4 and 5 of the sandbox giant;
  - both ids in the ability library;
  - `growth.02` unlocks Boulder Hurl.
- **P05's two follow-ups.** `ability.boulder_hurl` is in `growth.02`'s `unlocks`, and the
  first-person-while-holding test uses `tests/support/crosshair_probe.gd`.

**Eligibility.** The checks run in this order, each with its reason:

| Reason | When |
| --- | --- |
| `nothing_in_reach` | no grabbable actor within `grab_reach_m` + `grab_lunge_m` (horizontal, centre to centre) |
| `no_los` | the eye cannot see the target's `Anchors/Grab` (WORLD and BREAKABLE) |
| `too_tall` | the target is taller than half the giant |
| `too_heavy` | its mass class is above the stage's `grab_capacity` |
| `too_heavy_to_crush` | a crush only: its mass class is above the action's `grab.max_mass` |
| `immune` | `Grab` is in its immunities |
| `no_attach_profile` | its `grab_attach` is `none` or not in the stage's `grab_attach` |
| `not_grabbable` | `can_be_grabbed()` is false (dead, held, flying, dormant) |
| `not_staggered` | a living target's poise is not broken: it is not STAGGERED, or only in the 0.6 s recovery a release leaves (`has_broken_poise`; M3 gate) |

The same check is the precheck of every grab request. It runs again when the hand closes, with
the distance limited to `grab_reach_m`; a target that slipped away there is a miss, with a HUD
message.

**Requests.**
- E (`_on_interact`):
  - holding: put the body down;
  - a candidate: `try_grab()`;
  - nothing in reach: the edge goes to PlayerInteract (shrines, NPCs);
  - something in reach that cannot be grabbed, and no interactable either: `try_grab()` anyway,
    so the controller rejects it with the reason (nothing spent, HUD text).
- Light or heavy while holding (or during the grab): `try_throw()`.
- A slot whose action is a crush (Crushing Grip) or Boulder Hurl, and every slot or the ultimate
  while holding: `try_slot()`, with `ability_reason()` as the precheck.
  - holding: everything but a crush is refused with `holding`;
  - a crush on the held body checks its `max_mass`;
  - a crush with an empty hand needs a candidate;
  - Boulder Hurl needs `throw_boulder`.
- A grab or throw that the controller can only buffer (9 ticks: a heavy's recovery is 33) is
  retried for `QUEUE_TICKS` (0.6 s). A newer input replaces it.

**Hold.**
- The socket is `SOCKETS[attach][first person?] × k`:
  - torso_fist (-0.95, 2.05, -1.35), scruff (-0.95, 2.1, -1.35) and two_hand (0, 2.3, -1.25) in
    third person;
  - (-1.05, 1.75, -1.0) for every profile in first person (low and to the left).
- During a throw's windup the socket moves to the wind-back point (-1.0, 4.3, 0.2) (two hands in
  third person: (0, 4.6, 0.3)).
- The body faces the giant; a wolf hangs nose up (55°).
- `rig_state` writes `holding` (the attachment profile), `grab_phase` (`reach`, `hold`, `crush`,
  `throw`) and the pose.

**Releases** (`grab_ended` reasons):

| Reason | When |
| --- | --- |
| `thrown` | the throw's projectile took the body |
| `dropped` | E while holding |
| `crushed` | a crush with `release_after` |
| `interrupted` | the holder staggered or grabbed; a growth refused because of the hold; a pending checkpoint whose only blocker is the hold |
| `holder_died` | the holder died |
| `target_died` | the held body died |
| `timeout` | `hold_timeout_s` |
| `unloaded` | the giant left the tree, or the held body vanished |

Every release of a living body leaves it STAGGERED 0.6 s (`release_grabbed`), with the release
reason as its `stagger_reason`. That recovery is not a broken poise, so the body cannot be grabbed
again until a new blow breaks its poise (M3 gate, below).

**Release spots.** `EnemyActor.on_released` puts the body at the first free spot of:
1. where it is held;
2. beside the giant, at the two capsules' radii + 0.6 m (front, left, right, behind);
3. the giant's own position (outside every wall), as the last resort.

A body that is free where it is held drops from there by gravity.

**Boulder Hurl and throw preview.**
- Every windup tick emits `ability_effect(tick, "player", id, "aim_preview", point, splash radius,
  facing, 0)`.
- `point` is `predict_landing`: the runner's launch (resolver origin and aim,
  `Projectile.launch_velocity`) traced in 0.1 s segments against the world.
- The arc lands at the first contact, or at the aim point when that is near the ground (a
  target), and is dropped onto the ground under it.

## Decisions and deviations

1. **Doc name.** This file is `docs/systems/P07.md`, as the task and §1.1 item 5 say; §3.7 names
   `P07-grab-throw.md`.
2. **The grab shape is registered from `PlayerGrab.setup`** on the player's runner (§2.7.2
   allows it). `attack_runner.gd`, `hit_factory.gd` and `attack_context.gd` are unchanged.
3. **A held body is out of the simulation through `process_mode = DISABLED`.** This is P04's
   dormant-tier mechanism:
   - Sim skips its `sim_step`, so there is no AI and no movement;
   - its body and hurtboxes leave physics, so nothing collides with it or hits it, and the throw
     can never hit its own body;
   - its `Visual` stays PAUSABLE, so the rig keeps animating;
   - only `carry()` moves it.

   It cannot be locked while held, and the giant's lock on it is released when it is grabbed. An
   AI LOD change during the hold (`set_lod_tier` would restore the process mode) is undone by the
   next `carry()`, and the release restores the process mode the LOD tier asks for.
4. **The lunge drives the motor's knockback channel.** The plan says "through the motor". The
   player motor has no `motion` support, and `player_motor.gd` is not P07's file. PlayerGrab
   therefore sets `player.knockback_velocity` before the motor runs each windup tick:
   - toward the target, at up to `motion.speed_mps` (9) × k;
   - pre-compensated for the motor's 28 m/s² decay;
   - cleared when the lunge ends.

   The distance is measured, so walls and bodies in the way only shorten it.
5. **One-hand holds use the off (left) hand**, because the club stays in the right. In first
   person every hold, two_hand included, is the off hand's, low and to the left. With both hands,
   the weapon arm crossed the view centre at a steep downward look; the test now catches that.
6. **While holding, every ability but a crush is refused (`holding`)**, the ultimate included.
   Dodge, block and jump are allowed. E during a throw or crush does nothing.
7. **Release fallbacks.** The plan says "1.5 m in front of the holder, then the holder's
   position". The front spot is measured from the giant's capsule surface (radii + 0.6 m), so it
   never overlaps a stage-3 giant. Left, right and behind are tried before the giant's own
   position.
8. **Checkpoints.**
   - A pending checkpoint makes the hold let go only when the hold is its sole blocker
     (`checkpoint_blockers() == ["holding"]`).
   - The held enemy is usually aware and blocks the checkpoint by itself, so dropping it would
     not let the checkpoint happen; then the hold goes on.
   - A growth refused with `holding` always lets go (F6/F7, the shrine API).
9. **Throw numbers** (the plan gives only the cost and the aim):
   - P40 × 1.2, poise 30 (× size scale 2 = 60 against humans: it staggers soldiers, archers and
     mages);
   - 20 m/s, range 20 m, a 1.5 m splash, 0.30 / 0.05 / 0.40 s.

   The landing hit on the thrown body is the same action, "from" the throw's origin, so its
   knockback carries the body on along the flight. The projectile is at least the body's radius
   + 0.25 m wide.
10. **Crushing Grip timings** are 0.25 / 0.10 / 0.35 s, and it is not crit-eligible (the plan's
    `round(40 × 1.15 × 1.5 vs armor 15) = 60` is exact). The hit is queued and the body released
    on the same tick, before resolution, so a kill never double-releases.
11. **Corpses are candidates** reported as `not_grabbable` (§3.7 lists dead under `can_be_grabbed`).
    Living candidates are tried before dead ones.
12. **Hotbar.**
    - `preview_params` gives the HUD a precheck built from the candidates cached every 3 ticks, so
      no physics query runs outside the tick.
    - An empty reach shows as the HUD's existing `no_grab_target` ("No target").
    - The other codes are capitalised by the HUD ("Not Staggered", "Holding", "No Permission",
      "Too Heavy To Crush").
13. **An input queue for grab and throw** (0.6 s, longer than the controller's 9-tick buffer), so
    E pressed during a heavy's recovery still grabs the soldier it staggered.
14. **The aim preview also shows for the throw**, not only for Boulder Hurl.
15. **Existing tests changed.**
    - `test_abilities_m2._check_hotbar` (P03) compares the HUD with `preview_reason` called with
      the same params the HUD uses (the components' `preview_params`). Before, it assumed no
      component adds a precheck to a shipped slot, which P07's grip and Boulder Hurl slots now do.
      This is the hook P03 built for P07.
    - `test_growth.test_growth_unlocks_are_learned_slotted_and_announced_once` (P05) empties slot 5
      first: `giant.json` now fills all eight slots (the plan's slots 4 and 5).
    - Suites whose check counts grew because they iterate what now exists: `test_anchors`
      263 → 267, `test_character_state` 107 → 109, `test_growth_rules` 231 → 232,
      `test_schemas_v2` 306 → 312, `test_abilities_m2` 468 → 486.
16. **The soldier's 1.5 s stagger against a larger attacker** changes the seeded sandbox soak's
    numbers. The soak still breaks fences, fills the debris pool and awards loot (see Tests).
17. **Faction.** Grabs do not check factions: a neutral villager or guard is grabbable when its
    `grab_attach` allows it and its poise is broken (P11b decides with data).

## M3 gate fixes (wave-3 review, on the integration branch)

Review findings at the M3 gate in this package's lane, fixed after the wave merge.

### A released body counted as broken poise

- **Finding.** `check_target` took any STAGGERED state of a living target as broken poise. Every
  release (`EnemyActor.on_released` → `CombatController.release_grabbed`) leaves the body
  STAGGERED 0.6 s, with the release reason as its `stagger_reason` and no poise damage at all.
  `ability.grab_hold` costs nothing and closes the hand 0.25 s after the request, so E straight
  after a drop always landed inside that window. The reviewer's probe staggered a soldier once and
  then repeated "hold 2.5 s, drop, E" six times: 6 of 6 re-grabs, 18.5 s held, no hit landed.
  - The 5 s timeout protected nothing, because a timed-out body was STAGGERED too.
  - A thrown body that landed could be grabbed and thrown again.

  This broke spec §8 (a living target needs broken poise) and let one poise break keep an enemy
  out of the fight forever, at no cost.
- **Fix.** `PlayerGrab.has_broken_poise(e)` is true while `e` is STAGGERED and its
  `stagger_reason` is not one of `RELEASE_REASONS`, the plan's §2.4 `grab_ended` reasons.
  `check_target` refuses a living target without it, with the existing `not_staggered` code. Every
  user of the check follows:
  - E and the grab request's precheck;
  - the re-check when the hand closes;
  - Crushing Grip from an empty hand;
  - the "Grab" prompt and the hotbar;
  - P13's "grabbable" filter.

  The poise meter already ignores poise damage while the body is staggered and for its
  `immunity_s` afterwards (soldier 1.5 s, wolf 1.0 s). So a released body can be grabbed again
  only after a new poise-breaking blow, at the earliest 0.6 s + `immunity_s` after the release.
- **Why the check is in PlayerGrab.** A flag set by `release_grabbed` would also cover release
  reasons nobody uses yet, but it would add state to P01's controller beyond §2.6.1. The release
  vocabulary is a plan contract (§2.4), and PlayerGrab is the only holder: no enemy grabs, and
  large-monster grapples are out of scope. So the rule stays in this package's own file. A new
  release reason must join `RELEASE_REASONS`.
- **Tests** (`test_grab_throw`: 339 → 459 checks):
  - `test_a_body_put_down_needs_a_new_poise_break`:
    - put down, then E at once: refused `not_staggered` (nothing spent, the HUD text, no "Grab"
      prompt, no `grab_started`, the soldier still in its recovery);
    - a 1000-poise blow during the recovery, and another during the immunity after it, break
      nothing;
    - once the immunity is over, a new poise break makes it grabbable, and E grabs it.
  - `test_timed_out_crushed_and_thrown_bodies_are_not_grabbable`:
    - a hold that timed out: refused through the request (nothing spent);
    - a crushed body: `check_target`, `find_candidate` and Crushing Grip from an empty hand all
      give `not_staggered`;
    - a thrown body where it lands: `not_staggered`, also after its landing hit has resolved.
  - `test_every_release_reason_leaves_a_recovery_not_a_broken_poise`:
    - the plan's eight §2.4 reasons, written out in the test: each is in `RELEASE_REASONS`, and a
      release with it leaves a recovery that `check_target` refuses `not_staggered`;
    - `poise_broken`, `guard_broken`, `parried` and the tests' `test` stagger count as broken
      poise.
  - The suite's `_grab` helper staggers a body unless `has_broken_poise` (a release's recovery no
    longer counts).
  - With the old check, the three tests fail (41 failures: 22, 11 and 8); every other check
    passes. The suite as it was at the wave tip (339 checks) passes unchanged with the fix. The
    reviewer's probe now gets 0 of 6 re-grabs, each refused `not_staggered`.
