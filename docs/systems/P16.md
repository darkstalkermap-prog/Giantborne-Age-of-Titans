# P16 — UX, controller and usability pass

Wave 8, lane B (beside P15). Branch `pkg/P16`. Plan: `docs/plans/M2_M5_PLAN.md` §3.18, with §1.5
(hotspots: `input_bindings.gd`, `main.gd`; this wave also `settings.gd` and `settings_pages.gd`),
§2.10 (screens and HUD widgets) and spec §16 (the usability gate). It also fixes the nine items of
`docs/plans/FOLLOWUPS.md` assigned to P16 (M2 and M3 reviews, wave 6 layout), each with a regression
test.

## What was built

- **Pad and keyboard navigation of every screen.** Each `UiScreen` lists its focusable controls in
  `focus_rows()` (rows top to bottom, each row left to right); `UiNav.link_rows` wires explicit
  `focus_neighbor_*`, `focus_next` and `focus_previous` paths from them, leaving out hidden and
  disabled controls, with wrap-around in every direction (up from the first row reaches the last,
  right from a row's end reaches its start). Up and down keep the column (the nearest control
  horizontally). Every screen has a `first_focus`, and the stack puts the focus back when it is
  lost or sits on a control that cannot have it any more (a button that became disabled, a hidden
  page): `UiNav.keep_focus` each frame on the top screen. Screens whose rows change (a rebuilt
  list, a page switch) call `refresh_focus()`; the stack relinks once more after the layout ran.
  Covered: Title (and its Settings and Controls pages), Pause (Main, Settings, Controls), Shrine
  (Rest, Grow, Loadout, Attributes), Dialogue, Inventory, Attributes, Forge, End.
- **Pad A and B.** Godot 4.6's default `ui_accept` and `ui_cancel` hold no pad button, so a
  controller could move the focus but never press a button or back out (P03's "pad B cancels"
  never worked). `UiNav.ensure_pad_actions()` (main and every `ScreenStack`) adds pad A to
  `ui_accept` and pad B to `ui_cancel` at run time. B closes the top screen (the title never
  closes; on its Settings or Controls page B returns to the menu entry), and on an open option list
  B closes only the list.
- **LB / RB** switch the pages of the pause menu and the tabs of the shrine screen (wrapping).
- **Menu footer.** While a menu is open the stack shows how to move, select and go back with the
  device in use: "D-pad / left stick: move  A: select  B: back" or "Arrows / Tab: move  Enter:
  select  Esc: back  (or use the mouse)".
- **Rebinding.** Esc still cancels a capture (the menu stays open); a capture nobody answers gives
  up after 6 s ("No input: the binding was not changed."), since a controller has no Esc.
- **Last-device glyphs.** `main.gd` feeds every input event to `InputBindings.note_device`: a pad
  button press or a stick or trigger past 0.5 means the pad; a key press, a mouse button or a mouse
  movement of 6 px or more means keyboard and mouse (releases, drift and twitches change nothing).
  `InputBindings.last_device_is_pad()` then decides the interact prompt ("[X] Rest at the shrine"),
  the hotbar (the pad combo large and the key small, or the other way round), the HUD mode line
  ("Start: menu and controls" for a pad), the lock hint ("flick the right stick to switch"), the
  tutorial texts (`TutorialText.resolve`), the new-ability notice ("[LT+Rt]"), the shrine's
  Loadout slot titles, the "Click the game" hint (never for a pad player) and the menu footer.
  The new setting **Button prompts** (Settings page: Auto (last device) / Keyboard and mouse /
  Controller; saved as `prompt_device`) pins them.
- **Help overlay from the live loadout.** F1 / H shows a three-column table (action, keyboard /
  mouse, controller; the column of the device in use is highlighted): movement and combat, lock
  and switch, camera, **interact / grab / talk**, one row per filled loadout slot ("Runic Bolt
  (slot 6)") and the ultimate with its fury cost, **inventory**, pause and the overlay itself. It
  is rebuilt when it opens and whenever the loadout, the bindings or the device change.
- **HUD layout.** Every fixed panel reports its region (`Hud.layout_regions(view)`, the widgets'
  `HudWidget.layout_regions`, computed from anchors and offsets) and the layout test checks them
  at 1280 x 720, 1366 x 768, 1600 x 900, 1920 x 1080 and 2560 x 1440: on screen, no two
  overlapping except where designed. Changes: the boss bar is 540 px wide (it overlapped the quest
  tracker by 36 px at 1280 px; it now ends 14 px before it); the target frame of a locked non-boss
  target moves below the bar (y 64) while a boss bar shows and back up afterwards; the new-ability
  notice moved to the top centre (y 132..170; above the hotbar it overlapped the interact prompt
  at 720 px, and a growth at a shrine shows both); the hotbar is 570 px wide (9 slots of 58 px and
  8 gaps: 568 let the row grow 2 px) and the checkpoint toast 34 px high (its line and margins grew
  past 28 px).
- **Mode-dependent death hint and pause items**: already in place since P06 (Load last checkpoint
  where checkpoints exist, Restart sandbox outside the Valley; "Enter / A: load last checkpoint" or
  "Press Enter / A to restart the sandbox"); P16 keeps them and covers the sandbox pause items in
  `test_ui_focus`.
- **The usability script** `docs/USABILITY_SCRIPT.md`: the spec §16 gate (3 new players, 2 of 3
  finish the tutorial encounter unaided and name heavy attacks, blocking and dodge timing), setup,
  a 30-minute protocol (5 minutes of Sandbox free play with its sparring post, the first 10 Valley
  minutes with the tutorial stage times), the observation sheet, the questionnaire, the 15-item
  real-controller checklist (including the web build) and the reporting rules.
- **The nine follow-ups** (table below).

## Public APIs

### UiNav (`ui/ui_nav.gd`, `class_name UiNav`, new)

```gdscript
const PAD_ACTIONS := [["ui_accept", JOY_BUTTON_A], ["ui_cancel", JOY_BUTTON_B]]
static func ensure_pad_actions() -> void          # idempotent
static func has_pad_button(action: String, button: int) -> bool
static func focusable(c: Variant) -> bool         # in the tree, visible, focus mode, not a disabled button
static func link_rows(rows: Array) -> Array       # wires neighbours with wrap-around; returns the rows kept
static func keep_focus(screen: Control) -> Control  # first_focus(), else the first focusable of focus_rows()
static func page_step(event: InputEvent) -> int   # LB -1, RB +1, else 0
static func focus_owner(node: Node) -> Control
static func hint_text(pad: bool) -> String        # the menu footer
```

### Screens (`ui/screens/`)

- `UiScreen`: `func focus_rows() -> Array` (virtual, `[]`), `func refresh_focus() -> Array` (links the
  rows, marks `focus_dirty` so the stack relinks after the layout, keeps the focus when on top),
  `var focus_dirty`.
- `ScreenStack`: the footer label (`hint_text() -> String`, by `InputBindings.device_serial`);
  `_process` relinks a dirty top screen and keeps its focus; `_focus_top()` links before focusing.
- `PauseMenu`: `const PAGES`, `tab_button(page) -> Button`, `show_page(page)`, LB / RB; the capture
  timeout runs in `_process`; `item("Quit to desktop")` now resolves too.
- `TitleScreen`: `show_page(page)`; `first_focus()` is Back on a page; B on a page returns to the
  page's menu entry.
- `SettingsPages`: `const CAPTURE_TIMEOUT_S := 6.0`, `const PROMPT_OPTIONS`,
  `static func prompts_row(grid) -> OptionButton` ("Button prompts"), `settings_rows() -> Array`,
  `controls_rows() -> Array` (`[keyboard, controller]` per action, then Reset),
  `binding_button(action, pad) -> Button`, `update_capture(now_ms := -1) -> bool`. The scroll
  containers follow the focus.
- `ShrineScreen`: `tab_buttons() -> Array[Button]`, `leave_button() -> Button`, LB / RB;
  `ShrineTab.focus_rows()` (default `[[first_focus()]]`), `GrowTab`, `LoadoutTab.focus_rows()` (the
  nine lists), `static LoadoutTab.slot_title(key, pad := false)`, `AttributesTab.focus_rows()`.
- `AttributePanel`: `signal refreshed`, `focus_rows()` (`[-, +]` per attribute, `[Confirm, Reset]`),
  `buttons_of(key) -> {"minus", "plus"}`. `AttributesScreen.close_button()`; its focus moves to
  Close when nothing is left to spend.
- `InventoryScreen`, `ForgeScreen`, `DialogueScreen`, `EndScreen`: `focus_rows()`.

## Decisions and deviations

1. **Doc name** `docs/systems/P16.md` (the task and §1.1 item 5); §3.18 names
   `P16-ux-controller.md`.
2. **Pad A and B join `ui_accept` and `ui_cancel` at run time** (`UiNav.ensure_pad_actions`), not in
   `project.godot`: the project file is not P16's, and the stack works the same wherever it runs
   (tests included).
3. **`InputBindings` grew beyond the two named functions**: `prompt_mode`, `set_prompt_mode`,
   `device_serial` (readers cache their labels), `reset_device` (tests) and
   `describe_for_last_device`.
4. **A "Button prompts" setting** (`prompt_device`): §3.18 does not ask for it, but a player whose pad
   drifts or who rests a hand on the mouse can pin the glyphs. Default `auto`.
5. **Rebinding capture timeout** (6 s) and the **menu footer**: not in §3.18; a controller has no Esc,
   and new players need the A / B hint.
6. **LB / RB page switching** in the pause menu and the shrine screen (not in §3.18).
7. **Layout changes beyond the boss bar**: ability notice to the top centre, the target frame below
   the boss bar, hotbar 570 px, checkpoint toast 34 px high. The plan's ability notice place
   overlapped the interact prompt at 720 px; the other two were found by the layout test (a panel
   grown past its region). `ui/hud/hud_widgets.gd` region comments updated.
8. **The bolt is refused, not only shown out of range** (the follow-up allowed either): a cast the
   HUD calls unavailable must not be accepted. The range is `range_m + radius_m` from the launch
   point to the target's anchor (horizontal for arcs), so a target the bolt can still clip counts.
9. **Follow-up fixes outside the hotspot list**: `player_attack.gd`, `player_grab.gd`,
   `player_growth.gd`, `game_session.gd`, `save_io.gd`, `save_slots.gd` (FOLLOWUPS names these files
   for P16), and `grow_tab.gd` (its combat row reads `combat_gate`).
10. **Growth clearance counts actors in every path** (the shrine and the debug grow alike): a blocked
    growth changes nothing, so the debug key behaves like the shrine.
11. **An extra test suite** `tests/integration/test_followup_fixes.gd` for the nine follow-ups (the
    plan lists only `test_ui_focus` and `test_hud_layout`).
12. **The death hint keeps "Enter / A"** for both devices (`main.gd` accepts both), so P06's text and
    test stay as they are; the pause items by mode are P06's.
13. **The tutorial prompt widget no longer tracks the device itself**: it reads
    `InputBindings.last_device_is_pad()` like every other prompt.

## M4 gate fixes (wave-8 review, on the integration branch)

### Edge warnings under the TIP box and on the hotbar

- **Finding.** `HudWidgets.WIDGETS` is the draw order ("later entries draw on top"), and it listed
  `threat_arrows.gd` fourth and `objective_marker.gd` ninth, before `tutorial_prompt.gd`. The TIP
  box (82 % opaque, 16..396 x 290..430 at 1280 x 720) therefore painted over both overlays, which
  sit 24 px (arrows) and 36 px (marker) inside the left edge at whatever height the threat or
  objective projects to. With a stage-1 giant's camera, 7 of 12 threat arrows and 4 of 12 clamped
  markers for points 80-120 degrees to the left at 20-120 m fell inside the TIP box; a threat or
  objective straight behind landed on the middle hotbar slots ((640, 696) and (640, 684), the
  hotbar region being 345..935 x 612..720). So during the Hollow's tutorial the screen-edge warning
  of decision 25 for a flanking wolf or an archer was hidden. `test_hud_layout` exempted the
  overlays, so nothing caught it.
- **Fix.**
  - **Draw order.** The two edge overlays are now the last widgets: the objective marker, then the
    threat arrows on top. They draw over every panel and stay under the death and help overlays,
    which `Hud` builds after the widgets.
  - **Keep clear of the panels.** `Hud.overlay_keep_out(view)` lists the fixed panels drawn now:
    each visible region control with something in it (an empty message stack, loot line or status
    row is none), at its layout region, the target frame where it is (below the boss bar or not).
    `HudEdge` (`ui/hud/hud_edge.gd`, new, pure) holds the edge geometry shared by both overlays:
    `edge_point` (as before), and `clear_of(point, view, inset, footprint, keep_out)`, which slides
    an edge point along its own inset rectangle, past corners, until the overlay's footprint (the
    arrow's triangle; the marker's diamond, arrow and distance label) keeps a 4 px margin from every
    panel. Of the two ways, the one whose place seen from the screen centre stays nearer the
    point's own direction wins (the angle grows steadily round the rectangle), then the shorter
    one: a threat behind and to the left stays on the left (up the left edge past the resource
    panel) rather than jumping to the right of the hotbar.
  - Both overlays keep their contract: an arrow's tip is still 24 px inside the edge and a clamped
    marker 36 px, and each still points at its threat or objective; only where along the edge
    changes. On-screen markers stay at their projection (now drawn over any panel). The threat
    arrows' `tip_for(view, dir, keep_out)` and `footprint(dir)`, and the marker's `FOOTPRINT` and
    `placement(..., keep_out := [])` expose the geometry; existing callers see no change.
  - Examples at 1280 x 720 with the TIP box, the tracker and the hotbar on screen: an arrow straight
    left (24, 360) goes to (24, 274), just above the TIP box; straight behind (640, 696) to
    (951, 696), right of the hotbar; up and to the right (1200, 24) to (1256, 138), below the
    tracker. Directions clear of the panels (straight right, left and a bit down) do not move.
- **Tests.**
  - `test_hud_edge` (new unit suite, 196 checks): the edge point and the arc round the inset
    rectangle; a clear point stays; the TIP box sends a point above or below it by its half; behind
    with the hotbar and the resource panel goes right of the hotbar, behind-left without the
    resource panel left of it, and behind-left with both up the left edge (the angle rule against the
    shorter arc); round the tracker's corner; a footprint 30 px tall; every point of the left edge
    against the TIP box and the resource panel; nowhere clear leaves the point.
  - `test_hud_layout` (204 → 493 checks): the overlays draw over every region control and under
    the death and help overlays, and the registry lists them last; with the TIP box, tracker, boss
    bar, lowered target frame and loot line on screen, the reviewer's points (80-120 degrees left
    and behind, 20-120 m, the giant's real camera) give arrows (at 1280 x 720 and the test screen)
    and markers that clear every panel on their edge rectangle, pointing the same way, where the
    old placement put at least 6 under the TIP box and 4 on the hotbar; every direction (5-degree
    steps) at the five supported sizes clears the panels; the live widgets: a real archer's shot
    from behind gets its arrow off the hotbar, and the objective marker for an objective behind
    and one to the left clears the hotbar and the TIP box. With the old order and no keep-out the
    suite fails 84 checks. The panels the overlays must clear are taken from the layout regions,
    not only from `overlay_keep_out`.
  - Existing suites unchanged and passing: `test_telegraphs` (an arrow 24 px inside the edge for an
    archer behind) and `test_quest_runner` (a marker 36 px inside the edge for an objective behind)
    hold because the overlays slide along their edge rectangle.
