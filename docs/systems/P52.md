## The launcher's notes (as shipped)

> **New in 0.11.0-m11.** Your giant moves and looks more like a living body. He sprints with a run of his own (hold
> Shift), Heavy Footfall lifts a foot and stamps it down, and he turns on the spot with steps instead of sliding. His
> hands close round the weapon's handle, and the free hand rests open. Hair, braids, beards and capes swing as he
> moves and settle when he stops; braids are now round plaits with beads, and short hair, stubble and short beards
> are drawn as layers of strands. His eyes catch the light and follow where you look and the target you lock. A hit
> makes him flinch away from the side it came from. In first person you see your body's shadow on the ground and
> carry the weapon low in the lower right corner. His skin is warm in sunlight instead of pale. Your look, saves and
> games from 0.10.0-m10 carry over; the fighting, the world and the enemies are the same as in 0.10.0-m10.
>
> **What it is not yet.** Only your giant changed: the soldiers, villagers, beasts and the world are still the
> game's simple shapes. The clothing is still the six outfits of 0.10.0-m10. Hair is still made of shaped strips and
> layers, not single strands, and a black short beard seen face-on against the sun reads very dark. In first person
> you do not see your legs or feet, and at the last growth stage the unreforged sword shows only as a sliver at rest.
> (The rest of the paragraph - the bot's balance, siege trains, the single-threaded browser build - is 0.10's.)
>
> **The next build.** 0.12.0-m12 brings more customization: clothing and armour piece by piece (head, shoulders,
> chest, arms, waist, legs, shins, feet and cape), more hair, beards, braids and beads, and a larger character
> creator. After it, 0.13.0-m13 makes the people and beasts around you realistic too.

The masthead reads "the giant refined: motion, hair and skin - build 0.11.0-m11"; the download note "About 38 MB".

## Decisions and deviations

- **The release's Web build is in the scratch folder, not `builds/web/`**: built with `--out=`, so `build_web.sh`
  did not write the BUILD line into `web/launcher.html` itself; the line was copied from the build's own
  `play.html` (the same numbers), and the pack does not contain the launcher, so the bytes do not depend on it.
- **The Chromium checks ran on the first of the two identical builds**, so that nothing they write lands beside the
  files the lead publishes from the second.
- **The native budget's figure lived in one comment only** (`ui_probe.gd`); no test or tool asserted 0.12, so the
  owner's 0.15 changed the plan's row and that comment.
- **No test moved between tiers**: `--strict-times` did not fail (below), so the slow tier's headroom stays P53's.
- **Not run**: the Linux preset's pack exported on its own to list `tex_hi/` (P47 did; nothing in the presets
  changed since), the Windows export launched, the strict Web forms, anything on a GPU.
