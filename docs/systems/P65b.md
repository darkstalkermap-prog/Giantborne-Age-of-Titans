# P65b — The second look pass: the horse's gallop, the rival giants, the women and the elders, the clothes

Plan: `docs/plans/M11_PLAN.md` §3.18 (P65, of which this is the second half), §2.8, §2.10, §2.12. Built on 21 November
2026 on `pkg/P65b` from `ee99670`, on the shared container (4 CPUs, no GPU: llvmpipe), beside P64. The owner's bar:
"it needs to look and feel realistic and not clunky", "make things look natural", proportions natural at any size.

Every judgement below was made from renders before and after with the game's code (the cavalry, the talkers and the
rival giants spawned from their scenes and records, PersonModel for the close views) in a plain sunlit scene or the
formation test level's light, llvmpipe. The frames are not committed (the package's report lists them).

## 1. The horse's gallop (`scripts/presentation/skinned_quadruped_rig.gd`)

**What was wrong.** P62 planned every swing in the world: a lifted hoof was carried from where it left the ground to
where it would land along a smoothstep in world space. At a walk the hoof's lag behind the body is a few centimetres;
at the gallop (13 m/s, a stride of 5.5 m, the hoof in the air 85 % of the cycle) the smoothstep left a swinging hoof
up to half a metre behind the body early in the swing and half a metre ahead late in it, so the solve threw the legs
out nearly level (the "rubber" legs) and the skin stretched over the shoulder and the stifle. The legs' bones never
stretch (the rig only turns them); the look came from those angles.

**What changed** (all of it weighted by the gallop's share, which is exactly 0 below 5.5 m/s: the walk and the trot
are unchanged; their frames differ from P62's by at most 1/255 in a few pixels):

- **The swing in the horse's own space** (`_gallop_swing`, `swing_progress`): from where the hoof left the ground
  (held in the actor's space at the lift) to where it will land under the horse, along a cubic whose ends keep a
  little of the hoof's backward travel (`GALLOP_RETRACT`: it meets the ground nearly at rest), lifted as the leg folds
  (`GALLOP_LIFT`, peaking early: `GALLOP_PEAK`).
- **Keyed legs** (`GALLOP_FORE_KEYS`, `GALLOP_HIND_KEYS`, after Muybridge's plates of the gallop): each segment's angle
  in the side view at six shares of the swing; between the solves at the lift and the landing
  (`GALLOP_KEY_BLEND`) the leg turns toward them, segment by segment, lengths kept. The foreleg leaves the ground
  behind, tucks up (the knee shut, the hoof turned up under the elbow), swings forward and opens out to reach; the hind
  leg leaves the ground far behind with the hock bent, comes forward under the belly and reaches.
- **The arc**: a swinging leg's hoof is kept within `FORE_ARC_DEG` / `HIND_ARC_DEG` of the plumb from its hip or
  shoulder (the hoof's height kept); the hind hoof lifts at a reach 8 % shorter (`GALLOP_REACH`).
- **The body rocks** (`GALLOP_LOW`): the bob's lowest point and the nose's lowest at the forelegs' landing, the
  highest in the suspension; pitch 4°, bob 5 cm, the neck's nod 10° at the gallop. The transverse gallop's suspension
  (22 % of the cycle after the leading foreleg's push) is the gathered moment: the forelegs folded under, the hind legs
  reaching forward.
- **The horse's asset** (`tools/giant/kit/quadruped.py`, `HORSE.leg_sigma` 0.6): the falloff between a leg's own bones
  is 3.6 cm (6): folded at the tuck, the knee and the hock were a smooth curve over about 12 cm (a rubber leg); they
  crease now. The skin's 99th-percentile stretch at the gallop's extremes stays under 2 (`TestHorseCloseUp`).
- **Held** (or thrown), the legs hang limp (`LIMP_FORE`, `LIMP_HIND`: every joint a little bent, the pasterns hanging)
  and sway slowly; P62's legs hung straight at rest.
- **Dead**: the legs go limp over the fall; the horse is lifted by `DEAD_LIFT_M` as the brain's roll lays it on its
  side (QuadrupedRig's fall turns Body about the ground: the skinned horse's spine lay 0.28 m under it and the horse
  all but vanished, P62 and before); **the rider** slumps forward (`RIDER_SLUMP_DEG`) and goes down with the horse for
  `RIDER_SLUMP_S`, then leaves the saddle on the people's `death` clip (cross-faded in), its pelvis led on a short arc
  to the ground beyond the horse's back (`RIDER_LAND`) as it turns to face the horse, and lies there. The lance stays
  in the fist.

Kept: the 25 bones, the cavalry's and the rider's interfaces (the seat, the stirrups, the lance's carry, couch and
thrust), the hooves' plan (a planted hoof never slides: `test_rider_rig`'s 1e-4 m, the solve reaching every standing
hoof within 2 cm), the box-rig fallback. No scapula bone was added (the shoulder joint's slide stays P62's).

## 2. The rival giants (`tools/giant/kit/giant_garment.py`, `data/appearance/preset_npc_harrok.json`)

- **The hide mantle** reached the lower chest in front and met the war harness's two crossing straps there: below
  its ragged hem the straps' lower V and the boss showed as a band of leather with bare skin between them, which read
  as a torn shirt with holes across the chest and the waist. The mantle is now open in front in a V from the throat
  (over the collarbones and the shoulders' fronts only; down the back as before), so the harness crosses a bare
  chest and reads as straps. `Garment_hide_mantle.glb` only; the giant's share estimate 13.51 MiB of 13.6.
- **Harrok's hair and beard**: `haircolor.brown` at grey 0.6 and 0.66 (copper at 0.45 read orange): a greying brown.

## 3. Women's faces and the elders (`kit/people_roles.py`, `kit/people_bake.py`, `kit/people_outfit.py`, records)

- **The women's faces** (`FACES`: `fair`, `fair_lean`, `fair_round`): the brows raised and arched, a smaller and
  narrower jaw and chin, an oval (Tamsin a round) head, fuller lips with a cupid's bow, a smaller nose, larger eyes,
  rounder cheeks, a slimmer neck, the head's age target lowered. Eleven CC0 targets join the vendored subset
  (`fetch_mpfb2.py --extract`; `PEOPLE.sha256`; the people's added targets 3.87 MB of plan §2.12's 4). Edda, the
  ferrywoman and the herald take macro weight 0.5 (and Edda muscle 0.5): their 0.45, 0.48 and 0.42 (and 0.55) named
  targets that were never vendored and were skipped, so they drew as 0.5 already; vendoring them would cost 0.6 MB
  over the 4 MB.
- **Long hair** is fuller over the crown (`LONG_HAIR_VOLUME_M` 6 mm) when nothing is worn over it. A first try that
  let it fall past the ears read as ear muffs and was dropped.
- **Old women**: the elder role gains `elder_c` (Elder Maren: 72, long grey hair, a skirt over the kilt) and
  `elder_d` (Sedge: 68, hooded, the same skirt), appended (the elders' indices kept), both on `aged_fair` (the head's
  age, the eyes' bags, the laugh lines, the mouth's corners down, over the softer features). **The elders' atlas draws
  age in the face** (`people_bake.AGE_LINES`, `age_lines`): three lines across the forehead, the crow's feet, the folds
  from the nose's wings past the mouth, the lines from the mouth's corners down and the eyes' bags, as soft creases in
  the albedo (the face's normal map is flat since P65a), from the first variant's landmarks; the two old men share them.
- **The records**: `npc.elder` names `person_role.elder` / `elder_c` (it wore `villager_e`), `npc.sedge` names
  `person_role.elder` / `elder_d` (it wore the notables' young `sedge`, which stays in the notable role unused);
  their palettes are unchanged. `person_role.elder`'s pool stays the two men (its description says so).
- 4,321 and 4,389 triangles; the share estimate +0.23 MiB for the two (below).

## 5. The Matriarch, the troll, the horse's tack (`kit/brute.py`, `kit/quadruped.py`)

- **The Matriarch's back** (and the King's): on the hunched back the shoulders' top stands above the neck's joint and
  the mantle's cut there left a band of mossy skin between the collar and the mantle; behind, the mantle now reaches
  `MANTLE_NAPE_M` (7 cm) up the nape. Fixed.
- **The troll's moss** lighter (its tint 0.50/0.68/0.33 times 0.68-1.30, from 0.40/0.58/0.26 times 0.55-1.30).
- **The saddle** (`SADDLE_SEAMS`, `saddle_panels`): seams along the seat's edge, round the flaps, across the cantle
  and the pommel, each a dark groove with a lighter stitched thread beside it, in the albedo (8 mm a texel: the thread
  reads as a dashed line). The cheek's facets were not touched (they are the low mesh's silhouette).

## 6. Talkers turning on the spot (`scripts/presentation/person_rig.gd`)

PersonRig had no turn code: a standing person pivoted its idle pose on planted feet. A person standing and turning
faster than 1.1 rad/s (to 0.5 to stop) now steps round on the walk clip, a cycle every 2.4 rad of turn
(`TURN_STEP_*`, `_step_turn`).

## How it looks against the bar

- **The horse's gallop - at the game camera it reads as a galloping horse**: the legs fold and reach in turn, the
  forelegs tuck, the hind legs come under the belly, the body rocks; nothing is thrown out level. **At 3 m better but
  not natural yet**: the hind legs trailing at their push are long and fairly straight, the planted foreleg at its
  reach reads stiff, the tucked foreleg's dark points make it a dark flipper from some angles. Walk and trot as P62's.
  Held: limp legs, natural. Dead: the horse lies on its side on the ground and the rider falls clear; the rider's
  fall is quick and the lance swings wide while he falls.
- **The rival giant - passes at the game camera and at 3 m**: a hide over the shoulders and the back, the harness
  crossing a bare clawed chest; no holes. **Harrok** greying (his long hair reads nearly white at 3 m in full sun).
- **The women - at the game camera they read as women (hair, build, skirts); at 3 m softer than before, still young
  and plain** (fuller lips and rounder cheeks show; the low faces and flat shading keep them boyish next to a real
  woman's). **Elder Maren and Sedge read as old women** at 3 m (grey hair, forehead lines, folds by the mouth); the
  lines are a little hard at 1 m.
- **The clothes**: the iron guard's tabard is whole and plain; hoods clean; the mail skirt hangs close with no hole
  (a thin crack at the cleft at 1 m); the women's tunics softer under the bust, villager_d's still marked.
- **The Matriarch** covered to the collar; **the troll** a touch lighter from behind; **the saddle** has seams.
