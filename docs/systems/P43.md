# P43 — The skinned giant in the game

Wave 26, lane A, beside P44 (the character creator). Branch `pkg/P43`, from `2b55676` (the W25 merge and its
review). Plan: `docs/plans/M10_PLAN.md` §3.3, with §1.5-§1.7 (shared files, test tiers and caps), §2.5 (the
model's index), §2.7 (the code rules), §2.9 (the skinned rig, tip matching, paint) and §2.13 (test conventions).
The realistic model (P41's `giant_model.tscn`) is the player: the pose brain stays and drives it, its clips are
computed by the asset pipeline, tip matching (P42) places the weapon arm, and nothing that plays changes - the
gate's determinism suite proves it with positive controls. No partner-lane symbol is used.

## What was built

### The clips (`tools/giant/kit/clips.py`, `build_giant.py --clips`, `assets/giant/giant_clips.glb`)

Computed keyframes, no hand animation, written by Blender in the pinned venv and imported as an animation
library (`animation/fps=30`, `remove_immutable_tracks`; loops are set in code). The kit ports the research kit's
two helpers (the local rotation of a bone toward a target in its parent's frame, and the left/right mirror of a
pose) and computes every pose from foot and hand targets in Blender's armature space:

| Clip | Frames | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `idle` | 60 (2 s loop) | breathing in the spine and clavicles, the arms hanging relaxed, the feet planted |
| `walk`, `run` | 32 each (one two-step cycle) | the brain's 3.2 m cycle (1.6 m a step): stride measured 3.200 m (0.00 %), feet sliding 0 m, the walk's duty 0.55 and pelvis drop 0.19 m, the run's 0.36 and 0.25 m (`clips_info.json`, `manifest.json` "clips"); the left heel strikes at a quarter cycle, the right at three quarters |
| `airborne` | 1 | the legs gathered |
| `death` | 30 | the knees buckle, the torso slumps (seeked by the brain's fall fraction) |
| `grip_r`, `grip_l`, `open_r`, `open_l` | 1 each | the fists closed on a weapon or a body, the off hand open |

Each arm is aimed explicitly (upper arm and forearm toward their targets) so the relaxed arms do not add the
rest pose's elbow bend. `build_giant.py --clips` writes the library and its info into the manifest (`--verify`
regenerates it bit-exactly with the other outputs); `test_giant_tools.py`'s `ClipsTest` checks the stride, the
contacts and the keys.

### `SkinnedRig` (`scripts/presentation/skinned_rig.gd`, extends `ActorRig`)

`Visual`'s script. `ActorRig._process` still computes every pose of the brain on the frame clock (the stylized
pivots `HipL`, `HipR`, `Torso`, `ShoulderL`, `ShoulderR` and their `Hand` markers stay, with no meshes), so every
`is ActorRig` reader, the footfall audio and the camera shake keep working. The rig then:

- **The switch** (decision 6): `layers_enabled()` - a display, `--skin` or `enable_for_tests()`; never with
  `--no-skin`. With the layers off the run-time nodes are not made, the skeleton rests, `GiantLook` only shows and
  hides meshes, and `WeaponSocket` follows the brain's `ShoulderR/Hand` (where the stylized club hung).
- **The run-time nodes** (never saved): an `AnimationPlayer` with the library `giant` (a copy with the loops set),
  an `AnimationTree` (a BlendTree built in code, processed manually from the rig's `_process`) and the
  `PoseBrainModifier` under the skeleton.
- **The legs' sources**, in precedence: *dead* (the walk and idle stop, the death clip follows the brain's fall
  fraction under the body's fall), *airborne* (off the floor more than two frames; the airborne pose blends in at
  45/s, past half on the third frame), *motion* (an action whose `motion` block moves the giant in this phase -
  Shoulder Charge's dash, the grab's lunge - runs the run clip on the skin's own clock, ground speed / stride),
  *walking* (the brain walks: idle, walk and run blended by speed / k, the run from 3.5 to 6.5 m/s; the clips'
  time is `fposmod(walk_phase + π/2, 2π) / 2π × length`, so a heel strikes on every brain footfall) and otherwise
  the *stance* (the idle's legs under the body's lean: dodges, staggers, knockbacks faster than 0.5 m/s, standing
  actions). `legs_source()` and `blend_weights()` expose the choice. A clip is seeked only while its branch
  shows (the tree's optimisation skips the zero-weight branches that do not seek) and the blend parameters are set
  only when they change.
- **The hands**: the right fist always closed on the weapon (`grip_r`, filtered to the fingers), the left closed
  on a held body (`grip_l`) or open while reaching or casting (`open_l`).
- **The held body**: in `skeleton_updated`, after the modifier, the held body's `Visual.position` moves by the gap
  between the visible palm and the socket the simulation holds it at (at most 0.33 x k); it is put back on a
  release, a new hold, the body's death and the rig leaving the tree (`_restore_held`). Its collision and grab
  point never move.
- **Paint**: `GiantLook.build_materials` gives every surface its own ShaderMaterial; `flash()` and
  `set_status_tint()` reach them through `RigPaint.paint_shaders` (the `tint` and `flash` uniforms) and the weapon's
  flat copies as before. `apply_look(look)` (PlayerAppearance calls it) is `GiantLook.apply` on the model.
- **The weapon**: `set_weapon_visual` parents the weapon at `Visual/Body/WeaponSocket` (its scale as
  `PlayerEquipment` sets it).
- **Reads**: `model()`, `weapon_socket()`, `skeleton()`, `modifier()`, `animation_tree()`, `layers_on()`,
  `visible_weapon_segment()` and `palm_global(left)` (valid inside `posed`), `look_name()` (the preset when the
  giant's record is that preset's, else "custom"), `shown_meshes()`, `skin_stats()`, and the static
  `run_stats()` the Autopilot prints as `SKIN_STATS`.

### `PoseBrainModifier` (`scripts/presentation/pose_brain_modifier.gd`, a `SkeletonModifier3D`)

Runs inside the skeleton's update, after the clips: the brain's torso twist over `spine_01`-`03`; the weapon arm
by tip matching during a weapon arc (`TipMatching.match_swing_at` from the shoulder where the clips put it),
otherwise by the generic mapping (the palm at the shoulder + direction x 0.9 x reach, the weapon 41° off the hand
toward the front, toward the sky for the tree and the pine), crossing over at 12/s so the hand never jumps; the
walk's arm swing turned a quarter cycle so it peaks on the clips' heel strikes; the off arm on the brain's off pose
while an action, a block, a dodge or a pose override runs (the clip's arm in plain locomotion); during a hold the
off palm on the socket, the clavicle protracted up to 18° and the arm straight when the socket is out of reach
(the gameplay socket is the stylized arm's, 1.5·k out: the visible palm then comes within 0.23·k of it at stage 1,
and the held body's visual closes the rest); `WeaponSocket` in the fist along the weapon. Every arm is an analytic
two-bone solve (`TipMatching.two_bone`, the elbow toward `TipMatching.pole_for`) set as whole frames, and solved
again with the hand turned toward the forearm until the wrist bends at most 45° (the palm stays on its target). At
death the drive fades out so the arms follow the death clip. `stats` holds the last run's numbers (`arc`,
`fist_error_m`, `wrist_deg`, `off_w`, `us`).

### The player's scene (`tools/builders/player.gd`, `scenes/player/giant.tscn`)

The stylized meshes and the merged rig are gone; `Visual` is a `SkinnedRig`; `Visual/Body` holds the brain's
pivots with their `Hand` markers, `Model` (an edit-state instance of `giant_model.tscn`, so the saved scene keeps
the instance alone and never a stale copy of the model's index) and `WeaponSocket`. `giant_model.tscn` was
rebuilt for the regenerated manifest.

### Hotspots (§1.5, W26)

- `actor_rig.gd`: read accessors for the brain's outputs (`rig_state`, `arm_pose`, `off_pose`, `walk_phase`,
  `is_walking`, `arm_swing_share`, `torso_twist`, `fall_fraction`); the brain itself is unchanged.
- `rig_paint.gd`: `paint_shaders(materials, tint, flash, flash_color)`.
- `camera_rig.gd`: `TORSO_LAYER` 2, `HEAD_LAYER` 3, `CULL_FIRST_PERSON` (culls 2 and 3), `CULL_NO_HEAD` (pressed
  close: culls 3, and 2 when `PRESSED_CULLS_TORSO`, false - see the passage below). Its geometry is unchanged.
- `weapon_visuals.gd`: the canopy and the needles on layer 3 (`FP_HIDDEN_LAYER` is now a bitmask).
- `tip_matching.gd`: `match_swing_at(shoulder, ...)` (`match_swing` calls it with the landmark shoulder).
- `autopilot.gd`: `--skin-stats` prints `SKIN_STATS {...}` just before `AUTOPILOT_RESULT` and outside it;
  `--scenario=capture_m10`. P39's `perf.sections` and P39f's `WORLD_WALK_UNLOAD` are untouched.
- `build_giant.py`: the `--clips` step (`CLIP_SOURCES`, the import settings of `giant_clips.glb`).

### The capture tour (`scripts/debug/capture_m10.gd`, `--scenario=capture_m10`)

Idle, walk, run, sprint, a jump; the light and the heavy of the club, the sword, the hammer and the axe at stage 1,
the tree's light at stage 3, the fists at stage 4 (their required stage), the pine's heavy at stage 5; a block, a
roll and a sidestep, Shoulder Charge, a grab, hold and throw of the arena's soldier, Heavy Footfall, Thunder Roar,
Stone Skin, a stagger, a knockback, stages 2-6 with Earthquake at 6, first person ahead and looking down, the
death fall: 38 beats, one frame each (`m10_NN_<beat>.png`). Run it with a display and the skin:

```
xvfb-run -a -s "-screen 0 1280x720x24" tools/gd.sh --path . --rendering-driver opengl3 --resolution 960x540 \
  --fixed-fps 30 --write-movie <out>/m10_tour.avi -- --no-user-settings --scenario=capture_m10 --skin \
  --capture-dir=<out>
```

`AUTOPILOT_RESULT`'s `capture_m10` lists the frames and any beat the game refused. Recorded on P43's branch
(the command above, Xvfb, llvmpipe): 2,328 frames, 77.6 s of video at 30 fps, the 38 frames saved, no beat refused,
`"errors":0`; 8 min 30 s of wall time with two Blender renders on the same four CPUs. Headless (no frames saved) the
tour runs in 11 s, which is how its beats are checked after a change. When it ends the tour lets go of its beats
(their lambdas hold it), so the run exits without leaked instances.

## Decisions and deviations

1. **`test_skinned_rig`'s motion checks are a slow suite** (`tests/slow/test_skinned_rig_motion.gd`, a file the plan
   does not list). With the layers on a frame costs the runner about 0.1 ms more, and the walk, the three stance
   cases, the charge, the jump, the death, the stage-4 heavy and the hold are about 1,200 frames: they would take
   the whole 0.6 s default cap. The default suite keeps the node contract, the clips, the headless default, a
   stage-1 heavy, paint, the look, the layers and the counts.
2. **`test_grab_first_person` and `tests/support/crosshair_probe.gd` changed** (neither is P43's): the probe's rest
   AABB of the skinned limbs covers the centre ray in every frame; the probe now measures skinned meshes by
   their posed bones, and the two first-person crosshair checks turn the layers on.
3. **The hold reaches further than "its farthest point toward the socket"**: the plan's socket is the stylized
   arm's, 1.72 m from the real shoulder at stage 1 against a palm reach of 1.385 m. The modifier protracts the
   clavicle (up to 18°) and straightens the arm (`SOLVE_MARGIN` of the palm's reach), which brings the palm to
   0.23 m of the socket - under the 0.33·k the held body's visual may move - so the body shows in the hand.
4. **One grip axis for every family** (`TipMatching.GRIP_AXIS_DEG`, 41°): the contract holds for every family
   (P42's test and the gate's frames), and the wrist limit below replaces a per-family axis. The elbow poles are
   per side and cross-body (P42's `POLES`, `CROSS_POLES`); over every family's arcs at stages 1 and 3 the elbow
   comes at most 0.13 m across the body's middle.
5. **The wrist limit**: tip matching's hand bent up to 52.6° on the end of some arcs; the modifier now solves the
   arm again with the hand turned toward the forearm (up to four times) so the bend stays at 45° and the fist on
   its target.
6. **A weapon carried past its fit stage** (P42's follow-up): the unreforged club, sword or hammer at stage 4 or 6
   keeps its stage-2 length, so its tested capsule starts beyond the real hand's reach. The look is that the
   weapon points at the tested tip and falls short of it; the stage-4 and stage-6 checks use weapons that fit
   (the slow suite makes the club fit stage 6, the gate's frames use the reinforced club).
7. **Airborne**: "off the floor for more than two frames" is kept; the pose then blends in at 45/s so it is past
   half on the third frame off the ground.
8. **The knockback's stance**: the brain walks whenever the giant moves in `LOCOMOTION`; the skin shows the
   stance while `knockback_velocity` is over 0.5 m/s, so a slide never walks.
9. **`look_name()`** compares the giant's record with the preset's record as stored and as `Appearance.sanitize`
   writes it (the creator stores sanitized records).
10. **The player builder instances the model with its edit state**: a plain instance saved the whole index into
    `giant.tscn`, where it would have shadowed a regenerated model's index.
11. **`SceneKit.FP_HIDDEN`** (2) is left as it was: nothing builds with it now (`test_scene_kit` still reads it).
12. **The hair's `head_motion`** is not written: P46 has not added it to the hair shader.
13. **The stomp's leg lift** stays on the cut list, as the plan has it.
14. **The capture tour** took 8 min 30 s of wall time for its 77.6 s of video on this machine (two Blender renders
    shared its four CPUs), more than the plan's assumed four minutes; it was recorded once, at the end, as planned.

  the torso's garment core is culled on layer 2 and the limb part has no cap there. For the gate's frame review
