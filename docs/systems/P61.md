## 1. The quadruped kit (`tools/giant/kit/quadruped.py`)

**The body is a signed distance field**, not lofted rings (the plan's §2.8 wording): about seventy ellipsoids and round
cones (tapered capsules) on the anatomy of a riding horse of 1.58 m at the withers - the barrel and the belly, the chest
and the breast muscles, the withers, the back, the croup and the two halves of the rump, the points of the hip, the
thighs to the stifles, the buttocks down to the gaskins, the shoulder blades and the upper arms, the arched neck in
seven sections (deep and narrow, the crest above the straight line), the head (the cranium, paired jowls, the flat
nasal line, the lower jaw, the muzzle and the chin, the nostrils cut, the bony orbits, the ears) and per leg the
forearm or gaskin with its muscles, the point of the elbow, the knee or the hock with the point of the hock and the
hamstring, the cannon with the flexor tendons behind it, the fetlock and the ergot, the sloping pastern and a hoof (a
cone sheared forward, the coronet band falling to the heels). A polynomial smooth union joins them, so the joints read
as bulges and the muscles run into one another; the dock of the tail is part of the body.

Why a field: a smooth union gives one closed surface whatever the parts' overlaps (the knees, the hocks, the shoulder's
muscles over the barrel), which rings lofted along a spine and tubes for the legs do not without a join per limb. The
field is **polygonized by marching tetrahedra** (the project's own code, a Kuhn split of each grid cube, so the
triangles of neighbouring cubes conform: closed and manifold by construction) on a 12 mm grid (180,000 vertices,
360,000 triangles), smoothed by Taubin's filter (6 passes, the volume kept), **decimated by Blender's collapse** to
4,700 triangles (symmetric; the head and the lower legs weighted to keep more), **unwrapped** by the script (the
people's smart projection) and weighted.

**The skeleton (25 bones)**: `Root`, `pelvis`, `spine_01`, `spine_02`, `neck`, `head`, `jaw`, `tail_01`, `tail_02`, and
per leg `<fore|hind>_upper`, `_lower`, `_cannon`, `_foot` with `_l` / `_r` (the foreleg: shoulder joint to elbow, elbow
to knee, knee to fetlock, fetlock to the sole; the hind leg: hip to stifle, stifle to hock, hock to fetlock, fetlock to
the sole). The forelegs hang from `spine_02`, the hind legs and the tail from `pelvis`.

**Weights** by distance to the bone chains (the nearest bone and the others within 3.5 falloffs of 3 cm, four
weights): a vertex belongs to its leg as far as it stands outside the body's **core** (the field without the shoulder
blades, upper arms, thighs, buttocks and legs: `core_sdf`), to the spine, neck, head, jaw or tail otherwise. The first
version weighted the belly to the upper legs and the gallop's extreme swelled the body by 22 %; the core rule keeps it
within 3.4 %. `Root` takes nothing; every vertex is weighted.

**The mane, the forelock and the tail are cards** (alpha strands in the atlas's hair tile: one surface with the body,
alpha scissor): fifteen cards lying down the neck's right side and a short fringe on the left, an upright crest card,
two forelock cards; the tail five crossed cards along a curve from the dock to above the hocks, weighted to `tail_01`
and `tail_02`.

**The tack**: a saddle from the same field (a shell of the trunk standing off the housing, thicker at the pommel and the
cantle, its flaps down the sides, the girth under the barrel), the headstall (crownpiece and cheekpieces in one strap
over the poll, the browband, the noseband) and the reins as flat straps on the head's surface, the stirrup leathers, the
bit rings and the stirrup irons, and **a housing** (a cloth over the barrel from behind the shoulders to before the
stifles, falling straight from the widest line to a dagged hem) **in the faction's colour through the palette band**.

**The atlas** (one per horse, as a person's role): albedo 512 px RGBA (A: the strands' coverage), normal 256 px (the
dense field's normals in the low mesh's MikkTSpace frame: the muscles the decimation removed come back as shading), and
the ORM map (R ambient occlusion ray cast against the horse, G roughness, B **the person shader's palette band**:
the coat in the `skin` band (the coat's colour), the points - the legs below the knees and hocks, the ears' tips - and
the mane and tail in `hair`, the housing in `faction`; the hooves, the eyes, a white left hind sock, the leather and the
metal in `none`). So P60's `person.gdshader` draws a horse unchanged: a bay is coat brown and hair black, a chestnut
coat red and hair flaxen, a grey both pale, and the housing takes the faction's row.

**The gait's extremes** (`POSES`, a check, not clips: P62's `QuadrupedRig` drives the bones): stand, the trot's two
diagonal extremes, the gallop extended and gathered, as rotations about the lateral axis at the joints; exported as
one-frame clips in `horse_poses.glb`, and checked on the skinned body:

## 2. The rival giants' gear (`tools/giant/kit/giant_garment.py`, `giant_hair.py`, `export_game.py`)

- **The hide mantle** (shoulders, leather, 2,472 triangles): a thin hide over the shoulders, down the back further than
  in front, a ragged edge.
- **The war harness** (armour, leather, 516 triangles): two broad straps crossing the chest and the back from each
  shoulder to the other hip, a round boss where they cross.
- **The braided-beard rings** (`Beads_iron`, a bead set: broad iron bands on the anchors of the five
  anchored beards only, 540 triangles; the set's `styles` filter `Beard_`).

**Every earlier giant file is bit-identical**: the two garments are built after the whole wardrobe (`NPC_ORDER`, last in
`ORDER`), each over the envelope of the slots inside its own (`Wardrobe.build` keeps the envelope before each slot), so
no earlier garment's envelope, mesh or push changed; the giant's manifest gains the three parts, their index entries,
the layering table's new rows and the new kit hashes (`quadruped.py` among them) - nothing else (checked file by file
against `60c16b4`: every other mesh, map, clip and part has its old sha256). `scenes/player/giant_model.tscn` was
rebuilt by its builder from the new index.

## 3. The looks (`data/appearance/preset_npc_rival_giant.json`, `preset_npc_harrok.json`)

The rival giant: a wild mane, a braided beard in iron rings, the hide mantle over the war harness on a clawed chest,
bracers, trousers, leg wraps, boots, an iron arm ring, a beast's jaw at the belt, ash round the eyes. Harrok: a long
braided mane in bronze rings, a long forked beard greying under iron rings, the hide mantle over a fur cloak, the war
harness, iron vambraces and greaves, the plated war belt, a kilt, gold at the throat and the arm, five scars, runes.

**The `npc` flag** (`appearance_preset_schema.gd`, `appearance_set_schema.gd`): on the two presets, the two garments and
the iron rings. `Appearance.is_npc`; `preset_ids()`, `option_ids()`, `options()` (the creator's rows) and Randomize's
pool leave them out; `option()` and `preset_record()` still find them, so `Appearance.to_look` and `GiantLook` dress a
rival giant as they dress the player. Both looks pass `data_errors` (sanitized with nothing dropped).

## How it looks (for the beast bar)

- **Reads as a horse at 10 m** from the game camera beside a soldier (`horse_game_10m_*`): the silhouette (the arched
  neck, the long head with its jowls and pointed ears, the withers, the deep barrel, the rounded croup, the four legs
  with knees, hocks and fetlocks, the black points and the hooves) and the scale against a 1.8 m man are right. At 30 m
  it is still a horse with a red housing; at 60 m it is a few pixels, a brown four-legged shape.
- **What is off, close up**: the head is faceted (4,700 triangles over the whole body); the eye reads as a dark square
  with a pale corner instead of an almond with a lid; the mane is a thin dark band from the left (the cards lie on the
  right side) and the tail's strands are sparse at the end; the chest is a little full and round low in front; the
  saddle is lumpy and the stirrups hang inside the housing's skirt; the coat is a flat colour (no dapples, no hair
  direction in the normal map). The gait extremes pose cleanly (the knees and hocks fold, the body keeps its volume),
  but the shoulder and the stifle stretch at the gallop's extremes.
