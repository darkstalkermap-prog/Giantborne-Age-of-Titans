## Part "ui": the HUD, the map and the screens

The HUD, map and screen rows of FOLLOWUPS' "From the M8 screenshot pass", and that section's scenery-fade
shader test. Each problem was reproduced before it was fixed, on a snapshot of `wave-22`:

- **A reproduction suite** (`repro/test_p39f_repro.gd` in the evidence folder, not committed: it measures
  the old layout, the committed suites assert the new one) failed 12 of its 13 checks on `wave-22`: the HUD
  visible under the pause menu; RAMPART 52 px, FOOTFALL 55 px and STONEWALL 66 px in a 42 px slot title; the
  red note ending 2 px past the screen's edge; the loot line's 594 px of text in a 244 px line, with no
  backing; a three-line tracker step shown on two; "in The Old Kingdom"; the shrine's tab row the same for
  every tab; the footer's "Enter: select" against the yield prompt's "Hold Enter"; the shrine's name 6.0 px
  tall from 30 m. On the branch 2 still fail by design (it reads the old STONEWALL label, and compares the
  prompt with UiNav's line where the fix hides the footer).
- **Frames**: the same screenshot scenarios rendered at 1280x720 (Xvfb, the compatibility renderer) on the
  `wave-22` snapshot ("before") and on the branch ("after"): the HUD at the Kingsgate shrine at stages 3 and 6,
  the map whole and zoomed, the journal, the shrine's Talents and Ancestry tabs, Corvane's and Harrok's yield
  prompts, Harrok's duel, the end screens, the title, and the Kingsgate shrine's name from where fast travel

The evidence folder is the lane's scratch folder `m9_w23/P39f/`: `shots/before/` and `shots/after/` (same
names), `pairs/` (each pair side by side, named by the row it shows), `repro/`, `mutations/` and `logs/`. The
M8 frames the rows cite are in the screenshot pass's `shots_m8/`.

### The HUD hides behind every open screen

FOLLOWUPS: "the HUD stays drawn round an open menu", "a tutorial tip stays behind the shrine window", "the last
message shows through the end screen".

- **Cause.** Nothing hid the HUD while a screen was open. The screens' panels are see-through and none covers
  the whole view, so the stack's footer hint ("Arrows / Tab: move  Enter: select  Esc: back") printed over the
  hotbar's cost labels and red notes, the quest tracker stood beside the shrine window and the journal, the TIP
  box showed behind the shrine window (its lines cut by the panel's edge: they were whole, half hidden), and the
  last message's tail stuck out under the end screen's buttons. The tutorial tip and the message are HUD
  widgets, so the three rows had one cause.
- **Fix.** `Hud.MenuWatch`, a child that runs while the game is paused, hides the HUD while
  `ScreenStack.any_open_in(tree)` (any stack has a screen open: pause, shrine, dialogue, yield prompt, map,
  journal, inventory, attributes, forge, ancestry, end) and shows it when the last one closes. The HUD itself is
  pausable, so nothing on it changes meanwhile and it comes back as it was. The whole HUD hides, not parts of
  it: every screen covers the middle of the view, nothing on the HUD can be used under one, and all but the
  title (which has no HUD) pause the game.
- **Test.** `test_hud_layout.test_the_hud_hides_behind_an_open_screen`: a busy HUD (boss bar, lock, tracker,
  TIP, messages) hides under the pause menu, the journal and a screen that does not pause
  (`tests/support/test_overlay_screen.gd`), and shows again when they close.
- **Evidence.** `pairs/02_hud_round_menus__*` (shrine tabs, journal, Corvane's yield, both end screens),
  `pairs/06_tip_behind_shrine__*`, `pairs/08_end_screen_message__*`.

### The yield prompt gives one instruction

- **Cause.** The stack's footer always showed UiNav's line, "Enter: select", over every screen; the yield
  prompt's choices are held, not selected ("Hold Enter, or hold the mouse button on a choice, to take it").
- **Fix.** `UiScreen.nav_hint(pad)`: the footer says what the top screen asks for, UiNav's line by default.
  `YieldPrompt` returns "" and the footer hides: its own line is the only instruction.
- **Test.** `test_yield_protocol.test_the_prompt_alone_says_what_to_do`: no footer over the prompt (keyboard or
  pad), the prompt's own line says to hold; the pause menu opened over it has the footer, hidden again when it
  closes.
- **Evidence.** `pairs/09_yield_prompt__*`.

### The loot panel and the tracker show their lines whole

FOLLOWUPS: "long HUD lines are cut with '...'".

- **Cause.** The loot line was one unbacked 244 px label of every item carried, trophies and keys included,
  trimmed with an ellipsis ("Loot: Iron Ingot x1, Ogre King's Tu..."). The tracker gave the tracked step two
  lines and the second quest's step one, each trimmed, in a fixed 118 px box.
- **Fix.** The loot line is a backed panel (`LOOT_WIDTH` 300 px, bottom right, growing upward) of the materials
  the giant carries (stackable items: iron, titan iron, wood scrap, the forge's stock), packed whole onto lines
  (`Hud.loot_lines`: an item is never split; at most `LOOT_MAX_LINES` 3). One-of-a-kind trophies, keys and
  quest items are listed by the inventory screen, and every pickup is still announced by its message. The
  tracker wraps each step onto up to `TEXT_LINES` (3) lines and its panel is as tall as what it shows; its
  layout region is its tallest (`max_height()`).
- **Test.** `test_hud_layout.test_the_loot_panel_lists_the_materials_whole` (every stackable item in the data at
  once fits `LOOT_MAX_LINES` lines with every entry whole; the live panel lists the materials and no trophy, on a
  backing, as tall as its lines and inside its layout region) and `test_tracker_steps_show_whole` (every quest
  step and title in the data, more than 60, fits the tracker whole; the longest takes three lines; the live
  tracker shows it whole inside its region).
- **Evidence.** `pairs/03_loot_and_tracker__*` (the before frames show "Hollowm..." and "Tu..."; after, the
  whole steps and the backed panel).

### Ability names fit their hotbar slots; the red notes clear the edge

FOLLOWUPS: "ability names too long for their hotbar slot are cut with no '...'", "the red notes under the
ability slots touch the screen's bottom edge".

- **Cause.** A 58 px slot less its box's margins left a 42 px title, too narrow for RAMPART (52 px), FOOTFALL
  (55 px) and STONEWALL (66 px) at the HUD's small size; the trim dropped whole letters and had no room for
  "...". The row's bottom sat 16 px above the edge and the red note 8..22 px under the slot, so the note's
  last 2 px fell off the screen.
- **Fix.** Nine 60 px slots with 4 px gaps (572 px; it was 570), the title spanning the slot's whole inside
  (`SLOT_TITLE_WIDTH` 56 px); the row's bottom 20 px up (`HOTBAR_BOTTOM`), the note one small line from
  `REASON_GAP` (6 px) under the slot, ending at least `REASON_EDGE_PX` (3 px) above the edge. Stonewall Stance's
  `hotbar_label` is now STANCE (`data/abilities/stonewall_stance.json`): at 66 px it fits no slot of a nine-slot
  row that leaves the bars and the loot panel their room at 1280 px. The cost labels fit too ("100 FUR", which
  read "100 FU").
- **Test.** `test_hud_layout.test_hotbar_names_and_notes_fit`: a slot's title spans 56 px and every ability's
  hotbar label in the data (25 or more; 5 or more were wider than the old 42 px) fits it in the HUD's font;
  every slot's note is a whole line, ends 3 px or more above the edge and stays in the hotbar's region.
- **Evidence.** `pairs/04_hotbar_names_notes__*` (RAMPA, FOOTF and the cut "Too big" before).

### The compass draws its marks above its letters

- **Cause.** The strip (4..28) drew the marks (objective diamond, lead outline, place squares, the pin) and the
  direction letters on one row, so a mark at a letter's heading covered it (the N of NE and NW).
- **Fix.** The strip is 30 px tall (4..34): the marks have a row of their own (`MARK_Y` 9), the letters sit
  under it (baseline 27), the minor ticks only between letters, and an unknown place's "?" in the marks' row.
  `Compass.mark_rect()` and `letter_rect()` give what is drawn. The target frame (44) stays clear.
- **Test.** `test_compass.test_marks_never_cover_the_letters`: every kind of mark at every letter, every 5
  degrees of heading, no overlap and the marks' row above the letters'; and a live strip with a town due north
  and the pin north-east. Putting `MARK_Y` back on the letters' row fails it (3 checks, `mutations/`).
- **Evidence.** `pairs/05_compass__*`.

### The shrine's tab row shows the open tab

- **Cause.** The tabs were plain buttons; opening one swapped the body and nothing else.
- **Fix.** `UiKit.set_tab_open(button, open)`: the open tab stands on a lit box with a 3 px gold underline and a
  gold title; the others are plain with a muted title (white when hovered). An open tab keeps the theme's margins,
  so the row does not move. The shrine screen and the pause menu's pages mark theirs; the focus outline stays
  the theme's, so focus and the open tab read apart.
- **Test.** `test_shrine.test_the_tab_row_shows_the_open_tab`: opened by `show_tab`, a click and pad RB, only the
  open tab is marked, sizes unchanged; the pause menu's pages the same.
- **Evidence.** `pairs/07_shrine_tabs__*`.

### The Kingsgate shrine's name reads

- **Cause.** The name was the shared world sign: a 64 px font at 0.006 m a pixel (0.38 m tall), shown from 7 to
  40 m, so about 6 px tall from 30 m (the M8 frame's distance) and gone past 40 m.
- **Fix.** The shrine builder makes it a fixed-size label (`tools/builders/shrine.gd`): a 24 px em at the
  default 75 degree field of view on a 720 px view (15.5 px at the widest, 100 degrees), shown from 7 to 90 m (a
  stage-6 camera zoomed all the way out stands within it), 5.8 m up, more than 1 m over the cap. Rebuilt with
  `build_scenes.tscn -- --only=shrine`; the world's chunks use `shrine.tscn` by path. Once readable, it read
  through the shrine window's panel (between the tab's body and Leave): `ScreenStack` hides the
  `hide_under_screens` group while a screen is open, as the HUD hides, and the `Shrine` puts its name in it.
- **Test.** `test_shrine.test_the_shrine_name_reads_from_the_pad`: fixed size, at least 18 px at 75 degrees and
  14 px at 100 (the old sign: 6.0 px), within range of every stage's farthest camera at the pad, 1 m over the
  cap; hidden while a screen is open (a shrine made then starts hidden), shown when it closes or its stack leaves
  the tree. Without the group the three hide checks fail.
- **Evidence.** `pairs/10_shrine_name__*`: from the fast-travel spot (`shrine_ft_*`), beside the giant
  (`shrine_side_*`) and from 40 m (`shrine_far_*`: before, nothing; after, the name over the stone).
- **Left as it is.** The name keeps its depth test, so the giant hides it when the stone is right in front of
  him, as he hides the stone: on the pad facing the stone, and at stages 5 and 6, where the stone is knee-high to
  him and the camera looks over his shoulders (`shrine_side_s6`, `shrine_far_s6`). On the pad the interact
  prompt names the shrine ("[E] Rest at the Kingsgate Shrine"). Drawing the name over everything (no depth
  test) was weighed and not taken: it would read as a name tag on the giant's body and show through hills. At
  stage 6 the fast-travel spot puts the stone under the camera and its name behind the hotbar (`shrine_ft_s6`).

### The saved-game line writes "in the Old Kingdom"

- **Cause.** `TitleScreen.describe_save` put the level's display name, "The Old Kingdom", mid-sentence as it is.
- **Fix.** `TitleScreen.mid_sentence(name)`: a name with a leading "The" takes a small "the", another name gets
  "the" before it: "Saved game: level 7, stage 3, at the Kingsgate Shrine, in the Old Kingdom".
- **Test.** `test_title_checkpoint_names`: its world-save lines are updated to the new wording (a deliberate
  change of what they encoded) and `mid_sentence` is checked.
- **Evidence.** `pairs/11_saved_game_line__title_skip_prologue.png`.

### The map lays its labels out

- **Cause.** The map drew each place's name with its icon, centred under it, and the regions' names at their
  centres, in list order, checking nothing already drawn: names printed over names, icons and region titles,
  whole and zoomed in.
- **Fix.** Every mark is drawn first (icons, the pin, objectives, the giant's arrow), then the words are laid
  out (`MapScreen.layout_labels`) in order of importance - the place in focus, the regions (with their
  recommended stages), the settlements, the sealed places' "Stage N", then the other names - each on the first
  of its candidate spots that covers no mark and no label and lies inside the view: a place's above its icon,
  below, right, left, then the four corners; a region's at its centre, in half-block steps round it, then on a
  grid over its own ground, nearest the centre first. A label with no free spot is left out (the panel names the
  place in focus, and zooming in makes room); the place in focus always shows, kept inside the view. The Valley's
  district names are labels too.
- **Test.** `test_map_screen.test_the_world_map_labels_never_overlap`: the whole world found, at 1280x720 and
  1920x1080, zoom 0 to 2, the focus on every settlement and on the Kingsgate shrine and shelf: no label covers a
  label or a mark or leaves the view, the place in focus shows, and at zoom 0 all five regions and every
  settlement are named. Dropping the labels or the marks from the free-spot check fails it (`mutations/`).
- **Evidence.** `pairs/01_map_labels__*` (whole, zoomed on the Kingsgate and the Greywood, zoom 2).

### The scenery fade's test runs the shader's stages

- **Cause.** `test_scenery_fade` checked the shader's fade rule against `SceneryFade`, but not the lines of
  `vertex()` and `fragment()` that feed it: the world position, the eye, the vertex colours' albedo.
- **Fix (tests only).** `ShaderRule.run_body(stage, env)` runs a stage's own statements with the built-ins the
  test hands it (swizzles and swizzle assignment, `discard`, the conditional operator, statements over several
  lines, the vector constructors, `pow`, `mix`, `lessThan`). `test_scenery_fade.test_the_shader_stages_feed_the_rule`
  runs 200 random model matrices through `vertex()` (the world position), 64 vertex colours (sRGB to linear as
  `Color.srgb_to_linear`, only with `vertex_color_albedo` and without `OUTPUT_IS_SRGB`) and 200 fragments through
  `fragment()` (a discard exactly where `SceneryFade.fade_at` with `INV_VIEW_MATRIX`'s eye and the 4x4 Bayer
  threshold says so, none in the shadow pass, the albedo, roughness and metallic). Mutating any one of four lines
  of the shader fails it (1, 118, 40 and 64 failures).
- **Evidence.** The test's own line: 117 fragments discarded, 83 kept, 40 shadow-pass; worst colour error
  2.4e-7, worst position error 0.

### Left, and why

- **Far people and giants as plain posts** (the distance LOD's one-box proxy, P22). Each actor scene's
  `LodProxy` box is built by its builder (soldier, wolf, npcs, big, formations, heavy, ranged, siege) and copied
  by the rig as its far proxy. A figure-shaped proxy means rebuilding every actor scene, then measuring the far
  LOD's draw calls in the slow and gate tiers again and looking at every family at 45 to 90 m. Not cheap, and
  the owner finds the graphics fine.
- **Faint seams in the distant haze** where the loaded hills meet the skyline backdrop. A fix is a matter of
  the skyline's and the fog's tuning at the streaming edge (`scripts/world/skyline.gd`, the render presets),
  to be judged by eye at many viewpoints and every quality preset, the web's included. Not cheap either, for a
  seam the pass calls faint.
