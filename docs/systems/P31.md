### Schemas and GameData (anchors P31)

- `action_schema.gd`: `evolves_from` (ref), `granted_by` ("" | talent | ancestry), `radius_scale`,
  `line` {count, length_m, start_m, interval_s, radius_m, height_m} for hit shape `line_pulse`,
  `fury_on_cast`, `heal_over_time` {status, max_health_fraction}, `clears_status_tags`,
  `on_hit_stagger` {seconds, archetypes}; `projectile.launch_from` ("actor" | "above_target") and
  `launch_height_m`; `telegraph.at` ("actor" | "target"). Checks: a line pulse needs `line` (start
  not beyond length) and only it may have one; nothing evolves from itself; `radius_scale` only with
  `radius_from: "melee_reach"`; a granted ability is not an evolution.
- `weapon_schema.gd`: visual shapes `axe`, `pine`; `upgrade.forge_tier` (iron | titan); `perk`
  {kind (formation_poise | pierce | heavy_shockwave | flat_penetration), text, poise_mult, actions,
  range_m, radius_m, power_mult, flat_penetration} with the fields each kind needs.
- `game_data.gd` post-pass: `Progression.evolution_errors` (the base exists, is learnable and of the
  same kind; the evolution's stage is above its base's; chains end; an evolution in the library has
  its chain's root in the library) and `Progression.stage_moveset_errors` (a weapon that scales, fits
  stage 4 or more and can be carried below stage 4 names a stage moveset from stage 4 at the latest,
  whose actions exist). `Progression.data_errors` lifts the one-step rule: `refit_chain_errors`
  (chains never loop and never need an iron forge after a titan one; each step fits at least as high,
  as before).

### Evolutions (`scripts/progression/progression.gd`, `scripts/actors/player/player_progression.gd`)

`Progression`: `base_of(id)`, `root_of(id)` (the chain's first ability: `preferred_slot` follows it,
so Earthquake's slot is Footfall's), `granted_by(id)`, `evolutions_known(base, known)`,
`evolutions_met(lib, known, level, stage, extra = [])` (sorted by stage); `newly_met` skips granted
abilities (P33 grants them).

`PlayerProgression`: an evolution is learned on reaching its `unlock_stage` at or above its
`unlock_level` - by a level-up, a growth, a growth profile's unlocks or a reward - and takes its
base's slot and its place in `known_abilities` (the base stays known and listed); it emits
`ability_learned` (that slot) and `Events.ability_evolved(tick, base, evolution, slot)`. A gated
giant's growth learns them in `on_profile_changed` (before PlayerGrowth's unlocks, which then find
them known) and announces them on `growth_changed`; a restore announces nothing. An evolution taught
another way - an ungated debug growth's unlocks (they go into the first empty slot), or a reward
before its stage (then it is only known and the base keeps its slot) - moves into its base's slot at
the next growth or level that allows it (`_evolve_known`). `evolved_into(id)` (a known evolution the
stage allows); `slot_options` leaves an evolved base out and `set_slot` refuses it with reason
`evolved`. Cooldowns stay per id.

### Refit chains and forge tiers (`scripts/world/forge.gd`)

`Forge.tier` ("iron" by default: the Valley's two, the Kingsgate anvil, Brannock's; "titan": the Titan
Forge, Fort Aldmere's, Skarn's hearth), `does_tier(step_tier)`, reason `forge_tier` (after
`unknown_upgrade`, before `missing_items`) with `TIER_MESSAGE`; `offer()` carries `forge_tier` and the
new weapon's `perk` text. The forge screen shows the next step and the refusal through its existing
status line.

### Combat (`scripts/actors/attack_runner.gd`, anchor P31 and its section)

- **`line_pulse`** (Earthquake): from the first active tick, every `line.interval_s` (rounded to
  ticks) one cylinder pulse at the next point of the line from `start_m` to `length_m` along the
  facing the strike began with; pulse id i + 1; line of sight from the crack; `area_pulse` per pulse.
  `AttackRunner.line_pulse_points(a, origin, yaw)`, `line_pulse_interval(a)`.
- **`radius_scale`** multiplies the melee reach of a `radius_from: "melee_reach"` pulse.
- **Titanforged perks** on the weapon arc (`p31_perk_for(a)`, `p31_apply_perk(perk, event, target)`):
  the club's Heavy blows deal x1.25 poise to formation members (`EnemyActor.formation_id`); the
  axe's blows +10 flat penetration; the sword's listed light 3s run on into the nearest foe standing in
  line behind the one they struck (within range_m beyond it, not struck by the swing; its event
  carries pulse id 2 and it is registered as the swing's own); the maul's heavy ends in a shockwave
  (`p31_shockwave_radius(perk)`) under the head as it leaves ACTIVE (pulse id 3, 0.5 x power, the
  blow's own targets spared). Perk distances are stage-1 metres and grow with the weapon's scale.
- **Cast effects** as an ability enters ACTIVE: `fury_on_cast` (Wild Surge), `heal_over_time` (the
  status's heal_per_tick sized so its 12 heals add up to 20 % of the maximum health), and
  `clears_status_tags` (the caster's slows end, with `status_expired`).

### The giant (`player_attack.gd`, `player_motor.gd`)

- **Storm Call's strike from above**: a projectile with `launch_from: "above_target"` starts
  `launch_height_m` above the locked target's anchor (else the point under the crosshair within the
  lock range) and falls on it; it is never refused as out of range.
- **The giant's own telegraphs**: an ability with a telegraph announces it as its windup begins
  (`Events.telegraph_started` from the giant, with the lock target); a telegraph `at: "target"` starts
  on the target.
- **Shoulder Charge**: PlayerMotor runs the scripted `motion` of an action whose hit is a
  `body_sweep` (the grab family's motion stays PlayerGrab's lunge): during `motion.phase` the giant
  moves along its facing at `speed_mps` (times its slows) for at most `max_distance_m`, passes
  through other actors (its actor collision is off while it dashes) and stops against a wall, an
  untrampled prop, a blocker or a size gate with `stop_on_wall` (`motion_end_reason`,
  `motion_travelled`, `is_dashing()`). PlayerAttack's `body_sweep` sweeps the giant's capsule (+0.4 m)
  from where it stood the tick before; each hit comes from behind the target on the charge line's
  side (`charge_shove_origin`), so it is thrown forward and aside.
- **Thunderhead**: `on_hit_stagger` staggers each target of the listed archetypes it struck and did
  not kill (`CombatEntity.stagger(1.5, "stunned")`; soldier, archer, mage - P30's pikemen, shieldmen
  and bannermen are soldiers).
- **Statuses**: walking and running speed x the product of the statuses' `move_speed_mult`
  (`PlayerMotor.status_speed_mult()`); no knockback while a status tagged Barrier is on the giant
  (`has_barrier()`: Bulwark).

## Decisions and deviations

- **The reinforced and titanforged sword and maul are longer**: the Kingsguard Blade 1.6 -> 1.9 m
  (hit radius 0.3 -> 0.36), the Warmaul 1.5 -> 1.8 m (0.5 -> 0.55), so that their stage 4–6 movesets
  land at 0.9 x the melee reach (above). At stage 3 the blade is 4.1 m (was 3.5) and the maul 3.9 m
  (was 3.25). `test_weapons` read the old length as a literal and now reads the record.
- **Hook lines outside the anchors** (one-line calls into the P31 sections): `attack_runner.gd`
  (`pulse_radius`: `radius_scale`), `player_attack.gd` (`setup`, `on_action_started`,
  `resolve_launch`, `range_reason`), `player_motor.gd` (`update_movement`, `apply_knockback`),
  `projectile_system.gd` (`_move_visual`, `_make_visual`), `telegraph_visuals.gd` (`_ends`,
  `_draw_record`), `weapon_visuals.gd` (`build`, `shape_of`, `idle_pose`, `material`).
- **Bulwark takes x0.4 from every direction** (§5.12: frontal hits): `damage_taken_mult` has no
  direction; a frontal rule needs `DamageResolver` (P33's passives work, below).
- **Status keys the Combatant does not apply yet**: `poise_damage_taken_mult` (Stonewall's poise
  immunity) and `poise_threshold_add` (Bedrock Ward's +50) are in the records and wait for P33's
  passives; `move_speed_mult` is applied by PlayerMotor (product of the statuses).
- **Status files and ids**: `stonewall.json` and `regrowth.json` (the plan's names) hold
  `status.stonewall_stance` and `status.primal_regrowth`, the ids P28's expiry cues
  (`sfx.status.<short id>.off`) already use.
- **Shoulder Charge runs at 26 m/s** (§5.12 gives only the 12 m): 12 m in 28 of the 30 active ticks, so
  the dash ends by distance; the giant passes through actors while it dashes.
- **Perk distances grow with the weapon** (the pierce's 1.5 m and the shockwave's 3 m are stage-1
  metres: x3 at stage 4, x5.17 at stage 6); at a literal 3 m the shockwave would end inside the
  stage-6 maul's own 2.8 m head.
- **Storm Call sets Burning on everything it strikes** (§5.12 "sets Burning on trolls": only P34's
  trolls react to it).
- **The library has 23 actives and the ultimate**: Rampart Grip (the 24th) is P32's record; the
  library test counts 24 once `ability.rampart_grip` exists and asks for it in the library.
- **Titan Roar's dialogue lines**: no dialogue reads a known ability yet (`QuestConditions` has no such
  condition); the dialogues' authors add one when they write the lines.
