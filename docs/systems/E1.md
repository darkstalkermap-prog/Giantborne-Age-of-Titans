# E1 — Harrok's return: Orrik the Fetterer and the challenge for the Stone Throne

Milestone E's first package, on `pkg/e1` from the milestone D gate's merge (`4419b8b`). The version stays
**0.17.0-m17** (prepared, not shipped). The sixth signature titan of the expansion's story ("Harrok returns - an ally
if spared, his clan come for the throne if slain"): the lead D2 left (`quest.harrok_lead`: look in at the Moot) now pays
off. Decisions E1-1..E1-12 are in `docs/DECISIONS.md` §2.7; the duels are BALANCE_LOG "E1"; what is left is FOLLOWUPS
"From E1".

## 1. The story

After the Stormcaller's fall Maera tells the pilgrims' rumour: Harrok's banner is on the road. At the Moot the giant
finds what it meant. Harrok's sister-son **Orrik the Fetterer**, the Skarn's war-leader, has grown at the Stones to a
colossus's size and stands before the Stone Throne; the Skarn have staked their **fetter-winches** inside the Moot's
ring. Act one decides the rest (decision E1-5):

- **Harrok slain** - Orrik comes to avenge him and take the throne back for the clan. The giant fights alone.
- **Harrok spared** (or knelt to the talk ending) - Orrik rejects his uncle's peace with the giant on the throne.
  Harrok comes in by the ring's gate and **fights beside the giant** (§3).
- A save that never settled Harrok (not reachable in play: the throne asks for a settled champion) takes the vengeance.

Both branches fight the same Orrik, the same winches, in the same ring; they differ in who stands with whom.

## 2. How the fight plays

**The arena** is the Moot's own ring (120 m across, its 9 m wall knee-high to a colossus, the Stone Throne at its north
edge). Five fetter-winches stand staked inside it, each served by a 10 m Skarn giant. Stepping into the ring engages
the fight (`ThroneChallenge`, a BossArena: the boss bar, the music, no checkpoint and no fast travel while engaged).

**The mechanic: the fetters** (decision E1-2; `PlayerFetter`). A winch draws its great bow behind an aim line that
follows the giant's legs for 70 % of a 2.2 s draw, then freezes: a step aside after it dodges the harpoon. Where the
harpoon bites, its chain holds the giant to a circle round its winch:

| Chains | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 72 % of his speed; Orrik's Throne-breaker comes for him |
| 2 | 45 %; his legs are bound - no dodge, jump or sprint |
| 3 | pinned (6 %) |

**The answer is his size.** Pulling against a taut chain strains it (by stage: stage 9 tears it in about 1.2 s, stage 8
in 1.7 s, stage 7 in 3.3 s, stage 6 in over 8 s); a **heavy begun while chained is a heave** that tears the tautest chain
at once from stage 8 (half of one at stage 7). A torn chain wrenches its winch out of the ground (it breaks). A stage-8
giant **strides through a winch** (it is trampled); a blow or two breaks it at any stage. The HUD's **chain meter** (in
the water meter's place) says how many chains hold him and how to tear them; "PINNED" in its warning colour.

**Orrik** (decision E1-3; `HeirEnemy`, 31.5 m, health 10,500, armour 70, a 13 m bearded axe; his blows scale by his size against the
giant's: x1.76 on a stage-7 giant, x1.16 on stage 8, x0.79 on stage 9):

- **Phase 1** (two winches): the axe's **sweep** and **back-stroke** (cone warnings), the **cleaving chop** (a 40 m
  line on the ground; a shock line: one hit a body), the **shoulder charge** (a 70 m line), the **stamp** at a giant
  hugging his legs.
- **The Throne-breaker**: for a giant with a chain on him, he walks in and raises the axe for 2.6 s (its line on the
  ground) - tear free and step out of the line. It is not swung at a giant who tore free before it began.
- **Phase 2** (62 % or two plates): his **war-horn** - three more winches join - and his **chain-hook**: thrown at a
  giant 18-80 m off behind an aim line; it bites and **the haul** drags the giant toward him (the chain shortens 16 m).
  Torn from his fist (a pull or a heave) it **yanks him off his feet** (3 s): the opening.
- **Phase 3** (28 %): his rage; the warband's **fire-pots** fall in pairs round the giant (red circles 1.8 s before).
- **His plates are his armour** and come off his look as they break: the horned **helm**, the bone **spaulders**
  (both broken, the trophies fall), the **chain-coil** on his left forearm (broken: no more hook), the **greaves**
  (broken: unbalanced, poise x1.5).

## 3. Harrok beside the giant (decision E1-4; `HarrokAlly`)

Harrok (15 m, act one's body in his look, his axe) comes in by the gate as the fight engages. He is not a combat entity
(no enemy aims at him) and his blows land through Sim's hits as the settlements' garrisons' do (no XP, no loot). Every
half second he picks, in order: a winch's chain on the giant (he runs to its drum and hews it through: the chain goes),
a winch in the fight (he cuts its crew down, then the winch), else Orrik's legs (a blow every 2.4 s: damage and poise).
He keeps clear of the giant's feet and finds his way round the ring's stones on the navmesh. He speaks as he comes, as
he cuts and when Orrik falls. A new attempt sends him away; he comes again with the next.

## 4. Preparation and aftermath (decision E1-8)

- **The winch camp** on the old battery lines below the Moot: two fetter-winches and their crews (they shoot whenever a
  giant is in reach). Break both (`quest.winch_camp`, 200 XP) and the Moot's two **reserve winches** - and their crews -
  are never there (`world.moot.camp_broken`).
- **Skarn** saved or rebuilt in act one stands with the giant: the ring's **west winch** is not staked.
- **After Orrik's fall**: his banners by the throne are struck, the warband breaks (gone or fleeing), the clan stands
  down (`harrok_clan` reconciled). With Harrok slain, the **clan's eldest** at the oath-stone beside the throne takes the
  clan's oath; the **sworn clans** stand at the Moot's stones. With Harrok spared, **Harrok holds the Moot**: his green
  banner over the throne, him beside it; he gives his **oath-ring**. Both tell the herders' rumour from the east - a road
  burned black toward the kingdom (`quest.cinder_lead`: a lead only, nothing of the Cinder Titan is built).

## 9. What is weak

Judged from the stills and the film (the hand-back's folder), the duels and the tests:

- **Orrik's swings are the giant's posed arms, not authored strikes.** His body is the player's (the SkinnedRig's clips
  and its pose-brain arms): the Throne-breaker's windup holds the axe out at shoulder height, not raised over his head,
  and his sweeps read small for their reach. He has **no down**: the giant's clip library has no kneel (a torn hook's
  yank is a stagger). FOLLOWUPS.
- **His look is the wardrobe's.** The chalk face paint is blotchy close up, the kilt and greaves near black, the bone
  spaulders read as tan cloth; there is no modelled trophy or chain on him. The axe's head is the weapon kit's at five
  times its size: an untextured pale blade.
- **The winches and the chains are box-built** (as P32's engines): the links are boxes in a MultiMesh; a harpoon in
  flight trails no chain.
- **The curve is the test arena's.** In the real ring the one stage-8 duel alone died with Orrik at 21.7 %; the ally
  branch is easier by design (Harrok cuts chains and staggers him). The Throne-breaker is seldom swung above stage 7:
  the bot tears chains in about a second.
- **Harrok is a help, not a companion at risk**: he cannot be struck and has no fall (C4-5's team rule); after the
  fight the ally stays where he fought until the scene reloads, while Harrok "holds the Moot" by the throne as a
  separate, standing figure.
- **Software rendering only, no human playtest**: llvmpipe at `low`; the chain meter, the aim lines and the heave were
  judged by bots, tests and stills.
