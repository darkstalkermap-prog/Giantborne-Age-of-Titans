# P05 — Growth stages 1–3, shrines, clearance and the 3-size route

Wave 2, lane A (beside P06). Branch `pkg/P05`. Plan: `docs/plans/M2_M5_PLAN.md` §3.5, with the
contracts in §2.2 (nav layers, render layer 2), §2.4 (growth and shrine events), §2.7.1
(PlayerGrowth), §2.7.4 (Shrine), §2.8.2 (growth_profile and player fields), §2.8.3 (the shrine

§4.8 (profile values), and decisions 4, 5, 12, 13, 22 and 29.

## What was built

- **Three growth stages** (spec §9). `growth.01..03` carry the §4.8 values: weapon scale, grab
  reach, lunge, capacity and attachment profiles, resource bonus, arc pitch offsets, dodge style,
  camera orbit scale, navigation layers and footstep shake.
- **PlayerGrowth** (`scripts/actors/player/player_growth.gd`, the `growth` component of
  `GiantPlayer.COMPONENTS`):
  - It checks a growth first; a failed check changes nothing and reports why.
  - A passing growth is applied in one tick.
  - It handles the F6 / F7 debug grow and shrink keys and the `growth_stage` save key.
  - It shakes the camera on each footfall at stage 2 and above.
- **Growth changes everything together.** In one tick it sets:
  - the stage and the controller's unlock gating;
  - the capsule and floor snap;
  - height, resource maxima (fractions kept) and the navigation layers;
  - the weapon geometry (× `current_weapon_scale()`), lock range and half-angle;
  - the camera (zoom fraction kept, the arm never snapped);
  - the rig's body scale, and hurtboxes and anchors rebuilt from their stage-1 sizes × k (never
    by node scale).

  It then learns the profile's unlocks and emits `growth_changed`.
- **Shrines.** `Shrine` (`scripts/world/shrine.gd`) and the `shrine` record type
  (`shrine_schema.gd`), with five records (hollow, grove, thornwick, kingsfall, sandbox). A shrine:
  - activates on the first approach within 14 m per load: a free Rest, then `checkpoint_reached`
    and `shrine_activated`;
  - offers Rest;
  - gates growth by its record (`allows_growth`, `max_stage`, `growth_requires_flags`);
  - opens the shrine screen on E.
- **Shrine screen** (`ui/screens/shrine_screen.gd`). A pausing UiScreen with the pre-declared
  `TABS`:
  - `Rest` and `Grow` (P05), on the new `ShrineTab` base class;
  - `Loadout` and `Attributes` are P12's files.

  The Grow tab lists the offered stage and every requirement, with a Grow button. After a growth
  it summarises the changes.
- **Camera.**
  - `CameraRig.apply_profile()` keeps the zoom fraction.
  - The lock orbit scales with `camera_orbit_scale`.
  - In third person the camera hides render layer 2 (head, chest, belt) when the arm is pressed
    short (see decision 2 below).
- **Rig.**
  - `ActorRig.set_body_scale(k)` scales `Visual/Body`; the pivots are divided by k.
  - The arc pitch offset is shown exactly during ACTIVE, as the hit sweep uses it.
  - A sidestep dodge pose for stages 2–3.
  - A `footfall` signal.
- **Scenes** (through the builders):
  - `scenes/world/shrine.tscn`;
  - `scenes/test/growth_course.tscn` (stage-3-sized doorway, low passage, ramp and platform, wall
    lane, growth-trap alcove, a shrine);
  - the sandbox shrine (`sandbox.shrine`, record `shrine.sandbox`) at (−40, 0, 40).
- **The 3-size route** (slow suite) with a crosshair probe (`tests/support/crosshair_probe.gd`).

## Public APIs

### PlayerGrowth (`class_name PlayerGrowth extends PlayerComponent`; `player.growth`)

```gdscript
const REASONS := ["no_profile", "already", "shrine_max", "flags", "level", "busy", "holding",

	# check_growth, then the one-tick apply; + "from", "to", "summary" on success;
	# a refusal emits growth_blocked and a HUD warning
func debug_set_stage(target: int) -> Dictionary  # F6/F7: busy, holding and clearance only
func apply_state(state) / capture_state(out)     # key "growth_stage"; a restore is silent
static func body_scale_for(profile) -> float     # k = height / 3.6576
static func resource_bonus(profile) -> Dictionary

static func summary_lines(summary) -> PackedStringArray
static func result_ok() / result_fail(reason, message := "") / default_message(reason)
```

**Reasons and messages.**

| Reason | When | Message (default) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `no_profile` | no profile for the target stage | "You cannot grow any further." |
| `already` | the target is the current stage | "You are already stage N." |
| `shrine_max` | no shrine, a shrine that cannot grow, or beyond its `max_stage` | "Only a shrine can make you grow." / "This shrine cannot make you grow." / "This shrine can only make you grow to stage N." |
| `flags` | a `growth_requires_flags` flag is not set | the record's `growth_locked_message` |
| `level` | `controller.level` below the profile's `required_level` (99 = off) | "You must reach level N to grow to stage M." |
| `busy` | not LOCOMOTION, or not on the floor | "Stand still on solid ground before you grow." |
| `holding` | `controller.holding_id` set | "Put down what you are holding before you grow." |
| `combat` | `pools.in_combat()` | "Enemies are near. You cannot grow while fighting." |
| `clearance` | the new capsule overlaps, or something is above the head | "Not enough room to grow here. Step into the open." |

**Clearance.**
- A capsule of the new radius and height, lifted 0.05 m off the floor at the giant's position,
  must not overlap WORLD | BREAKABLE | BLOCKER (the giant's own body excluded).
- An upward ray from the current head to the new height must be clear.
- Actors are not tested: growing beside a soldier pushes it aside.

**Applying a growth** goes through `GiantPlayer.set_profile()`, so a save restore and a test's
`set_profile` get the same body:
1. `set_profile` sets the profile, the stage, `controller.set_growth_stage` and `nav_layers`.
2. It applies the capsule and floor snap, then `camera_rig.apply_profile`.
3. It calls every component's `on_profile_changed`:
   - PlayerAttack re-reads the geometry;
   - PlayerTargeting re-reads the lock range;
   - PlayerGrowth sets the height, `growth_bonus = resource_bonus` + `recompute_derived()`, the
     rig's body scale, and the hurtboxes and anchors.
4. It places the rig's shoulder.

`grow_to` and `debug_set_stage` then add:
- **Unlocks:** learned, `ensure_action`, slotted into the first empty slot, `ability_learned`, and
  `loadout_changed` when slotted.
- **Events:** `growth_changed` and a HUD notice.

The debug path shows "DEBUG: stage N" and prints a log line.

### Shrine (`class_name Shrine extends Interactable`; group `shrines`)

```gdscript
@export var world_id := ""                 # = checkpoint id, e.g. "valley.shrine.grove"
@export var shrine_id := "shrine.sandbox"  # the shrine record
const HOOK_ORDER := 50                     # Sim POST_RESOLVE (GameSession drains at 200)
func record() -> Dictionary; func display_name() -> String; func activation_radius_m() -> float
func is_activated() -> bool; func growth_pad_position() -> Vector3
func activate(player) -> bool              # first call per load only
func rest(player) -> Dictionary            # {ok, message, healed}
func can_grow(player, target) -> Dictionary; func grow(player, target) -> Dictionary
func offered_stage(player) -> int          # stage + 1 when this shrine offers it, else 0
```

**Activation.** A POST_RESOLVE hook polls the horizontal distance to the living player. The first
time it is within `activation_radius_m`:
1. a free Rest (records with `first_activation_rest`);
2. `checkpoint_reached(tick, world_id, "shrine")` (records with `checkpoint`);
3. `shrine_activated(tick, world_id)`.

The rune lights up. A scene reload makes a new shrine, so it activates again once.

**Rest.** Health, stamina and essence go to their maxima (records with `rest_heals`), and every
`Debuff` status is removed (each with `status_expired`, so tints refresh). Fury, buffs and
cooldowns are kept. It then emits `shrine_rested(tick, world_id)`. The free Rest does not emit
`shrine_rested`.

**Interact (E).** The prompt is "Rest at the <name>". `interact` activates the shrine if needed,
then emits `ui_screen_requested("shrine", {"shrine": self, "world_id": …, "shrine_id": …})`.

**Children.**
- `SpawnPoint`: a CheckpointMarker; `_ready` sets its `checkpoint_id` to `world_id`.
- `GrowthPad`: a Marker3D 6.5 m in front of the stone, with stage-3 clearance.
- `Stone`: StaticBody3D on WORLD.
- `Visual/{Dais, DaisMoss, Cap, Rune, Glow, Name}`: visual only.

The shrine faces −Z.

### CrosshairProbe (`tests/support/crosshair_probe.gd`, for P07 and P08)


breakables or actors, the roots' bodies excluded) within 40 m.

## Decisions and deviations

1. **Doc name.** This file is `docs/systems/P05.md`, as the task and plan §1.1 item 5 say. §3.5's
   owned-paths list names `P05-growth-shrines.md`.
2. **The camera also hides the head when the arm is pressed close behind it.**
   - **Plan rule.** Layer 2 is hidden only while the third-person arm is shorter than 1.2 × the
     capsule radius.
   - **Why that fails.** Under the growth course's 8.6 m ceiling a stage-3 arm is cut to 3.26 m.
     The camera is then right behind the head but outside that rule (1.98 m). The probe found the
     back of the head covering the crosshair for 113 consecutive ticks.
   - **Added rule.** The camera always looks along the arm's backward axis. Its centre ray
     therefore passes the pivot's height at a sideways distance of
     `shoulder_offset × arm / full_arm`, and the pivot sits in the head. The head is now also
     hidden while that distance is under 0.5 × the capsule radius (`CameraRig.hides_head`).
   - **Effect.** This covers every case the 1.2 × rule covers. A free arm (and the doorways and
     the wall lane) still shows the head.
3. **Arc pitch offsets.**
   - **The plan's test passes as specified.** With the plan's −6 / −12, every light and the heavy
     reach a 1.8 m soldier and a 0.9 m wolf at 0.6 × melee reach straight ahead, at stages 2 and
     3, in both cameras.
   - **The wave note asks for more.** It asks to keep the M2 gate result: the light chain reaches
     a wolf at the giant's feet, where a lunge stops (a 0.1 m capsule gap: 1.70 m from a stage-2
     centre, 2.15 m from a stage-3 one).
   - **What fails there.** Light 1 missed at every bearing at both stages, so "lunge, then punish"
     whiffed its first blow.
   - **Stage 2:** −8 fixes it (within the plan's −10).
   - **Stage 3:** no single tilt works.
     - Light 1 needs −14 or deeper.
     - Light 3, an overhead chop from the right shoulder, ends further right and shorter the
       deeper it ends. From −13 on it misses even the plan's straight-ahead 3.9 m target (at −12
       its capsule just touches).
     - So growth profiles gain `arc_pitch_offsets_deg` (per-action exceptions): stage 3 keeps −12
       and light 1 uses −16 (within −18).
   - **Coverage now** (bearings −15 / 0 / +15; wolf and soldier):
     - Lights 1 and 2 and the heavy land at the feet and at 0.6 × reach at every bearing, at
       stages 2 and 3.
     - Light 3 lands at 0.6 × reach straight ahead and to the right at both stages, and at the
       feet straight ahead at stage 2. It lands only to the right at a stage-3 giant's feet: its
       arc ends 10° left of the right shoulder, 1.8 m right of the giant's centre.
     - The real lunge-and-punish chain lands lights 1 and 2 inside the wolf's recovery, and
       light 3 connects, at both stages.
     - Stage 1 is unchanged (no tilt).
   - **The rig** shows the same tilt as the sweep: the arm is within 1° of the swept segment
     (tested).
4. **Extra reason `already`**, for a growth to the current stage; shrines only grow. The debug
   keys use it too.
5. **`busy` also covers being airborne** (not `is_on_floor()`), so a growth never starts in the
   air.
6. **Shrine events carry the world id** (= the checkpoint id and the SpawnPoint's id):
   `checkpoint_reached`, `shrine_activated` and `shrine_rested`. The screen request's args add
   `world_id` and `shrine_id` beside `shrine`.
7. **Rest interpretation.** "Heals all pools and refills Mend-like states" means:
   - health, stamina and essence to their maxima;
   - debuffs (status tag `Debuff`, e.g. a slow) removed, with `status_expired` so tints refresh;
   - fury, buffs and cooldowns kept.

   Rest is not gated by combat: shrines are placed away from encounters (§4.3). The free Rest of
   the first activation emits no `shrine_rested`.
8. **Activation uses a Sim POST_RESOLVE hook** (order 50), so it happens inside the deterministic
   tick, before GameSession's checkpoint drain (order 200). It requires a living player.
9. **A save restore is silent.** `apply_state` sets the stage through `set_profile` and emits no
   `growth_changed`, unlocks or notices, so a load never triggers a checkpoint (P06 listens to
   `growth_changed`). The saved resources are applied after it (GiantPlayer's order).
10. **Profile swaps fit the body.** `PlayerGrowth.on_profile_changed` re-fits the body for any
    profile swap. Existing tests that call `set_profile(growth.02)` directly now get a fully sized
    stage-2 giant. They all pass unchanged.
11. **The player's mass class** is "large" by code default (the schema default). Adding it to
    `giant.json` belongs to a later editor of that file (P07).
12. **`growth_locked_message`** is an optional shrine field (the player-facing text for `flags`).
    **`ShrineTab`** is a new base class in `ui/screens/shrine/shrine_tab.gd`.
13. **The growth course** runs along −Z. Its features:
    - a doorway in a 14 m wall;
    - a passage whose walls carry the 8.6 m roof;
    - 10 m wide ramps up and down a 4 m platform;
    - a 12 m lane wall whose face is 1 m beside the waypoints;
    - the trap alcove, 8 × 8 m with a 6 m ceiling, open toward the start. A stage-2 giant fits in
      it, and stage 3 does not.

    `Route/WP01..11` are the route's waypoints.
14. **Route steering.** The route test looks along each path segment and steers toward the
    waypoint with the stick. Aiming the camera straight at a waypoint that a stage-3 giant cannot
    reach (the wall lane's, 1 m from the wall) would swing the arm away from the wall, and the
    lane would no longer force a retraction.
15. **Existing test counts changed by design.**
    - `test_player_components` 102 → 101: the `growth_stage` passthrough check is skipped once
      the growth component exists.
    - `test_schemas_v2` 307 → 306: shrine records validate now.
    - `test_scene_kit` 83 → 85 and `test_anchors` 235 → 250: they iterate over the new builders
      and scripts.

    No existing test was edited.
