beside P62. Built on 21 November 2026 on `pkg/P63` from `d4f62b6`, on the shared container (4 CPUs, no GPU: llvmpipe),

**Delivered**: the ogre and the troll (the whelp the troll's variant), the Ogre King's and the Troll Matriarch's gear,

## 1. The brutes (`tools/giant/kit/brute.py`)

A brute is the people's human (`people_body.build_person`'s path: the giant's CC0 MakeHuman data and code) with
MakeHuman's macros at a brute's values (the ogre muscle 1.0 and weight 1.0; the troll 1.0 and 0.9; the whelp a youth of
18 at 0.95 and 0.75) and the giant's own face modifiers scaled up (the ogre with its own nose, jaw and brow), reshaped
twice before the joints are read (so the 53-bone rig fits the new body):

1. **`giant_body.proportioned` with a brute's table** (`OGRE_PROP`, `TROLL_PROP`, `WHELP_PROP`): shorter legs, a longer
   torso, the head sunk between the shoulders and smaller, the arms and hands set by the reach bands, wider shoulders,
   the trapezius raised;
2. **`brute.reshape`**, the project's vertex functions on named regions: the gut forward (the ogre), **the hunch** (the
   upper back bent forward about the mid-back by 24° (ogre) or 32° (troll), the arms riding with their shoulders), the
   head turned back up about the neck so the face looks ahead, a heavier and wider jaw (the ogre's underbite), a
   jutting brow ridge, the troll's long hooked nose and pointed ears, thicker hands and feet.

Each kind is a "role" in the people's sense: one shared mesh (the first variant's body with the giant's garment shells -
the ogre a loincloth, a belt and bracers; the troll a loincloth -, culled, merged, decimated to about 8,800 triangles,
unwrapped into one atlas), its variants moved onto their own bodies through the hm08 topology they share (the whelp is
the troll's mesh on the youth's body), then each variant's own pieces (the eyes; the ogre's tusks). The atlas is
`people_bake.bake_role`'s (the five palette bands), then `brute.repaint` redraws the skin band: the ogre's hide (large
blotches, a darker back, a paler belly, warts and creases in the normal map), the troll's grey skin with moss (a
darker, greener tint of the skin's own band over the head's top, the shoulders and the back); the ogre's metal tile is
gold and its fur tile ivory (the tusks), the troll's fur tile bone.

**The bosses' gear** is skinned to the first variant's skeleton as a separate mesh, shown with the body: the Ogre King
(the ogre at 8.5 m: x 2.5) the hide mantle in the faction's band (his red), the plated war belt in gold and a gold crown
of six points; the Troll Matriarch (the troll at 9 m: x 1.636) the hide mantle (leather) and a bone crown of nine
points. **Far meshes**: each variant in the idle's first key, decimated to 300 triangles.

**The clips** (`<kind>_clips.glb`, 30 fps, on the kind's own 23-bone skeleton: `clips.py`'s generator with the kind's
proportions and ActorRig's stride, 1.5 m at 3.4 m): `idle` (60), `walk` and `run` (32; `BRUTE_GAITS`: a longer stance,
the knees bent, the trunk pitched, the arms hanging 20° out from the bulk; no slide, the stride the rig's), `slam` (36:
the stance widens, both fists over the head, down together to the ground in front with the knees deep), `roar` (24),
`brace` (1), `death` (30, onto the back), `grip_r`, `grip_l` (the hand only). The whelp is posed by the troll's clips
(its bones take the troll's rest orientations at its own joints, as the people's variants do).

### The reach bands (plan §3.16 tests: "within 5 %")

The box rigs' arm is a sphere of radius `arm_length_m` about `weapon_pivot_m` (`data/enemies/*.json`, as
`tools/builders/heavy.gd` and `big.gd` build them); the realistic shoulder sits nearer the body and higher than the box
pivot. The band is met when **every grip the box arm reaches lies within the brute's own reach** (upper arm, forearm and
wrist to the knuckles, from its own shoulder) **and that reach is no longer than needed by more than 5 %**: reach / (|pivot
- shoulder| + arm) within 1 ± 0.05. `brute.reach_bands` records it, the build fails outside it, and `TestBrutes`
recomputes it from the glTF's skeleton and checks the box numbers equal the records.

## 2. The wolf (`kit/quadruped.py`: `WOLF`, `wolf_sdf`, `build_wolf`)

The quadruped kit on a grey wolf of 0.80 m at the withers (the box wolf's 0.9 m with its ears), 1.46 m nose to the
tail's tip: a signed distance field of the wolf's own parts (a deep, narrow rib cage, the brisket, a tucked waist, the
croup, the shoulder blades and thighs, a neck thick with the ruff, the head from a broad cranium, the furred cheeks, the
stop and a long muzzle with the nose pad and the lower jaw, the mouth's line cut, tall triangular ears with their hollow,
digitigrade legs on the same 25 bones - the foreleg's upper arm, forearm, metacarpus and toes, the hind's thigh, gaskin,
metatarsus and toes -, oval paws of four toes, a bushy tail) polygonized on a 5.5 mm grid, decimated to 4,500 triangles
(closed and manifold; slivers and fins the collapse leaves are removed: `drop_slivers`). The joints and the weights are
the horse's code generalised (`joints`, `weights`: the spine's joints, the fore/hind split, the jaw's reach and the
falloffs by the animal's scale).

- **Fur**: 196 triangles of cards in the strands' tile (alpha), in the coat's band: the ruff in three rings round the
  neck lying back, the cheeks' tufts, the breeches behind the thighs, a fringe under the belly, and the tail's brush as
  four crossed cards. **The shell**: `wolf_shell` (the body decimated to 1,800 triangles with its UVs, weighted as the
  body) and `wolf_fur.png` (256 px: RG the coat's flow in the tangent frame, B the fur's length over 4.5 cm: long on the
  ruff, the back, the thighs and the tail, short on the face and the legs, none on the pads), for P64's shell shader
  (12 layers on the desktop, 6 at `low`: probe 6). No frame here draws the shell.
- **The coat**: agouti grey (the skin band: the palette's coat colour), pale throat, chest, belly, legs' insides and
  cheeks, a darker saddle over the back and the shoulders (a tint of the same band, so no stair-step), the hair band for
  the tail's tip and top and the ears' backs, black pads, nose and lips' line; the eyes almond with lids (the horse's
  eye piece generalised) in the **cloth band: the palette's eye colour** (amber for the wolf, ember for the dire wolf);
  the normal map the dense field plus streaks along the coat's flow.
- **Sizes and palettes** (`index.wolf.sizes`, `palettes`): the wolf 1.0 (`#8e8476` coat, `#3a3430` marks, amber eyes),
  the dire wolf 2.0 (`#5a5550`, `#181514`, `#f06a2c`) - big.gd's dire wolf is the wolf's plan x 2.
- **Poses** (`wolf_poses.glb`, a check as the horse's): stand, the trot's two diagonals, the gallop extended (the back
  extended) and gathered (flexed), the lunge (the forelegs reaching, the jaw open) and `jaw_open`; volumes 0.974-1.014,
  nothing sinks more than 1.8 cm, the skin's 99th-percentile stretch over the shoulder and the stifle at most 1.61.
- **Triangles**: `wolf` 4,754 (body 4,498, eyes 60, fur 196), `wolf_shell` 1,800, `wolf_far` 150. 25 bones.

## 4. The pipeline (`tools/giant/build_people.py`)

`--horse`, `--brutes`, `--wolves` (and `--all`) build the creatures' groups (`CREATURE_GROUPS`: horse, ogre, troll,
wolf), each its kit script in its own interpreter, three at once; a group not built keeps its manifest entries. The
creatures' manifest records every file, the toolchain, the kit hashes of every group's sources (the brutes' also the
vendored CC0 lists' hash), each group's facts and the index P62/P64 read. `--verify` regenerates all four groups beside
the roles and compares bit for bit; `--budget` counts them, the brutes' clips by keyed bones and keys. Import settings:
`*_clips.glb` as the people's clips, `*_poses.glb` as the horse's poses, `wolf_fur.png` lossless.

| horse | 110-215 |

**The giant's manifest** hashes every kit module, so it was refreshed by `build_giant.py --body --designs --pack
--clips` (the cache's bakes): only `inputs.kit` changed (`brute.py` added, `quadruped.py`'s hash); every giant file
bit-identical; `scenes/player/giant_model.tscn` rebuilt by its builder (the index carries the manifest's sha256).

## How it looks (the bar: reads as what it is at 10 m from the game camera, does not look clunky at 3 m)

Judged from the review frames (llvmpipe, the formation test level's light, the person shader with the strands'
scissor), by eye and honestly:

- **The ogre - passes at 10 m; borderline at 3 m.** From the game camera it is a hunched, heavy-shouldered, long-armed
  green brute in a loincloth beside the giant, its scale and bulk the box ogre's; the heavier walk (the knees bent, the
  trunk pitched), the slam's wide stance and the fall on its back read. Close, the body is good (traps, gut, bowed legs,
  big hands and feet), the face reads as an ogre (the brow ridge, the broad flat nose, the underbite with two ivory
  tusks), but it is still a human face pushed, not sculpted: the skull's top is flat and faceted from the decimation,
  there is a dark blocky patch at the ear (the ear's texels), and the warts read as dark spots at 1 m.
- **The Ogre King - passes at 10 m; at 3 m the mantle is weak.** The crown fits the head and reads; the hide mantle in
  the faction's red covers the chest like a short ragged top rather than a royal mantle, and the gold belt is a plain
  thick ring.
- **The troll - passes at 10 m; borderline at 3 m.** Taller, leaner-limbed, more hunched, a long hooked nose, mossy
  shoulders and back: clearly not the ogre. Close, the face is a long-nosed old man's more than a troll's (small eyes,
  a receding chin), the ears' points are slight, and the moss is a flat tint without relief.
- **The Troll Matriarch - passes at 10 m.** The bone crown reads; the hide mantle is the same ragged shape as the
  King's.
- **The whelp - passes at 10 m; at 3 m it is a smaller troll** with a less aged face (its youth shows mostly in the
  skin and proportions, not in a distinctly young face).
- **The wolf - reads as a canine at 10 m (a grey dog or a wolf: the ears, the long muzzle, the tail); does not pass at
  3 m.** The head is too long and narrow (borzoi-like), the mouth's line reads as a toothy grin, the ruff's cards look
  like scattered scales and the tail's crossed cards like a flat fan, the legs are thin; the coat (agouti, the darker
  saddle, the pale underparts) and the almond amber eyes are right. The dire wolf (x 2, dark, ember eyes) reads as a
  big dark wolf. **Recommendation**: keep the box wolves (plan §6.3 cut item 1) unless P64's fur shell and a reworked
  head in P65 fix the close view; the files cost about 0.4 MiB if shipped unused.
- **The horse - improved; passes at 10 m as P61's did; at 3 m better but not finished.** The almond eye with lids
  reads, the mane has volume from both sides, the tail is fuller, the coat has shading and grain, the gallop's
  shoulder and stifle no longer stretch, the chest is leaner, the stirrups hang outside the housing. Still off: the
  saddle reads as a leather slab (smoother, but its shape is the same), the cheek keeps some facets, and with the
  game's shader as it is the mane and tail draw as solid black cards (see §3).
