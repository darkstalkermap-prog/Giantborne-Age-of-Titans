## What was built

- **Merged rigs** (design 1, decision 9). Every humanoid and quadruped rig is one vertex-coloured
  mesh per animated pivot plus the weapon, all with one material:
  - `SceneKit.merge_vertex_colored(parts)` bakes each part's transform into one `ArrayMesh` surface
    with a vertex colour per part (its flat albedo); `SceneKit.merge_rig(body, groups, rest_name)`
    does it for Body and every plain `Node3D` pivot under it (hips, torso, shoulders; the
    quadruped's head, tail and legs), one mesh per render layer, after merging the named groups
    (the weapon, and the giant's chest, belt and head on render layer 2, which first person culls)
    into meshes of their own. `SceneKit.rig_material()` is the one material
    (`assets/materials/rig_vertex.tres`: vertex colour as albedo, sRGB, white albedo, emission on
    and black), embedded in each scene like the palette materials. `lod_proxy()` is a vertex-coloured
    box with the same material.
  - The builders (`player`, `soldier`, `wolf`, `ranged`, `heavy`, `npcs`) call `merge_rig`; the
    regenerated scenes have, under `Visual/Body`: soldier 6 meshes (was 16), wolf 7 (19), archer 6
    (19), mage 7 (16), ogre 6 (23), Ogre King 6 (31), villager 5 (12), elder 5 (14), the giant 8
    (19: the five pivots' meshes, the club, the chest, the belt and the head; `ShoulderL/Hand` and
    `ShoulderR/Hand` are `Marker3D`s where the hand boxes were). The sparring post keeps its parts
    (only its `LodProxy` changed).
  - `RigPaint` (new, `scripts/presentation/rig_paint.gd`) is what `ActorRig` and `QuadrupedRig`
    share: one material copy per source material per actor (`unique_copy` / `make_unique` /
    `forget`), a hit flash as emission (`flash_emission`: colour x strength x 0.85), a status tint as
    an albedo multiplier (`tint_multiplier`: white.lerp(tint, alpha)), and the distance LOD. A weapon
    visual's own flat materials (the giant's) get copies too and leave the list with the weapon.
- **Distance LOD** (design 2). `render_lod_m` (enemy schema, anchor P22; `data/enemies/*.json`:
  soldier, archer, mage 45 m, wolf 40 m, ogre 70 m, the Ogre King 0 = never; people 45 m from
  `NpcsBuilder.PEOPLE_RENDER_LOD_M`). `RigPaint.apply_render_lod(visual, body, lod_m)` sets every
  geometry under `Visual/Body` to `visibility_range_end = lod_m + 5` and puts a copy of
  `Visual/LodProxy` under `Visual/Body` (`FarProxy`) with `visibility_range_begin = lod_m - 5`: the
  renderer swaps them by distance at AI LOD tiers 0 and 1 with a 10 m overlap. `EnemyActor` applies
  its record's value (`_p22_ready`); `ActorRig.render_lod_m` / `QuadrupedRig.render_lod_m` are
  exported (`set_render_lod(m)`). Tier 2 is unchanged (Body hidden, `Visual/LodProxy` shown).
- **`QuadrupedRig.footfall`** (design 3): emitted once per half gait cycle (a diagonal paw pair
  landing) while moving; `footfalls` counts them.
- **Thornwick** (design 4). Guard posts 3 and 4 moved from (88, -26) / (88, -14) to (73.5, -30) /
  (73.5, -10) and the captain from (94, -23.5) to (74, -20) (`tools/data/valley_layout.json`
  `guard_post_3/4`, `data/world/encounter_village_guards.json`): each at least 22 m from the
  barricade's centre (96, -20). A faction member at peace (not hostile) with a living enemy within
  12 m steps back 10 m from it (`FactionState`'s hook every 15 ticks calls
  `EnemyActor.faction_step_back`; its post becomes the step-back point through the brain's
  `post_resolver`, facing the fight) and walks back to its post 2 s after no enemy is within 14 m.
- **The yard pocket** (design 5): two invisible fill boxes on the blockers layer
  (`yard_fill_w`, x 62-72.5, z -50 to -33.5; `yard_fill_n`, x 72.5-77.5, z -50 to -42.5, 12 m high)
  fill the yard between cottages 3-5, the watchtower and the north palisade. The rebaked navmeshes
  (`assets/nav/valley_*.res`) have no polygon there; the giant layer lost its island (8 components,
  was 9).
- **Factions by record** (design 6, §2.12, decision 31). `FactionState` reads `faction.<short>`
  records (`data/world/faction_village.json` ships Thornwick with M5's texts, 2 offences in 30 s,
  neutral, no give-way): `record_for`, `known_factions`, `offences_to_hostile`, `offence_window_s`,
  `initial`, `give_way_m`, `flag_id` (under the record's `flag_prefix`), `text_for`. Several
  factions keep their own offence chains (`offence_result_n`: N offences in a row, each within the
  window of the one before). States: `standing()` is "reconciled" (wins over hostile; members treat
  it as neutral) > "hostile" (the flag, or `initial` "hostile") > "stood_down" > "warned" >
  "neutral"; `reconcile(faction)` sets `<prefix>.reconciled`, and an offence against a reconciled
  faction counts again (turning hostile clears `reconciled`). Offences: a direct connected blow on a
  member (M5), and since P22 any player damage (blows, abilities, trampling) to a prop of a faction
  tagged `house` or `engine` (`prop_owner` / `prop_tags`: the prop's `faction` / `prop_tags`
  property, its node meta, or its record's `prop_tags`). Announcements (`faction_changed` and the
  HUD line) for hostile, stood down and reconciled from any writer. Civilians of a faction with
  `civilians_give_way` that is at peace with the giant (not warned, hostile or stood down) step out
  of a giant's path (`CivilianBrain.GIVE_WAY`: to `give_way_m` + the giant's height beside its line
  of travel, re-planned every 0.5 s, then they stand and watch) instead of fleeing; combat noise and
  the faction's news still send them running. Thornwick keeps fleeing.
- **The web release without test content** (design 7). The test-only records moved to `data/test/`
  (`level_director_test`, `level_stress_arena`, `encounter_director_test_{budget,soldiers,wolves}`;
  `GameData` loads the folder like any other, so tests and desktop builds still have them). The
  `Web` preset excludes `scenes/test/*, assets/test_world/*, data/test/*`; a new `Web (test)`
  preset (not runnable, `builds/web_test/`) keeps them with the release's options; the desktop
  presets keep them. `tools/build_web.sh [--preset=NAME] [--out=DIR] [--budget-mb=N]`: the pck's
  raw, gzipped and shipped size against the milestone's budget (7.5 MB to M6, 21 MB at M7, 23 MB
  after, from `config/version`; a WARNING over it), the pck's test content
  (`tools/pck_files.py --check-no-test`: an error for the release), and for the release the data it
  ships validated without `data/test` (`tools/release_data_check.gd`, the game's own
  `DataValidator`; a WARNING naming the records). Only the release build into `builds/web/` writes
  the `BUILD` byte counts back into `web/launcher.html`. The launcher passes `?args=a,b,c` to the
  engine (`launchArgs()`: the game's own arguments after `--` unless the list has its own `--`).
- **Ranged positioning** (design 8). `tactics.front_arc_deg` (150) and `tactics.off_camera_aim_s`
  (1.2) for the archer and the mage (enemy schema, anchor P22). A new firing point is taken in
  front of the giant's camera first (`EnemyActor.front_arc_firing_point`: the nearest free
  elevation, then cover marker in the band and the arc, else a ring point in the arc from the arc
  direction nearest the unit's bearing, 20 degree steps), before P09's search; a held point that left
  the arc is given up once the camera has turned 20 degrees since the pick, at most every 3 s and
  never during a shot (`EnemyBrain.keep_front_arc`); a shot drawn from outside the camera's view
  (`EnemyActor.in_camera_view`, the horizontal field of view) runs a copy of the shot whose windup is
  at least `off_camera_aim_s` under the same action id (`EnemyActor.prepare_shot` through
  `EnemyBrain.prepare_ranged_shot`): the archer's 0.9 s becomes 1.2 s (54 -> 72 ticks), its aim
  still locking at the shot's tracking fraction.
- **The nav-link flake** (design 10): `test_world_props.test_a_broken_gate_opens_its_nav_link`
  waits for the map's iteration id to pass the one before each change (and, for the expected path,
  for two later iterations) instead of a fixed 3 s.
- **`RunMetrics`**: `faction_hostile` per run (Thornwick hostile at the end), and its off-camera
  test is `EnemyActor.in_camera_view` (the same one the ranged units use).

## Decisions and deviations

- **Mesh counts differ from the plan's estimates**: the ogre has 6 meshes (plan 7: its hips carry no
  part of its own) and the giant 8 (plan 9: the hand boxes merged into the shoulders; the hands are
  markers now). The tests pin the built counts.
- **The far proxy lives under `Visual/Body`** (a copy of `Visual/LodProxy`), not on
  `Visual/LodProxy` itself as the plan says: under Body it falls, staggers and hides with the body
  (a dead actor's corpse never turns into a standing box, a tier-2 actor shows only the one proxy),
  it needs no change to `set_lod_tier`, and the civilians get it without an edit to `civilian.gd`.
  `set_lod_tier` is unchanged (the plan's anchor note "leaves the proxy to the renderer at tiers
  0-1" holds without code: the renderer swaps the far proxy, the tier-2 proxy stays the actor's).
- **The rendered draw-call check measures the stress arena's twelve combatants, not the whole
  arena**: the twelve drop 65 % (221 -> 77 draw calls); the stress route's whole view drops less
  (see Measurements) because the props, the debris, the floor and the giant are most of it.
- **Guard posts**: the plan's x 72 / captain (76, -20) became x 73.5 / (74, -20): x 72 at z -30
  stands in cottage 5's wall, and (76, -20) is 20 m from the barricade, under the plan's own 22 m.
- **`_p22_ready()`** is one call in `EnemyActor._ready` after `_p11b_ready()` (the pattern P04 and
  P11b use), outside the anchor; the rest of the code is under `## @anchor P22`.
- **Reconciled members are at peace**: `EnemyActor._apply_standing` already treats anything but
  "hostile" as neutral, so "reconciled" needed no actor change.
- **The give-way civilian is pure brain logic**: the actor's perception already reports the giant's
  position and speed; the brain tracks its direction of travel itself (two perceptions), and reads
  the giant's height from the PLAYER entity when the perception has none.
- **A test-only tool file**: `tests/support/rig_draw_probe.gd` (the rendered probe) and the slow
  suite `tests/slow/test_rig_draw_calls.gd` are new files the plan's list does not name (it asks
  for "a rendered draw-call check" without a file).
- **Tests changed for the new rig structure** (deliberate): `tests/integration/test_camera_review_fixes.gd`
  and `tests/integration/test_growth.gd` read the giant's arm mesh at `Visual/Body/ShoulderR/Mesh`
  (the separate arm boxes are merged).

## Merge notes

- **Cross-lane, for the W13 merge (P21)**: `data/audio/mix_default.json` (P19's file, P21's in W13)
  names `level.stress_arena` and `level.director_test` in its surfaces. Those records now live in
  `data/test/`, which the Web release leaves out, so the release's data does not validate:
  `tools/build_web.sh` prints a WARNING (`tools/release_data_check.gd`) and the released game would
  stop at its data error screen. Fix at the merge: drop those two surface keys (or move them into a
  test-only mix record under `data/test/`). Desktop builds and the tests are unaffected.
- **For P21's threat cues**: an archer or mage shooting from outside the camera's view now winds
  up for 1.2 s (72 ticks) under the same action id; its aim locks at the tracking fraction of that
  windup (0.72 s for the archer's shot). A cue that times itself from the running action
  (`controller.library[id]` / the controller's phase) follows; one computed from the record's
  `windup_s` would lead the shot by 0.18-0.3 s.
- **For P21's footsteps**: `QuadrupedRig.footfall` (no arguments) is the wolves' paw step.
- **For P23/P24a**: `tools/build_web.sh --preset="Web (test)"` builds into `builds/web_test/` with
  the test levels; `play.html?args=--mode=world` passes arguments.
- **For P29 (reconcile) and the world's factions**: `FactionState.reconcile(f)`; give-way factions
  need `civilians_give_way` and `give_way_m` in their record; house and engine props need a
  `faction` (property or meta) and `prop_tags`.
- After the merge: import, `tools/check.sh --only=player,soldier,wolf,ranged,heavy,npcs,valley`
  (the scenes and navmeshes come out byte-identical), the full suite.
