# P55 — The creator II: pages, thumbnails, the filter, slot rows and sliders

Plan: `docs/plans/M11_PLAN.md` §3.8 (and §2.7, §2.5, §2.6, §0.3's creator row, §1.1, §1.5-§1.7, §2.11, §2.13). Wave W32,
lane B, beside P54 (the wardrobe). Built on 19 November 2026 on `pkg/P55` from the tag `wave-31` (0.11.0-m11 with
P53); the version stays the release's. Every number here was taken on the shared container (4 CPUs, no GPU: llvmpipe
natively, SwiftShader in Chromium), the other lane's bakes and checks running beside it at times; the loads are given
where they matter.

The goal (§2.7): a creator that holds about 190 options and is still quick with a pad, a keyboard and a mouse. Every
row is built from the sets' data, so P54's 25 garments and 8 accessories and P56's catalogue appear without an edit
here; today the garment set holds M10's ten garments and there is no accessory set (an empty grid). The screen was
judged on today's data and on a test-only catalogue at the target's size (`tests/fixtures/appearance/
creator_catalogue.json`: 208 options, P54's and P56's names, slots and materials on M10's meshes).

## What was built

### 1. The eleven categories and the slot rows (`ui/screens/creator/option_rows.gd`)

| Category | Rows |
| --- | --- |
| Presets | the presets (M10's buttons; the focused one's description under the list) |
| Body | Skin tone (swatches); Brawn, Girth (signed sliders from `set_build.json`, their ends named by its `low`/`high`) |
| Face | Eye colour; a signed slider per option of the face set (P56's; none today) |
| Hair, Beard | Style (grid), Colour (swatches; the beard's "Same as hair" first), Grey (slider); Beads (grid) once the bead set exists |
| Tattoos | Arms, Chest, Back, Face, Legs (grids; P53's fifth region), Ink, Ancestry mark |
| War paint | Face, Body (grids: P53's two places at once), Colour |
| Scars | a toggle per scar (at most six) |
| Clothing | Outfit (a grid of starting points: puts every slot on at once, one undo step; "Custom" once a slot differs), then the ten slots in `Appearance.SLOTS` order (grids) |
| Accessories | Neck, Arm ring, Belt (grids) |
| Colours | Cloth, Leather (the dye set), Metal (the metal set); then, for every worn piece that takes a dye, "Chest: Tunic" with "Outfit's dye" first and its material's palette (rebuilt when the worn pieces change) |

A **grid row** names its value ("Chest   Tunic") and how many it has ("4 >"; "none yet" for an empty set); A, Enter or a
click opens its grid; left and right step through its choices and apply each, as M10's cyclers did. M10's category
names (Skin, Eyes) open Body and Face (`CATEGORY_ALIASES`), and M10's record paths (`outfit.primary`,
`outfit.secondary`, `paint.design`) still read and write (P53's `LEGACY_KEYS`), so the sheets and the probes that name
them keep working; `CreatorScreen.pick(key, value)` opens the category that has the row.

**Sliders** (§2.7): the build and face sliders are signed; every slider steps by 0.1 with the pad or the arrows
(`SLIDER_PAD_STEP`) and a drag snaps to 0 within 0.05 (`SNAP_ZERO`; the greys to their 0 end); one slider's moves are
one undo step. **The build sliders stop at 0.6 each way** (`BUILD_LIMIT`; P53's follow-up: at +1 the thighs nearly
double, at -1 the tunic's sleeves show steps); the record keeps -1 to 1. While a build slider has the focus the preview
shows the **stage-1 rear Game view** (`GiantPreview.GAME_STAGE1`: the game's third-person camera at stage 1's profile,
whatever stage Game view shows at a shrine).

**Randomize** (Y, R) over every row (`OptionRows.random_record`): Body the skin and the build (within
`Appearance.RANDOM_BUILD`, in steps of 0.1), Face the eyes and the face sliders, Clothing dressed by slot
(`random_clothing`: a random outfit as the starting point, then each slot changed to another of its pieces 35 % of the
time, an empty one filled 20 %, a worn one emptied 10 % - never the legs; a cloth, a leather and a metal dye; **an
armour is never left over a bare chest unless the outfit it started from wore one so**), Accessories (each slot 40 %),
Colours (the three dyes; now and then a piece's own); the other categories Appearance's. "Randomize all" keeps the
clothing and accessories unless Clothing is open, and draws the build and the face too. The clothing is drawn first, so
the designs drawn after it never go where it covers.

### 2. The option grid (`ui/screens/creator/option_grid.gd`, `class_name OptionGrid`)

In the options panel, in place of the rows while open (the label reads "Clothing  >  Chest"): the name filter's box and
the material buttons (only the materials the row's options have, and only when there are two or more), a page of up to
twelve cells (4 x 3; the cells grow with the panel from 84 to 150 px: 117 px at 1280x720), the focused option's name
(its material, or why it is locked), and the pager ("<", "Page 1 of 2", ">") with Done.

- "None" is first wherever the row is optional (garments, accessories, tattoos, paints; beads' `bead.none`) and is kept
  through every filter (it takes the piece off). An empty set shows "None" alone and "Nothing to choose here yet.".
- A cell shows the option's thumbnail and its name until the thumbnail arrives (the Web's first page shows names first);
  the record's value has a gold border.
- **Moving the focus from one cell to another** (the D-pad, the arrows) puts that option on - the swatches' rule:
  moving previews and keeps; the focus entering the cells from elsewhere (the grid opening, the filters, the pager, a
  page turn) lands and changes nothing. A or Enter puts the focused option on and closes the grid; **a click puts it
  on and keeps the grid open** (Done, B or Esc close it; a tab, LB or RB close it and change the category). A grid's
  browsing is one undo step (the screen's merge key per opening).
- **Pages**: LT / RT (one page per pull), PageUp / PageDown, the wheel over the grid, or the buttons; they wrap.
- **The material filter** (All, Cloth, Leather, Fur, Metal) on X (pad) or F (keys), or its buttons. **The name filter**
  on the keyboard: typing a letter while a cell has the focus starts it (Q, E, R, V and F keep their meaning), "/"
  puts the focus in the box where every letter types, Backspace takes one off; a match anywhere in the name, case aside.
- The footer's hint names the grid's keys while it is open; the grid is made on the first row opened, never while the
  screen opens.

**Pad moves** (§2.7, §3.8's test): from a category (its rows shown) to any option is at most 4 moves - the row, A,
the page (pages wrap, so with at most three pages one press either way), the cell. `test_creator_grid` computes it
from each grid's geometry (a real `OptionGrid` holding the row's choices as the screen opens it): **3** at most for
today's data (70 grid options) and for the catalogue (142), and still within 4 for a hair set of 35 styles (three
pages). The literal presses from the category's first row are reported beside it: **at most 9, 4.8 on average** with
the catalogue - the ten slot rows make the row the long part (FOLLOWUPS).

### 3. The thumbnails (`ui/screens/creator/thumbnail_cache.gd`, `class_name ThumbnailCache`)

- A garment, an accessory, an outfit, a style or beads: drawn by a second, unshown `GiantPreview` (`thumbnail`: still,
  MSAA off, its viewport 96 x 96 and `UPDATE_ONCE`, the studio light) wearing the record's skin, hair and beard and
  nothing but the option, in the slot's framing (`OptionRows.SLOT_FRAMINGS`: head, shoulders, chest, arms, waist, legs,
  shins, feet, back; hair styles from behind, close), then read back into an `ImageTexture` in the next frame.
- A tattoo or a war paint: a flat picture of its own layer and channel of the design array (`designs.png`, read once
  per layer and cut to 256 px), the record's ink or paint colour over the skin tone, cropped to the design's ink. No
  render.
- **One a frame** (`THUMBS_PER_FRAME`), **for the page shown only**: a page turn, a filter or closing the grid replaces
  the queue; a thumbnail started in the frame before the turn finishes. **Cached by the option id, the skin tone and the
  dyes** (hair styles by their colours) for the screen's life; a cached one is handed back at once. Nothing is made
  while the screen opens: the unshown preview is made on the first thumbnail. Headless nothing is drawn and a blank
  texture stands for each frame (the same scheduling). `reset_for_tests()` is P53's pre-declared `ResetHooks` entry.

### 4. Framing (`ui/screens/creator/giant_preview.gd`)

- **By side** (M10's follow-up): the index's design `side` (P48's) mirrors the face, chest, arms and back framings
  (`<framing>_left`: the yaw and the target's x negated; "arms_left" follows the left hand); the brow scar, the chest
  claws, the claw stripes and the knuckles' neighbours are shown from his left. `GiantPreview.region_framing(region,
  side)`, `OptionRows.row_side`.
- **Slots**: while a slot's grid is open the preview frames the upper or the lower half (`upper`, `lower`), the head or
  the back (`OptionRows.SLOT_PREVIEW`); the slot framings (`head`, `shoulders`, `waist`, `legs`, `shins`, `feet`,
  `hair_close`) serve the thumbnails.
- **The eyes look toward the camera** (P51's `look`; the follow-up row): as `SkinnedRig._look_eyes` does in play, at
  most a quarter of the iris's radius, eased, straight ahead from behind; not in a still preview.
- A framing already shown is not reset when the focus moves between rows of the same framing (the user's turn and zoom
  stay).

### 5. The shrine's tab (`ui/screens/shrine/appearance_tab.gd`)

The summary's clothing line by slot, each piece with the dye it is drawn in and the accessories after them:
"Clothing (warrior's tunic): Chest: tunic, undyed; Armour: jerkin, dark leather; Arms: bracers, dark leather; ...";
"Clothing (custom): ..." once the slots are no outfit's. `AppearanceTab.summary_of(record, ancestry)`,
`clothing_line()`.

### 6. Layout

At 1280x720 the screen keeps M10's frame (the preview about 720x544, the options panel about 512x544); the eleven tabs
fit one line (they end at x = 709); the ten slot rows fit the panel without scrolling; a grid's twelve cells of 117 px,
its filters, name and pager fit the panel with room under them. `test_creator_layout` checks at 1280x720, 1366x768,
1600x900, 1920x1080 and 2560x1440 that every part, button and the open grid's filters, cells, name and Done lie inside
the window, above the footer and inside the options panel, nothing overlapping, the cells at least 84 px.

## Decisions and deviations

- **"At most 4 pad moves" is counted in steps** - the row, A, the page, the cell - as the plan's sentence names them
  ("the slot row, the page, the cell"); the test computes them from each grid's geometry and requires at most three
  pages per grid (the wrap puts every page one press away). The literal presses are reported beside it (at most 9,
  4.8 on average with the catalogue): ten slot rows are the long part. Left and right on a grid row step through its
  pieces without opening the grid (M10's cyclers), which is often the quickest way.
- **Moving the focus over a grid's cells puts each option on**, as the swatches do (moving previews and keeps); one
  grid opening is one undo step; a page turn or a filter moves the focus without putting anything on. A click puts on
  and keeps the grid open (to compare pieces with the mouse); A or Enter closes it.
- **The name filter and the creator's own keys**: typing starts the filter except Q, E, R, V (the creator's) and F (the
  material filter, §2.7's key); "/" or a click puts the focus in the box, where every key types. Esc in the box first
  ends Godot's editing; the next closes the grid.
- **The build sliders stop at 0.6** (`BUILD_LIMIT`; P53's follow-up offered it). The record and `sanitize` keep -1 to 1.
- **Randomize all draws the build and the face too** ("all" kept the build in P53; the plan's item 7 asks Randomize over
  every new row); it keeps the clothing and accessories unless Clothing is open, as M10 kept the outfit.
- **The outfits are a grid row** (thumbnails of the whole outfit), not a cycler; "Custom" once a slot differs.
- **Per-slot dye overrides are built** (the split point - moving them to P57 - was not taken): the Colours category
  lists a piece's own dye for every worn piece that takes one, rebuilt when the worn pieces change.
- **Thumbnails are 96 px** (§2.7) and are scaled up in larger cells (117 px at 1280x720, 170 px at 1920x1080 in
  canvas units times the window's scale): soft at 1920x1080.
- **`CreatorScreen.pick(key, value)` opens the category that has the row** (M10's tests and the sheet tool name keys
  whose rows moved: the dyes to Colours, the skin to Body).
- **Test files beyond the plan's owned list**: `tests/fixtures/appearance/creator_catalogue.json` and
  `tests/support/creator_catalogue.gd` (the test catalogue the brief asks for), `tests/support/creator_probe.gd` and
  `.tscn` (the rendered thumbnail suite's child and the captures). **Other packages' tests edited** because the
  creator's contract changed (none loosened): `tests/integration/test_creator_layout.gd` (P44's: a grid at the five
  sizes added; the catalogue installed) and `tests/slow/test_creator_controls.gd` (P44's: the categories' names and
  order; the new walks added).
- **Some of §3.8's tests are in the slow tier** (the default tier's share is 0.8 s): every option with the pad, the
  keys and the mouse, the 200 moves, Undo through 64 changes, Randomize by slot, the thumbnails' scheduling and their
  rendered check. The default tier keeps the rows from data, the pad moves, the filters, a grid's pick, the sliders,
  framing by side, the layout with a grid, the summary by slot and the later build's record.

## Known gaps and what still looks weak

- **The pad's literal presses**: up to nine from a category to the last slot's piece; the "4 moves" are steps.
- **Thumbnails at 1920x1080** are 96 px scaled to about 170 px of window: soft. Thumbnails of garments that share a mesh
  (the catalogue's borrowed M10 meshes) look alike - P54's real pieces will read better, but the slot framings were
  chosen on M10's meshes (helms, greaves and sandals may want closer ones).
- **A design's flat picture** shows every island of its UV map: a design on both arms reads as scattered strokes.
- **The Web**: the thumbnails are not measured there (`creator_check.mjs` does not open a grid; P57). The creator's
  first frame stays over its 3x rule as before.
- **The preview** does not run `SecondaryMotion` and does not keep a category's parts resident (FOLLOWUPS).
- The grid view leaves the lower part of the options panel empty when a slot has few pieces (one line of cells).
