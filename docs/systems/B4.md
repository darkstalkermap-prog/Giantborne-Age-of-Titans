# B4 — Milestone B's gate: what was broken, what was fixed, what is left

B4 reviewed milestone B (B1-B3, merged since the shipped `0.14.0-m14`: functional armour in seven slots, crafting and
reforging at forges that work titan parts, the Quarry Maul; the Ogre King as a signature titan with his trophies; the
Rootwarden in his Greywood grove; the warning posts and the line of sight) independently of the packages' own claims,
on `pkg/b4` from the dev head (`61d2b8a`, `0.15.0-m15`). Every suspicion was run before it was called a finding: the
real game under Xvfb driven by the UI probe (a new titan tour), saves the released `0.14.0-m14` build wrote (a clean
checkout of the tag, its own user folder, its own bots), the real Valley and the real grove in the gate and titans
tiers, and the Web release in headless Chromium. Confirmed findings were fixed, each with a test that fails before its
fix; what is a design choice or large is in `docs/plans/FOLLOWUPS.md`, "From the milestone B gate (B4)". The decisions
are in `docs/DECISIONS.md` §3.9.

## The UI probe's titan tour (`scripts/debug/ui_probe.gd`)

    xvfb-run -a -s "-screen 0 1280x720x24" tools/gd.sh --path . --rendering-driver opengl3 --resolution 1280x720 -- \
      --no-user-settings --mode=valley --new-game --start=valley.shrine.kingsfall --ui-probe=titan \
      [--ui-probe-titan=ogre_king|rootwarden] [--ui-probe-stage=N] [--ui-probe-skip=<open|TICK|end>] \
      [--ui-probe-skip-key=<enter|esc|space|pad_a|pad_b|pad_start>] [--ui-probe-die]

(the Rootwarden's: `--mode=world --new-game --start=world.cp.hollowmere`) prints one `UI_PROBE {"t":"titan", ...}` line
(paused, the input context, the HUD, the camera, the giant shown and on the ground, the titan shown, awake and
fighting, the arena engaged and its gate, the trophies broken, the unbalance, the down, the music, seen, played) and,
with `--ui-probe-die`, one `{"t":"titan_retry", ...}`, and quits with 0 when both are right.
