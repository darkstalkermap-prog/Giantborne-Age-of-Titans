# C1 — Act one ends at the throne; the colossi (stages 7-8); the land beyond the rim

Milestone C's first package, on `pkg/c1` from the milestone B candidate (`0.15.0-m15`, `bb73c37`). The project's
version is **0.16.0-m16**. Three parts, as the brief asks: the growth track's tech past the crown with stages 7 and 8
in play; the Stone Throne as act one's end and act two's start; and the first region of the land beyond the rim,
the Hoarfell, reached by the Breach. Decisions C1-1..C1-15 are in `docs/DECISIONS.md` §2.7.

### 1.5 Size, scale and what a colossus meets (decisions C1-4, C1-7, C1-8)

- **Damage**: the size multiplier keeps its power law past 28 m (cap 3.1): x2.32 / 2.55 at stages 7 / 8 (2.78, 3.02 at
  9, 10). Every titan's blow against the giant is still `versus_size` (A2-3), so a colossus takes a third less from
  Stoneback than a stage-6 giant.
- **Awe**: humans break before a giant 13 times their height (stage 7: 13.2 x a 1.8 m man): `awe_tiers` 13.0 / -200
  morale on soldiers, archers, pikemen, shieldmen, mages and bannermen, `awe_ratio` 13 on crews and riders (a horse
  rears: `sfx.voice.horse.awe`); Corvane's iron guard hold until stage 8 (14.5 / -140). A banner halves it - still all
  of a formation's 90. Stages 1-6 are untouched (a stage-6 giant is 10.5 x).
- **Trees are grass** (decision C1-7): the chunks' trunks (solid cylinders no wider than 1.5 m) have a body of their
  own in group `scenery_trunks`; a giant with `stride` gets a collision exception with each as chunks bring them
  (`PlayerMotor.sync_trunk_passes`). Everything else - the camera, sight, every other actor - meets them as before.
- **Walls, houses and towers**: a colossus steps over anything up to half its height with Colossus Stride; it does not
  trample stone (decision C1-8). The Hoarfell's boulders have no solids at all.
- **The old world**: every world checkpoint a stage-8 giant may wake at has room for it (`test_c1_world`: 26 of 26);
  the Valley's checkpoints say `max_stage` 6, so travel and respawns there refuse a colossus (as for every level the
  giant has outgrown); the Breach and the Stonehold's gates are below it.
- **Navigation** (decision C1-5): no colossus layer. The player never paths; the titan layer (radius 4 m, height 19 m)
  serves the bots and the titans, and its gate bits 256-2048 gate stages 7-10. The Hoarfell is built for it: no passage
  narrower than 40 m, nothing over a road lower than 45 m.

## 3. The land beyond the rim (decisions C1-6, C1-9)

### 3.1 The world's shape

The streamed world grows **north by three rows of 256 m chunks** (`grid_min [0, -3]`, `grid [8, 11]`: c0_-3 ... c7_-1;
2,048 x 2,816 m). Chunk (0, 0)'s corner stays at `origin_m`, so every chunk of the Old Kingdom keeps its id, its file and
its cell; a save's positions, its `map.seen.<chunk>` flags and every fast-travel point are as they were. The rows are
the right side: the story's heights are north of the Stonehold, and the throne looks at them.

A **land** (`world.json` `lands`) is walkable inside its rect with a rim of its own (top 440 m, a run that wanders with
a slow noise); where its rim and the Old Kingdom's meet, the nearer interior's owns the ground across 16 m, so every
sample of the Old Kingdom's eight rows is the one of the build before - but the north edge row of row 0, quantised now
in the lands' coarser step (16 mm: the Hoarcrown stands 720 m) and so moved by at most 8 mm on the unwalkable rim. A
**pass** (`rim.passes`: the Breach) is walkable and baked; its ground is the features' (a ramp). The builder, the
streamer, the skyline's mask, the map's fog and its picture (1,024 x 1,408 px), GameData's chunk check, the world walk
and the index read the grid from `grid_min`.

What changed in the Old Kingdom's files: c4_0 (the Breach's mouth), c4_1 and c3_1 (the Rim Road), c5_0 (its scenery
shifted by the global spacing), row 0's north edge (8 mm); the navmeshes of c4_0, c5_0, c4_1 (the Breach, the road);
c1_0, c2_0, c3_0, c6_0's navmeshes only in their bake box's height (their polygons and vertices identical, checked).

Limits: a land is a rect (its walkable edge is straight; the mountains' feet wander); a feature that crosses a chunk
border inside walkable ground must stay under the border rule's 35 degrees (the Hoarfell's steep places - the crags, the
Chainpit, the Colossus Steps, the Hoarwatch - each lie inside one chunk); the 16-bit heights hold -32 .. +1,016 m in the
lands' rows.

### 3.2 The Hoarfell (`tools/data/world/hoarfell.json`, `region.hoarfell`)

A high cold upland (176 m and its noise, a pale stony ground) 1,000 x 430 m walkable, with its own rim of mountains to
440 m and three peaks - the **Hoarcrown** (720 m, north-east: where the Frostbound will come down from) and the two
**Sundered Horns** (600 and 560 m, north-west, a cleft between them: the finale's split mountain).

- **The Breach**: a gorge up through the rim from the Stonehold (85 m) to the Hoarfell (165 m) over 374 m, 64 m wide,
  its jaws leaning slabs of the split rim with the old kings' chain snapped across, steps 6 m tall at its mouth and a
  **stage-6 size gate** ("The cracked rim stands sheer as a wall here: only a giant of the old height can haul itself
  up the Breach."). The Rim Road leads to it from the Ironway's high road east of the Moot.
- **The colossal kind** (`tools/world/kinds/colossal.gd`): the **Fallen King** (a 90 m giant-king's statue thrown down:
  plinth and stumps, torso, crowned head on its side, arm and fist, rubble), **chain anchors** (iron-banded bollards 40
  m tall with a ring and a chain of 8 m links running down into the mountain), the **Kings' Causeway**'s piers (27 m
  apart), the **Rimstone** (a 84 m standing stone and its leaning ring), cairns of giant-cut blocks.
- **Places**: the Breach-top waystone, the Kings' Causeway, the Fallen King, the Chainpit (two anchors, the Rimward's
  camp and engines at its mouth), the Rimstone (the shrine: stages 7 and 8), the Giants' Barrows, **Last Hearth** (a
  hold-outs' camp of tents, a stone hall and store, a bonfire, six folk and Maera; faction `hearthfolk`; a waystone),
  the **Colossus Steps** (36 degrees, a stage-7 gate) up to the **Hoarwatch**. Nine POIs (the Breach and the Hoarwatch
  vistas reveal 700 and 900 m), four caches, a 120 XP region.
- **Its look**: a pale stony ground (`ground_tint`, a region field new in C1: the terrain blends it instead of the
  map's pale `map_tint`, which washed the ground out to white in the first stills), a colder, darker fog than the map
  colour. A thrown boulder is a faceted rock now, tumbling (every boulder: the box it was read badly at Mountain
  Hurl's 5 m).
- **Encounters** (decision C1-9): two **rimkin giants** (16.5 m: the rival giants' body re-kitted, Harrok's first-phase
  moves, no yield; `appearance_preset.npc_rimkin`) by the Fallen King; the **Rimward warband** at the Chainpit (pikes and
  shields of eight with banners, four archers, two iron guards, two ballistae and a catapult with their crews; faction
  `rimward`); four **trolls** in the crags; six **dire wolves** on the upland. A wander table (a rimkin, dire wolves,
  trolls). No titan.

## 6. What is weak

Judged from the stills and the two videos (outside the repository), bluntly:

- **The Hoarfell is grey geometry, not yet a place.** Its ground, rim and peaks are one flat-shaded stone grey (better
  than the first stills' blown-out white, still monotonous); no snow, no texture, no grass, almost no small detail
  besides the boulders. The rim walls of the Breach are smooth vertical sheets.
- **The colossal works read as blocks.** The Fallen King is a heap of boxes and a pillar - nobody would see a thrown
  statue in it from most angles; the Kings' Causeway reads as a row of trilithons; the chain anchors (banded
  bollards and ring links) read best; the Rimstone is a tall slab in a ring of stones.
- **The Hoarcrown, the Frostbound's origin, is a smooth grey cone**; the far view does not sell "something vast
  stirs".
- **The finale** works (28.6 s, skippable, its card) but nothing moves but the camera and dust: the rim does not
  visibly crack, the chain does not snap on screen.
- **The colossi are act one's giant scaled up** (no new garment, hair or armour detail at 29 m; the close-ups hold),
  and the encounters are act one's people far below them: at stage 7-8 soldiers are specks and the rimkin barely
  register.
- **The new abilities' effects are act one's at a larger size** (Shattering Tread's crack line, a tumbling rock for
  Mountain Hurl - no longer a box - and the roar's ring); the stride's arc reads in motion, less in a still.
- **The 44 s walk up the Breach** ends facing a rim wall (the walk's turn toward the Fallen King is too early).
- **Web**: software-WebGL frame times are what they always were (p50 566 ms on this machine); the carried 16 m/s
  stream case misses its 200 m margin at stage 6 and 8 alike.
