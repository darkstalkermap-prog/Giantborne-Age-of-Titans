# B3 — The Rootwarden, the Greywood's signature titan

The third of the ten signature titans and the last of milestone B: the warden the giant kings set over the Greywood,
grown into the wood he kept - a huge humanoid of flesh, bark and root, asleep and rooted in a grove of colossal trees
in the deep Greywood, his living bark growing back over the wounds a giant opens unless fire reaches them or the
knot of his root-arm is broken. His asset in the kit, his fight (a generic, data-driven plate regrowth), his grove in
the world, the woodfolk's quest at Hollowmere (whose words follow what the giant did to the Troll Matriarch), his
intro, the aftermath (the grove greens; Wren's woodworks make the Warden set from his wood), three new sounds, and
B2's carry-overs. Built on `pkg/b3` from the dev head (`0.15.0-m15`, B2 merged); the version stays **0.15.0-m15**.
The seeded 120 s soak is unchanged (41 attacks, 33 hits resolved, 4 enemy kills, 2 fence breaks, 7,201 frames, 0
errors).

## 1. The Rootwarden, as an asset (`tools/giant/kit/rootwarden.py`, `rootwarden_moves.py`; `build_people.py --rootwarden`)

**Design.** Not a walking tree: a man-shaped giant, long-limbed and tall (21.3 m, 69.9 ft, to the crown of his head;
his antlers' boughs reach about 24 m), on the brutes' modules (the people's CC0 human with MakeHuman's macros, the
giant's proportions function with a table of his own: a long neck and shin, a narrow waist, big hands) whose
skin is a dark grey-brown hide of bark. His silhouette is his own: a tall, stooped, long-limbed figure, the left arm
a mass of roots trailing to the ground (11 m of them, driven in when he sleeps), antlers of dead wood off a mossed
brow, bark plates heaped on the shoulders and the shins, and amber glowing where his sapwood shows. The asymmetry
(the left arm's roots against the right forearm's bark) reads from any side.

| Plate (piece) | What it is | Where it rides |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bark_shoulder_l`, `_r` | a heap of thick bark over each shoulder and the trapezius, split by meandering fissures, lantern-fungi shelving off its edge | the skin's weights under each point, stiffened |
| `bark_back` | a broad shield of bark from the shoulder blades to the small of the back, moss in its furrows | the same |
| `bark_forearm_r` | a bracer of bark on the right forearm | the same |
| `bark_knot` | the knot where the roots leave the left forearm: a burl seamed with glowing sap (his weak point that withers him) | the same |
| `bark_shin_l`, `_r` | greaves of bark down each shin (his topple) | the same |
| `sap_<plate>` | under each plate, the sapwood: pale wood and amber sap, shown glowing when the plate is gone | the same |
| `roots` | the root-arm's roots from the left hand to the ground | the hand and the forearm |
| `antlers` | two dead-wood boughs off the brow with tines, a beard of lichen | the head |
| `tree` | the dead tree he rips up and hurls (shown in his `rip`/`hurl` clips) | the right hand |

The body is one 8,930-triangle mesh (`rootwarden.glb`, 1.79 MB with its pieces; clips 1.03 MB), its bark a tileable
texture of its own (`rootwarden_bark_albedo/normal.png`: furrowed ridges, the grain down V), the glows vertex colours
of alpha 0.35 that `stone_plate.gdshader` lights (`glow_energy`: the sapwood, the knot's seams, the fungi). The shader
learned two states for a living plate: `grow` (a regrowing piece eaten away in a mottled front, fresh green bark at its
edge) and `charred` (the sapwood seared black with embers).

**His clips** (`rootwarden_moves.py`, on Stoneback's key machinery): `dormant` (slumped, the root-arm driven into the
ground), `idle`, `walk`, `run`, `roar` (his wake), `uproot` (tearing the roots out of the ground: his second phase),
`lash` (the root-arm swung round in a long arc), `erupt` (the root-arm driven into the ground), `snare` (the roots
pointed: the circle closes on the giant's feet), `rip` and `hurl` (a dead tree torn up and thrown), `stomp`, `reel`,
`kneel`, `rise`, `death` (forward like a felled tree). Each strike's keys are in the manifest (`clip_keys`).

## 2. His fight (`data/enemies/rootwarden.json`; decisions B3-1..B3-4)

He is a `TitanEnemy` (`scenes/enemies/rootwarden.tscn`, built by `tools/builders/titans.gd` `build_rootwarden`: the
plates' hurtboxes and anchors from the manifest's idle pieces, all following his kneel), on `TitanBrain` (now on
`HarrokBrain`'s arena hazards for his second phase). Health 12,500.

| Plate | Health | Kept of a blow | Weak x | Regrowth (after, over, health) | Its break | Loot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| shoulder_l, shoulder_r | 520 | 85 % | 2.2 | 14 s, 6 s, 50 % | a weak point | 1 bark |
| back | 650 | 85 % | 2.8 | 16 s, 7 s, 50 % | a weak point | 1 bark |
| forearm_r | 420 | 60 % | 1.6 | 12 s, 5 s, 50 % | a weak point | 1 bark |
| knot (left forearm) | 600 | 80 % | 2.4 | never | **wither**: no wound regrows for 45 s; his second phase | 2 amber sap |
| shin_l, shin_r | 680 | 70 % | 1.4 | 20 s, 8 s, 40 % | **topple**: down on a knee 6 s (again after 200 damage) | 1 bark |
| hide (torso, legs, root-arm) | - | 50-60 % | - | - | armour | - |

**Regrowth, generic** (decision B3-1): any plate may name `regrow: {after_s, over_s, health_frac, burn_sears}`
(`PlateRules.parse_regrow`, `regrow_at`): its wound stays open (a weak point) for `after_s`, the bark grows back over
`over_s` (still a weak point), then it is whole again with `health_frac` of its health (`PlateSet.regrow_step`, every
tick; `Events.plate_regrowing`, `plate_regrown`). The loot counts a plate broken once (`PlateSet.ever_broken`) even
if it grew back. **Fire** sears an open or regrowing wound for good (`plate_seared`) while the titan burns: a brazier
or a resin pot smashed within `fire.brazier_radius_m` (16 m) of him sets him burning for `fire.burn_s` (8 s). **The
knot** is a new generic break effect, `wither` (`on_break.block_s`): every wound's clock stops for 45 s
(`titan_withered`), and a phase may enter on a plate's break (`phases[].enter_on_plate`).

**Asleep and rooted** (decision B3-2): `dormant` - his AI off, his `dormant` clip - until a blow wakes him (`struck`) or
his territory engages (`engaged`: after the woodfolk's call, the giant walking into his hollow); `rooted_until_phase`
2 - he turns but never walks until his second phase. His attacks:

| Attack | What | Power | Its answer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Root-lash | the root-arm swept round 200 degrees to 25 m (1.5 s windup) | 58 | out of its reach, or under it at his feet |
| Roots erupt | a line of roots bursting out of the ground 46 m toward the giant (the line telegraphed) | 78 | aside from the line |
| The snare | a circle (6.5 m) at the giant's feet closes: rooted 2 s (90 % slower) | 28 | out of the circle before it closes |
| Rip and hurl | a dead tree torn up (shown in his fist) and thrown (a tumbling trunk) | 85 | aside |
| Stomp | his answer to a giant hugging his legs (within 5 m for 2 s): a ring of 11 m | 72 | do not stand at his feet |
| Lash (phase 2) | the lash quicker to recover (1.45 s windup, 1.2 s recovery) | 41 | as the lash |
| Falling trees (phase 2) | the grove's dead trees fall into telegraphed circles every 9 s (an arena hazard) | 25 | out of the circle |

**Phase 2** (half his health, or the knot broken): the `uproot` (he tears his roots out of the ground), then he walks
(3.8 m/s, runs 7.6) and hunts within his territory, his lash quicker, the trees falling. A shin broken drops him to a
knee and his shoulders, back and knot come down into a stage-5 giant's reach (his hurtboxes follow the kneel).

**His territory** (decision B3-2; `TitanTerritory`, the arena kind's `wake_on_sight` / `wake_flag`): a giant he sees
only discovers his grove (`poi.rootwarden_grove`) - he sleeps on; his hollow's engage trigger does nothing until
`world.rootwarden.called`; a blow wakes him anywhere. Uprooted, he hunts within 110 + 20 m of his grove and walks home
whole after a disengage: never out onto the rides.

## 3. His grove (`tools/data/world/greywood.json`, `tools/world/kinds/grove.gd`; decision B3-3)

At the end of a new ride (`warden_ride`, off the old root ride) in the deep Greywood, a hollow levelled at 17.5 m
(`rootwarden_grove` flatten feature): a ring of twelve colossal trees (6.5 m through, 46 m tall, buttress roots and
broken limbs), entered under a root arch (30 m span, 26 m high); his dead trees standing round his spot, stumps of the
ones he tore up, two fallen trunks, a dark pool. Four of the woodfolk's old watch-fires (braziers) stand within his
fire's reach, two resin pots join them once Wren calls the giant (`world.rootwarden.resin_laid`). The new layout kind
`grove` (styles `trunk`, `root_arch`, `dead`, `fallen`, `pool`, `stumps`, `saplings`); a scripted element of any kind
may now be flagged (the braziers: `Layout.flag_wraps`, `_flag_wrap`). The lookout on the ride (`rootwarden_lookout`,
96 m from him) shows him asleep among his dead trees; the warning area there teaches the prompt. Chunks `c0_4` (new),
`c0_5`, `c1_4` and their nav layers, the map, the skyline and the structures were rebuilt; a second build changes
nothing.

## 4. The woodfolk's quest (`quest.rootwarden`, `dialogue.woodwright_wren`; decision B3-4)

**Wren the woodwright** stands by her benches at Hollowmere. Her words follow the Greywood's story:

| The world | Wren |
| --- | --- |
| first met | who she is; what the warden is, that he wakes to a blow, how he fights (regrowth, fire, the knot, his shins) - outcome `told` |
| the Matriarch holds her den | "lift her blight first": no woodfolk will help wake him (`told_again`) |
| the Matriarch slain | the elders call the giant to lay him to rest before he strangles what she left (`call_slain`) |
| the Matriarch driven out and spared | the elders argued: if the troll-mother could be spared, why not the warden? He cannot be driven off; give him rest, quick and clean (`call_fled`) |
| called | the resin pots laid among his watch-fires (`called_again`) |
| he is at rest (with or without the call) | her thanks, a share of his heartwood; her woodworks open (`rested`), then `after` |

The call sets `world.rootwarden.called` (his hollow now wakes him) and `world.rootwarden.resin_laid`; its reward is an
iron ingot, 100 XP. The quest (`look`, `wren`, `blight`, `call`, `rest`, `thanks`) starts at Wren's words, Sedge's
(her sworn node's `warden`), the lookout or his grove's discovery; a giant who goes straight to him and strikes him
completes every stage up to `thanks` by his rest. Old saves load unchanged: nothing of B3 is set until the giant
meets it.

## 5. His intro (`cutscene.rootwarden`)

13 s, skippable at once, once a game (`GameSession.TITAN_INTROS`): played the first time his territory engages - his
wake. Over a stage-5 giant's shoulder at the root arch, the warden asleep among his dead trees; close on the root-arm,
its knot and the glowing fungi; a woodfolk's eye at his shin as he lifts his head and roars; the title as he stands.

## 6. The aftermath, his parts and the Warden set

His rest (`boss.rootwarden.defeated`) opens Wren's woodworks (a `condition_forge` that `works: ["warden_wood"]`) and
stacks the woodfolk's heartwood by her benches; once the giant has gone on beyond his line his territory sets
`world.greywood.grove_greened`: the dead trees and fallen trunks go, saplings stand where they were, and four
woodfolk gather his heartwood in the grove (`encounter.greywood_heartwood`). Hollowmere's settlement record mirrors
both (`warden_rested`, `grove_greened`). The roster's third entry is his (21.3 m; his outcome and defeated flags, his
grove as his sighting, his loot).

**His loot**: 1 heartwood (`item.warden_heartwood`), 4 bark (`item.warden_bark`), 3 amber sap (`item.amber_sap`); each
plate broken in the fight adds its own (the knot 2 sap, the others a bark). With Wren's thanks (a heartwood) and one
plate broken it pays for the whole set.

**The Warden set** (`armour_set.warden`, at Wren's woodworks once he is at rest): the Warden Mantle (shoulders,
physical 7), Bracers (arms, 3), Greaves (legs, 4) and the Heartwood (a trinket; no mesh); two pieces: health regrows
0.5 % a second after 4 s unhurt (`health_regen_frac_s`, the combatant's passive pools); four: poise +10 and 10 % less
poise damage taken. Its perk `regrowth` (0.3 % a second) is on every piece's list. The pieces are modelled
(`giant_garment.py` `_rigid_warden_*`): overlapping bark scales over the shoulder cap, trapezius and blade, round the
forearm, in two offset columns down the shin with a burl at the knee - no straps, no flat boards.

## 7. Sound (decision B3-5)

Three new world sounds from the generators (`tools/audio/gen_world_sfx.py`: `warden_lash` - a long creaking whip of
roots; `warden_erupt` - earth and roots bursting; `warden_regrow` - wood creaking and growing), 3.0 s registered. The
world's registered-seconds cap rises **170 -> 175 s** for 0.15.0-m15 (`build_audio.py` `REGISTERED_S_CAP`, versioned;
the tests' caps with it): 172.86 of 175 s, imported 9.274 of 9.5 MB. The bank routes them (the lash and its rage, the
erupt and the snare's impact, the regrowth and the sear) and the cue router plays a plate's regrowth, its searing and
the knot's wither (`plate_regrowing`, `plate_seared`, `titan_withered`); his rip and hurl reuse Stoneback's; his
region music is the Matriarch's set.

## 8. The carry-overs

- **The Ogre King's bell** reads as old dark bronze (`BRONZE_RGBA` 0.30/0.245/0.15, the verdigris greener and darker)
  and its two iron straps now run round the shell's front (the shell's outer face sampled at each strap's own height:
  its rings lay farther apart than a strap is wide, so the bands passed under the shell and showed only their ends).
- **No bot breaks the King's keg**: tried (a lock-switch plan of keg then crown, going round to his back in his
  recoveries): in three 240 s duels the stage-2 expert's 18-21 blows all landed on his front (torso, bell, a wheel),
  never his back - he turns on it faster than it gets round, as B2 found. The chain stays exercised by synthetic blows
  (`test_ogre_king_rig`, the capture tour). FOLLOWUPS keeps the row.
- **No Stoneback corpse prop** (not cheap enough beside the rest; the row stays).
- The duel bot's new rules (the knot first, fire, the falling trees, the stomp's ring) are its own: the ring's evasion
  answers a titan with a `hug` only (the Ogre King's slam keeps B2's band).
