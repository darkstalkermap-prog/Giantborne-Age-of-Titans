# P17a — Valley bot, invariants, metrics and the 30-minute stability run

Wave 9, solo. Branch `pkg/P17a`. Plan: `docs/plans/M2_M5_PLAN.md` §3.19, with §5.1 (the bot, its
skill profiles, the assisted mode, the metrics and the diagnosis), §5.2 (the stability run and its
invariants), decision 30 and gameplay review items 6–7. It also fixes the four items of
`docs/plans/FOLLOWUPS.md` assigned to P17a, each with a regression test, and one engine-error bug
the bot found (below).

## What was built

- **The Valley bot** (`scripts/debug/valley_bot.gd`, `class_name ValleyBot`): a goal-driven player
  that plays the whole quest line of the Valley from New Game under a village policy
  (`help_cache` | `help_ogre` | `intimidate` | `force`), with a novice or expert skill profile and an
  optional assisted mode. Every 6 ticks it reads the objectives (`Quests`) and picks a task; every
  tick it carries the task out through the input router (stick, edges, holds), the camera yaw and
  the lock key, as a player's input would. Task order: a fight when aware hostile enemies are near
  (elevated or unreachable ones are left to the ranged abilities); the Hollow's optional tutorial
  (two camera toggles, the dummies: light x3, heavy, Footfall, a lock; the sparring post: a block
  from its defence and a dodge rolled through the swing; bounded by 120 s); errands (rest below
  45 % health at a shrine within 70 m, reforge at an open forge with two iron, the armory's sword
  and crate, the quarry chest; each given up after 45 s of trying in a level load); the village
  policy at the `village` stage; else the `bot` hint of quest.main's current stage (`go`,
  `fight_encounter`, `kill`, `grow`, `hurl_at`, `break`, `interact`, `talk`, `wait`; below 50 %
  health before a `kill` objective such as the forest ogre it first rests at a shrine within 250 m
  on its own side of the target). It spends
  attribute points (Strength and Endurance alternating), fights with the best one-handed weapon it
  owns (a reforged weapon, else the sword, else the club; the oak only for the Deadfall, whose hint
  names it: its swing passes over a wolf or a soldier at the giant's feet) and plays on after deaths.
- **No screens.** The bot runs `PROCESS_MODE_ALWAYS` (the M1 Autopilot is pausable) and calls the
  screen-backed APIs directly: `Quests.start_dialogue` + `DialogueSession.choose_outcome` +
  `Quests.end_dialogue`, `Shrine.rest` / `Shrine.grow`, `Forge.upgrade`,
  `PlayerProgression.allocate`, `PlayerEquipment.equip_weapon`. Pickups, racks and the oak use the
  real interact edge. A pausing screen it finds open anyway (the end screen at the crown, a shrine
  screen an interact opened) is recorded and closed with `ScreenStack.close_all_static()` after
  0.25 s of real time (none had to be in the runs below); one open longer than 2 s is an invariant
  failure.
- **Movement** follows `NavigationServer3D.map_get_path` on the giant's current navigation layers,
  re-planned every 2 s, when the goal moves, or when the giant strays 3 m off the path. Stuck (the
  distance to the goal has not dropped by 1 m in 6 s while traversing): a sidestep and jump, then a
  re-plan, then the nearest `bot_route_points` marker toward the goal, then a teleport to the next
  path point (counted in `teleports`); one more is a `stuck` breach. When no path leads on from where
  the giant stands but one leads on from where it was (it chased an enemy into a pocket the bake
  did not join: the giant fits through 4 m gaps the giant layer's 2 m agent radius closes), the
  re-plan step walks it back along its breadcrumbs (one every 1.5 m, the last 48) to the newest one
  with a path. Props: the bot measures the gap from its capsule to the prop's own collision (a ray
  that looks past other colliders), walks within 60 % of its reach and hits; blows that take
  nothing off the prop move it 0.8 m closer after every three (the armory crate, 4.2 m away, sat at
  the very edge of a stage-2 club's swing).
- **Skill profiles** (`scripts/debug/bot_combat.gd`, `class_name BotCombat`). A blow is "dodge
  worthy" when it is unblockable, a dash or charge, an area slam or a projectile; the others are
  blocked or parried.
  - *novice*: perceives a telegraph aimed at the giant (`Events.telegraph_started`, however far:
    the decal shows) and the windup start (`Events.ability_started`) of an enemy after the giant
    that is on camera and within 8 m; it decides 250 ms (15 ticks) later, timing its answer from
    what it saw, never from controller phases. A blockable blow: a parry attempt half the time
    (±150 ms / 9 ticks of timing noise, so some become plain blocks), otherwise a late block half
    the time, else nothing. A dodge-worthy blow: a dodge 70 % of the time, a tenth of those toward
    the attacker. Abilities are decided at most once a second.
  - *expert*: reads the controller phase of every enemy winding up at the giant within 12 m (any
    distance for shots and charges; the Hollow's sparring post too, which is not in the "enemies"
    group), parries 6–10 ticks before the impact, always dodges a dodge-worthy blow (sideways and
    away), decides abilities every 0.1 s.
  Ability rules (plan §5.1): Mend below 50 % health, Stone Skin at the boss, Titan Surge with full
  fury at the boss or a crowd of three, Thunder Roar at three enemies within 8 m, Footfall or
  Sweeping Blow at two in reach, Runic Bolt at a casting mage, a far archer or an elevated target,
  Boulder Hurl at a far archer or an elevated target; Crushing Grip on a staggered small target,
  a grab of a staggered target when two or more threats are near (the throw locks the next one).
  The giant fights the enemy its lock points at: a different threat already locked and within 10 m
  stays the target instead of toggling the lock each time the nearest enemy changes (the blows
  follow the lock). Fleeing, routed and returning enemies are no longer threats. Against a man-sized
  target (radius under 0.8 m: soldiers, archers, mages, wolves) it stands where its swing lands:
  `reach_band` samples the light's and the heavy's weapon arc (`AttackGeometry`, as `PlayerAttack`
  sweeps it, at the current stage and weapon) against a target of the enemy's radius and height,
  giving the nearest and farthest distances that hit; the bot closes to 45 % into the light's band,
  backs off when within 0.25 m of its inner edge, and swings only inside a band (the heavy when the
  target guards or is staggered, or when only the heavy reaches). A large target (ogres, the Ogre
  King) is fought from 80 % of the giant's reach (the King: 90 %) plus half its radius.
- **The assisted mode** (`--bot-assist`): the hidden status `status.bot_assist` (damage taken
  x0.25, 600 s, refreshed below 60 s and after every load).
- **Metrics** (`scripts/debug/run_metrics.gd`, `class_name RunMetrics`): everything §5.1 lists,
  in AUTOPILOT_RESULT's `valley_bot` key. Per segment of §4.9 (`1_hollow` .. `7_return`, by
  quest.main's stage): raw and human-estimate seconds, combat / traversal / other seconds, deaths,
  damage taken, the lowest health fraction, checkpoints, rests (free ones apart), dialogue nodes,
  text read, screens, new districts. Per objective (every active quest's stage): seconds, visits,
  deaths, damage taken and dealt. Deaths with cause (archetype, action, position, objective,
  stage). Damage taken by archetype and by action; readability (hits whose attacker was off camera
  at its windup, hits that landed less than 0.45 s after their telegraph); time-to-kill by
  archetype and stage; mean engagement distance by stage; the enemies' AI-state histogram;
  ability uses, damage and share, and "used when ready" windows; the action counts (light, heavy,
  blocks, parries, blocked hits, dodges, evasions, grabs, throws); the XP timeline, level-ups and
  growth times; stuck events (position, objective, recovery); rewards granted and duplicates;
  peak active enemies, debris and hostiles within 40 m; node counts (every 60 s and 5 s after each
  load); the flags hash; and the human estimate (raw + 4 s per dialogue node + text at 15 chars/s
  + 8 s per screen a player would open + 10 s per district entered for the first time).
- **Diagnosis** (`scripts/debug/bot_diagnosis.gd`, `class_name BotDiagnosis`). A watchdog breach
  (240 s on one quest.main stage, 480 s on the boss; more than 5 deaths on one stage in an
  unassisted run; stuck after every recovery) records the reason, the bot's task, the policy and a
  diagnosis: is there a path on the giant's layers to the objective (closed breakable nav links
  count as open; goals near a ledge are snapped to the layer), and can the objective's condition
  still hold in this world (kill targets alive or killed as asked, a prop the giant can still
  break at its stage, a flag not set to something else for good, items still somewhere, through
  `all` / `any` / `not`)? **world_blocked** = no path or a condition that cannot hold (a softlock);
  **bot_failed** = both fine (the bot could not do something possible; tuned in P17b). No path from
  where the giant stands but one from one of its breadcrumbs (`came_from`) is a pocket it walked
  into, so `bot_failed`, with the evidence saying so.
- **Invariants** (`BotDiagnosis.check_invariants`, every tick; plan §5.2): the player's position is
  finite and above y −50; the lock target exists and is alive; pending hits < 512; debris ≤ 40; at
  most 2 melee and 2 ranged tokens on any entity; no reward granted twice; active combatants ≤ the
  director's cap; no entity id registered twice and the Sim registry holds the node in the tree; no
  pausing screen open more than 2 s; and node growth (the node count 5 s after each load, spawned
  actors left out, growing by more than 10 % over 5 consecutive reload cycles). Any failure ends
  the run with exit code 3.
- **The Autopilot** (hotspot `scripts/debug/autopilot.gd`): `--scenario=valley` (with
  `--mode=valley --new-game`) makes the bot, adds a PerfProbe and reports AUTOPILOT_RESULT with
  `valley_bot` and `invariants_ok`; exit code 2 (`EXIT_SCENARIO_FAILED`) on a watchdog breach (a
  loop run: only on a `world_blocked` one) or an unfinished single run, 3 (`EXIT_INVARIANT`) on an
  invariant failure; errors still win (1). `--result-file=user://...` also writes the result JSON
  there. The Autopilot runs `PROCESS_MODE_ALWAYS` for the bot and ends a finished run even while
  the end screen pauses the tree.
- **--bot-loop** (the 30-minute stability run): after each completed slice the bot starts a new
  game with the next village policy (rotation from `--bot-village`) and goes on until `--autoplay`
  ends; a `bot_failed` breach reloads the last checkpoint and continues; every slice is summarised
  in `loop_runs`.
- **--bot-trace=S**: one line every S simulated seconds (the task, the giant's position, health,
  controller state and lock, a fight's target with its health, state and AI state) for diagnosing
  a stall.
- **`tools/run_bots.sh`**: the batch runner P17b uses (below).
- **`tests/slow/test_bot_smoke.gd`** (below), and the follow-up regression tests in
  `tests/integration/test_followup_fixes_p17a.gd` (default suite).

## Command line

```
tools/gd.sh --headless --path . --fixed-fps 60 -- --mode=valley --new-game --scenario=valley \
    --autoplay=2400 --bot-village=help_cache --bot-skill=novice --bot-seed=1 [--bot-assist] \
    [--bot-loop] [--bot-trace=2] [--result-file=user://bot_runs/x.json] [--save-dir=user://bot_saves/x]
```

`--save-dir` keeps the bot's checkpoints out of the player's slot. A checkpoint save of a run can be
replayed from where it stopped with `--continue` instead of `--new-game` (with the same
`--save-dir`), which is how the stalls below were reproduced.

`tools/run_bots.sh [--preset=assisted|balance|stability] [--jobs=3] [--timeout=900]
[--autoplay=S] [--tag=NAME] [--extra="ARGS"] [--dry-run] [--summary] [policy:skill:seed[:assist] ...]`
starts each run as its own headless Godot process through `tools/gd.sh` with a wall-clock limit
(`timeout 900` by default), at most 3 at once, writes `user://bot_runs/<tag>/<name>.json` (the
AUTOPILOT_RESULT), `<name>.log` and `<name>.exit` (exit code and wall seconds), and prints a
summary table (run, exit, wall s, done, simulated s, human-estimate s, deaths, breaches and their
classes, invariant failures, errors, loops, the last objective of a failed run, a note). `<name>` is
`<policy>_<skill>_<seed>[_assist][_loop]`. Presets: `assisted` (the four policies, novice, seed 1,
assisted: the M4 complete-loop gate), `balance` (P17b's matrix: novice x {help_cache, help_ogre,
intimidate} x seeds 1–5, novice x force x seeds 1–3, expert x the four policies x seed 1) and
`stability` (`--bot-loop --bot-assist`, help_cache first, `--autoplay=1800`). The exit code is 0
when every run exited 0. Start it with `run_in_background`; `--summary --tag=NAME` reprints the table.

## Decisions and deviations

1. **Doc name** `docs/systems/P17a.md` (the task); §3.19 names `P17a-valley-bot.md`.
2. **Result files** go to `user://bot_runs/<tag>/<policy>_<skill>_<seed>[_assist][_loop].json`
   (§3.19: `user://bot_runs/<policy>_<skill>_<seed>.json`): a tag per batch keeps batches apart and
   the assisted and loop runs apart from the plain ones. The Autopilot writes the file itself
   (`--result-file=`), so a run killed by its time limit leaves its log and `.exit` file and the
   table reports it as a failure with its last objective.
3. **`DialogueSession.choose_outcome`** rather than `Quests.dialogue.choose`: it picks the choice
   that sets the policy's outcome (`offer_cache`, `help_cache`, `intimidate`, ...) wherever it is in
   the tree.
4. **The tutorial is played** (it is optional in the game): the bot plays the Hollow's tutorial
   (bounded by 120 s) so the metrics cover the first ten minutes as a new player meets them.
5. **The invariants live in `BotDiagnosis`** (a static, stateless file) and the bot calls them every
   tick; the pausing-screen invariant is also checked while the tree is paused (no tick runs then).
6. **Assisted mode is a status**, not a damage hook: `status.bot_assist` (x0.25 damage taken), so the
   game's own damage path is unchanged.
7. **The bot's lock decides the target** when it holds another threat within 10 m (the blows follow
   the lock); see "What was built".
8. **Additions to §5.1's rules**, each found needed in the runs: `--bot-trace=S`; the errand give-up
   (45 s); the rest before a `kill` objective (below 50 % health, a shrine within 250 m); the reach
   band (§5.1 says "approach to 0.8 x reach", from which a stage-3 club's swing misses a soldier);
   the oak only for the Deadfall; the walk back out of a navmesh pocket.
9. **Follow-up fixes outside the owned paths**: `tests/test_runner.gd`,
   `scripts/world/encounter_director.gd`, `scripts/core/game_session.gd`, `scripts/quests/quests.gd`,
   `scripts/main.gd` (the four FOLLOWUPS items) and `scripts/actors/enemy_actor.gd` (the bug the bot
   found). One existing slow test follows the spread spawns: `test_valley_walkthrough`'s
   `_talk_to_elder` waits up to 10 ticks for the elder (the village folk's seventh member now spawns
   on the fourth tick of a proximity activation; it asserted her presence on the first).
10. **A completion checkpoint ignores aware enemies only**: dead or holding a body still defers it,
    and Quit to title commits it.
11. **One PerfProbe per bot run** (under the tree root, so a death reload or a loop's new game does
    not restart it): PERF_RESULT and `perf` cover the whole run. Other scenarios keep theirs under
    the Autopilot.

## Known gaps

- The skill profiles approximate people; their numbers (latency, parry and dodge rates, noise) are
  §5.1's and not fitted to playtests (the usability sessions are the owner's).
- The bot uses the screen-backed APIs, so screens' own flows (focus, buttons) are covered by
  P16's tests, not by the bot.
- `bot_failed` breaches are tuning material for P17b (the bot could not do something possible);
  only `world_blocked` ones are stability failures.
- **Where blows land is not `melee_reach_m`** (3.5 / 4.8 / 6.5 m by stage): measured with a soldier
  in front (centre to centre), a club's light lands to about 3.3 m at stage 1, 4 m at stage 2 and
  3.5 m at stage 3 (the heavy to about 4–5 m), and the oak's light only from about 3 m out to 6 m (it
  passes over a target at the giant's feet). The bot measures this itself (`reach_band`); for P17b:
  a player standing at "reach" distance misses.
- **A pocket of the village the giant layer's navmesh does not join**: the yard between cottage_03,
  cottage_04, cottage_05, the watchtower and the north palisade (about x 62–72, z −50 to −35). Its
  ways in are 4 m gaps, closed by the giant layer's 2 m agent radius, yet a stage-2 or stage-3 giant
  (capsule 1.2 / 1.65 m) walks through them. A force-run bot chasing the ambush archer in there found
  no path out (fixed in the bot: it walks back along its breadcrumbs). For P17b / a layout nudge:
  widen one gap to 5 m or more, or block them all.
