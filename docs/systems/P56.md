## What was built

### 1. Hair and beards (`tools/giant/kit/giant_hair.py`)

Braids are P51's tubes (`braid_tube`) with ties where the style says (`ties`; M10's styles keep their two rings) and, new,
closed for the circlet. Every style is within M10's caps (hair 6,000, beards 4,000). Each style's index entry carries
`bead_anchors` (a count); M10's braided hair and braided beard have anchors too (between their own rings), so 11 styles take
beads.

### 2. Beads (`giant_hair.bead_shape`, `export_game.bead_arrays`)

Three bead sets, each **one part** (`parts/Beads_bone|bronze|silver.glb`, 1,548 / 3,096 / 2,064 triangles): the set's bead
(a long bone bead, two flat bronze rings, a round silver bead) on every anchor of every anchored style, each vertex skinned as
the nearest vertex of its braid (the bead rides the braid's chain) and carrying its style's code in UV2.x. The index entry's
`styles` maps a style mesh to its code; `GiantLook.bead_mask` sets the cloth shader's new `bead_mask` (a bit a code) from the
look's hair and beard and their beads, and the cloth shader collapses the beads of every other code. So a set is one draw call
whatever the styles, a style without anchors wears none (the plan's rule, enforced by the data), and a helm's cut leaves the
hair's beads off. The hair's and the beard's beads are chosen apart (`hair.beads`, `beard.beads`): the fullest look draws two
sets (§2.12's 24 counts them). `data/appearance/set_bead.json`: none (the default), bone beads, bronze rings, silver beads.

### 3. Designs (`tools/giant/kit/designs.py`)

Twenty more: **twelve tattoos** (four for the legs, regions 11-13: thigh bands, a serpent coiling down the right leg, shin
runes, thigh claws; and two more a region: forearm runes, shoulder spirals, a chest raven, a rune column, a sun wheel, spine
chevrons, temple spirals, tear lines), **four war paints** (two for the face: an eye band, half the face; two for the body: a
chest slash, leg stripes) and **four scars** (a split lip, a beast's bite on the shoulder, a thigh gash, a spear scar under the
ribs). 49 designs in 8 layers (6 before).

- **The two-paint rule** (`designs.paint_problems`, run by `build` with the packing rule): a face paint draws in the face's
  region alone and a body paint never in it, so the two worn at once never share a region's area (the skin's `region_paint`
  holds one paint a region).
- **Ten scars in the two scar layers**: the skin shader reads two scar layers (`scar_layer`, `scar_layer_b`); the four new
  scars share channels of those layers with scars of other regions under the packing rule (`designs._free_spot`), and the
  skin shader gates each scar channel by the worn scars' regions (new `scar_regions`, `scar_regions_b`, a bit a region;
  `GiantLook.scar_uniforms`). Six are worn at once (P53's `MAX_SCARS`).
- Strokes keep M10's 4 cm rule where they read in play (the bands, the claws, the serpent, the stripes, the wheel, the
  chevrons); the face's are finer, as M10's face designs are.

### 4. Face keys (`export_game.FACE_KEYS`; probe 3 passed, so they were built)

Six shape keys on `Body_Head` named as `Appearance.FACE_KEYS` (`jaw`, `chin`, `nose`, `brow`, `cheeks`, `age`), each a mix of
the vendored CC0 detail targets the giant's own phenotype uses (chin width and bones and head-square; chin prominence and
height; nose volume, hump and width; brow and forehead forward; cheekbones and cheek volume; head age, eye bags and laugh
lines), at -1 the same displacement negated. Copied to every beard and shell, to the hairline strips (each hair style's
fringe, `giant_hair.STRIP_RANGES`) and to the bead sets: every card vertex follows the skin under its card's nearest root
corner (`card_root_vertices`), a shell its own skin vertex; a mesh takes a key only where the key moves it by 0.5 mm. The
creator's Face category shows them from `data/appearance/set_face.json`.

What it took beyond probe 3's export settings (`export_tangents`, `export_morph_tangent`):

- **The renderer's blend shapes lose the tangent's sign.** Seen twice: (a) every hair card's strand highlight flipped at any
  key's weight (a debug render of `BINORMAL` turned from one sign to the other at weight 1); (b) at a key's +1 the head drew
  a black quad between the brows and a black spot by the nostril (the binormal went to zero there: the normal map's furrows)
  even with morph tangents exported, while -1 was clean. Fixes: the hair shader turns `BINORMAL` to the strand's root-to-tip
  way found from the screen derivatives of the position and UV (`giant_hair.gdshader`); the body's colour moved to the
  corners with its alpha the tangent's sign (`export_game.tangent_sign_alpha`; P48's alpha was reserved at 1) and the skin
  shader rebuilds `BINORMAL = cross(NORMAL, TANGENT) x sign` (unchanged drawing at rest: the default face differs from the
  shader without it by 0.0026 of a channel on average, with the opposite sign by 0.14).
- **`giant.glb` is imported without `ensure_tangents`** (it carries its own tangents now); the parts keep it and their keys
  carry positions only (Blender derives morph normals from the keyed shape's own normals, not the cards' baked volume
  normals).
- **The shell stack copies the keys** (`GiantLook.shell_stack`): a shell beard or crop keeps them on every layer; `apply`
  sets the face again after a fresh stack.

Measured (`tests/slow/test_catalogue_render.gd`): every key at -1 and +1 against the same shape blended on the CPU, the darkest
9 x 9 patch -2.4/255 (the bound 8). Before the binormal fix: -50/255 at +1 for four keys. The beard roots move with the skin
under them within 2 mm at -1 and +1 (`CatalogueTest`; before the nearest-corner rule, 23 mm at the jaw's edge, where the
targets' displacement changes fast). The base shape is unchanged (P48's freeze test hashes the head's positions and passes).

### 5. Presets (`data/appearance/preset_*.json`)

Fourteen: M10's eight unchanged (each is its M10 record migrated: the migration's oracle checks them) and six new that show
the wardrobe, the new styles, beads, designs and the face and build sliders: **the Huscarl** (the huscarl outfit in steel and
woad, long loose hair under the iron helm, a long forked beard with silver beads, an eye band), **the Bronze Lord** (horned
helm, bronze breastplate, kilt, fur cloak, twin braids and a chin braid ringed in bronze, gilded torc and arm ring, forearm
runes), **the Bone Reaver** (bone spaulders, chest wrap, loincloth - the bare hips of FOLLOWUPS LA-5's row dressed - a side-
shaved head and a braided moustache with bone beads, half the face in ash, a serpent down the leg, three new scars), **the Far
Traveller** (the traveller's gear without the hood, long loose grey-shot hair, mutton chops, temple spirals), **the Highland
Herdsman** (kilt and vest without the cap, a circlet braid with silver beads, shoulder spirals) and **the Berserker** (bare-
chested under a fur mantle, a raven and a slash of blood across the chest, a top knot and a long forked beard, shin runes).

### 6. GiantLook and the shaders

`giant_look.gd`: bead visibility and `bead_mask`; the scar regions; the long forked beard's tips thin as the long beards' do
(`LOCK_BEARDS`); the face re-applied after a shell stack; the stack copies blend shapes. Shaders: `giant_cloth` (`bead_mask`),
`giant_skin` (`scar_regions`, the binormal from the stored sign), `giant_hair` (the strand's way). `build_giant.py`: the index
copies `bead_anchors`, `styles`, `face_keys`; `giant.glb` without `ensure_tangents`.

## How it looks (the package's own captures)

- **Reads well**: the long loose hair from behind (the best of the five: long, layered, loose strands); the twin braids over the
  chest; the side-shaved top from the side and behind; the chin braid and the braided moustache; the long forked beard; the
  bronze rings and silver beads; the leg serpent, thigh bands, sun wheel, spine chevrons, raven and claws; the eye band and the
  half face; the thigh gash and the shoulder bite; the nose, chin and brow keys; the huscarl, bronze lord, bone reaver and
  berserker presets.
- **Weak**: the circlet still stands a little off the head at the temples; the top knot's tuft is thin and the top knot's braid
  does not sway (it lies above every chain's root); the parting of the twin braids and the top of the long loose hair show the
  scalp's tint in dark blotches; short-card beards (the chin braid's and the chops' base, the moustache) read as square
  patches up close, as M10's braided beard's base does; the bone beads look grey-blue in the creator's light (the light comes
  from behind them there); the jaw, cheek and age keys are subtle at the Face framing; the far traveller and the young giant
  are the likest presets from the front; a scar's thumbnail (P55's flat picture of its channel) shows the other scars of its
  channel, as the tattoos' already did.

## Deviations

- **Owned paths stretched**: `assets/shaders/giant_cloth.gdshader` (the bead mask; §1.5 gives W33's P56 every giant shader,
  §3.9 names hair and skin), `tests/unit/test_giant_look.gd`, `tests/unit/test_appearance_schemas.gd`,
  `tests/integration/test_giant_assets.gd`, `test_giant_parts.gd`, `test_skinned_rig.gd` (counts and the bead category),
  `scenes/player/giant_model.tscn` (regenerated by its builder from the manifest), the new probe and slow suite.
- **The scars share channels by region** instead of a third scar layer (the shader's two layers stay; a region gate per
  channel).
- **The body's colour alpha now carries the tangent's sign**, and `giant.glb` is imported without `ensure_tangents` (probe 3
  did not show the sign loss: it measured the brow key only and the hair not at all).
- **The rendered suite is over the slow cap**: about 12-13 s against 5 s (styles, 24 face frames, 14 presets); it is the
  plan's own test list, drawn once.
- **Hair under a helm cut by a plane** (P54's row to P56) was not built: the new styles take P54's cut by length (the top knot,
  the side-shaved top, the long loose hair and the circlet are hidden under the helms, the twin braids drawn whole).
