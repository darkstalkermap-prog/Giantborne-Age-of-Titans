## What was built

### 1. A whole look in a third of a millisecond again (`scripts/presentation/giant_look.gd`, `giant_model.gd`)

`tests/integration/test_giant_preview.gd`'s "a whole look is applied in under 1 ms" read 1.04-1.5 ms after P56
(73 meshes, 69 of them parts). Measured by part (headless, the best of 40 alternating looks, µs): the walk over the
index's meshes 205, the materials 275 (after the first fix; 450 before: a `get_meta` and `set_meta` on every
uniform), the parts' release 35-110, the region codes and scars 70.

- `apply()` walks a **plan** made once per root and index (`_plan`: id, category, node, shader kind, own colour,
  dye slot; made again when the index or a node changes); `mesh_node` caches by mesh id.
- Each kind's uniforms are worked out **once per look**; a material whose last look set the same values is
  skipped (`_put`, `META_LAST`), and a hidden piece keeps its last values until it is shown again. `set_param`
  forgets a material's last values (so `set_garment_fade` and any later set are honoured); `forget_applied(root)`
  for a raw `set_shader_parameter`.
- `set_param` keeps its names' Dictionary in place; the scar layers are found once per index;
  `GiantModel.release_unused` passes a part that is already hidden and let go at once.

The test's best of three: **1.06 ms -> 0.32 ms** (alternating two looks: 0.45). It also checks that a changed value
lands, a value set outside the look is set again, and a garment shown again wears the new dye.

### 5. The thumbnails on the Web: names (plan §2.7's rule, measured)

On the Web build the first frame of a grid read **3.3-3.5 s** (the outfits' and the armour's), every one the
read-back of a picture's frame (`readback_max_ms` 3,894; most read-backs 0.7-3.9 s), while the parts' loads took
1-11 ms each (59 loads, 132 ms in all). Plan §2.7: a thumbnail above the stall rule on the Web means the garment grids
there show names with the live preview only. So on the Web no picture is made (`ThumbnailCache.pictures_wanted()`);
`--creator-pictures=on|off` overrides it (a browser on a real GPU may read back in milliseconds: the owner's to try).
The first piece put on from a grid (`pick`, the part loaded and drawn in the live preview) read 0.65-0.94x.

### 6. The thumbnails on the desktop (`ui/screens/creator/`)

- A picture is made at **the cell's size in window pixels** (96 to 256 px in steps of 32; the size is part of the
  key): at 1920x1080 a cell is about 175 px, and its 96 px picture was stretched soft. Now 192 px.
- A tattoo's or war paint's picture is **the design alone on the giant** at its region's framing from its side (no
  beard over a face design): P55's flat picture of its layer and channel showed the other designs of that channel
  and every island of the UV map. The flat pictures' code is gone.
- An outfit's picture frames the head to the knees (`outfit`); a belt accessory's aims at the piece itself
  (`GiantPreview.frame_on`, `aim_box`, framing `AIM`: its mesh's box, from the side it hangs on).
- The parts a picture shows are loaded ahead of it, for at most 4 ms a frame.

### 7. The stack check (`tests/gate/test_wardrobe_stack_render.gd`; `wardrobe_render_probe.gd --stack`)

200 random slot looks (half dressed as Randomize dresses him, half with nearly every slot and every accessory slot
filled: 7.7 garments a look), each standing, at the run's mid-stride and in the roar, from the front, behind or
three-quarters; each garment in a flat colour, two frames half a millimetre apart at 320x240. **The measure** is the
share of the giant's pixels that change in patches (a changed pixel with at least three changed neighbours).
**The positive control** (P54's row): the garment most seen from the front (the mail hauberk) drawn again on its
skeleton 0.01 mm out along its normals. Measured: the 200 looks' worst view 3 px in patches (0.0001 of the giant)
against a floor of 0.0005; the control **413 px (0.0086)**. The fullest look's own measure (interior pixels that
flip) read the control as 48 pixels against 41 in a clean look: it would not have seen this z-fighting, so it is
kept in the report only. About 105 s in the gate tier.
