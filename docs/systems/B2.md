# B2 — The Ogre King as a signature titan; the armour look pass

The Valley's boss - the tutorial's finale, fought as a small giant - raised to the standard Stoneback set: his own
asset in the kit, his trophies as breakable plates with their own break effects, his intro, Thornwick's change after
his fall, his titan parts and a set made from them; then the look pass the owner asked of B1's armour, and A5's and
B1's small carry-overs. Built on `pkg/b2` from the dev head (`0.15.0-m15`, B1 merged); the version stays
**0.15.0-m15**. The seeded 120 s soak is unchanged (41 attacks, 33 hits resolved, 4 enemy kills, 2 fence breaks, 7,201
frames, 0 errors).

## 1. The Ogre King, as an asset (`tools/giant/kit/ogre_king.py`, `ogre_king_moves.py`; `build_people.py --ogre_king`)

**Design.** An old ogre gone to fat who has made himself a king out of what he took from the Valley's people - not a
re-skin of Stoneback (a quarried-stone giant with a trunk): a bloated, olive-grey brute on the brutes' modules (the
people's CC0 human with MakeHuman's macros, the giant's proportions function with a table of his own, the brutes'
reshape: a great gut pushed out and down, a heavy jaw with its tusks, the hunch 14 degrees against the ogres' 24 so he
stands over a stage-3 giant), wearing his trophies:

| Piece | What it is | Triangles | Where it rides |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `trophy_crown` | a town gate's iron ring hammered round his brow, seven shield shards riveted to it as its points (faded paint over the wood), an iron boss at the front | 608 | rigid on the head |
| `trophy_bell` | Thornwick's great bell split and beaten into an oval breastplate over his gut - its shoulder curling in at the chest, its mouldings raised, its lip flared at the belly's foot, bronze patched with verdigris - on two iron straps round his trunk | 1,260 | the skin's weights under each point, stiffened |
| `trophy_wheel_l`, `_r` | a broken cart wheel dished over each shoulder (hub, ten spokes - two gone to stubs -, a length of felloe and iron tyre missing), its rim bent out onto the shoulder's round | 976 each | rigid on the blended weights of the shoulder's top |
| `trophy_keg` | a hooped cask (bulged staves, four hoops) across the small of his back on a rope strap | 672 | the back's skin, stiffened |
| `chain_belt` | a heavy chain round his hips sagging under the gut, five men's helmets (kettle hats and nasal helms) hung by their chin straps - man-sized against him | 1,980 | the skin under each link, stiffened |
| `ram` | a town's battering ram: an oak log with a rope grip, three iron bands, two carrying rings and an iron cap; carried on his right shoulder | 832 | rigid on the right hand |

The body and his loincloth are one 8,926-triangle mesh in one atlas (the bake's skin band repainted with the ogres'
hide), his pieces' wood a tileable 256 px oak texture (`ogre_king_wood_*.png`; the grain down V), their iron and the
bell's bronze vertex colours on the stone shader (whose cracks learned `crack_grain`: splits along the grain, the
fresh wood pale at their lips; dents on metal). A far mesh of 300 triangles.

**His height: 8.5 m to the top of his head, 9.03 m to his crown's points** - P11a's 8.5 m kept (his fight's reach,
hurtboxes, capsule and the lock's 1.3 ratio are set to it). A stage-3 giant (7.92 m) comes to his brow, a stage-2
giant (5.49 m) to his chest; his walk carries the crown at 7.7-8.1 m, his idle at 8.4-8.5 m (`checks.crown_m`).

**Clips** (each key solved as Stoneback's - legs by their feet, the arms holding the ram by `Holder.hold` - and
slerped on eases; the left fist re-solved onto the ram every frame it holds it):

## 2. His fight: the trophies as plates (`data/enemies/ogre_king.json`; decision B2-1, B2-2)

He is a `TitanEnemy` now (on `OgreBoss`: his phases, sequences, chained combo, charge and arena are as they were; his
brain a `TitanBrain`, which is an `OgreBossBrain`). P11a's capsule, anchors (torso, knees) and head, torso and legs
hurtboxes are kept where they were; each trophy adds a hurtbox and a lock anchor (`tools/builders/titans.gd`
`KING_HURTBOXES`, `KING_ANCHORS`), every one following his kneel.

| Trophy | Region | Stone | Keeps from the body | Poise kept | Broken | Its break | Loot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| crown | head | 180 | 40 % | none | head x1.5 | stagger 2.6 s and **unbalance**: every later blow's poise x1.6 | crown-iron |
| bell | belly | 260 | 30 % | none | **belly a weak point x1.5** | stagger 2.0 s | bell-bronze |
| wheel_l, wheel_r | shoulders | 150 each | 30 % | none | x1.3 | stagger 1.4 s | 2 wood scrap each |
| keg | back | 140 | 25 % | none | x1.3 | **topple**: down on a knee for 4 s (once), his crown and shoulders into a stage-2 giant's reach; every blow x1.15, a Heavy x1.3 besides | - |

An intact trophy keeps a quarter to two fifths of a blow and none of its poise: a new player's blows still stagger
him in his recoveries (x3.5 there, as P11a), and the trophies are a depth for aimed play, not a wall. **The lock of a
small giant frames only what it reaches comfortably** (`lock_comfortable_only`: a plate above 0.9 of the giant's
height is not framed on its own - his knee at stage 1 as P11a's, the bell at stage 2), so a novice's swings land; the
lock-switch (`{target_next}`, a flick)
picks a trophy in reach, and a stage-3 giant's lock frames the trophy nearest its aim among those it strikes
squarely. The chain to the crown: the keg (round to his back in a recovery, or after his charge into a pillar) drops
him to a knee, and his crown comes down to 5.8 m, within a stage-2 giant's reach.

**Generic rules (reusable)**: `PlateRules.EFFECT_UNBALANCE` ("unbalance", `on_break.poise_mult`; `PlateSet.
poise_taken_mult`, reset by a new attempt); `PlateRules.trophy_cues` (a plate's own break cue, and the wood's beside a
trophy of wood and iron); the enemy record's `lock_comfortable_only` and `ai.corpse_keep_s`.

**His presentation** (`OgreKingRig`, `OgreKingModel`): the clips from the brain's state as TitanRig's; each trophy its
own material, cracked below 60 % of its stone and badly below 30 % (the wood split along its grain, the metal
dented); broken, it hides, a static copy of it falls from where it was, tumbling, and lies on the ground; splinters
(long thin debris in the wood's colour, iron bits for the metal pieces) and dust; the slam's dust and shake where the
ram's head strikes; dying, the ram falls from his fist. Broken trophies come back whole on a new attempt.

**Sounds** (no new file; the registered SFX seconds stay 169.86 of 170 in the world, the Valley's 108.57 of 110): the
bell's cracked toll (the alarm bell pitched down to 0.58), the crown's iron (the guard break at 0.72) and the
splintering wood, the wheels' and the keg's wood (the keg's burst a crash pitched up). `sfx.break.trophy.*` in
`data/audio/bank_world.json`.

## 3. Around his fight

- **His intro** (`data/cutscenes/cutscene_ogre_king.json`, 13 s, 780 ticks): played by `GameSession` the first time his
  fight engages (the arena's gate closing behind the giant: `boss_engaged`; `TITAN_INTROS`), once a game
  (`cinematics_seen`, and not twice in a session: a death before the next checkpoint does not bring it back), never in
  a tool run or headless; skippable at once. Four shots: over a stage-2 giant's shoulder at the gate, up close from the
  bell to the crown, his roar at a small giant's eye level, the title as he comes. Its lines teach the trophies. A
  cutscene record may name its `level` now: `--cinematic=ogre_king` (and `tools/export_cinematic.sh`) plays it in a
  Valley New Game that saves nothing.
- **The Valley folk's warning**, sparingly: `tutorial.ogre_king_trophies` on the causeway into Kingsfall (what he
  wears; wood and iron splinter; the lock-switch picks a trophy), `tutorial.ogre_king_down` when the keg drops him
  (`titan_toppled`, a new tutorial event: his crown is in reach); `tutorial.titan_plates` speaks of plates or trophies.
- **Thornwick after his fall** (as Millford after Stoneback's): the layout's `aftermath` - a FlagVisibility shown once
  `world.ogre_king.defeated` holds: a trophy stand in the square (two timber posts and a beam, trestles) with his
  pieces on it (`TrophyDisplay`: his crown hung from the beam, his wheels against the posts, his ram on the trestles,
  the bell set down before it) and two of Thornwick's banners; four more of its folk come out to it
  (`encounter.village_feast`, never while the village is hostile). The save mirrors it (`poi.thornwick`
  `kings_trophies`).
- **His record**: the roster's second titan with his enemy, his arena's boss id, his defeated flag, his height and his
  loot table; an old save past the Valley shows him slain (`sync_from_flags`), a mid-Valley one unmet.
- **His body** stays where he fell for five minutes (`ai.corpse_keep_s` 300, Stoneback's too: `EncounterDirector.
  corpse_keep_s_of`).

## 6. The carry-overs

- **A cutscene closed by any path gives the camera back** (B1's last-pass row): `CutsceneScreen._exit_tree` restores the
  camera, the hidden giant and titans and frees its stage when it is freed without a skip (`ScreenStack.close_all()`).
- **The Equipment tab's slot rows wrap** a long reforged name and its perk onto a second line (never cut short).
- **A titan's body stays a landmark** for five minutes after his death (`ai.corpse_keep_s`); a static prop at the
  reopened quarry was not made (FOLLOWUPS: the world's chunk scenes would be rebuilt for it).
- **Stoneback's left-knee kneel** was not made: its own pose, its own `KNEEL_FOLLOW` positions and the clips
  regenerated are more than a carry-over (the FOLLOWUPS row stays).
