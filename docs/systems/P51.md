# P51 — The giant's look pass II

Wave 30, lane B (the wave's asset writer), beside P50 (the giant's motion). Branch `pkg/P51`, from the tag
`wave-29`. Plan: `docs/plans/M11_PLAN.md` §3.4 (design items 1-7), with §2.4 (the giant's shaders II), §2.12
(budgets), §0.4 decisions 8 and 9, §7's probes 5 and 6, and §1.7 (test caps: default 0.3 s, slow 5 s, gate 0).
The goal: every row of Part A's gate that is graphics, each against a frame.

Every frame here was drawn by Mesa's llvmpipe (a software renderer: this container has no GPU) under Xvfb with
the Compatibility renderer; frame times are a software renderer's and are not the owner's machines'.

## What was built (plan §3.4's design list)

### 1. Hair shading (`assets/shaders/giant_hair.gdshader`, `kit/giant_hair.py`)

- **Head-centred normals baked into the cards.** `giant_hair.volume_normal`: outward from the head's centre
  above it and from a vertical axis through the centre below it (hanging hair and a beard face out, not down).
  `NORMAL_ROUND` is 1: a card's mesh normal is that volume normal (P46 had half of the direction from the
  head's centre).
- **`volume_normal`** (GiantLook: `VOLUME_NORMAL` 0.75): the shader blends each card's own facing (its
  triangle's, from the screen derivatives, taken outside every branch) towards the mesh's volume normal.
- **Two highlights along the strands**: `spec_primary` (white, shifted to the tip by `spec_shift`) and
  `spec_secondary` (tinted by the hair's colour, shifted to the root by `spec_shift_2`, broader), both shifted
  per strand by `strand_noise` x the atlas's strand id. M10's `spec_strength` stays declared and unused.
- **Softer card-on-card shadow** (`shadow_soft` 0.7 to 0.8) and a little light through the thin edges with the
  light behind. `density` (1.3) lifts the atlas's coverage a little (the volume shading showed the scalp through
  the thinnest cards more).

### 2. Hair geometry (`kit/giant_hair.py`, `kit/export_game.py`, `assets/shaders/giant_shell.gdshader`)

- **Shells** for the crop and the short beard (`giant_hair.SHELLS`). The kit cuts the skin under the style's
  mask (the body's `m_scalp` or `m_beard` per vertex, faces whose mean is above 0.003), lifted 0.6 / 0.5 mm off
  the skin, with the body's own UVs; that is the part's mesh (`parts/Hair_crop.glb` 1,052 triangles,
  `Beard_short.glb` 2,212). The index entry carries `"shell": {"mask", "height_m"}`. At load time
  `GiantLook.shell_stack` copies the surface `shell_layers()` times into one surface (12 on the desktop, 6 on
  the Web, probe 6), the layer in UV2.x, with two LODs (a third of the layers, then the base) - one draw call a
  style, no shipped bytes for the layers, never a shadow. `giant_shell.gdshader` lifts each layer along the
  normal and combs its tips (`comb`, mesh space), keeps a strand of `shell_strands.png` (a cell a strand: its
  section, its length, its id) while the layer is below its length and its section wider than the layer, reads
  the style's density from the skin's own mask map a few mip levels down (the beard area ends in a hard line on
  the cheeks; the shells thin out over a few centimetres instead), darkens towards the skin and shades clumps.
  Under a shell beard the skin keeps 0.7 of its stubble and beard tint (`SHELL_SKIN_TINT`). The cards of both
  styles are still grown, and dropped, so the random stream of every later style is as it was.
- **Braids as geometry**: `giant_hair.braid_tube` - a seven-sided tube along each braid's path, its radius
  rippling with a plait's three strands that turn along it, narrowing to the tie, with two bead rings (at two
  fifths and at the tie). The hair shader draws a tube (UV2.x = 1) with the atlas's dense column and chevron
  grooves, a bead (UV2.x = 2) opaque in `bead_color`; one draw call with the style. The braids hang whole from
  where they leave the head (`giant_hair.BRAID_MASKS` replaces `export_game.braid_vertices`' arithmetic).
- **Loose strands** (`LOOSE`): thin strips (6 mm human scale) from the wispy column on the four long styles,
  from a random stream of their own; a strand that would fall forward over the face is dropped.
- **A beard that exists from behind**: the full and the forked beard gain a row of cards under the jaw
  (`UNDER_JAW`), and GiantLook no longer sets `back_fade` on beards (the shader keeps the uniform).
- **Within M10's triangle caps** (`test_giant_assets.TRI_CAP`, hair 6,000 and beards 4,000): outer layers gave
  up cards for the new ones - the long hair 190 to 180 outer cards, the wild mane 190 to 168, the full beard's
  outer layer 140 to 120 and the forked beard's 130 to 120. Triangles now: long back 5,954, long braided
  5,548, warrior's tail 4,986, wild mane 5,978; full beard 3,960, forked 3,936, braided 2,828.
- **Not done: a coarser last LOD for the cards.** The importer makes the LODs; the far primitives were met at
  the M10 gate (3,920 at 121-130 m) and the long styles have no more triangles than then. FOLLOWUPS.

### 3. The skin (`kit/pack.py`, `assets/shaders/giant_skin.gdshader`, `GiantLook.drawn_tone`)

- **The tone, re-derived against the approved render.** `pack.grade_albedo` (`SKIN_GRADE`): in linear light the
  baked albedo keeps 0.42 of its saturation (0.75 on the lips) and is lifted by 1.2. The default look's lit
  chest in the creator's light (the probe's chest framing, a 20 x 16 patch on the right pectoral of a 320 x 240
  tile): **(208, 141, 106) on the W29 tree, (203, 169, 155) now; the approved studio render (198, 170, 157):
  5/255 at most a channel** (the row: 12). The grade is a pack step: no Cycles input changed.
- **The subsurface term** (`light_model` 3, the default on both tiers): `tex/skin_lut.png`, 64 x 64, Penner's
  pre-integration of d'Eon and Luebke's six-Gaussian profile (the profile's distances divided by 3.5, `SCATTER_SCALE`:
  at the giant's true scale the band is too thin to see), sampled in `light()` by (N.L x 0.5 + 0.5, curvature),
  imported lossless without mipmaps; `sss_strength` blends it over Lambert, `curvature_scale` scales the
  curvature; model 2's back-light on the thin parts stays.
- **Curvature, its own map** (`tex/curvature.png`, 256 px): the mean curvature from the bake's rest position and
  normal maps (smoothed, differenced over three texels, kept off the islands' edges), 1/r over 260 /m.
  **Deviation**: plan §2.4 put it in `masks.png`'s spare alpha (the lips' mask, read by nothing). Measured there,
  under the import's VRAM compression it spoiled the masks' RGB: the brows' tint drew blocks on the forehead at
  the eye close-up (the frames of the first try). A map of its own costs about 25 KB shipped.
- **The other tones on the graded albedo** (`giant_skin.gdshader` `tone_scaled`, mirrored by
  `GiantLook.tone_scaled`): the tones' ratios to the default were made for M10's saturated bake; on the graded
  one they drew the pale tones near white and the deep ones grey. A tone's ratio now keeps its hue
  (`tone_chroma` 1), its lightness above the default is eased (`tone_shoulder` 0.4), and a darker tone takes back
  the bake's saturation by its darkness (`tone_resat` 0.8). The default tone (ratio 1) draws the albedo exactly.
  Measured on the forearm at the creator's Full framing: fair (229, 174, 137) at W29, (221, 193, 177) now.
- **The swatch shows the colour drawn**: `GiantLook.drawn_tone(option)` = `tone_scaled` of `DRAWN_DEFAULT` (the
  measured chest, (199, 170, 157)) by the tone's ratio to the bake tone in linear light; `OptionRows.choices` gives the skin row those colours.

### 4. Eyes (`assets/shaders/giant_eye.gdshader`)

renderer gives no reflection), a duller sclera redder in the corners. The eye's frame was read from a debug

(FOLLOWUPS): the eye close-up still shows the skin's painted brows.

### 5. Scars and marks (`kit/designs.py`)

48 to 145, cheek 88 to 320, chest claws 242 to 624; the others unchanged within noise. **The rear Game view and

### 6. Cloth (`assets/shaders/giant_cloth.gdshader`, `kit/pack.py`)

The detail maps at 256 px (the same tiles, twice the texels); `rim` (GiantLook `CLOTH_RIM`: wool 0.3, fur 0.45,
leather 0.1, metal 0); `kind` 1 metal (`cloth_metal.png`, hammered dents and scratches; metallic 0.85; no
garment uses it until P54); the darkest dyes lifted to `DARK_FLOOR` (0.035 linear, keeping the dye's hue), so
the dark leather is not black in the world's shadow. The braies' and the tunic's caps in first person were left
to P50 (its `giant_first_person.gdshaderinc`).

### 7. `--giant-full`

The bake inputs did not change (the grade and the curvature are pack steps), so `--giant-full` regenerates the
same Cycles maps; it was run on the final commit (Checks).

### Also: parts on demand (P48's row)

`GiantLook.apply` now calls `ensure_part(id)` for every mesh the look shows and `release_unused(shown)` after,

## Gate A (M11): the skin in the world's light, the first-person shadow, the braies' patches

A fix lane of the Part A gate review, after the W30 merge found the skin worse than 0.10.0-m10's in the world's
light. Every frame and figure here is the software renderer's (Mesa llvmpipe under Xvfb, the Compatibility
renderer; this container has no GPU).

### What was wrong (found by drawing variants of one frame: the Wanderer at the Face framing in the world's light)

- **The ragged, near-white skin.** Two causes, each confirmed by switching it alone. (1) The subsurface lookup's
  rows were Lambert almost everywhere: the lookup's v is the curvature and the giant's skin is nearly flat at the
  diffusion profile's scale (the chest's curvature reads 0.01-0.03 of `CURV_MAX_PER_M`, a ring 13-38 cm across even
  after `SCATTER_SCALE`), so `light_model` 3 drew Lambert's hard terminator where M10's model 2 had wrapped the light
  round it. Under the world's sun, which grazes the chest and shoulders, the normal map's grain and the body's own
  shadow broke that terminator into ragged islands. Drawing the same frame with `light_model` 2 gave M10's soft
  falloff back; with the normal map off the islands lost their ragged edge; the shadow proxy was not the cause (the
  frame with the body's own meshes casting was the same). (2) `SKIN_GRADE` (saturation 0.42, gain 1.2), made to match
  the studio render in the creator's warm key, left an albedo so grey that the white sun drew it near white and the
  cool ambient drew the shade grey-mauve (the lit chest (199, 177, 171), the shade (83, 72, 76) in sRGB). Nothing
  clipped: the "snow" is a pale grey-pink lit side against a cool grey shade.
- **No body shadow in first person.** W29 put the shadow proxy on the head's render layer (3), believing a
  shadows-only mesh is not culled by the camera's mask. It is: the renderer culls shadow casters by the camera's cull
  mask too, and first person (and the pressed third-person camera, `CULL_NO_HEAD`) culls layer 3. Moving the proxy to
  the limbs' layer (1) brings the shadow back (drawn).
- **The braies' small bright patches** (the crotch, in the world's light): they are front faces of the braies (a
  debug colour by `FRONT_FACING` showed it; the body hidden, the patches stayed), seen edge-on with the sun behind
  the giant. P51's `rim` lit them: the renderer's rim light does not look at N.L, so a sliver of a fold seen edge-on
  takes the sun's rim light though it faces away. With the rim off they are gone (drawn).

### What changed

- `kit/pack.py`: `SKIN_WRAP` (0.28, 0.24, 0.22): the lookup pre-integrates a light wrapped round the terminator per
  channel, so its flat rows are a soft wrap and its curved rows softer and redder still. Evener than M10's model 2
  wrap (0.36, 0.25, 0.20): the self-shadow cuts the wrapped light a little past the terminator, and M10's red reach
  drew a red band there (it still shows, fainter, on the shoulders). `SKIN_GRADE` eased to saturation 0.8 (lips 0.9),
  no gain. `--pack` regenerated `tex/albedo.png` and `tex/skin_lut.png` (no Cycles input changed; `--verify` bit-exact).
- `giant_skin.gdshader` / `GiantLook.tone_scaled`: `TONE_RESAT_MAX` 1.25 (1 / 0.8); a tone away from the default,
  lighter or darker, takes back the bake's saturation by twice its lightness's distance (`tone_resat` 1.0): the pale
  tones were the greyest in the world's light.
- `GiantLook.CLOTH_RIM`: weave and leather 0 (fur keeps 0.45: its folds are soft and it reads right).
- `GiantModel.SHADOW_PROXY_LAYER` 1. Third person's draw calls do not change (every camera there kept layer 3 too):
  the proxy is still one shadow draw a cascade; first person gains that one draw a cascade (0.10 drew the shown
  meshes' shadows there, nine). `tests/support/crosshair_probe.gd` skips shadows-only meshes (the proxy is never
  drawn, but its new layer put it in the probe's centre ray).
- `GiantLook.DRAWN_DEFAULT` (220, 160, 131): the creator's swatches follow the drawn tone.
