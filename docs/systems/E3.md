# E3 — The Hollow Titan and the Kingsward

Milestone E's third package, on `pkg/e3` from E2's merge (`92a7095`). The version stays **0.17.0-m17** (prepared, not
shipped). The eighth signature titan of the expansion's story ("Stage 9: ... behind him the Hollow Titan, the shell of the
last giant king who fought the Worldbreaker and failed"): the rumour E2 left (`quest.hollow_lead`: an empty king on the
ridge north of the Breach) now pays off. Decisions E3-1..E3-12 are in `docs/DECISIONS.md` §2.7; the duels are BALANCE_LOG
"E3"; what is left is FOLLOWUPS "From E3".

## 1. The story

Ormund was the last of the giant kings. When the first titan stirred under the mountains for the last time he took his
champions down after it, swearing on the oath-stones of the Kingsward - the old kings' high field under the Sundered Horns -
that he would hold its chains or not come back. His champions did not come back. His armour did: the oath was stronger than
the man, and it walked his empty harness home and set it on watch, kneeling on the field with its sword planted, the
oath-chains from the stones running into it. The throne's fall stirred it; on the night the Cinder Titan came over the rim
it stood on the barrow-ridge north of the Breach, and then walked back to its field.

- **The lead** (`lead.hollow.heard`, Ysolde's last word in the Ashfell) starts `quest.hollow`. **The Empty Stand**, the high
  grazing north of the Breach where Brann saw him, is reached by the Watch path and **the King's Track** west; Brann is back
  at his shieling, and the empty king's tracks run west in the frost. Brann tells who he was, that the chains from the
  oath-stones draw back whatever is torn off him, that what falls off him must be stamped into the ground or thrown back at
  him, and that what holds him is behind his breastplate.
- **The preparation** (optional; `quest.hollow_stake`, 250 XP): the old kings rooted the oath-chains in **the Oath-stake**,
  an iron stake as tall as a tower driven into a boulder at the field's edge. A stage-9 giant heaves it out (a smaller one
  cannot): the chains go slack (`world.kingsward.stake_pulled`) and his torn plates come back half as fast.
- **The fight** in **the King's Field** (§2).
- **The aftermath**: his knot broken, his harness comes apart and the oath-chains drop. Once the giant has gone beyond his
  line (or on any later load: `world.kingsward.at_rest`, the territory's reopened flag) the herders have raised a barrow
  over the empty armour where he knelt - his helm on the head-stone, the Kingsword in its crest - and Brann is there. Told,
  Brann closes the quest and shows the link in the knot that is not the king's: a cut link of the first titan's own chain,
  the last lock, which held while the oath held. The ground under the Horns has begun to groan
  (`lead.worldbreaker.heard`, `quest.worldbreaker_lead`: a lead only, 0 XP; nothing of the Worldbreaker or of stage 10 is
  built).

## 2. How the fight plays

**The arena** is the King's Field, a high field 180 m across in the Kingsward, ringed by the old kings' broken muster-wall
and its drum-pillar gate, the champions' barrows and their slabs along its south and west, the crags under the Horns behind
him. He kneels on his vigil at its north side before his two banners; **three oath-stones** stand round the field, an iron
chain from each collar sagging to his back. A giant he sees wakes him (`TitanTerritory`, `wake_on_sight`): he lifts his
head, rises and draws his sword out of the ground (3.6 s, no blow). The boss bar, the music, no checkpoint and no fast
travel while engaged.

**The mechanic: his hollowness** (decision E3-2).

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Nothing inside** | His hide is mail over nothing: a blow on his shell keeps 18 % of its damage. A torn plate lays bare an empty socket (its weak multiplier under 1). |
| **The oath-knot** | Behind the breastplate, a ball of chain lit pale gold - what drives the shell, the one thing in him that can be hurt: x3.2 damage, x1.5 poise. Its hurtbox and lock anchor exist only while the breastplate is off (the lock then frames it first). It sits at a stage-9 giant's chest; a stage-8 giant must bring him down on a knee (his greaves) to reach it. |
| **Torn plates come back** | A plate broken falls 8 m out from him and lies (the giant may **hurl** it: the interact). After 14 s (28 s with the Oath-stake pulled) the chains draw it back over 3 s and it is on him again at 60 % - **while an oath-stone stands**. A heavy or a stamp that reaches it **crushes** it; a hurled plate strikes his breast (the knot when bare) as the giant's own blow (power 60, poise 700). Either way it never comes back. |
| **The oath-stones** | Each a chain into him. Brought down (a giant's blows: 700 health), its chain snaps and he reels 2.5 s; with none standing nothing torn off him comes back. |
| **His echo** | From his second phase every blow that lands on his shell rings in him (a heavy twice); his echo gives it back as a ring of force 30 m round him at 0.35 (struck seldom) to 1.6 (rung full) of its power. The shell's edges shimmer as it fills. Blows on the knot do not ring. |
| **The unbinding** | His last phase: the breastplate bursts off whole or not, and nothing torn off him comes back. |

**His blows** (each telegraphed; scaled against a smaller giant by size, exponent 2.6: x1.34 on stage 9, x2.19 on stage 8):

- **Phase 1**: the **cleave** (the Kingsword two-handed over his head, 1.8 s; a 54 m line), the **reap** (a 210-degree
  sweep, the circling answer), the **thrust** (a narrow 46 m line, 1.3 s), the **draw** (his arms flung wide and his breast
  thrust out, 1.5 s, a 36-degree cone 70 m long: the chains lash out of the shell and a giant they catch is dragged to his
  feet) and the **embrace** (1.15 s, a 17 m circle: the hug answer, and what follows the draw).
- **Phase 2** (60 %): the **Hollow Call** (his helm thrown back, a sound out of the empty shell: his echo wakes), the
  **lash** (the oath-chains whip down along the lines from each standing stone to him: four circles each, 1.8 s; round his
  own feet with none standing), the **echo**.
- **Phase 3** (25 %): the **unbinding**; stones fall from the crags on the giant by themselves (the phase's arena hazard).
- **His plates**: the crowned **helm** (broken: unbalanced, poise x1.3), the three-lame **pauldrons**, the **breastplate**
  (the knot's cover), the sword arm's **vambrace**, the **greaves** (broken: down on a knee 6 s, as the other titans).
  Health 7,000, armour 70, poise 900; 39.6 m (130 ft).

**Being a giant**: the knot is at a stage-9 giant's chest and over a stage-8 giant's swing; the shards are a giant's to stamp
into the ground or to heave up and throw; the oath-stones are a giant's to bring down; the Oath-stake moves only for a
stage-9 giant.

## 3. Him as an asset (decision E3-1)

No body: **a harness of plate made at load** (`HollowArmour`, `HollowModel`) and hung on the Stormcaller's 23 bones (his
mesh, bronze, rod and beard hidden). Each plate is lofted from sections with a real thickness - an outer face, an inner
face and the rolled edge between - so a plate seen edge-on is a plate, not a sheet; the inner faces are near black and lit
only by the knot where they face it (`hollow_armour.gdshader`: old dark iron, rust running down the plates, gilt trims worn
through on every edge; the cracks a plate shows as it fails; the echo's shimmer). The great helm's eye slit is open on the
dark. A ragged mantle hangs from his shoulders. **The Kingsword** (15.5 m) is held where the Stormcaller held his rod.
**The oath-knot** is a ball of chain links with strands into the shell (`hollow_knot.gdshader`, its light).

**His clips**: `HollowClips` solves key poses (where the feet stand, where the sword's grip is and where its blade points,
where the left hand is, how the spine, neck and head bend, how far the hips drop) onto the bones - legs and arms by
two-bone IK with a knee or elbow pole, the sword hand turned so the blade points as the key says - eases between keys and
bakes them at 30 fps into a second AnimationLibrary at load: his **stance** (a loop: the sword up before his right shoulder,
the shell swaying a little as a thing hung on chains sways), his **vigil** (on one knee, both hands on the pommel of the
planted sword, the helm bowed), the **wake**, the **draw**, the **embrace**, the **echo** (the sword driven point-down into
the ground) and his **fall** (the knees go, then he sinks onto them). The cleave, reap, thrust, lash and call are the
Stormcaller's (the rod held as a sword), and so are his walk, run, reel, kneel and rise.

**In play** (`HollowRig`): a torn plate falls off him to where the sim lays it, is drawn back home in an arc, flattens into
the ground when crushed, flies into his breast when hurled; the oath-chains are one MultiMesh of links (every chain of the
fight one draw call), one from each standing stone's collar to his back, three from his breast to the giant while his draw
hauls it; his echo's ring; his fall: after the knees go his harness comes off him piece by piece and lies on the ground,
the knot's light dying.

## 4. The world (decision E3-3)

A new land, `kingsward` (`tools/data/world/world.json`): 380 x 320 m at [-880..-500] x [-1620..-1300], the Hoarfell's ground,
opening onto the Hoarfell along its west edge north of the Hoar crags (the King's Track comes in there), its other edges
its own rim of mountains. Features: the King's Field (a flatten at 198 m), the Kingsward Way up into it, the champions'
barrows, the crags behind him. The road: **the King's Track** from the Watch path west past the Empty Stand to the field.
On it (`tools/data/world/hoarfell.json`): the arena (`world.kingsward.hollow_territory`), the three oath-stones (kind
`oath_pillar`: one shared scene, `scenes/world/oath_pillar.tscn`), the empty king's tracks (kind `giant_prints`), his banners,
the muster-ring's walls and gate, the barrows' slabs, a cairn and boulders, the Oath-stake (a seal, `prop.oath_stake`),
Brann's shieling and talker at the Empty Stand, and the aftermath (the barrow, kind `kings_barrow`, and Brann's talker at the
field). Encounters: him, Brann and his boy at the shieling, the herders at the barrow. POI `poi.kingsward` (90 XP).
