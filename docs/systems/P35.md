# P35 — The Greywood and the Drowned Steps

Plan M6–M9 §3.19 (wave 21, lane A, beside P36), with §5.4, §5.5, §5.7, §5.8, §5.10 and §5.13 for these
two regions. The Greywood: Hollowmere and Sedge's oath, the troll den and the Matriarch's two endings,
the woodcutters' camp and its fire lesson, the great pines, the Needle and the Hidden Grove, the old
root. The Drowned Steps: Wenna's five sunken bells and the Stoneknuckles, the island's warden, the
sunken vault behind the causeway's rockfall, the Titan's Bones and the Titan Forge, the Titan's
shoulder. Their encounters (replacing P25's light content), wander tables and templates, quests,
dialogues, faction, props and cairns; the world rebuilt from the layout tables.

## What it adds

### Layouts (`tools/data/world/greywood.json`, `drowned_steps.json`)

- **Hollowmere**: P25's nine huts become `stilt_hut` elements (same ids and places: a hut on six stilts
  over the pond's edge); `world.greywood.hollowmere_lights` (lantern posts round the pond, shown by
  `oath.wood.done`); Sedge stands among the huts on the south shore, 27 m from the green.
- **The troll den** (−760, −330): `den_arena` (the arena: BossArena with the Matriarch's id, her
  encounter, the engage area `world.area.troll_den_engage`, r 45, `boss.matriarch.defeated`; the fire
  pit, group `fire_pits`), `den_wall` (the back wall at (−790, −350) across the cave mouth: a DenWall,
  record `prop.den_back_wall`, with its HeavePoint), three `nest`s (BroodNest, `prop.brood_nest`) with a
  `brazier` beside each, the exit marker `world.marker.troll_den_exit` in the wall's opening. P25's
  radial spur "wall" and its bone "nests" are gone.
- **Troll sites' fires**: braziers at the den guard, the great pines and the Titan's Bones; the
  woodcutters' camp has a bonfire (`prop.bonfire`) and a brazier, and the Woodcutter's Greataxe on a
  rack. Every troll site has a fire within 40 m.
- **The great pines**: P25's three pines are GreatPineTrees (`uproot_prop` prop.great_pine, weapon
  `weapon.tree_pine`): stage 4 uproots one.
- **The Needle**: `narrow_pass` `world.gate.needle` (an Area3D sign in the 4.9 m slot: fits stages
  3–4, refuses 5 with the plan's message; the slot's geometry decides). The Hidden Grove's second key is
  P25's stage-5 rockfall on the north ridge.
- **The Mere**: five `bell`s (`prop.bell`: struck, a bell rings - its broken state is the rung bell)
  at (−150, −60), (130, 20), (−66, −182), (20, 252) in the Wend's deep reach and (52, −102) on the
  island; the Titan Forge (`forge_site`, tier `titan`, waiting for `world.titan_forge.lit`) and its
  `forge_fire` (a ForgeFire lit once the scavengers are cleared: `loot.titan_bones`, 2 titan iron);
  Wenna's landing, the Stoneknuckles on a reward rack (`world.drowned.bells_rung`); cairns for the
  sunken vault and the Titan's shoulder (stage 6, on the ledge); the old root's cairn (stage 5).
- Markers for every quest stage, the vault's area, the camp's area (the fire lesson).

### Kinds (`tools/world/kinds/`)

`stilt_hut`, `bell`, `brazier` (styles brazier, bonfire), `nest` (the plan's four) and `den_wall`,
`den_arena`, `rack` (weapon rack; style `reward`), `narrow_pass`, `forge_fire`, with `_breakable_kit.gd`
(a breakable prop of its own record: CombatEntity, blocks, hurtboxes, Intact/Broken meshes in the
scenery group, an optional static Frame). The breakable kinds name their records (`record_of`) and the
nest and the wall the flags their nodes write (`written_flags`) for the content tests. Two small
builder edits: `tools/builders/world.gd` gives a `pines` entry with `uproot_prop` the GreatPineTree
script; `cairn.gd` passes `requires_stage` and `ledge_m` to the Inscription.

### Runtime (`scripts/world/`)

- `brood_nest.gd` (BroodNest): a nest that only fire harms; burns when a fire-tagged prop breaks
  within `ignite_radius_m` (9 m) or a burning held body lands there; when the den's last nest burns it
  sets `world.troll_den.nests_burned`.
- `den_wall.gd` (DenWall): the woodfolk open the wall 20 s after the nests flag (or at once on
  Stormborn's bargain, `world.troll_den.bargain`) by setting `world.troll_den.wall_open` (the record's
  `open_when_flag`); a stage-5 giant heaves it (the record's `requires_permission: heave`).
- `narrow_pass.gd` (NarrowPass): the Needle's sign (`size_gate_blocked` and the HUD line once per 4 s).
- `reward_rack.gd` (RewardRack): a weapon rack that waits for flags.
- `great_pine_tree.gd` (GreatPineTree): an UprootTree that also asks the record's stage (4).
- `forge_fire.gd` (ForgeFire): a BeaconFire that offers "Light the Titan Forge" and steps aside once lit.

## Decisions and deviations

- **Sedge's stage-4 spot** is among the huts on the pond's south shore (−616, −4), 27 m from the green
  the oath's marker names, not before the long hut: a giant following the lead finds her; at stage 5–6
  the director moves her onto the green (the settlement's `max_inner_stage` 4).
- **Moved encounters**: the den guard to (−645, −268) (the plan's point is the den's waystone); the dire
  pack to (−540, −140) (the plan's point is on a 36° ridge); the bell robbers to the tower's west shore
  (−243, −217) (the plan's point is in the drowned tower's water, P25's 24 m from the waystone).
- **The fourth bell** hangs in the Wend's deep reach (20, 252), a stage-4 gate region, instead of a deep
  pool at (190, −120): the terrain (world.json, not P35's) has no stage-4 pool there. The third stands on
  the north shore at (−66, −182).
- **The oath has three stages** (meet, den, swear) instead of meet → resolve: one marker each (the den,
  then Hollowmere); a two-marker resolve stage would trip OathRules' lead rule.
- **The Stoneknuckles' rack waits for `world.drowned.bells_rung`** (set by the bells' stage), not the
  quest's done flag: the quest's last stage is taking the gauntlets. The woodcutters' and the bells'
  quests end when the rack's weapon is equipped.
- **The troll pack after the drive** is a wander entry (stage 5, once she fled) placed on the rides
  like every wanderer, not a spawn in the den itself.
- **Extra kinds and scripts** beyond the plan's four kinds (den wall, den arena, rack, narrow pass, forge
  fire; BroodNest, DenWall, NarrowPass, RewardRack, GreatPineTree, ForgeFire), and the two builder edits
  (world.gd's pines, cairn.gd's stage and ledge).
- **The bell's material is iron** (with two new break cues) since the mix's materials are a fixed enum.
- **Region notes** (`region_greywood.json`, `region_drowned_steps.json`, P25's files) updated: the M7
  notes promised what this package ships.

## M8 gate

Findings of the M8 gate review in this package's content, fixed on top of wave 21 (lane m8s).

- **F38: the drive-out is checkpointed like the slay.** Her flight (boss_disengaged "fled") made the
  den's arena DEFEATED and set her outcome, but only boss_defeated took a checkpoint, so a death to
  the den's whelps before the next waystone reloaded the fight from before it (the nests whole, the
  wall shut, the Matriarch back). GameSession now requests a checkpoint `boss_fled` on a flight, as it
  does `boss_defeated` on a kill, the respawn unchanged and no victory cue (P20.md, "M8 gate";
  `boss_arena.gd` is unchanged). Tests: `test_checkpoint_triggers` (the row), `test_den_checkpoints`
  (gate: both endings with the shipped den parts and the Matriarch; a death after the drive-out keeps
  her outcome, the burned nests and the open wall).
- **F4 (with F47): the Greywood shrine's growth pad has room for stage 5.** The pad (and the SpawnPoint
  on it) stands 6.5 m before the stone; at yaw 30 it fell 2.1 m from a pine of the shrine's clearing
  (`Solids/Batch4` of c1_4), so a stage-4 giant there was refused stage 5 ("Not enough room to grow
  here") - where the bot grows and where a respawn at the shrine wakes. The shrine now stands at yaw -10
  (`greywood.json`): its pad faces the clearing's open north side, 6.3 m from that pine and 7.9 m from
  the next. The pines stand in the clearing at all because the world builder's no-scenery grid never
  takes a mark (P25.md, "M8 gate"; FOLLOWUPS), so a giant beside the stone on the pines' side can still
  be told to step into the open. Rebuilt: c1_4 and its eight neighbours (`--chunks=c1_4`): the shrine's

  of the world's five shrines, its ring loaded, a giant of the stage below on its pad and on its
  SpawnPoint has room for the stage the shrine grants (it failed for the Greywood's pad and SpawnPoint
  before).
- **F45: a stage-4 giant's light swing rings the deep-reach bell.** The bell kind hung every bell so its
