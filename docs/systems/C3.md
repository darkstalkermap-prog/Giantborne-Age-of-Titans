# C3 — Playability: the swings heard, the aim told, the inventory, the forges and waystones, a giant that moves

Milestone C's third package, on `pkg/play` from C2's merge. The version stays **0.16.0-m16** and the save stays schema
3. The owner played the Web build and reported five things; each was taken as a defect to find in the real Web build,
not a feature to add. Decisions C3-1..C3-7 are in `docs/DECISIONS.md` §2.7; what is left is FOLLOWUPS "From C3".

## 1. The swings and hits heard (decisions C3-1..C3-3)

**Reproduced in the Web build.** `tools/web/sfx_check.mjs` (new) plays the sandbox's seeded autopilot in headless
Chromium (`--mode=sandbox --autoplay=40`) with the game printing an `AUDIO_CUE` line for each cue (`--audio-trace`,
new) and the page instrumented: a recorder on the output, every Web Audio buffer source logged, the gains of the engine's
per-sample bus node followed, and (for the diagnosis) every call of the engine's sample-bus bindings logged
(`--engine-log`). With the music and the ambience muted (`--audio-mute=Music:Ambience`, new) the C2 build's output was
**-200 dBFS - silence - through 14 swings, 12 hits, 90 footfalls and every UI cue**: all 166 sample sources started,
all silent. The engine log showed why: `AudioServer.add_bus()` calls `godot_audio_sample_bus_add(-1)`, and the page's
`Bus.addAt(-1)` moves the new bus with `splice(-2, 0, bus)` to the front of the list, so after the four `add_bus` calls
Master was no longer bus 0 and `set_bus_send(i, "Master")` disconnected Master's output from the speakers and sent it
into a new bus that sends back into Master. Music and ambience are streams the engine's audio worklet mixes straight to
the destination, so they played; every SFX and UI sample went through the broken graph. (P23's Web audio check
measures the Valley's music, so nothing noticed.)

**The fixes**:
- the buses are made by raising `AudioServer.bus_count` (C3-1);
- every 3D sample started at gain zero and was panned a frame later (on the Web the gains are set after the source
  starts): the 16-40 ms transient of a hit or a footfall was cut. A 3D cue heard within its class's unit distance now
  plays on one of eight near voices at its modelled level (C3-2); the threat and alert classes stay 3D;
- the giant's swing is pitched by his height (bigger: longer, lower), and from stage 4 a new procedural mass layer
  (`sfx.swing.mass.light` / `.heavy`, `gen_world_sfx.py`'s `swing_mass`: a deep brown-noise rush, a pressure swell, a
  buffet) plays under it, +5 dB a doubling of his height over 10 m (C3-3). The world's registered SFX seconds:
  **174.21 of 175** (+1.35 s; the budget is not raised). The impacts by material (flesh light / heavy, armour light /
  heavy, wood, stone for a titan's plate, iron, the guard's block, the guard break) and the footfalls of stages 1-8
  existed and are unchanged: they were silent only by the buses.

**Measured on the Web** (C2's build with the trace, then C3's; the sandbox at stage 1, 40 s, `--audio-mute` for the
effects alone):

| | C2 (before) | C3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| swings heard, effects alone | 0 of 14 (-200 dBFS) | **14 of 14**, median -23.4 dBFS RMS, -9.2 peak over 0.3 s |
| hits heard, effects alone | 0 of 12 (-200 dBFS) | **12 of 12**, median -21.0 dBFS RMS, -5.3 peak |
| footfalls heard | 0 of 90 | 91 of 91, -29.3 dBFS RMS |
| sample sources started at gain zero | every 3D one | 0 swings, 0 hits (6 of 91 far footfalls: 3D voices) |
| the music and ambience bed (full mix) | -34.1 dBFS RMS | -34.6 dBFS RMS: a swing ~11 dB and a hit ~14 dB over it |

## 2. The aim (decision C3-4)

`PlayerAttack.swing_plan(action)` is the one answer the swing and the HUD share:
- **what it aims at**: the hard lock (as before); else what the screen centre's ray from the camera meets - a hurtbox
  (a man, a titan's plate), a breakable's body (a house, a wall, the Millrace Dam), or, where it meets the ground, the
  body standing there within 0.6 m + 6 % of the giant's height (only what he may target: a villager beside the cursor
  is never pulled in); else the soft target in front (as before);
- **where it faces**: what the cursor picked (at most 40 degrees off the camera's yaw; a bare point under the cursor
  keeps the camera's yaw - the shoulder camera's parallax would turn him a few degrees for nothing);
- **its tilt**: toward the body (P17b's aimed tilt, now also below the stage's default down to -30 degrees: a man
  nearer a big giant than the stage's tilt brings the weapon down), toward the titan's plate under the cursor
  (A2's plate tilt), or, for a bare point high on a wall, toward it;
- **what it will strike**: the arc swept as the runner sweeps it (the same weapon segments tick by tick through its
  active phase, `HitQueryAdapter.sweep_capsule` with the same origin, radius and occlusion, `HitFactory`'s order) at
  that facing and tilt; a titan's region as `HitFactory` lands it (`predict_titan_region`: the plate nearest the aim
  within the titan's snap distance, held back up to three ticks, as for a locked plate). `HitFactory._aim_point` now
  aims an unlocked swing at the plate under the cursor (it aimed only a locked one).

The swing starts from the plan (its facing, through the windup: §5; its tilt; its aim point), and the HUD's
**aim indicator** (`ui/hud/aim_indicator.gd`) draws it every third frame while no lock is held and something strikable is near: warm corner brackets
round what the swing will strike (a titan's plate with a dot on it), its name and plate ("Stoneback - left shoulder"),
the swing's reach as a dotted arc on the ground, and grey brackets with "Out of reach - 3 m closer" (or "too close")
round an aimed target it will not strike.

`tests/gate/test_c3_aim.gd` puts the cursor on a man, a fence and a titan's plate at every stage 1-8, for the light and
the heavy (48 swings), and compares what the indicator says with what the swing struck: **the same entities in the
same order and the same titan region in all 48**; a target it says is out of reach is never struck. The men and fences
are reached at stages 1-6 (24 of 24); at stages 7 and 8 the colossus's swing does not come down to a man or a fence
before its feet (they stamp and stride there), and the indicator says "Out of reach"; titan plates are reached from
stage 2 (14 of 16).

## 3. The inventory on I (decision C3-5)

I (pad Back) opens the inventory; C (pad Y) the Character menu, as before. The inventory (`ui/screens/
inventory_screen.gd`, rebuilt; `ui/item_icon.gd`, new):
- **worn**: the giant himself in the middle (the creator's preview in his worn look; a silhouette where no display
  draws one) between his eight slots - head, shoulders, chest, arms on the left; weapon, waist, legs, trinket on the
  right - each a tile with its piece's drawn icon and name, or the slot's outline; a slot chosen lists what he owns for
  it (Put on / Take off) and his armour's numbers;
- **his numbers**: health, stamina, height, armour (the share of a blow it turns), poise, weight, the weapon's damage;
- **the bag**: 8 x 5 cells (more rows as it fills): the weapons and armour he owns but does not wear, then trophies,
  quest items, keys and materials, each a drawn icon (a shape by slot, weapon family or material; a colour by its set
  or what it is made of) with its count, and **NEW** on what came since the bag was last looked at - loot lands there.
  A cell chosen shows its kind, words and (a piece) its numbers before -> after; chosen again (Enter / A, a second
  click) it is put on;
- Attributes and Character buttons; the loadout and talent points under the bag; I again closes it.
It shares the Character menu's rules (`slot_options`, `compare_lines`, `apply_option`: out of combat only) and its
state, so the two never disagree. The marks are the session's: no save change (schema 3).

## 4. The forges and the waystones (decision C3-6)

Every **forge** wears a smithy (`scenes/world/smithy.tscn`): a drystone hearth round a fire-bed of glowing, flickering
coals and flames, a hood and a coursed chimney 13 m high with smoke from its crown, bellows of boards and hide, an anvil
on an oak stump with its hammer, a bar heating in the coals, a quench trough, a tool rack, a charcoal heap and bar iron,
a low wall behind and a lean-to of boards on posts over the anvil; the fire's own orange light and embers. A forge that
is shut (its flags, its quest condition or its faction) is **cold**: dark ashes, no light, no smoke. A forge built at a
giant's scale (the titan-tier `condition_forge`s: Fort Aldmere's, Skarn's, Millford's quarry forge, the woodwright's at
Hollowmere, the mason-forge at the Bastion keep) wears it turned to its frame and 1.55 x as large.

Every **waystone** wears a monument (`scenes/world/waystone_monument.tscn`): a leaning menhir of weathered stone on a
plinth of two rough slabs ringed by kerb stones, two rune bands and a rune eye cut in it, a crown of three small
standing stones round a notch. **Shut** (its flags unmet): its runes dark. **Open, not yet reached**: a faint slow
pulse. **Known** (reached, or its visited flag): the runes burn, a ring of six rune stones turns over the crown and a
beam of pale light stands 90 m into the sky, thinning near the camera, seen from far off at every stage.

Both are built offline (`tools/builders/forge.gd` with `tools/landmark_mesh.gd`: rough-hewn prisms, blocks, logs and
cones from a seeded generator, their material in the vertex colour's alpha; `assets/shaders/landmark.gdshader`: stone
worn uneven with lichen, grime and cracks, oak grain, rusting iron, hide, and the glow; `landmark_beam.gdshader`) and put
on at run time by the `Forge` and `Waystone` scripts over the old boxes (hidden; their solids kept - the looks add
none: new solids blocked the Valley's stage-3 corridor by the bridge), so every instance
wears them: the Valley's two forges and five waystones, the world's eight forges and nineteen waystones, the test
levels'. No chunk was rebuilt.

**Named as the giant comes near**: the HUD's place marker (`ui/hud/place_marker.gd`) draws over the nearest two (within
30 m + 7 x the giant's height) an icon - a standing stone, an anvil over a flame - their name, and their state:
"Reached: travel here from the map (M)", "Not yet reached: walk up to it", "Sealed"; "Lit: reforge and craft (E) -
works titan stone", "Cold: ...". The **map** draws a waystone as a standing stone on its plinth with its rune band lit
once reached, a forge as an anvil over its fire; the **compass** an anvil and a standing stone.

## 5. The motion (decision C3-7)

The stiffness was found in three places: (1) every turn (walking, blocking, a lock) ran at the stage's constant rate and
stopped dead on its target; (2) every unlocked swing snapped the whole body round to the camera in one frame before its
windup; (3) a light pressed in a swing's windup or blow - more than 0.15 s before the recovery's cancel window - was
dropped, so a mashed combo stalled. Now:
- a turn's speed **builds** at 6 x the stage's rate a second and **eases out** over its last stretch (7 / s of what is
  left): a stage-8 giant (160 degrees a second) leans into a turn and settles;
- an unlocked weapon swing **turns into its blow** through the windup - the hips and shoulders lead, the arm follows -
  at twice the stage's turn rate, or faster if that is what it takes to face the blow by the windup's last tick (so the
  arc strikes where the aim indicator says: §2); a locked swing still faces its target at once, as the duels were tuned;
- a light or a heavy pressed during an action **waits 0.25 s** (`CombatController.ATTACK_BUFFER_TICKS`) for the
  chain's cancel window; every other request keeps 0.15 s.
The rig's own blending (P50's balanced tree, the 16 / s blends) was not the cause and is unchanged. The eased turns
are the player's walking turns (a guard and a windup track at the old constant rate). **The bots keep their controls**
(`PlayerAttack.bot_controls`: no cursor, no low tilts, the old turns and the 0.15 s buffer): the first full check
moved the duels' tables with these changes (the Ogre King's keg, a stage-6 Bastion seed, Stoneback's armour
comparison), and the duels measure the titans, not the controls; they were not re-tuned.

## What is weak

- **Heard in headless Chromium only.** The effects reach the output and were measured there (a swing about 12 dB and a
  hit about 13 dB over the music and ambience in the sandbox); no real browser, speaker or ear has heard them, and the
  mix's balance is still the P19 plan's. If the owner wants them louder, `audio_mix.default`'s SFX bus is the knob.
- **Near voices are centred**: within a class's unit distance (at stage 8 that is 26-48 m) a sound plays without
  panning, so a blow landing 20 m to a colossus's left plays in the middle.
- **A colossus cannot swing at a man or a fence before its feet** (stages 7-8): the indicator truthfully says "Out of
  reach" (or "too close"); its stomps reach them. The arcs were not changed - a design question for the owner.
- **The indicator predicts standing targets**; a man stepping during the windup can leave the arc it showed. And at
  stage 6 a soldier at the giant's feet shows "Out of reach" (the stage's arc passes over him): true, but it will
  surprise a player who expects a giant to hit what stands on his toes.
- **The camera cannot look down past 70 degrees**, so at stages 7-8 a man within about 8 m cannot be put under the
  cursor (the soft target and the ground's pull still find him).
- **The landmarks are one size**: built for a stage 1-3 giant (1.55 x at the titan-tier forges); at stage 8 a smithy is
  about his shin and a waystone his knee. The beam, the smoke, the fire's light and the place marker carry them; a
  colossus does not "work" a forge the way a small giant does. They draw to 240 m (the smoke 420 m, a known beam
  2.4 km) to keep the static world within 40 draw calls a view at low; an unreached waystone is not seen past 240 m.
  The smithy's smoke reads as dark lumpy puffs against the sky from some angles.
- **The looks have no solids of their own**: the old posts and boxes remain the solids, so a plinth's edge, the
  chimney above the hearth, the lean-to and the trough can be walked through.
- **The motion work is the motor's and the controller's**: eased turns, the swing's turn into its blow, the attack
  buffer. No new clips, no foot-locking, no start / stop transition animations, no camera-lag change: the rig's blends
  were judged sound and left alone. The bots keep the old controls (the duels were not re-measured with the new ones).
- **The capture tour's first segment after a New Game's first travel draws a stale "Loading..." frame** (the evidence
  shots of a man and a building were retaken after a second travel); a tour quirk, not the game's.
- **The Web world walk failed its music criterion as at C2** (6 holes the main thread did not explain, among about 600
  it did; C2's tree passed the same walk once the same evening). Recorded, not explained.
- **Human playtest not run.**
