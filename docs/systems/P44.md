# P44 — The character creator, the shrine's Appearance tab and the character sheets

Plan: `docs/plans/M10_PLAN.md` §3.4 (design 1-5), §2.10 (the creator's screens and flows), §2.13 and decision 18
(the rendered option check), §4.2 (sheet A). Wave W26, lane B, beside P43 (the skinned giant). Everything a
player sees of the look before play is here: New Game opens the creator, every shrine has an Appearance tab,
the slow tier draws every option of the floor catalogue and measures it, and a tool renders the owner's sheet.

## What was built

### The creator (`ui/screens/creator/creator_screen.gd`, `class_name CreatorScreen`, a `UiScreen`)

`ScreenStack.SCREENS["creator"]` (P42 declared it) is now real. It opens with
`{"context": "new_game", "start": {"mode", "options"}}` (the title) or `{"context": "shrine", "player",
"shrine", "appearance"}` (the tab) and closes with `{"appearance": record, "begin": true}` (Begin),
`{"appearance": record}` (Keep this look) or `{"cancelled": true}`.

- **Layout** (the creator brief's mock-up): the title row "Shape your giant" with a line under it (New Game:
  "you can change all of this later at any shrine"; shrine: "Changes are free..."); the category tabs (Presets,
  Skin, Eyes, Hair, Beard, Tattoos, War paint, Scars, Clothing); the preview at the left (about 720x544 at
  1280x720) with Full / Face / Back / Game view under it; the options panel at the right (about 512x544), a
  scrolling column of rows with a status line; the actions Randomize, Randomize all, Undo, Reset,
  Presets..., then Back (Cancel at a shrine) and Begin your journey / Begin in the Old Kingdom / Keep this
  look. Anchors grow the panels in larger windows; every part stays inside the window and above the stack's
  footer at 1280x720 up to 2560x1440 (`test_creator_layout`).
- **Rows** (`OptionRows`): swatch rows (a named swatch per option; focusing a swatch applies it, like a
  cycler: moving the focus previews and keeps), cyclers (left and right step, a click on the left third steps
  back), sliders (the greys, in steps of 0.05, labelled with P42's grey words), toggles (the scars, at most 4)
  and the preset row. A locked option says why ("Choose your line at a shrine of the Old Kingdom"). A design
  row whose region the outfit covers says "Hidden by your clothing", and while it has the focus the covering
  garments fade to 0.7 in the preview. The ancestry mark row ("Ancestry mark: shown / hidden") sits in
  Tattoos.
- **History**: every change (a swatch, a slider's drag as one step, Randomize, Randomize all, a preset,
  Reset) enters an undo history of 64 records; Undo steps back through it.
- **Randomize** uses the screen's own `RandomNumberGenerator` (seeded from the clock; `Sim.rng` is never
  touched): Y / R randomizes the open category, "Randomize all" everything but the outfit (unless Clothing is
  open), up to 8 draws until the record differs. **Reset** returns to the look the screen opened with (a
  shrine) or the default preset (New Game).
- **Back, Cancel, Esc and pad B** close at once when the record equals the opened one; otherwise "Discard
  your changes?" with Keep editing (focused) and Discard.
- **Keys** (P16's focus rules; `focus_rows()` = the tabs, the open category's rows, the actions): LB / RB and
  PageUp / PageDown change category; the right stick, a mouse drag, Q and E turn the preview; LT / RT, the
  wheel, + and - zoom; X / V cycle the view; View / Ctrl+Z undo; Ctrl+Enter is Begin or Keep. The footer hint
  is the screen's own `nav_hint()` (cached per device).
- **Framing per category** (`category_framing`): Hair three-quarter from behind; Beard and Eyes the face;
  Tattoos, Scars and War paint the focused design's region (its design's first body region from the index:
  face, chest, back, arms, or the left hand for a hand design); Skin, Clothing and Presets the full body.
- While the creator is on top the root viewport's 3D is off (`get_tree().root.disable_3d`; on again when it
  closes or another screen covers it). At a shrine the game stays paused (`pauses_game()`), and the preview
  holds the equipped weapon.

### The option rows (`ui/screens/creator/option_rows.gd`, `class_name OptionRows`)

The categories' row specs, the record paths each row reads and writes (`value_of`, `with_value`, `with_scar`;
an edit clears the record's `preset`, so the look reads as custom), the numeric-tolerant record comparison
(`same`), a design row's region and the outfit's coverage (`row_region`, `coverage`, `row_hidden`, computed once
per change), and the row controls. Styleboxes are cached; a category's rows are built on its first opening and
kept.

### The preview (`ui/screens/creator/giant_preview.gd`, `class_name GiantPreview`, a `SubViewportContainer`)

A SubViewport with `own_world_3d` and its own stage: a dark backdrop, ambient light, a key light with shadows,
a cool rim light and a stone disc (the studio); in Game view and first person the world's light instead (its
sky colour, ambient, a shadowed sun at the world's angle, fog from 120 to 700 m, a grass ground plane). It
holds `giant_model.tscn` with `GiantLook`'s materials (built only with a display, or when a test sets
`materials_headless`), plays the idle clip when the model has one (`play_idle`, P43's) and stands it in
`pose_relaxed()` otherwise or when `still` is set. Framings: full, face, hair (from behind), back, arms,
chest, back_design, hand, hand_l, eyes (the sheets), "game" (the growth profile's default third-person
camera, the model scaled to the stage, a soldier figure beside it) and "first_person" (the eye at the
profile's eye height and 0.18 m forward, looking down at the arms at -62 degrees and 22 degrees to his right,
render layers 2 and 3 culled as the game's first person does). `UPDATE_WHEN_VISIBLE` while live,
`UPDATE_ONCE` for a portrait (the container's own forcing of `UPDATE_ALWAYS` is undone on every visibility
change). At a shrine the equipped weapon sits at the hand bone, aimed along `TipMatching.idle_grip`.
`world_light` puts the world's light on any framing (the sheets).

### The title (`ui/screens/title_screen.gd`)

New Game (after the replace-the-save confirm when a save exists), Play the prologue and Skip to the Old Kingdom
open the creator; Begin calls `start_game(mode, options + {"appearance": record})` from the button's own
pressed handler, in the same call stack (pointer lock in browsers). Back returns to the title as it was, the
save untouched. `GameSession.creator_start` (the launcher's `--creator`) opens the creator at once for a world
start. **The warm-up**: with a display the title draws the default look in a hidden 64x64 SubViewport for two
frames under the studio light and two under the world's light, and shows "Preparing..." with New Game and
Continue disabled until it is done (or 6 s); headless the buttons are enabled at once. The warm-up viewport is
freed when a game starts. `describe_save` and `mid_sentence` are unchanged.

### The shrine's Appearance tab (`ui/screens/shrine/appearance_tab.gd`)

The last of `ShrineScreen.TABS`: the look in words (`Appearance.summary_lines`) and "Change your look...",
which opens the creator over the paused shrine screen. Keep → `player.appearance.set_record(record)`: the live
giant changes, `Events.appearance_changed` fires once and GameSession re-takes and writes the shrine's
checkpoint (P42's F15 hook); the tab says "Your new look is kept." Cancel / Discard restore the look in memory
without a write ("Your look is unchanged."). The tab's button reads "Appearance (new)" until the tab is first
shown in a save, which sets `appearance.tab_seen`.

### The rendered option check (`tests/slow/test_appearance_render.gd`, `tests/support/appearance_render_probe.tscn`)

A child Godot under Xvfb (the Compatibility renderer, two llvmpipe threads, the web tier) draws grids of
sixteen 320x240 GiantPreview tiles, one frame a grid (two for the first, which compiles the shaders), MSAA off,
every tile still. 131 tiles: 11 bases, every option of the 13 sets in its creator framing (a line's options
for that line), the 8 presets, and each of the 17 designs also in the stage-1 rear Game view and in first
person. For each tile it measures the share of pixels off the backdrop (`lit`) and the share that changed
against its base (every pixel, a pixel counting when its channels move 0.08 together; rows equal byte for
byte skipped), runs `GiantLook.validate()`, and saves the grids and `index.json` to
`user://m10_frames/options/`. The suite requires no engine or shader error, `validate()` empty, no blank tile,
every option above its set's floor, and in the rear or first-person view at least one option of each design
category above that view's floor — except a category in `KNOWN_MISSES`, which must still draw and is reported
(the scars; see "Known gaps").

Run it (a main scene, not a `-s` script: Appearance and GiantPreview need the autoloads' data):

```
LP_NUM_THREADS=2 xvfb-run -a -s "-screen 0 1920x1080x24" tools/gd.sh --path . --rendering-driver opengl3 \
  --resolution 1920x1080 --audio-driver Dummy res://tools/character_sheet.tscn -- \
  --out=docs/screenshots/m10/ --renders=<folder with test_studio_full34.png and test_outdoor_head.png>
```

Options: `--out=DIR` (relative to the project or absolute; default `docs/screenshots/m10/`), `--renders=DIR`
(the approved renders; without it page 1 shows a dark tile captioned "not given"), `--pages=1,2,3,4a,4b,5,6`,
`--sheet=a` (the file prefix; sheet B uses `--sheet=b`), `--jpg` (JPEG at quality 0.9 instead of PNG). It
prints `CHARACTER_SHEET {"pages", "tiles", "ms", "tier", "adapter"}` and quits; a watchdog quits it after
900 s. Without `--giant-tier` the pages use the "auto" tier (hi where `tex_hi/` exists); page 6 builds both.

### What the renders show (recorded for P46; nothing here is plainly broken)

- **The cloth detail strengths** (weave 0.35, leather 0.25, fur 0.45, the detail normal 0.6, the tile repeated
  48 times): in the world's sun (the views from behind) the braies read as coarse quilted panels about a hand
  wide rather than a weave; in the shaded front views the cloth reads flat. Leather at 0.25 does not show on
  the dark-leather dye (the jerkin and tunic read as flat near-black) and shows faintly on madder and woad. The
  furs read as a mottled grey mantle whose jagged card edges carry more than the fur detail.
- **The eyes**: the ten colours are hard to tell apart even in the sheets' eye close-up (the iris is small and
  dark under the brow in the world's light); the option check measures 17 to 39 changed pixels at the face
  framing. Only storm-lit stands out.
- **The forehead** shows fine horizontal ridges in the close-ups (both tiers), the skin's normal detail or the
  scalp's edge under the low sun.
- **The hair and beards** read as shingled cards at the Face and Hair framings (P46's item 1, as the plan
  expected); the crop from behind reads as a cap of cards.
- **Designs on his left side** (the brow scar, the chest claws, the claw stripes, the knuckles) are framed from
  his right by the face, chest and arms framings (the index carries a design's body regions, not its side);
  the knuckles' hand design frames the left hand.

## Decisions and deviations

- **The sheet tool is a main scene** (`tools/character_sheet.tscn`), not the plan's `-s` script: a `-s`
  SceneTree script cannot compile `Appearance`, which reads the GameData autoload (the precedent is
  `tools/build_scenes.tscn`).
- **Sheet A has seven pages**: the designs page is two (tattoos; war paint, scars and marks), each design also
  shown in first person beside the Game view, which is where the arm designs read.
- **The creator's pad and keyboard walks and the shrine's saves run in the slow tier** (`test_creator_controls`,
  `test_shrine_appearance_saves`): a world start and a shrine scene, and a walk through every row, cost more
  than the default tier's 0.8 s; the default suites keep one pass of each flow.
- **War paint frames its design's region** (the face stripes the face, the shoulder bands the arms), as Tattoos
  and Scars do; the plan's table frames war paint at the face.
- **Hand designs frame the left hand** (`hand_l`): the floor's one hand design, the knuckles scar, is on the
  left hand (`tools/giant/kit`); a right-hand design would need the index to say so.
- **The preview builds no materials headless** unless a test sets `GiantPreview.materials_headless` (nothing
  draws; `GiantLook.apply` still sets visibility and stores the look), which keeps the default suites cheap.
- **The option check's view floors are in pixels** (18 from behind, 90 in first person) and its changed share is
  counted on every pixel: a design seen from behind covers a few dozen pixels of a 320x240 tile, below what
  every-second-pixel sampling resolves. The scars are a listed known miss rather than a failure (below).
- **The probe's tiles stand still** (`GiantPreview.still`): an option is measured against its base in the same
  pose, never against a later frame of an idle clip.

## Known gaps

- **The scars do not read in play.** No scar reaches the rear or first-person floor: the forearm slash changes 3
  px of the rear tile, the knuckles nick 1 px in first person, the brow and the chest claws face neither camera.
  They are thinner than the 4 cm stroke rule (§2.3) and the check lists the category in `KNOWN_MISSES` (it must
  still draw; it prints a KNOWN MISS line). P46's scars `cheek` and `back_lash` and bolder floor scars take it
  off (`docs/plans/FOLLOWUPS.md`).
- The view-only design reads (Game view from behind at stage 1) are small at the tile's 320x240 (the giant is
  about 75 px tall there); the floors are calibrated to that size.
- P43's idle clip had not met the preview on this branch (`play_idle` is called when present). At the W26 merge
  `GiantModel.play_idle()` was added and the preview idles: `tests/integration/test_giant_preview.gd` checks the
  clip plays, loops and poses the skeleton, and that a still preview plays none.

## Merge notes

- **P43's idle**: the preview calls `GiantModel.play_idle()` when it exists and `pose_relaxed()` otherwise; the
  option check's tiles and the sheets set `still` and always use `pose_relaxed()`. If the skinned model animates
  by itself (an AnimationPlayer or tree that starts on its own), a still preview must stop it, or the option
  check measures motion.
- **The option check on the skinned model**: the floors were set on the static model's pose; the first-person
  and rear reads depend on the arms' pose, so the merge reruns `tests/slow/test_appearance_render.gd` and
  records the shares (a design category falling under its view floor is a finding, not a floor to lower).
- **Sheet A** is rendered at the merge with the command above (`--renders=` the folder of the approved renders).
- **P45**: the launcher's world button passes `--mode=world --creator`; the first "Change your look..." at a
  shrine after the warm-up is the cost P45 measures.
- **P46** owns the option check's floors for its new content and takes the scars off `KNOWN_MISSES`.
