# B1 — Functional armour, crafting and reforging from titan parts

Milestone B opens with armour that changes the numbers, worn apart from the giant's cosmetic look, made and reforged
at forges from iron, wolf hide and the titans' stone - a reason to hunt titans. Built on `pkg/b1` from the dev head
(the shipped `0.14.0-m14`); the version is **0.15.0-m15**. The seeded 120 s soak is unchanged (41 attacks, 33 hits
resolved, 4 enemy kills, 2 fence breaks, 7,201 frames, 0 errors) and Stoneback's balance duels without armour give
A5's numbers exactly (`docs/BALANCE_LOG.md`, "B1").

## 1. Equipment that changes the numbers

### The records (`data/armour/`, `scripts/core/schemas/armour*_schema.gd`; GameData validates them)

| Record | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| `armour` | `slot` (head, shoulders, chest, arms, waist, legs, trinket: save schema 3's seven), `set`, `defence` {physical: armour points; fire, frost, lightning, arcane, poison: resistance shares}, `poise` (threshold added), `weight` (0-6), `modifiers` (STATUS_MODIFIERS keys: a light piece's stamina costs, movement), `perks` (one to four authored perks), `look` {`garments` (the wardrobe's options it shows) or `meshes` (the giant index's modelled pieces), `clears`, `clears_accessories` (the cosmetic slots a modelled piece empties), `dye`}, `min_stage` |
| `armour_set` | `pieces`, `weight_class`, `bonuses` [{pieces, name, text, modifiers}], `refit` [{tier 2 / 3, cost, forge_tier, works}], `perk_cost`, `works` (the material trait its forge must work) |
| `armour_perk` | `text`, `modifiers`, `resistances` |
| `recipe` | `makes` (an armour piece or a weapon), `cost`, `forge_tier`, `works`, `requires_flag`, `where`, `order` |

The targets (the owner's): 10-15 % less damage in a full Iron set, 20-25 % in a full Quarry set, with the weight's cost
- met at tier 1. Reforged to tier 3 (x1.3 defence and poise) the Quarry set turns 27 %, the Iron set 17 %.

### What wearing it does (`scripts/progression/armour.gd`, pure; decision B1-1)

`Armour.modifiers(equipment)` turns the worn pieces into passives: physical defence into `armor_flat` (a blow keeps
100 / (100 + A) of its physical damage: `DamageResolver.mitigate`), the elemental defences into resistances, poise into
`poise_threshold_add`, the pieces' and their perks' own modifiers, the active set bonuses, and the weight: the worn
pieces' weight beyond `WEIGHT_FREE` (3) slows stamina recovery by `WEIGHT_STEP` (2 %) a point (at most 25 %:
`stamina_regen_mult`, B1's new key, which `Combatant.PassivePools` applies to the stamina recovery).
`PlayerProgression.rebuild_passives` merges them with the talents' and the ancestry's into `Combatant.set_passives`.
Unarmoured the armour adds no key at all, so every pinned number holds (the soak's line is the same).

**Armour scales with the giant** (decision B1-2): its numbers are shares, not amounts - an armour value turns the same
share of a blow at every size (`test_armour_rules.test_the_sets_turn_their_share_of_a_blow`: the same share against a
stage-1 and a stage-6 blow), the poise threshold is the same at every stage (the blows' poise scales with the sizes:
`CombatRules.poise_scale`) - and its mesh is skinned to the giant's skeleton, which is scaled whole. A piece is made
once and kept as the giant grows: no re-crafting per stage.

## 2. Separate from the look

The creator's `character.appearance` (its cosmetic `armour` slot included) is not touched: `Armour.look_of(record,
equipment)` draws the worn, shown pieces over it - a piece's wardrobe garments in their cosmetic slots (its own slot's
first: a worn helm wins the head over the hooded mantle's hood), a modelled piece added with the cosmetic slots it
covers emptied (the Quarry pauldrons clear the shoulders' garment, the heart-stone the neck's accessory) - and
`PlayerAppearance.refresh`, the Character menu's preview and the stills draw that. Each slot has a **Shown / Hidden**
toggle (`equipment.hidden`): a hidden piece still counts, the giant's own clothes show there.

**The Quarry set's stone is modelled, not a shell** (decision B1-3): a skin patch pushed out (Stoneback's own plates,
A1) reads as a rounded rock; a slab with a flat top, chamfered edges and knocked-off corners whose foot hugs the skin
along its outline reads as quarried stone with its thickness at the edge. Each part rides rigidly on the blended
weights of the skin vertex under its centre (as a plate strapped to the body does), takes that vertex's build weights
(the build sliders push it as they push the skin under it) and is built twice - on the bare skin (its mesh) and over the
garments it may lie on (chest and armour for the pauldrons, sleeves for the bracers, trousers for the greaves) - so the
layering table pushes it out only as far as what is worn under it needs (the rings' machinery, P54). The cloth
shader's kind 5 draws it: cut stone mottled at two scales of the hammered map (its pits as chisel marks), its iron where
the mesh's band mask (UV2.y) is 1. The Iron and Hide sets use the wardrobe's proven garments (every stage, every build,
the layering): only the helm is new. A new index category, `armour`, holds the modelled pieces; every earlier giant file
is bit-identical (the helm is built after the npc garments over the envelope inside its slot; the pieces after the
accessories); the people's files too (their manifests follow the giant's garment code).

**What deforms** (the creator's sliders): the build's brawn and girth push every vertex along its skin's normal by
its build weights - the modelled pieces ride it as rings do; the face sliders move the head - the helm's nasal stands
3 cm off the nose (a crop's shells stay under the whole helm). The body's proportions and the stage's height are the
model's own; the armour follows them.

**Checked rendered** (`tests/slow/test_armour_render.gd`, `tests/support/armour_probe.tscn`): every piece at stages 1, 5
and 6 in the idle, a walk's stride and the stomp's raised knee and arms draws over the default look, most of it shows
where the body could cover it (the median share over the poses: 0.59 for the heart-stone under a full beard, 0.82 the
helm, 0.88-1.00 every other piece - not sunk into the body), it reads alike at every stage, and a hidden slot draws
none of it.

## 3. Crafting and reforging (`scripts/world/forge.gd`, `ui/screens/forge_screen.gd`)

The forge (not a parallel system) gains `craft` (a recipe), `refit` (a piece to its next tier) and `set_perk`, each
with its `can_*` verdict - closed, busy, the forge's tier (P31: iron or titan), the trait it must work
(`Forge.works`), the settlement's flag, the materials - and `Events.armour_changed` (the forge's sound; putting armour
on and off is silent). Its screen has three tabs (LB / RB, clicks, Enter): **Weapons** (the weapon in hand and its
reforge, P12, and the titan weapons), **Armour** (every piece's recipe by set: ready, owned, needs materials, or made
elsewhere with where), **Reforge** (every piece owned: its tier now and the next one's numbers, Reforge to tier N, and
its perks - one chosen, never rolled: the set's perk cost each change).

| Set | Recipes (tier 1) | Tier 2 | Tier 3 | A perk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quarry (works titan stone; once `world.quarry.reopened`) | pauldrons 3 slabs + 1 quarry iron; bracers 2 + 1; greaves 3 + 1; heart-stone his heart-stone + 1 quarry iron | 1 quarry iron, 2 iron ingots | 1 slab, 1 titan iron | 1 quarry iron |
| Iron | helm 2 ingots; hauberk 3; war belt 1 ingot + 1 hide; greaves 2 | 2 ingots | 1 ingot, 1 titan iron (a titan forge) | 1 ingot |
| Hide | mantle 3 hides; bracers 2; kilt 2 hides + 1 wood scrap | 2 hides | 2 hides, 1 ingot | 1 hide |

**The Quarry Maul** (`weapon.quarry_maul`, recipe 4 slabs, 2 quarry iron, 2 ingots at Millford's forge): a slab of his
plate on a banded oak haft, x1.5 damage, fits to stage 6; its heavy is **the Quarry Smash** (`ability.quarry_smash`;
from stage 4 `ability.quarry_great_smash`): his overhead smash brought down two-handed - power 44 / 46 x2.2, poise 70 /
85, a longer wind-up, and the `heavy_shockwave` perk's 4 m ring where it lands (power x0.6).

**Where** (decision B1-4): Millford's quarry forge - lit by A3 when Stoneback dies and the quarry reopens - is the first
forge that works titan stone (`works: ["titan_stone"]` in the world layout); the Quarry recipes wait for
`world.quarry.reopened` too. Every other forge makes the Iron and Hide sets and says where the Quarry set is made. A
later titan's settlement unlocks its recipes the same way: its forge's `works` and its flag.

**Materials**: iron ingots (the world's caches and crates, the toll gate), wolf hide (`item.hide`: each wolf of the
world's placed packs leaves one, a dire wolf two - `loot.wolf_hide`, `loot.dire_wolf_hide`; the Valley's wolves and the
roads' wanderers none, so nothing is farmable), wood scrap (fences, raiders). **Titan parts only from titans**
(decision B1-5): Stoneback stays dead (A3); his loot is his heart-stone, four slabs and four quarry iron (A2's three
raised to four), **each plate broken in the fight that kills him adds its own** (a slab each - shoulders, back,
forearms, shins - the brow a quarry iron: `plates[].loot`, `PlateRules.break_loot`), and after the quarry reopens its
pit holds a pile of four slabs and one of two quarry iron (`ItemPickup.requires_flag`). A kill that breaks only his
shins gives 6 slabs and 4 quarry iron, the piles 4 and 2: the set (8 and 4) and the heart-stone; the maul too wants a
fight that breaks more of him.

## 4. The Character menu's Equipment tab (`ui/screens/character_screen.gd`)

The weapon on a line, then the seven slots - each a row (what it wears, its tier and perk) with its Shown / Hidden
toggle; choosing a slot lists the pieces owned for it and Take off, each with its numbers before -> after (armour and
the share of a blow it turns, poise, weight, stamina recovery and costs, movement, health, resistances; the set bonus
it would complete or break), the giant beside it in the armour shown. Below, the bonuses earned, the next one of each
set, and the totals. **Armour goes on and off anywhere out of combat** (decision B1-6: `Armour.change_blocked` - the
checkpoint's own blockers, a menu aside: no fight engaged, no enemy hunting the giant, not grabbed or mid-action);
forges make and reforge it. The pad: the tab row, each slot's row (the slot, its toggle), the slot's options, Close;
every tab fits 1280 x 720 (`test_equipment_tab`).

## 5. Saves (decision B1-7)

Save schema 3's reserved `equipment` block holds it, no schema 4: `{"slots": {...}, "pieces": {armour id: {"tier",
"perk"}}, "hidden": {slot: true}}` - `pieces` and `hidden` are optional keys within schema 3 (`ExpansionState.from_doc`
reads them, `validate` checks their type, `sanitize` drops a piece this build does not know and names it), so an A file
loads with nothing owned and an A build reading a B1 file keeps its slots. Crafted armour and the materials spent are
saved together at the next checkpoint (as a weapon's reforge is).

## 6. Milestone A's carry-overs

- **The warning on the roads** (`tools/world/kinds/warning_post.gd`): the quarry folk's posts - a weathered post with a
  cracked slab of his stone, an old giant's skull and rubble at its foot - where the quarry road leaves the Woodway
  (164 m from his quarry's centre) and on the Woodway from the north (166 m), each with an area and a one-time prompt
  (`tutorial.stoneback_warning_*`) that ends with his death (`unless_flags`, new in the prompt schema).
- **His sight over the hill** (decision B1-8): `TitanTerritory.sees` adds a line of sight from his eye over his home
  (15 m up) to the giant's chest, sampled every 4 m over the world's ground (`sight_clear`): at 100 m from his quarry 18
  of 24 points are seen, 6 hidden behind the hill round his pit (`tests/gate/test_b1_world.gd`).
- **The plate hint**: the first lock on a plated titan shows `tutorial.titan_plates` ("{target_next} or a flick picks
  the next plate in reach"); the lock event carries `plated` (a new filter).
- **The intro after the loading screen**: `GameSession` waits for main's loading gate before it opens the intro
  (`_play_after_loading`; `test_cutscene_screen.test_the_intro_waits_for_the_loading_gate`).
