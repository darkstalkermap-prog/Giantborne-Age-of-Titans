# P36 — Aldmere, the Stonehold and the finale

Wave 21, lane B, beside P35 (the Greywood and the Drowned Steps). Branch `pkg/P36`. Plan:
`docs/plans/M6_M9_PLAN.md` §3.20, with §5.4 (the regions' places), §5.7 (the crown's oath and the main
thread's end), §5.8 (the XP and equipment budgets), §5.9 (choices and consequences), §5.10 (encounters,
wander tables, the siege of Skarn) and §5.13 (the Iron Regiment, Harrok). Of the partner lane only
`quest.oath_wood` and its flags are reached, as data, by tests that skip the wood's orders while the
record is not on the branch.

The kingdom's march and the giant territories are now playable to the end: the herald at the war camp
names the crown's three roads, Fort Aldmere has a real gate and the Iron Regiment behind it, the war
engines and Ballista Ridge can be wrecked, a parley walks up to the fort's gate unharmed; the Stonehold
has Skarn and its siege, the Moot battery, Harrok and the Stone Throne, and taking the throne ends the
campaign on "The Giant King".

## What was built

  archers (elevation markers at an absolute `y`: the bastion's top) and two ballistae; the keep with its
  steps (the Last Stand); the **real gate** (`breakable_site` style gate, `prop.gate_fort_aldmere`: health

  ridge's three ballistae (`engine`: P32's scenes with the element's world id and faction).

## The crown's three roads

| Road | How | Writes oath.crown.outcome | Consequences |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| breach | break the gate (stage 4), the battle until Corvane yields, Spare or Strike | quest.iron_regiment's last stage | spared: Aldmere reconciled, the fort opened, its forge; struck: the garrison routs, remnants wander |
| sabotage | the camp's four engines and the ridge's three (each an offence: Aldmere turns hostile) | quest.war_engines' last stage | the army withdraws (no cavalry squadrons), Aldmere hostile |
| parley | a token the herald accepts (Brannock's banner, the deserters' word, Stoneblood, stage 5), then the horn at the gate | dialogue.corvane | Aldmere allied and reconciled, the fort and its forge open, the crown's flags struck at the toll fort, its wanderers gone |

Every road pays the oath 800 (the meeting's 50 and the resolution's 750, worst and best); the deserters'
word and Stoneblood blood make the parley a first oath at stage 4 (OathRules: breach, parley, sabotage).

## For a new player

- **The choice is named before it is made.** The herald stands at the war camp's edge on the road in
  (the oath's first stage leads there) and names the three roads and their price: breaking the gate or
  the engines makes Aldmere an enemy, a parley keeps the peace. Her four tokens are always listed; one
  the giant lacks is greyed with what it needs ("You need the deserters' word (spare the deserters
  hiding at the burned mill)", "Return when you stand at stage 5"). Corvane's horn tells a giant who
  comes without the herald's word to go back to her; the deserters' sergeant says what sparing them is
  worth (his word for the herald, the camp's engines marked).
- **The battle warns and lets go.** The gate needs stage 4 and the herald says so, and what waits
  behind it; the bailey's portcullis closes only once the giant is inside (the way out is open before);
  the checkpoint `world.cp.fort_gate` is outside the battle within 80 m of its edge; the Iron Regiment
  keeps the formation caps on both platforms (the Wall's two formations: 22 members on the desktop, 16
  on the web; 37 members and 4 ballistae in all, 28 members on the web); Corvane yields and the giant
  chooses.
- **Nothing is lost for good on the main thread.** The siege of Skarn waits for the giant (its line holds
  its fire beyond 200 m) and a burned Skarn can be raised again at stage 6 (Ulfra says how); the Stair
  and the Moot turn a smaller giant back ("a giant of the old height") and Harrok's challenge stone says
  "stage 6"; Harrok's three endings all lead to the throne (the talk ending, open to a giant all three
  powers have sworn to and Skarn stands behind, names why it is offered); the campaign's end names every
  outcome, and Continue leaves the giant free to roam.

## Deviations

- **The siege line is the toll-riders'**, Aldmere's hired siege company (faction toll_riders), not
  Aldmere's own: a parleyed or spared Aldmere would otherwise stand its siege line down and never
  besiege Skarn. Its engines appear with the siege (ConditionSpawn), not before.
- **The siege's "fail timer"** is its bombardment: the line's engines bombard only while the giant is
  within 200 m of the longhouse they aim at, so nothing burns while the giant is away (the plan's pause
  beyond 200 m), and the chunk's unloading suspends it as P37 does. (M8 gate, F12: the event also has a
  backstop timer - 600 s of the giant within 200 m of Skarn burns it should the fire not -, see below.)
- **The oath's XP sits on its resolve stage** (750 on every road) instead of the plan's per-step split
  (gate 100 + yield 650; engines 2 x 150 + withdrawal 450): the totals are the plan's (800 worst and
  best) and P29's generic two-oath budget reads them as written. Ballista Ridge's 150 and the deserters'
  120 are side quests on top.
- **Two helper quests** write the breach and sabotage outcomes (quest.iron_regiment, quest.war_engines),
  first write wins; each is given up once the oath is won another way.
- **The wander tables' siege trains** (stage 5 in Aldmere, stage 6 in the Stonehold) are not shipped:
  P32's engines are placed props and a wander copy cannot bring one along (FOLLOWUPS).
- **The toll fort's tower ballista** stands on a new open gun tower east of the gate (the watchtower's
  closed cabin would hide it), and the tower and the crown's banners are additive lines in
  `crownmarch.json` (P29's table).
- **The fort forge and Skarn's** are built at a giant's scale from the world's vertex-coloured material
  (one draw call) instead of instancing forge.tscn (three).
- **The Moot's watchers** stand on a 66 m circle outside the ring (leash 70 m, plan §5.10's most).
- **The Iron Regiment's web composition keeps three of Corvane's six guards** (`web_formation_scale` 0.5
  on the Last Stand; the Wall's formations 0.7, 7 + banner each as planned): the bailey's formations are
  one cluster and the web's cap is 20 formation members (16 of the Wall and 4 of the guard). Its aggregate
  is therefore 11,740 on the web (the plan's about 12,300); the desktop's 14,080 is the plan's about
  13,700 with the wall ballistae's two crews (360) counted (FOLLOWUPS, for P39's tuning). (M8 gate, F9:
  the count left out the gate garrison's pikes and banner, 8 of the 20 when the gate is broken with the
  garrison alive; the battle now stands that garrison out when it engages - see "M8 gate" below.)

## M8 gate

- **F12: the siege line's shots burn Skarn.** A shot is the engine's (team ENEMY) and P10a's rule lets an
  enemy strike only the player's side, so every catapult stone and ballista bolt passed through the
  NEUTRAL longhouses it was aimed at: Skarn never burned, the siege could only be won, and its "burned"
  branch (and `quest.skarn_rebuild`) was unreachable in play. `SiegeEngine.p36_shot_target()` names the
  prop the engine bombards (one of its `prop_targets`, alive and neutral); `ProjectileSystem` notes it
  when the shot is spawned and lifts that one prop to a target for the shot's own sweep and splash only
  (as P30's provider target does for a melee swing): other props, civilians and Skarn's folk keep the
  rule. The siege also gets a backstop (P37's `fail.within_m`): its 600 s fail timer runs only while the
  giant is within 200 m of Skarn, so a line that cannot burn the halls still ends, and never while the
  giant is away. With the whole line firing (the giant west of the steading, out of every engine's reach)
  two longhouses fall in 117 s of play (51 hits on the halls).
- **F2 (P37's slot rule)**: `quest.siege_of_skarn` begins with an "await" stage ("Stand on Skarn's green:
  the toll-riders' siege line sets up when you are there", Skarn's green marked, a waiting text that the
  line sets up its engines only once the giant stands there) until the line sets up
  (`event.siege_of_skarn.runs`); Ulfra's waiting line says the line will not set up while the giant is
  away. A quest started before the stage existed keeps its own stage id in the save (`defend`).

Tests: the gate's `test_stonehold_walkthrough` - the saved run now also waits for the engines' shots to
