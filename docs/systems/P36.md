# P36 — Aldmere, the Stonehold and the finale

Wave 21, lane B, beside P35 (the Greywood and the Drowned Steps). Branch `pkg/P36`. Plan:
`docs/plans/M6_M9_PLAN.md` §3.20, with §5.4 (the regions' places), §5.7 (the crown's oath and the main
thread's end), §5.8 (the XP and equipment budgets), §5.9 (choices and consequences), §5.10 (encounters,
wander tables, the siege of Skarn) and §5.13 (the Iron Regiment, Harrok). Of the partner lane only
`quest.oath_wood` and its flags are reached, as data, by tests that skip the wood's orders while the
record is not on the branch.

The kingdom's march and the giant territories are now playable to the end: the herald at the war camp
names the crown's three roads, Fort Aldmere has a real gate and the Iron Regiment behind it, the war
engines and Ballista Ridge can be wrecked, a parley walks up to the fort's gate unharmed; the Stonehold
has Skarn and its siege, the Moot battery, Harrok and the Stone Throne, and taking the throne ends the
campaign on "The Giant King".

## What was built

### Layouts and kinds (`tools/data/world/{aldmere,stonehold}.json`, `tools/world/kinds/`)

- **Fort Aldmere** (`fortress_wall`, `keep`): the curtain wall with square corner towers and, at its
  gate, two flat-topped **bastions** (12 x 12 m, 16 m) whose platforms carry the gate garrison's wall
  archers (elevation markers at an absolute `y`: the bastion's top) and two ballistae; the keep with its
  steps (the Last Stand); the **real gate** (`breakable_site` style gate, `prop.gate_fort_aldmere`: health
  3,000, armour 50, stage 4, `open_when_flag` world.aldmere.fort_open, `on_destroyed_flags`
  world.aldmere.fort_breached); the **battle** (`arena` style battle: FortBattle, the bailey's engage
  trigger, the inner portcullis that closes behind the giant); the **parley horn** (`talker` style horn:
  dialogue.corvane); the **fort forge** (`condition_forge`, titan tier, open on a parley or a spared
  Corvane); four wall ballistae (two on the bastions, two inside).
- **The war camp and Ballista Ridge**: the camp's four engines (two catapults, two ballistae) and the
  ridge's three ballistae (`engine`: P32's scenes with the element's world id and faction).
- **Skarn** (`longhouse`): three breakable longhouses (`prop.longhouse_skarn`, faction skarn, tagged
  house) with their burned frame and, behind `rebuilt_flag`, the raised hall and its **RebuildPoint**
  ("Lift the roof beams back", stage 6, while world.skarn.burned holds); Ulfra's talker; Skarn's
  hearth-forge (titan, open once Skarn is saved or rebuilt); the siege line's five engines east of the
  steading (prop targets: the longhouses; bombard range 200 m; siege group).
- **The Moot**: the ring as Harrok's arena (`arena` style moot: MootArena), the challenge stone by its
  gate (`talker` style stone: dialogue.harrok), the **Stone Throne** (`throne`: StoneThrone), the battery's
  four engines; markers, flee points and safe markers of both regions.
- **The Crownmarch** (additive): the toll fort's open **gun tower** east of its gate (`platform`) with the
  stage-5 tower ballista on its deck, and the crown's two banners over the toll fort (`flag_banner`),
  struck after the parley.
- New kinds beyond the plan's four: `engine`, `talker`, `arena`, `condition_forge`, `platform`,
  `flag_banner` (`cairn` gained `requires_stage` for the ridge eyrie's sealed cairn).

### Runtime (`scripts/world/`)

- **FortBattle** (BattleBoss): the bailey stays at peace after a parley (entering engages nothing); a
  settled battle loads defeated; Spare reconciles Aldmere and opens the fort; Strike routs what is left.
- **MootArena** (BossArena): a settled champion never engages again; the talk ending (yielded_talk) keeps
  the ring quiet and reconciles Harrok's clan, as Spare does; Harrok's death empties the Moot
  (world.moot.empty: the watching clans leave, his clan's giants wander).
- **StoneThrone**: "Take the Stone Throne" once boss.harrok.outcome is settled (world.throne.claimed);
  the main thread's completion (campaign.completed) opens the campaign's end screen from it.
- **RebuildPoint**, **ConditionForge** (a Forge with an `open_when` condition).
- **ConditionSpawn**: a placed scene that is instanced only once a quest condition holds - the siege
  line's engines appear when the siege starts (event.siege_of_skarn.runs), the toll fort's tower
  ballista at stage 5. Before that nothing of them is in the world (no draw call, nothing to lock, hit or
  grab); a crew already in the world is handed to an engine that appears after it.
- **SiegeEngine** (P32's file, additive): `prop_targets`, `bombard_within_m` and `siege_group` - a siege
  line bombards its props while the giant is out of its reach and within 200 m of the target (the siege's
  clock), and when the group's last engine falls its soldiers rout (the line breaks); an engine's meshes
  are not drawn beyond 300 m (`P36_DRAW_RANGE_M`).
- **EndScreen** (hotspot): the campaign's end - "The Giant King", time, deaths, level and stage, each
  power's oath (or that it swore none), Skarn's fate, the champion's outcome last; Continue leaves the
  giant free to roam.

## The crown's three roads

| Road | How | Writes oath.crown.outcome | Consequences |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| breach | break the gate (stage 4), the battle until Corvane yields, Spare or Strike | quest.iron_regiment's last stage | spared: Aldmere reconciled, the fort opened, its forge; struck: the garrison routs, remnants wander |
| sabotage | the camp's four engines and the ridge's three (each an offence: Aldmere turns hostile) | quest.war_engines' last stage | the army withdraws (no cavalry squadrons), Aldmere hostile |
| parley | a token the herald accepts (Brannock's banner, the deserters' word, Stoneblood, stage 5), then the horn at the gate | dialogue.corvane | Aldmere allied and reconciled, the fort and its forge open, the crown's flags struck at the toll fort, its wanderers gone |

Every road pays the oath 800 (the meeting's 50 and the resolution's 750, worst and best); the deserters'
word and Stoneblood blood make the parley a first oath at stage 4 (OathRules: breach, parley, sabotage).

## Deviations

- **The siege line is the toll-riders'**, Aldmere's hired siege company (faction toll_riders), not
  Aldmere's own: a parleyed or spared Aldmere would otherwise stand its siege line down and never
  besiege Skarn. Its engines appear with the siege (ConditionSpawn), not before.
- **The siege's "fail timer"** is its bombardment: the event has no timer of its own; the line's engines
  bombard only while the giant is within 200 m of the longhouse they aim at, so nothing burns while the
  giant is away (the plan's pause beyond 200 m), and the chunk's unloading suspends it as P37 does.
- **The oath's XP sits on its resolve stage** (750 on every road) instead of the plan's per-step split
  (gate 100 + yield 650; engines 2 x 150 + withdrawal 450): the totals are the plan's (800 worst and
  best) and P29's generic two-oath budget reads them as written. Ballista Ridge's 150 and the deserters'
  120 are side quests on top.
- **Two helper quests** write the breach and sabotage outcomes (quest.iron_regiment, quest.war_engines),
  first write wins; each is given up once the oath is won another way.
- **The wander tables' siege trains** (stage 5 in Aldmere, stage 6 in the Stonehold) are not shipped:
  P32's engines are placed props and a wander copy cannot bring one along (FOLLOWUPS).
- **The toll fort's tower ballista** stands on a new open gun tower east of the gate (the watchtower's
  closed cabin would hide it), and the tower and the crown's banners are additive lines in
  `crownmarch.json` (P29's table).
- **The fort forge and Skarn's** are built at a giant's scale from the world's vertex-coloured material
  (one draw call) instead of instancing forge.tscn (three).
- **The Moot's watchers** stand on a 66 m circle outside the ring (leash 70 m, plan §5.10's most).
