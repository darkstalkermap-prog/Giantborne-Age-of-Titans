### Schemas and GameData (anchors P34)

- `enemy_schema.gd`: `regeneration`, `yield_at`, `drive_out`, `brood`, `boss_outcome`, and the boss
  phases' `enter_on_poise_breaks`, `hazard` and `summon`, with their semantic checks (a yield needs a
  health fraction or poise breaks and a `boss_outcome`; challenge conditions are quest conditions; flags
  are flag ids; a summoning phase needs a brood; `phase_hp_per_s` names real phases).
- `action_schema.gd`: `indirect` (a projectile with a circle telegraph) and `arena_hazard` (a cylinder
  pulse; `distance_m` is a [min, max] pair).
- `scripts/world/boss_rules.gd` (pure, GameData's post-pass, as FormationRules and SiegeRules): a yield's
  dialogue offers both outcomes; a brood's definition can be carried and its throw flies a body; a phase
  hazard has an `arena_hazard`; a regeneration status carries the tag Regeneration.

### The shared enemy (`enemy_actor.gd`, anchor P34; four one-line hooks)

- **Regeneration** (`p34_regen_step`, run by TrollEnemy): every `interval_s` a hurt troll that carries no
  status tagged `blocked_by_tags` (Burning) heals `hp_per_s` (`phase_hp_per_s` by phase) through
  `CombatEntity.heal` and refreshes its marker status. **Fire**: a prop tagged `fire` broken within
  `brazier_radius_m` sets Burning for `brazier_burn_s`.
- **The yield protocol** (`yield_at`): unkillable while armed; a blow that takes health to the floor
  holds it there and yields ("health"); `poise_breaks` (two under `challenge_conditions`: Wildheart)
  yield too ("honour"); BattleBoss yields Corvane when his guard breaks. At the yield the boss is IMMUNE
  (`accepts_hit` refuses everything but the confirmed strike, so blows, pulses, projectiles and burning
  made before it land on nothing) and harmless at once (AI off, its action cancelled, tokens back, its
  projectiles and hazards discarded, unlockable, the giant's lock released); `Events.boss_yielded(boss id)`
  and the yield prompt. Only the prompt decides (`Events.dialogue_outcome`, or `p34_decide`): Spare
  (`spared`, kneeling and immune for good), Strike with `on_strike` kill (one blow from the giant, the only
  hit it accepts) or fight (he rises and fights to the death, `slain`).
- **Lobs** (`indirect`): the windup commits the landing point at the target's feet; the runner aims there
  and the circle is announced there (P32's landing circle and P28's whistle follow).
- **Attempts** (`p34_reset_attempt`): the first phase again, full health, no statuses or poise breaks,
  the archetype's own reset, the walk home; `attempt_phases` lists the phases of the current attempt;
  `p34_stop_return` ends the walk home when a new attempt engages.
- `max_ranged_windup_ticks` leaves the troll and rival-giant lobs out, so the archers' and mages'
  launch gaps stay as they were (66 and 60 ticks).

### Trolls (`troll_enemy.gd`, `troll_brain.gd`, `matriarch_brain.gd`)

- TrollEnemy extends OgreEnemy (the ogre's super-armoured strikes and recovery punish); TrollBrain is the
  OgreBrain plus a burning, badly hurt troll pressing its attack. The regeneration shows as a slow green
  pulse on the rig, Burning as an orange flicker (`tint`).
- The fire pit: a node of group `fire_pits` (meta `radius_m`); a troll or whelp set down in it (thrown or
  dropped) comes out Burning; a burning body that hits the Matriarch sets her Burning for 10 s and burns
  her for 300 (the giant's fire).
- The Matriarch (MatriarchBrain extends OgreBossBrain): her whelp throw picks up the nearest free whelp
  within 16 m at its windup and the throw's projectile carries it (`ProjectileSystem.carry_body`); the
  brain skips the throw while no whelp is in reach. Her phase-2 roar calls three whelps (at most six
  living). The drive-out: with the nests burned and the back wall open, below 60 % she runs for the exit
  marker; there (or 10 s later) her outcome is `fled`, `Events.boss_disengaged(fled)` and she leaves.

### Rival giants (`rival_giant_enemy.gd`, `rival_giant_brain.gd`, `harrok_brain.gd`)

- RivalGiantEnemy extends OgreBoss (the Ogre King's charge, body sweep and sequences); the charge's
  recovery reels, a yielded giant kneels.
- HarrokBrain owns the Moot's stones (`hazard_step`: what to announce and what to strike); the actor
  announces each stone as a circle telegraph keyed `<id>#stone<n>` with the giant as its target (threat
  cues at the stone) and strikes it through `AttackRunner.p34_hazard_pulse`, so every stone is Harrok's.
  His second phase begins at 50 % or after two poise breaks, never while he reels.

### Arenas and battles

- `boss_arena.gd`: **the disengage rule** for every boss (beyond `inner_radius_m + 20 m` for 5 s: the arena
  waits again with its way open, the boss starts a new attempt, `boss_disengaged(left)`, the music back
  to the zone); the boss's brood and the arena's own encounter are not intruders; a yield's Spare wins the
  fight (`boss_defeated`); a boss that fled ends it (gate open, the defeated flag).
- `battle_boss.gd` (extends BossArena): the battle's phases over its encounters (force-activated once per
  attempt at 100 / 60 / 25 % of the aggregate, or when every unit of the phases so far has fallen),
  `boss_phase_changed` for each, the aggregate of the planned composition (stage ranges and the web's kept
  formation slots, the director's rules) and its engines, Corvane's yield when his guard breaks, the
  battle standing down on his yield, and the attempt reset.
- `encounter_director.gd` (anchor P34 and one line in `_spawn`): `p34_reset_encounter` (the fallen of a
  battle come back) and no unit spawning inside the giant's footprint.

## Decisions and deviations

- **Thunderhead's `on_hit_stagger` (P31)**: trolls and rival giants do not join it (its archetypes stay
  soldier, archer, mage, cavalry): a 1.5 s stagger on every hit would erase the trolls' super-armoured
  rhythm and the bosses' telegraphed patterns. Corvane and the iron guards are soldiers and are staggered.
- **Iron guards use the shieldman's scene** (`scenes/enemies/shieldman.tscn`, the definition set by the
  director at spawn): the package's scene list names no iron-guard scene.
- **No ProjectileSystem or TelegraphVisuals change**: the whelp rides the existing `carry_body`; the stones
  are cylinder pulses of the runner, and their circles are ordinary telegraph records keyed per stone
  (TelegraphVisuals draws them at their origin since no entity has that key). The anchors stay empty.
- **No `hud.gd` change**: the HUD already hides its target frame for whatever the boss bar shows, and the
  boss bar now shows every unit of an engaged battle.
- **The EnemyBrain anchor is unused**: everything brain-side lives in the new brains.
- `scripts/world/boss_rules.gd` is outside the owned list (GameData's post-pass needs a pure module, as
  P30 and P32 did); `data/test/faction_test_moot_clans.json` is a test faction beside the level and
  encounter records (the watchers' neutral standing).
- **Cancelling an action**: the yield, the drive-out and a new attempt end the running action through
  `CombatController._finish_action` (by `call`), as CombatController (not P34's) has no public cancel.
- **Burning deals no damage** in this game (a marker status): "Burning across the yield" holds trivially,
  and the test checks that nothing changes; a burning whelp's 300 on the Matriarch is an explicit fire hit.
- **The phase-spent rule** (BattleBoss): a phase whose units have all fallen gives way to the next even
  above its fraction, so no composition stalls (a web composition can weigh the last stand above 25 %).
- **The footprint rule applies to every director spawn**: a spawn point inside the giant's capsule (plus
  the unit's and 1 m) moves out along the line from the giant; nothing in the existing levels spawns
  there.
- **Test compositions are smaller than §5.13's**: the bailey test fields 20 units (aggregate 6,180; 5,140
  on the web), not the Iron Regiment's ~13,700; the region packages place the real battle, den and Moot.
- **Test tiers**: the default tier keeps one or two lean cases per suite; what takes simulated seconds
  moved to `tests/slow/test_big_battery.gd` (the charge at stone, dire wolves, the whelp's flight, a
  burning whelp thrown into the Matriarch through a real grab and throw, the full 5 s disengage, the web
  composition and a battle's new attempt, Harrok's stones in real time). The default disengage test
  shortens the 5 s to 0.25 s.

## M8 gate

Findings of the M8 gate review in this package's code, fixed on top of wave 21 (lane m8y).

### F7: fight inputs never decide the yield prompt

- **Finding.** The ability keys 1-4 were also the dialogue screen's choice keys, and the prompt only
  waited a fixed 0.75 s after each line: mashing abilities 1 and 2 through a yield confirmed Strike and
  killed Corvane, and pad A/B mashes spared him.
- **Fix** (`ui/screens/yield_prompt.gd`; two hooks in P13's `dialogue_screen.gd`; the yield dialogues):
  - the number keys never choose, and the choices carry no numbers (`numbered_keys()` false);
  - a line's choices arm only once every button - keys, mouse buttons, pad buttons, a stick or trigger
    past half - has been let go for `ARM_S` 0.75 s: when the prompt opens and after every change of
    line. A button still held as it opens keeps it unarmed ("Let go of every button to choose.");
  - on the deciding lines (Spare / Strike, and the confirm) a choice is taken by holding it `HOLD_S`
    0.8 s - pad A, Enter or the left mouse button, with a fill across the button; letting go, moving the
    focus or leaving the button cancels the hold. No tap decides;
  - the confirm lines of `dialogue_yield_corvane.json` and `dialogue_yield_harrok.json` offer "No - let
    me think again." first (focused), so Strike is never first nor the default; B / Esc goes back;
  - a line under the choices says what to do for the device in use (`YieldPrompt.hint_for`): on the
    pad "Hold A on a choice to take it", "D-pad / left stick: move", "B: back"; on keyboard and mouse
    "Hold Enter, or hold the mouse button on a choice, to take it", "Arrows: move", "Esc: back".
  - `press_choice` (refused, `not_armed`, until the choices arm) and `arm_now()` stay for tests and
    tools. Nothing else answers the prompt: the gate suites decide through `EnemyActor.p34_decide`, the
    call the bots will use (Known gaps).
- **Tests.** Real `InputEvent`s through the real ScreenStack (`tests/support/yield_input.gd`; a fake
  clock through `YieldPrompt.test_clock`):
  - `test_yield_prompt_input` (default, 66 checks): fight inputs (keys 1/2, 2/1, pad A/B, the mouse
    with RT) never arm or decide; the number keys do nothing once armed; RT held as it opens; the
    pad answer (a tap decides nothing, the D-pad, a held A, "No" first and focused on the confirm, B
    back, a half hold cancelled, a full hold strikes); the keyboard (Down, a held Enter, Esc back) and a
    held mouse button on Spare.
  - `test_yield_prompt_mashing` (slow, 1,544 checks): keys 1-4 and A/B/X/Y in all 24 orders, the D-pad,
    RB/RT/LT, both sticks, the mouse over each choice, unarmed and armed, on both lines, bursts with
    1 s pauses, buttons held as it opens; Corvane and Harrok. Nothing is decided.
  - `test_yield_prompt_wall_clock` (slow, 24 checks, 9.7 s by design): the review's mashes in real time
    (no test hooks): 9 failures before the fix, 0 after.
  - `test_yield_protocol` arms the prompt with `arm_now()`: it set the old private `_armed_at_ms`.

### F11: Harrok struck rises to a fight the bar and the music follow

- **Finding.** On Strike Harrok rises and fights to the death (`on_strike` "fight"): his AI and lock came
  back, but no event said so - the boss bar kept "yields" and the boss music, ended by the yield,
  never came back.
- **Fix.** `EnemyActor.p34_decide`'s fight branch emits `boss_engaged` again under the yield's boss id,
  the event its consumers already handle (plan §2.4): the bar drops "yields", the music re-enters the
  boss state. The cue router starts the boss music in the phase the boss fights in now and plays the
  arena gate's cue only on the arena's own engagement tick (P19.md, "M8 gate").
- **Test.** `test_yield_protocol`, `test_struck_he_rises_and_the_bar_and_the_music_follow_the_fight` (4
  failures before the fix): after Strike the bar does not say "yields", the music's boss is Harrok
  again, its pending state is the boss state, in phase 2; he is lockable, not yielded, not unkillable.

### F13: no boss can be grabbed

- **Finding.** Plan §2.10 and §5.13: no boss can be grabbed. Warlord Corvane (2.0 m, small, torso fist)
  had no Grab immunity: once his poise broke a stage-4 to stage-6 giant could seize him, and a blow to
  his 30 % floor then made him yield in the giant's hand.
- **Fix.** `data/enemies/corvane.json` carries `"immunities": ["Grab"]`, like Harrok and the Matriarch:
  E's grab, Crushing Grip, Rampart Grip and the fists' finisher refuse him with "immune" and spend
  nothing. `EnemyActor._p34_still` (a yield, a stand-down) ends the giant's hold on the actor
  (`grab_ended` "interrupted"), so no yield prompt opens with a boss in the hand.
- **Test.** `test_boss_no_grab` (default, 85 checks; 44 failures before the fix): at stages 4, 5 and 6 a
  staggered Corvane in reach is refused by every grab and nothing is spent; a held boss that yields is
  set down.

### F36: a boss in reach but out of sight walks round what hides the giant

- **Finding.** In the review's stage-4 drive-out the Matriarch stood for a minute (ticks 15000-18713) in
  her strike band with a scenery trunk between her eye and the giant, waiting to see him, while each
  blow she took kept her memory fresh: no attack, no step. The giant beat her from 1.00 to 0.598 for
  free and she fled. The same stall was in both approaches.
- **Fix** (in the AI; the den's scenery is unchanged). The perception snapshot says `occluded` (in sight
  range and in the view cone, the sight line blocked). In reach and occluded - a sequence entry's band
  (the Matriarch, Harrok, rival giants, the Ogre King) or the melee attack range (trolls, ogres, every
  melee archetype) - the brain steps round the target at its distance (`EnemyBrain._regain_sight`:
  0.6 rad the way `reposition_sign` says, the other way after 2.5 s still hidden) instead of standing,
  and strikes as before once it sees the target. A target out of range or out of the cone is never
  occluded: the control runs without a trunk are identical before and after (attacks, sight,
  displacement), and the 30 seeded boss runs never meet the case (`test_bosses_seeded`: 284 checks run
  alone, 282 inside the whole gate tier, as at wave 21; 0 failures).
- **Test.** `tests/slow/test_boss_sight.gd` (26 checks): the Matriarch, Harrok, a rival giant, the Ogre
  King, a troll and an ogre behind a 1 m trunk, hit every 1.5 s for 12 s - 24 failures before the fix (no
  attack, about 11.5 of the 12 s standing still), 0 after; the control without a trunk unchanged.
