# P37 — The living world: wandering encounters and world events

Wave 20, lane B, beside P34 (trolls, rival giants, three bosses). Branch `pkg/P37`. Plan:
`docs/plans/M6_M9_PLAN.md` §3.21, with §2.13 (the living world and its streaming rules, decision 27),
§5.10 (the Crownmarch's table and its two events) and §5.9 (what the choices change). Of the partner
lane nothing is used: the dire wolf template *prefers* `enemy.dire_wolf` (P34's archetype) by id and is
a wolf until that record exists.

The world now moves without the player: patrols, packs, raiders, militia and Aldmere's columns walk the
Crownmarch's roads, raiders fall on Millford, and an army column marches the King's Road — but nothing
acts on ground that is not loaded, every event shows itself to the player, and nothing can be farmed.

## What was built

### Templates and their copies (`scripts/world/encounter_director.gd`, anchor P37)

An encounter record with `"template": true` is never active by itself (`reload_encounters` skips it).
`EncounterDirector.p37_copy_record(tpl, n, centre, yaw, ground)` makes a copy: ids `<template id>.<n>`
(every spawn `<entity id>.<n>`, formation banners and engine crews renamed with them), its centre at the
anchor and every spawn point moved and turned by `yaw` about the template's own centre onto the loaded
ground's height, each formation's yaw turned with it, `persist_death` false, no loot table, no flags of
its own, the spawn's `prefer` definition when that record exists, the copy radii (activate 280 m,
deactivate 340 m: a wanderer is placed 120-220 m away). `p37_add_copy(rec, info)` registers it as an
encounter of the director, so activation, the budget and the formation cap, the leash, LOD, the
streaming rules (P24b), formations (P30), stand-downs (P29) and crews (P32) treat it as a placed one.
Nothing of a copy is saved: `_mark_cleared` writes no cleared flag for it and its members persist no
death. When a copy's member spawns (`_p37_on_member_spawned`) it gets:

- its record's kill XP times the copy's `xp_scale` (`combatant.xp_reward`; the existing kill-XP path pays
  it, no progression change): 1 for an entry's first three spawns, then 0;
- its spawn's `targets` (P30's provider for that unit alone: `EnemyActor.p37_set_targets`);
- the points it walks in turn (`info.patrol`: a wanderer's road nodes, a raid's round of the houses), each
  kept at its offset from the group's middle;
- in a moving column, the point it keeps to (`EnemyActor.p37_join_march`).

Its faction's standing comes with its faction (P11b): Aldmere's pike column is neutral while Aldmere is at
peace and walks past the giant holding its places in the column. `p37_move_copy` re-places a copy that is
not in the world (an abstract column), `p37_march` walks the formations of one that is (their pivots and
facings follow the column's point; escorts make for a place 6 m ahead), `p37_deactivate` sends it back
into the abstract, `p37_remove_copy` frees what is left of it, `p37_preload` loads the templates' scenes
at level load (no spawn hitch). Three hook lines sit outside the anchor (`_ready`, `reload_encounters`,
`_mark_cleared`), plus a `_dirty` mark when a copy moves (its activation is looked at again at once).

### Columns' members (`scripts/actors/enemy_actor.gd`, anchor P37)

`p37_set_targets(targets)` gives one unit P30's target provider; `p37_join_march(target)` makes an at-peace
member's post its place in the column (a formation slot while its formation stands, else its offset from
the column's point) 1.5 m ahead, facing the march; a hostile formation member follows its slot (P30).

### WanderDirector (`scripts/world/wander_director.gd`)

The runtime node `WanderDirector` that `LevelRoot.RUNTIME_NODES` makes in level.world (P20 declared it). It
runs in the campaign only (`is_live()`: `GameSession.is_campaign()`, or `test_active` in tests), from a Sim
PRE_ACTORS hook after the EncounterDirector: tables and triggers every 30 ticks, running events and
columns every 6.

**Wandering encounters** (`wander_table`, one per region). While the giant stands in a table's region and
its interval (a random 60-120 s; 10 s after an attempt that found nothing) has run out, `try_spawn` adds
one copy of a template, chosen by weight among the entries whose stage range holds the giant's stage,
whose `requires_flags` are truthy and `forbids_flags` not and whose `conditions` hold (faction standings
filter them this way). Refusals (counted in `refusals`): `cap` (max_active copies alive), `no_entry`,
`budget` (the director's combatant budget and formation cap must hold the whole copy), `no_place`. The
place (`find_place`): a road node of the region (or its markers or POIs) 120-220 m from the giant on the
ground plane, every spawn point on loaded, ready ground and outside the camera's frustum, the anchor on the
human navmesh within 4 m (`NavQuery.closest_point`, one query of the tick's budget - none left, the place
waits), never within a settlement's radius + 25 m, 90 m of a placed encounter that is not cleared, 120 m of
another copy or 120 m of a running event. The copy faces along the road and walks the road's nodes within
`patrol_m` (40) of the anchor. An entry with a `route` is a column placed on its route that marches to its
end and leaves the world there out of view. Each spawn counts in `wander.<table short>.<entry>.spawns`
and is logged (`spawn_log`, `Events.wander_spawned`). A copy goes when the giant leaves it (its encounter
dormant again after a member spawned), when cleared (its corpses gone) and, never reached, after 90 s.

**World events** (`world_event` records of the level; `scripts/world/world_event.gd` holds a run's state
and its pure rules). At most one runs; one starts when `WorldEvent.can_start_with` holds: the trigger's
stage range, flags and conditions, `max_runs`, the cooldown since the last run ended (or started:
`cooldown_from`) in play time, its centre chunk loaded and ready, the giant within `start_radius_m` (200 m).
Starting counts a run, says `start_text` on the HUD (with P28's alarm horn at its centre), puts its
objective in the tracker, journal, compass, map and marker (Quests' P37 section, tracked at once; the
player's choice comes back when it ends), sends the folk of the factions its raiders hunt to shelter (and
those who come into the world while it runs), and brings its encounters' copies into the world. While its
centre chunk is not loaded and ready it is suspended: its fail timer stops (its copies leave the world
with the chunk anyway); after `abandon_after_s` (600 s) of that it is given up with no outcome. The
protect objective lost (`lost_to_fail` of its targets destroyed or killed) or the fail timer out ends it
with `fail_outcome`; every copy defeated (each member dead or routed) with `success_outcome`; a column home
with `end_outcome`. An outcome sets `<id>.done`, `<id>.outcome` (the `event_done` condition), its
`set_flags` (first write wins), pays its XP and items once (`reward.<short>.<outcome>`), burns `burn` of its
protected targets at least, says its text, routs the survivors, records `<id>.last_end_s`, emits
`world_event_ended` and asks for a checkpoint. The giant's death resets the attempt (its copies go, its
clock restarts; outcomes already won stay). A running event is carried across reloads in a static table:
a respawn (or fast travel) within 120 m of it is refused with its `respawn_text` and moves to another
checkpoint (`GameSession.register_respawn_blocker`), and the reloaded level resumes it (a death from the
start of its attempt, the run counted once); the title, a new game or a continue forget it.

**Moving columns** (`route`: the level's road graph from one index place to another, there and back when
`round_trip`). The column is a point walked at `speed_mps` whatever is loaded (`WorldEvent.Route`; an
event's walk begins where its centre lies on the road and a round trip ends there), halting while a member
fights. Its copy is in the world only while that point lies on loaded, ready ground within its activate
radius; when the point leaves loaded ground the copy is despawned back into the abstract and comes back
where the point has walked to.

### Schemas, conditions and GameData (anchors P37)

- `encounter`: `template`; a template's spawn may take `targets` (teams, factions, prop_tags, priority,
  aggro_player_m) and `prefer` (a record id) - a placed encounter's may not.
- `wander_table`: `patrol_m`; an entry's `conditions`, `route` ({from, to, round_trip, stage}) and
  `speed_mps` (a path and a route exclude each other; a speed needs one of them).
- `world_event`: `trigger.max_runs`, `trigger.cooldown_from`; `objective.text` (`{standing}`, `{total}`),
  `objective.marker`; `outcomes.<name>.text`, `.burn`; `success_outcome`, `fail_outcome`, `end_outcome`
  (named outcomes must exist), `start_text`, `respawn_text`, `route`, `abandon_after_s`, `xp_full_runs`,
  `bot`.
- `QuestConditions`: `{"type": "event_done", "event": id, "outcome": optional}`.
- `GameData` (`WorldEvent.data_problems`): tables' and events' templates are templates of their level,
  leashed, persisting and dropping nothing; route ends and objective markers are places of the level's
  index; warnings for an unused template or an unknown protect target.

### Quests (`scripts/quests/quests.gd`, anchor P37)

`p37_set_event` / `p37_clear_event` keep a running event's objective (objective_for()'s shape, its live
position: a column's point moves). Four hook lines: `quest_state` (an event is ACTIVE while it runs),
`active_quest_ids` (events right after the main quest), `objective_for`, `reset_for_tests`. Nothing is
saved: a reload asks the WanderDirector again.

## Decisions and deviations

- **Campaign only.** The living world runs only in the campaign (and in tests that switch it on): the
  sandbox, the test levels and every existing test that loads the real world in sandbox mode are unchanged.
- **Hooks outside the anchors.** `quests.gd` is not on §3.21's hotspot list, but it has a P37 anchor (the
  section is there) and four one-line hooks outside it; `encounter_director.gd` has three hook lines and
  one `_dirty` mark outside its anchor. These are what make an event visible to a new player (tracker,
  journal, compass, map) and copies first-class encounters.
- **Spawn `targets` and `prefer`** (encounter schema, P37 anchor) are not in §2.13; the raid needs the
  provider per spawn, and `prefer` lets the dire wolf pack use P34's archetype without a data change.
- **Routes instead of patrol paths.** The streamed world has no patrol-route nodes, so columns walk the
  index's road graph between two places (`route`); `path` still works where a level has one.
- **The army column starts on the road east of the toll fort** (its centre, (524, 347)), not inside the
  fort where the fort's own pikes stand; its round trip comes home there.
- **Raiders** take props tagged `house` as well as faction millford (a house carries its faction on its
  node, not its entity, so P30's provider never matched it by faction) and raid each for himself
  (`alert_group` false: a raider who spots a villager would otherwise wake the others to a giant far off,
  whom they then flee). Their leash centre is the hamlet's east half (-350, 640) so the 70 m leash holds the
  five threatened houses; the event's centre is Millford's.
- **Abandonment**: an event unloaded for `abandon_after_s` (600 s) is given up with no outcome (not in
  §2.13, which only suspends it): a raid the player walked away from does not wait forever. The column
  never is (it marches on while unloaded).
- **Nothing farmable**: a template's spawns persist nothing and drop no loot (GameData errors otherwise),
  an entry's kill XP stops after three spawns, an event's after `xp_full_runs` runs (raid 2 = its max, column
  1), and outcome rewards are once-flags.
- **Deferred: the toll-riders' siege train** (§5.10, stage 6). Engines are props whose XP, loot and
  destruction deltas are keyed by world id: a copy would pay and record them again every time. It waits for
  a once-only engine copy (FOLLOWUPS).
- **Deferred: the reeve's changed dialogue after `fell`** (§5.10). `dialogue.reeve_tamsin` is P29's data;
  `world.millford.burned` is set for it (FOLLOWUPS).
- **Tests outside §3.21's list**: `tests/support/living_world_fixtures.gd` (the tiny streamed world the
  three integration suites share) and `tests/slow/test_living_world_slow.gd` (the real world: a wanderer
  on the Crownmarch's roads and the army column out of the world and back).
- **The gate test sets the director's AI LOD distances** (near 130 m, mid 220 m): the AI LOD puts actors
  beyond 90 m of the giant to sleep (P10a), so a raid goes on where the giant is; the giant watches from
  the meadow out of the raiders' way, the hamlet at full rate.
- **A stage-4 or stage-5 giant scares raiders off**: once a raider has burned the house it targeted and
  sees nothing else, P30's provider falls back to the giant, and an aware soldier outmatched by a stage-4
  giant routs (decision 26) - however far the giant is. The raid thins itself out at stages 4-5 (routed
  raiders count as defeated: it may end `defended`); at stage 3 it runs as designed (the gate test).
  FOLLOWUPS.

No existing test was changed.

## M8 gate

- **F2 (with F20): one event in play, not one event running.** §2.13's "at most one at a time" held for
  every running event, so a Millford raid the giant left behind (waiting or suspended at the Kingsgate
  shrine) or Aldmere's column marching the abstract kept the siege of Skarn - which starts once, after
  Ulfra's plea - from ever starting. The rule now holds for the events *in play* (`WanderDirector.in_play`:
  a member of its copies in the world, or its centre or route point within its start radius of the giant).
  An event left behind keeps no slot; while another is in play it waits (its attempt does not begin and a
  column's copy is held in the abstract, not brought near). `running_event()` names the event in play first.
  The abandonment clock is no longer the attempt's: a death keeps it, the carry takes it across a reload
  (`unloaded_s`) and the save holds it (the flag `<event>.unloaded_since_s`: the play time its centre was
  left, false while loaded). The giant's death resets every running event's attempt (the check asked for a
  living giant). The siege quest's first stage ("await") sends the giant to Skarn's green with a waiting
  text, and Ulfra's waiting line says the line waits for the giant (P36's data).
- **F19: a running event survives a quit to the title and a continue.** The carry is forgotten on every
  mode change, and the save holds only the run's flags, so a siege saved running was lost (its `max_runs`
  1 spent, its quest stuck). A run the save holds in progress (`WorldEvent.run_in_progress`: counted, its
  `last_start_s` after its last end) and not carried begins its attempt again on the level's next load
  (`_resume_saved`), waiting where it was, its run counted once and its abandonment clock from the save.
  `game_session.gd` is unchanged.
- **F18: wanderers stand on roads, never in a place.** The anchors were every road node of a region,
  and the index's graph has nodes at every POI, checkpoint and green (their connectors end there) and along
  the layout's extra edges (the Needle's 5 m slot, the Hidden Grove's rockfall route): the review's probe
  found 164 problems among 4,550 spawn points (wanderers' footprints on every road node, and the placed
  fights) - members in walls, off their navmesh or in deep water - and the live `find_place` chose a seal,
  the Needle's slot or a shrine in each of its six cases (197 of 480 places from the Greywood shrine in the
  sealed grove). The index now names each edge's kind (`WorldIndex.ROAD_KIND_*`, written by
  P25's index writer); `_build_roads` anchors only on nodes of a road's own polyline open to every stage
  (not a connector, an extra edge, a stage-limited or direct one), `PLACE_CLEAR_M` (30 m) from every
  checkpoint, POI centre, cairn and seal, never in a sealed place (a secret POI's radius +
  `SEALED_MARGIN_M`, a seal's 30 m), and patrols walk only those roads. `footprint_problem` checks each
  member where it would stand: its body (radius and height over the ground) touching no static or prop
  geometry, no water over 0.6 of its height (a titan-layer giant wades), its own nav layers within its
  radius + 2 m and 4 m up or down (the chunks' own navmesh regions; the whole footprint costs one query of
  the tick's budget, and a spent budget makes the place wait); it replaces the anchor's snap. Wanderers
  still find places in every table: 16-63 accepted anchors per entry.
- **The siege's fail timer** (P36, F12): `fail.within_m` (world_event schema, P37's anchor) runs an event's
  fail timer only while the giant is within that distance of its centre; the siege of Skarn's 600 s backstop
  uses 200 m.
- **N4.4 (fix-up 1, the audit's play pass): an event leads the tracker only while it is in play.** The
  director tracked an event when it started and gave the tracker back only when it ended, so a Millford raid
  the giant walked away from held the HUD marker and the compass 1.3 km off for its ten minutes, and Aldmere's
  column - resumed WAITING by a Continue far from the toll fort, `abandon_after_s` 0 - held them for the rest
  of the game: the main thread's leads (grow6's Ironway, the Standing Stones) never showed. `sync_tracking()`
  (every update of the running events) gives the tracker back - to the quest tracked before the event took
  it while that is still active, else to nobody (the main quest leads) - once a run is out of play
  (`in_play`), and takes it again when the run comes back into play with no other event in play
  (`WorldEvent.tracker_given_back`; `_take_tracker` / `_give_tracker_back`, which `_begin_attempt` and
  `end_event` use too). The event stays in the tracker's list, the journal and the map, where the player may
  track it: a choice the player makes while it is out of play stands. The army column's `abandon_after_s` is
  600 (as the raid's): a run left waiting with its centre unloaded is given up; marching it still ends by
  coming home. Tests: `test_world_events` (`test_an_event_left_behind_gives_the_tracker_back_until_it_is_in_play_again`:
  3 checks failed before; `test_a_column_left_waiting_far_off_is_given_up`).
- **N4.4 (fix-up 2, the re-audit): after the campaign an event left behind leads nothing either.** Giving
  the tracker back made "nobody" the tracked quest, and `Quests.tracked_id()` then falls back to the main
