# P33 — Talents and ancestries: three trees, passives, the ancestry choice

Wave 19, lane B, beside P32 (siege engines, cavalry and fists). Branch `pkg/P33`. Plan:
`docs/plans/M6_M9_PLAN.md` §3.17, with §2.11 (progression: talents, ancestries, `Combatant.passives`),
§5.12 (the trees, every talent's effect, the ancestries), §5.7 (`quest.ancestry`), §2.14 (the
`ancestry` screen and the shrine's Talents and Ancestry tabs), §2.3 (the flag `ancestry.chosen`),
§2.4 (`talent_learned`, `ancestry_chosen`) and decision 22: talents and ancestries are passives on the
`Combatant`, never statuses; three trees of nine talents; three ancestries chosen once at a shrine
after the world opens, and a migrated save gets the same choice. No symbol of the partner lane is used.

## What was built

### Data

- **27 talents** (`data/talents/talent_<tree>_<name>.json`, §5.12's table): Destroyer (melee, grabs,
  stagger), Guardian (protection, poise), Primordial (magic, fury); per tree two talents in each of
  tiers 1-4 (columns 1 and 2) and a crown in tier 5 (column 0, 2 points); every other talent costs 1.
  A talent of tiers 2-4 needs the one above it in its column (§2.11's example: Shoulder Charge needs
  Heavy Hands), a crown both talents of tier 4, so a whole tree costs 10 points. Each record carries
  a short player-facing `description`, `requires_level` (its tier's level) and its `effects`: the
  passives (`modifiers`, only `STATUS_MODIFIERS` keys) or the ability it grants (`grants`: Shoulder
  Charge, Cleave of Ages, Bulwark, Stonewall Stance, Rune Lance, Primal Regrowth, Storm Call - P31's
  records, `granted_by: "talent"`).

- **Three ancestries** (`data/player/ancestry_{stoneblood,stormborn,wildheart}.json`; `ancestry.neutral`

- `data/progression/progression_default.json`: `talent_tier_levels` `[6, 8, 9, 10, 12]`.
- `data/quests/quest_ancestry.json` (`quest.ancestry` "The Blood of the Old Kings", §5.7): starts at
  stage 4 while no ancestry is chosen (`stage_at_least` 4, `not ancestry_chosen`); one stage "Choose

  five world shrines with `markers_done_when` dropping each one not yet visited
  (`visited.<shrine id>`), so the objective marker leads to the nearest visited shrine. Optional: the

  Ancestry tab lets you choose your ancestry once." Dismissed by the flag `ancestry.chosen` (20 s

### Schemas and GameData (anchors P33)

- `talent_schema.gd`: `column` (0-2); semantic: a passive never uses `heal_per_tick` or
  `slow_fraction` (periodic heals and slows are statuses), every `_mult` is above 0, the crown stands
  in column 0 and the rest in 1 or 2.
- `ancestry_schema.gd`: `attribute_bonus`, `resistances`, `modifiers`, `signature`, `dialogue_tags`,
  `dialogue_note`; semantic: ids start `ancestry.`, the passive rule above, tags are plain lowercase
  words.
- `progression_schema.gd`: `talent_tier_levels` (an int array).
- `game_data.gd` post-pass (anchor P33) - `Talents.data_errors`: the tier levels (five, rising, within
  the level cap); each talent's `requires_level` is its tier's, its requirements are talents of its own
  tree in lower tiers, at most two talents share a tree's tier and none a column; a grant is an
  ability with `granted_by: "talent"` in the library, open at the talent's level; every
  `granted_by: "talent"` ability is granted by exactly one talent; a choosable ancestry has a signature
  with `granted_by: "ancestry"` in the library, and every such ability is exactly one ancestry's.

### The rules (`scripts/progression/talents.gd`, `class_name Talents`, pure)

Records and tiers (`ids(tree)` in tree, tier and column order, `at_tier`, `tier_levels`,
`level_needed`), learning on a character dictionary (`check` refuses in the order unknown, known,
level, requires, points with a sentence for each; `learn` spends once), passives (`passives_for`:
`_mult` keys multiply, the rest add; `combine`), the player-facing lines (`modifier_line(s)`:
"Heavy attack damage +10%", "Grab reach +1.5 m", "Stamina costs -15%"; `effect_lines`: the passives
and "New ability: Shoulder Charge - lower your shoulder and charge 12 m, bowling over everything in
your path (22 stamina, 9 s cooldown)"; `ancestry_summary`), the ancestries (`ancestry_ids`,
`is_choosable`, `attribute_bonus_of`, `signature_of`, `dialogue_tags_of`) and the data checks.

### The giant

- **`Combatant.passives`** (`scripts/combat/combatant.gd`): `set_passives(mods, resistances)`;
  `modifier(key)` folds the active statuses and the passives together (a product for `_mult` keys, a
  sum for the rest) and every accessor reads it - `armor_total`, `outgoing_damage_mult` (times
  `action_damage_mult` of the action being performed: `heavy_damage_mult` for heavies,
  `throw_damage_mult` for actions tagged Throw, `magic_damage_mult` for magic; the giant's controller
  is the combatant's `action_source`; a projectile freezes the multiplier at launch, as it does Titan
  Surge), `outgoing_poise_mult`, `damage_taken_mult`, `fury_gain_mult`, `resistance()`
  (`elemental_resist_add` and the ancestry's resistances), the maxima (`health_add`, `stamina_add`,
  `essence_add`), the poise threshold (`poise_threshold_add`), heals (`heal_received_mult`, through a
  `ResourcePool` subclass), poise damage taken (`poise_damage_taken_mult`, through a `PoiseMeter`
  subclass), fury on kill (`fury_on_kill_add` on `fury_gain["kill"]`) and `move_speed_mult`. A change
  of a derived key rescales the maxima (fractions kept) and the poise threshold (the accumulated value
  kept). Without passives every accessor returns what it did before (products of 1, sums of 0). P31's
  statuses that use the pre-declared keys now work through the same accessors: Stonewall Stance's poise
  immunity, Bedrock Ward's +50 poise threshold.
- **`PlayerProgression`** (`scripts/actors/player/player_progression.gd`):
  - `learn_talent(id)` (out of combat, then `Talents.check`; an ungated giant counts as level 99):
    spends the points once, learns the granted abilities (source `talent`: known, auto-slotted into
    the first free slot, `ability_learned`), applies `replaces`, rebuilds the passives, emits
    `talent_learned` and a HUD line ("Talent learned: Shoulder Charge (new ability: Shoulder Charge)").
  - `choose_ancestry(id)` (refused `chosen`, `unknown`, `not_yet` before the Old Kingdom, `combat`):
    adds the attribute points once (`attributes_changed`), rebuilds the passives, learns the signature
    (source `ancestry`; level-exempt when chosen below its level 7), sets the flag `ancestry.chosen`
    (the id), emits `ancestry_chosen` and "Your blood answers: you are Stoneblood".
  - `ancestry_offered()`: not chosen, and the world has opened (`world_opened()`: the flag
    `progress.world_entered`, which GameSession sets on entering the world, or the world being the
    level played).
  - `rebuild_passives()` on every talent and ancestry change, on load (`apply_state`) and on every
    profile change (a growth): the combatant's passives, then the two things a combatant cannot change
    alone - the stamina and essence costs and cooldowns of the actions in the controller's library
    (per-character copies of the records' `ActionDef`s, so the controller pays, the hotbar shows and
    a refunded grab returns the same numbers; the shared records are untouched) and the grab reach
    (the profile becomes a copy of the growth record with `grab_reach_m` raised while a passive adds
    reach; PlayerGrab reads it there).
  - Saved: `talents` (ids in learning order), `ancestry`, `ancestry_chosen`.
  - Lines for the screens: `ancestry_line()` ("Ancestry: Stoneblood — Endurance +2, Constitution +3;
    Armour +10, Poise (staggers less) +20; Bedrock Ward", or where and when to choose),
    `talent_line()`, `passive_lines()`; `derived_preview` includes the passives; the F3 panel shows a
    `talents` row.
- **`PlayerMotor`**: `status_speed_mult()` is `Combatant.move_speed_mult()` (statuses and passives,
  applied once: Wildheart x1.05 with Wild Surge x1.15); the sprint's drain follows
  `stamina_cost_mult`.
- **`CharacterState`**: `talents` and `ancestry_chosen` are progression keys; `ancestry_chosen` is
  normalised to "the ancestry is not `ancestry.neutral`".

### Screens

- **The shrine's Talents tab** (`ui/screens/shrine/talents_tab.gd`, every shrine): the three trees
  side by side, each two columns over tiers 1-4 and its crown, a talent drawn learned (green), ready
  (white) or not yet (grey); an intro that says how points come, how the columns and crowns work, that
  talents are permanent, and what the colours mean; a detail box for the talent the focus is on (its
  tree, tier and level, cost, description, everything it does, and whether it can be learned now or
  exactly why not); A / Enter / click moves to "Learn <name>", which spends the point and gives the
  focus back; a granted ability goes into the first free slot and the result line says which ("Talent
  learned: Shoulder Charge (new ability: Shoulder Charge). It is on slot 8."), or, with every slot
  taken, to put it on a key in the Loadout tab; the bottom line adds up every passive the talents and
  the ancestry give now.
- **The shrine's Ancestry tab** (`ui/screens/shrine/ancestry_tab.gd`; while the ancestry is offered):
  an intro, the three ancestries side by side (what the line is, its attribute points, lasting
  strengths, signature with what it does and costs, the peoples who answer to it) and "Choose
  <name>...", which opens the ancestry screen; afterwards it names the ancestry and the key its
  signature is on (or the Loadout tab), until the shrine screen closes.
- **The ancestry screen** (`ui/screens/ancestry_screen.gd`, `SCREENS["ancestry"]`): the three
  ancestries in a row (left / right shows each), everything the one shown gives, "This choice is
  permanent", Choose (refusals stay on screen with their reason) and Back. The focus starts on the
  ancestry, never on Choose, so a permanent choice always takes a deliberate move and press. Opened
  later it only names the ancestry.
- The attribute panel and screen and the inventory screen show the ancestry's and the talents' lines
  instead of the prototype's "no modifiers" and "talent trees are a full-game feature".

Pad, keyboard and mouse (P16): the Talents tab is one focus row per tier across the three trees (the
crowns last) and Learn, between the shrine's tab row and Leave, every direction wrapping; LB / RB
switch tabs; the ancestry screen is the ancestries' row, then Choose and Back.

## Decisions and deviations

- **The level cap stays 20.** The XP table runs to 20 (P12) and nothing in the plan lowers it;
  "level 12" (§0.1, M8) is where the last tier opens - the throne's level in the world's XP budget
  (§5.8: L12 at 3,850 XP, 4,120 guaranteed) - so no world XP or level milestone changes.
  `test_talents` keeps the two consistent: the world start enters at tier 1's level with its five
  points, stage 4's level 7 opens tier 1, stage 5's level 9 tiers 1-3, stage 6's level 11 tiers 1-4,
  and a level-12 giant's 11 points buy a whole tree (10) and one talent more.
- **Columns.** §5.12 lists two talents per tier; §2.11's one example (Shoulder Charge needs Heavy
  Hands) is generalised: each talent of tiers 2-4 needs the one above it in its column (a `column`
  field places it on the tab), the crown needs both tier-4 talents.
- **Costs and cooldowns are per-character action copies.** The controller (not P33's) pays and
  starts cooldowns from the `ActionDef`s of its library, so `PlayerProgression` keeps scaled copies
  there while a cost or cooldown multiplier is not 1 (and puts the shared records back when it is).
  The Talents tab's ability lines show the records' costs.
- **Grab reach through the profile.** `PlayerGrab` (P32's file this wave) reads
  `profile.grab_reach_m`; while a passive adds reach the giant's profile is a copy of the growth
  record with it raised (the record itself otherwise; a growth or a restore rebuilds it).
- **Heals and poise damage through subclasses.** `CombatEntity` (not P33's) calls `pools.heal` and
  `poise.add`; the combatant's pools and poise meter are a `ResourcePool` and a `PoiseMeter` whose
  `heal` and `add` scale by `heal_received_mult` and `poise_damage_taken_mult`. `HitResult.poise_damage`
  still reports the poise before that multiplier (the meter gets x0.85 under Juggernaut, 0 under
  Stonewall Stance).
- **The sprint costs less too.** Relentless and Tireless say "everything you do costs less stamina":
  the motor (P33's this wave) scales the sprint's drain by `stamina_cost_mult` like every action's
  cost.
- **`ancestry_chosen` is the ancestry itself.** `CharacterState.sanitize` and
  `PlayerProgression.apply_state` set it to "the ancestry is not the neutral one", whatever a save
  says, so a save marked chosen with the neutral ancestry is offered the choice again (P20 kept the
  stored value).
- **Where the ancestry is offered.** "After the world opens" (M7: leaving the Valley for the Old
  Kingdom) is read as: once the flag `progress.world_entered` is set (GameSession sets it whenever the
  world level loads, so a migrated save with the prologue finished gets it the first time it steps
  through the Kings' Door) or the world is the level played - not in the Valley before that, nor in
  the sandbox. The signatures open at level 7; chosen earlier (the world start is level 6) they are
  level-exempt.
- **Edits outside the listed hotspots**: `scripts/core/schemas/progression_schema.gd` (the
  `talent_tier_levels` field the plan's progression data change needs; the file has no anchors) and
  P12's `ui/screens/attribute_panel.gd`, `attributes_screen.gd` and `inventory_screen.gd` (one line
  each: their "no modifiers in the prototype" and "talent trees are a full-game feature" became
  untrue; they call the new lines when the component has them).

## Known gaps (for later packages)

- **Bulwark's frontal rule** (§5.12 "frontal hits x0.4"; handed on by P31): `DamageResolver.mitigate`
  asks `damage_taken_mult()` without the hit, so Bulwark takes x0.4 from every side (its description
  says so). A frontal rule needs the hit's direction in `damage_resolver.gd` (or `CombatEntity`),
  which no M6-M9 package owns; P39's balance pass or a gate fix can add it.
- **P36**: dialogues read `ancestry_is` / `ancestry_chosen` (P20's conditions); the ancestries'
  `dialogue_tags` and notes name who should answer (Stoneblood: the Moot's elders, Skarn, Aldmere's
  founding oath; Stormborn: Sedge's bargain; Wildheart: Harrok's shorter duel).
- **P38**: the bot's `choose_ancestry` and its talent policy call `PlayerProgression.choose_ancestry`
  and `learn_talent` directly.
- **P39**: every number is §5.12's initial tuning.
