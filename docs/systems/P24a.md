## What was built

- **ChunkData** (`scripts/world/chunk_data.gd`, `class_name ChunkData extends Resource`): one 256 m
  chunk - 16-bit little-endian heights (`height_min + v × height_step`, -32 m + v × 4 mm, so about
  -32 to +230 m) on a 67 × 67 grid: the 65 × 65 inner samples at 4 m plus a one-sample apron, so
  neighbours share their border samples and the border normals come out identical; scenery (mesh key
  → MultiMesh buffer, 12 floats an instance), `far_scenery` (the keys drawn to the terrain's distance:
  a settlement's merged buildings), solids (`[kind, x, y, z, sx, sy, sz, yaw]`), water rectangles,
  `nav` (P24b), `props_scenes`, 4 × 4 region cells (64 m) and 16 × 16 surface cells (16 m: soft,
  hard, wet). Lookups: `height_at_local` (bilinear), `ground_at_local` (the triangles the mesh and the
  collision shape use: split along (i, j)-(i + 1, j + 1)), `surface_*`, `region_index_at_local`,
  `water_at_local`; `id_for` / `coords_for` ("c<x>_<z>"); `decode_heights()` (no float copy is
  stored), `height_range()`, `counts()`.
- **TerrainChunk** (`scripts/world/terrain_chunk.gd`): one loaded chunk ("Chunk_<x>_<z>" under
  `Chunks`), built one step at a time, each step under about 3 ms on the desktop: `request` (the
  threaded load), `data` (polled; a deterministic run loads it in this step), `decode`, `rows` (about
  2,000 vertices a step: three for 65 × 65), `mesh` (the chunk joins the tree), `ground` (one
  `HeightMapShape3D` of the same triangles, uniformly scaled by the spacing), `water`, `scenery` (one
  MultiMesh a key; group `scenery` and the preset's `scenery_visibility_end_m`, or group
  `world_terrain` and `terrain_visibility_end_m` for `far_scenery` keys), `solids` (StaticBody3Ds of
  16 shapes), `props_dep` (one not yet cached dependency of a props sub-scene), `props_load`,
  `props_add` (one sub-scene each; their meshes in group `scenery` get the scenery distance). Terrain
  vertex colours come from the surface cells blended with the region's tint. `abort()`, `settle()`
  (takes every threaded load still in flight), `free_props()`, `next_is_heavy()`, `progress()`.
- **ChunkStreamer** (`scripts/world/chunk_streamer.gd`, node `Streamer`, group `chunk_streamer`,
  `PROCESS_MODE_ALWAYS`): loads the chunks within Chebyshev distance `load_ring` (1) of the focus
  chunk, nearest first (ties by id), one load at a time; unloads at `unload_at` (3) or more, so at most
  25 are loaded and a walk along a border never reloads a chunk. Per frame it runs steps until
  `budget_ms` (4 ms) is spent, or, when `deterministic` (headless or `--fixed-fps` runs, tests), a fixed
  quota of `steps_per_frame_headless` steps with synchronous loads, so headless runs stream the same
  way every time. A heavy step (a props dependency, load or instancing) runs alone in a frame. Unload:
  `chunk_unloading` (and `Events.world_chunk_unloading`) while every node is still whole, the props
  freed first, then the rest, then `chunk_unloaded`. Statistics (`stats()`): loads, unloads, thrash (a
  chunk loaded again within 10 s of its unload, by the streamer's own clock), work per frame p50 / p99
  / max, steps by kind (`step_breakdown()`), the most chunks loaded, the queue. It owns the shared
  meshes and materials (`shared_mesh`, `terrain_material`, `water_material`, `keep_resource` for the
  props' dependencies) and serves the loading screen (`load_ring_slice`, `ring_progress`,
  `ring_ready`).
- **WorldRoot** (`scripts/world/world_root.gd`, `class_name WorldRoot extends LevelRoot`, group
  `world_root`): configures the streamer from the level's `world` block, sets the depth fog
  (`fog_m`, `fog_max`), focuses the streamer on the player every frame, keeps the skyline's mask
  (`drawn_mask(view)`: the chunks loaded **and** within `terrain_visibility_end_m` of the camera,
  measured to the terrain's AABB centre as the renderer does), follows the region the player stands
  in (`Events.region_changed` once the player is 15 m inside a new region), and answers `region_at`
  (the index's region grid), `height_at`, `surface_at`, `water_at`, `chunk_at`, `chunk_data_at`,
  `is_ready()` / `loading_progress()` (the ring around the respawn point) for main's loading screen.
- **Skyline** (`scripts/world/skyline.gd` and `assets/shaders/skyline.gdshader`): node `Skyline`
  draws the coarse terrain mesh and the merged landmark silhouettes with a shader that discards the
  fragments over chunks whose own terrain is drawn (global uniforms `world_loaded_mask` (one byte a
  chunk), `world_grid_origin`, `world_chunk_size`), plus lit beacons (unshaded, fog-free columns, one
  MultiMesh; shown by flag on `Events.flag_changed`). Two to three draw calls, no shadows.
  `visible_at(pos)` is the shader's rule on the CPU.
- **The offline terrain library** (`tools/world/`, not exported): `HeightField` (`heights.gd`: a
  float grid, sampling, slopes, the 16-bit quantisation, and `relax_borders`, which smooths every
  walkable face within 8 m of a chunk border to 35° or less and fails naming the place when it cannot;
  `border_report`), `ChunkWriter` (`chunk_writer.gd`: a chunk from the field with its apron, surface
  and region cells, scenery buffers, solids, water, the split of a chunk's scripted props into
  sub-scenes of at most 150 nodes with the §2.15 containers, compressed saving) and `SkylineWriter`
  (`skyline_writer.gd`: the coarse grid at 32 m, each vertex the lowest fine height within 32 m minus
  2 m, its vertex-coloured mesh, and the landmark silhouettes merged into one mesh).
- **The test world** (`tools/builders/test_world.gd`; `BUILDERS` entry after `stress.gd`): 4 × 4
  chunks of 256 m (x and z from -512 to 512 m) at 4 m spacing: rolling noise, a 30° ramp crossing the
  x = 256 seam and a 35° ramp crossing the z = 256 seam (each to a plateau), a pond in c0_2, a rim
  beyond 448 m and invisible walls at the edge; 120 trees (one mesh: trunk and crown), 60 saplings
  without collision and 16 rocks per chunk, kept off the route, the ramps, the town and the pond; a
  Brannock-sized walled town in c2_2 (22 merged houses and the walls as far scenery with their solids,
  two open gateways, two fortified gates, 8 breakable houses, a square of four fences and four crates
  - the scripted props in two sub-scenes); two landmarks (a watchtower on the 30° plateau, a monolith
  on the 35° one) with their silhouettes; a beacon on the watchtower lit by `test_world.beacon.lit`;
  the skyline, the world index (start checkpoint, the route markers, the region grid:
  `region.test_west` west of x = 0, `region.test_east` east of it, a road along the route) and
  `scenes/test/test_world.tscn`. Every shared mesh (`assets/test_world/shared/`: tree, sapling, rock,
  wall, house, rubble, tower, monolith) is one vertex-coloured surface with the one material
  `mat_vertex.tres`. The build is deterministic (a second build is byte-identical).
- **Data**: `data/test/level_test_world.json` (`level.test_world`: the `world` block, `low` and
  `medium` render presets with `terrain_visibility_end_m` 700 and `camera_far_m` 2,400) and
  `data/test/encounter_test_world_gate_guards.json` (three soldiers inside the town's west gateway,
  so the world walk meets AI).
- **Hotspot edits**: the level schema's `world` block and the two render keys with their checks
  (anchor P24a), GameData's check that a streamed level has every chunk file of its grid (anchor
  P24a), LevelRoot's `camera_far_m` / `terrain_visibility_end_m` (anchor P24a, applied from
  `apply_graphics`), CameraRig's far plane from the level (`level_far_plane_m()`), main.gd's world
  loading screen (`hold_until_world_ready`: one ring slice a frame behind "Loading... N%" while the
  tree stays paused, the ring and the sample registration weighing the same, at most 8 s then a
  warning and on), the Autopilot's `--scenario=world_walk`.
- **The world walk** (`scripts/debug/world_walk.gd`, `WorldWalk`): the giant follows a route with the
  stick (sprinting; stuck for 5 s: a teleport to the waypoint) or is carried along it at
  `--walk-speed` m/s, invulnerable, after the starting ring (a `--route` that starts elsewhere holds
  the giant at its first point until the ring there is loaded). It records the margin to unloaded
  ground, the most chunks loaded, seam crossings, region changes, ticks under the terrain (walking)
  and the streamer's statistics; the PerfProbe's sections are the chunks. It prints
  `WORLD_WALK_START` and `WORLD_WALK_END`; `AUTOPILOT_RESULT`'s `world_walk` holds the report and its
  check failures.
- **The web stream check** (`tools/web/stream_check.mjs`): serves a `Web (test)` build, runs the world
  walk in headless Chromium (Playwright, software WebGL) through `play.html?args=...`, and reports the
  page's frame times while the giant walks, the streaming numbers, the draw calls, the console errors
  and the failed requests (below).

## Decisions and deviations

1. **The breakable houses use the record `prop.fence_wood`** (a timber breakable without flags or
   loot). A record of their own needs a material in `audio_mix.default`, P23's file this wave
   (`test_cue_router` checks it); the real world's house records come with P29.
2. **The props parts live under `scenes/test/test_world/`** (the plan's real-world path is
   `scenes/world/chunks/`): the test world is test content and stays out of the release.
3. **Scenery keys are the shared meshes' `res://` paths**; the streamer loads each once.
4. **`ChunkData.far_scenery`** (not in §2.7): the keys drawn as far as the terrain instead of the
   scenery's 220 / 300 m - the town's merged houses and walls stay on the horizon while its trees
   fade. Streamed props meshes in group `scenery` get the scenery distance too (LevelRoot only gives
   it to the scenery present at its start).
5. **The test world's static draw calls** led to one vertex-coloured material for every shared mesh
   (a tree, a house, a breakable house's intact state and a landmark are one draw call each) and a
   square of four fences and four crates (each two draw calls): see Measurements.
6. **The skyline's three global shader parameters are registered at run time**
   (`Skyline.ensure_globals()`, once per process, unless `project.godot` declares
   `shader_globals/<name>`), so `project.godot` is not edited; the renderer's list of globals is an
   editor-only query, so the registration is remembered in a static flag.
7. **Deterministic streaming loads synchronously** (`ResourceLoader.load` in the `data` and props
   steps) instead of a threaded request it would poll at once; desktop and web runs use the threaded
   request and poll it (§2.8's "the same code on both platforms" holds for every real run).
8. **Heavy steps run alone in a frame** and a props sub-scene's not yet cached dependencies (a
   building's or a gate's scene) are fetched one a step and kept by the streamer: a cold settlement
   part no longer costs one 8 ms step (work p99 about 4 ms on the desktop at 16 m/s).
9. **Border relaxation tolerance**: a face counts as within 35° up to 35.05° (`SLOPE_EPS_DEG`), so the
   35° ramp itself (35.02° once quantised) passes; a relaxation that stops improving for 25
   iterations fails at once, naming the place.
10. **The test regions have no records**: `region.test_west` / `region.test_east` exist only in the
    test world's index region grid and chunk cells (region records come with P25), so the terrain
    shows the surface colours alone (a region record's `map_tint` blends in when there is one) and
    the skyline uses the builder's two tints.
11. **`tests/support/tiny_world.gd`** (not in the owned test list): a 64 m-chunk world written at test
    time, so the integration suites stream rings in a few steps and stay inside the default suite's
    0.6 s.
12. **Two hook lines outside the anchor blocks** (as P22's `_p22_ready()`): `apply_graphics()` in
    `level_root.gd` calls `_p24a_apply_view(preset, out)` (defined in the anchor block), and
    `camera_rig.gd`'s setup sets `camera.far = level_far_plane_m()`.
13. **A web export does not compile a bare `free()` on self** ("Function free() not found in base
    self"): `TerrainChunk.abort()` uses `queue_free()` in and out of the tree. The stream check found
    it; the desktop never had.
14. **The world walk prints `WORLD_WALK_START` / `WORLD_WALK_END`**, and the stream check judges the
    page's frame intervals between them: the engine's own frame delta is capped at 8 physics ticks
    (133 ms), so its frame times cannot show a slower frame.
15. **A streamer that leaves the tree frees the chunk it was loading** (not yet in the tree) and every
    chunk takes its threaded loads before it goes (`settle()`), so a run that quits mid-load leaks
    nothing.
16. **The slow walk test judges the better of two walks** when the first one's work p99 is over 6 ms:
    the work is wall time, and on the shared container another process can stretch it (3.5-6.5 ms
    over repeated runs of the same deterministic walk). A real regression fails both walks.
17. **The stream check counts the console errors of the run** and lists apart what the page reports
    after the result, while the engine shuts down (the autopilot quits it; a player never does on
    the web): one run showed the engine's audio teardown error there.
