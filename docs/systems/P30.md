## What was built

### The Formation (`scripts/ai/formation.gd`, `scripts/ai/formation_rules.gd`)

`class_name Formation extends RefCounted`: ranks x files of slots around one pivot. Slot s is rank
s / files, file s % files (rank 0 the front rank, file 0 its left end seen from behind); in the
formation's frame slot (r, f) sits at x = (f − (files − 1)/2) · file_spacing_m, z = (r − (ranks −
1)/2) · rank_spacing_m; the banner (slot −1) stands one rank behind the last, centred. `anchor()` is
the front rank's centre at chest height (1.15 m): the group's lock point.

- **States** (`Events.formation_changed(tick, id, state)`; the history is kept for tests):
  `formed`, `advancing` (the pivot walks toward the giant at `advance_mps` along one `NavQuery` path
  per re-path - every 60 ticks or when the goal moved 2 m - within the director's per-tick path
  budget, never farther than `advance_m` from home), `braced` (the giant within `brace_m` of the front
  rank and in front of it), `broken` (fewer than 40 % of its members stand), `routed` (none stands and
  one fled; once). `banner_fell` is announced once when the banner dies.
- **Facing**: `facing: "threat"` turns the pivot toward the giant at `turn_deg_s` while any member is
  aware of it (kept 1 s after the last); `"fixed"` keeps `yaw_deg`.
- **The brace** (decision 19): braced, the formation - not each pike - runs `brace_action` through the
  front-rank member nearest the giant that is free to act, holding one of the giant's melee tokens for
  the pulse and giving it back at the next update, with a 0.6 s pause between pulses. The action is a
  `band_pulse` (a new hit shape in `AttackRunner`, anchor P30): one box along `front_band()` - the
  rank's width, `radius_m` deep, `height_m` tall - on the first active tick, line of sight from the
  rank's centre, one hit per target per pulse (`max_hits_per_target`), the attacker's height raised to
  the action's `attacker_height_override_m`, else the formation's `brace_height_m` (default 5 m), so
  its poise and knockback treat the brace as a bigger attacker. One `line` telegraph along the band
  is announced at the windup (`EnemyActor`), and `TelegraphVisuals` keeps it as the band (anchor P30).
  The other members only pose (`EnemyActor.BRACE_POSES`: pikes levelled, shields up).
- **The banner**: while it lives its formation's members lose half the morale of awe and ally deaths
  (`EnemyBrain._p30_morale_scale`), regroup to their slots, and every `rally.interval_s` it runs its
  rally action: +`rally.morale` to the members within `rally.radius_m`. Its death is the commander's
  death for its members only (`on_commander_died`; a soldier outside the formation is untouched).
- **Routs fan out**: a routed member runs to `rout_point()` - the formation's `flee_points` in turn
  (marker ids the director resolves), else five points 30–60 m away over a 140° fan facing away from
  the giant, alternating near and far - made reachable on its navigation layer.
- **Joining**: an outmatched soldier whose `outmatched_behavior` is `formation` joins the nearest
  intact formation of its faction within 40 m that has a free slot (keeping its morale), while the
  level's `max_formation_members` allows; at stage 4 and above one that finds none flees and routs
  (plan §5.14: single humans flee); below stage 4 P04's ad-hoc lines are unchanged.
- **Web compositions**: `web_kept_slots(slots, scale)` keeps the banner, then the slots in order (the
  front rank first) up to round(members x scale), at least one.

`FormationRules` (`class_name FormationRules`, no dependencies but `SchemaUtil`) holds the data rules,
so the encounter schema - loaded with GameData at start-up - does not pull the actors in:
`record_errors(encounter)` (plain unique ids, a stage range that is not empty, flee points, a
`brace_action` needs `brace_m`, every member's formation and slot inside the grid and not shared, one
banner per formation and the one it names, at least one member) and `cross_record_errors(records)`
(GameData's post-pass, anchor P30: a banner spawn's record has role `banner` and a rank-and-file
member's does not; around every encounter with formations, the members of its level's encounters
within 150 m that form at the giant's stage - for each stage 1–6, the formation's and the spawn's own
stage ranges - fit the level's `max_formation_members` on the desktop (default 40) and its
`web_overrides.max_formation_members` on the web (default 20), counting each formation's web
composition).

### The director (`scripts/world/encounter_director.gd`, anchor P30)

At each activation every `formations` entry whose stage range admits the giant's stage becomes a
Formation placed where its members' spawn points say (the pivot that best fits them, `yaw_deg` or the
first member's yaw); entries outside the range sit the activation out with their members (P24b's
skip set). On the web each formation keeps its `web_kept_slots` at the encounter's
`web_formation_scale`. Spawned members join in their slots, a CrowdRenderer per formation draws them,
and ten times a second every formation of a live, active encounter updates with the giant as its
threat; a deactivation dissolves the encounter's formations. Formation members never count toward
`max_active_enemies`: they count toward `max_formation_members` (members wait to spawn while the level
has that many). `formations()`, `formation(id)`, `crowd_renderer(id)`, `formation_member_count()`;
the F3 lines list each formation.

### Actors (`scripts/actors/enemy_actor.gd`, `scripts/ai/enemy_brain.gd`, anchors P30)

- **Members**: `p30_join_formation` / `p30_leave_formation` (a fresh member's morale becomes the
  formation's `morale_start`, 90), `p30_formation_update` (what the brain needs: intact, braced, the
  banner alive), `p30_run_action`, `p30_can_act`, `p30_force_rout`, `p30_brace_band`,
  `p30_brace_height`, `p30_rally`, `role()`; the brain holds its slot (HOLD with hysteresis, runs
  when more than 4 m off), faces the formation's threat and never attacks on its own while the
  formation is intact. `formation_id` is what the music director's special-threat count and the cue
  router's formation anchor read.
- **Shields**: `accepts_hit()` lets the actor see the hit being resolved; a guarded hit carrying one
  of `guard.breaks_on_tags` breaks the guard (the defence's stamina is 0 for it).
- **The target provider** (decision 20): with `targets` in its record the enemy fights the giant when
  it is engaged with it (aware of it, or it is in sight within `aggro_player_m`), else the nearest
  living NPC of a listed faction or intact prop with a listed tag in sight (`FactionState.prop_tags`);
  the perception gives such a target its own radius and no size (no awe or outmatched tactics against
  a house); the runner makes that NPC or prop hittable during this unit's hit queries
  (`AttackRunner.provider_target`: the M1 team rule otherwise lets an enemy hit only the player). A
  changed choice gives back the attack tokens held on the old one. Without `targets` nothing changes.

### Group lock (`scripts/actors/player/player_targeting.gd`, `scripts/presentation/camera_rig.gd`)

While the profile's `target_rules.formation_lock` holds (stages 4–6) `gather_candidates` folds each
formation's rank and file into one candidate `formation:<id>` (its anchor for direction, distance and
screen; the highest member threat; visible when the anchor or one of its three members nearest the
giant is; alive while a member is alive and not routed; flagged `group`) and keeps the banner as its
own candidate (flagged `banner`). `locked_entity()` resolves a group to `focus_member()` of the giant's
attack origin (a fleeing one when none stands), so the camera, the vertical aim and grabs keep
receiving a `CombatEntity`; the camera frames the formation's anchor (`CameraRig.p30_lock_group`). The
lock releases with `target_died` once no member is alive and not routed; switching moves between
formations, banners and single targets. `lock_label()` gives "Pike Regiment (8)", "⚑ Banner of the
Toll-Riders" or the target's name (P27 shows it); `is_group_locked()`.

### The crowd renderer (`scripts/presentation/crowd_renderer.gd`)

`class_name CrowdRenderer` (group `crowd_renderers`, one per formation): one MultiMesh per pose in use
(`hold`, `brace` - braced, or aware and standing: pikes levelled, shields up - `advance`, `flee`) of
the archetype's merged rig, built by `tools/builders/formations.gd` as `Visual/CrowdPoses/<pose>`, one
instance per member with its transform each frame. A member is promoted to its own rig (its Visual
shown and its rig processing) while it is within 20 m x the giant's distance scale (its height over
3.66 m, 1–5.2) of the giant, in an action, staggered or held - at most six per formation, strikers
first, then the nearest; the dead always show their own rig (the fall) and never count; a member that
leaves gets its rig back. The banner is never in the crowd: its flag (`Visual/Body/FlagPivot/Flag`,
2.5 x 1.8 m on a 7 m pole) sways and is painted in the formation's `banner_color`. A formation of
twenty costs its pose sets (1–2 draw calls), its promoted rigs and its banner.

### The mid distance (FOLLOWUPS: the Valley's draw calls at `low`)

At the `low` preset a rig draws as one merged mesh of its rest pose (`RigPaint`'s `MidProxy` under
`Visual/Body`, built once per actor scene and shared, with the rig's own material so flashes and
tints show) from 0.45 x `render_lod_m` to `render_lod_m`, between the full rig and the one-box far
proxy - a soldier costs one draw call from 20 m instead of six - and its full rig again while it acts
(an action, staggered, dead or held), so a strike, a stagger, a fall or a grab is never shown frozen.
`ActorRig` and `QuadrupedRig` hook it in `set_render_lod` and `_process`; at `medium` nothing changes.

## Decisions and deviations

- **`FormationRules` is its own file** (`scripts/ai/formation_rules.gd`, beyond the owned list): the
  encounter schema calls the record checks at start-up, and `Formation` needs the actors.
- **Hook lines outside the anchors** (each a one-line call into the P30 section, as P22 and P24b did):
  `enemy_actor.gd` (`_ready`, `_target`, `_sense`, `_on_any_entity_died`, `get_defense_state`,
  `get_rig_state`, `_on_phase_entered`, `_has_leader_now`, `_line_members`), `enemy_brain.gd`
  (`decide`, `apply_morale`), `encounter_director.gd` (`_on_pre_actors`, `_activate`, `counts`,
  `_spawn_waiting`, `_deactivate`, `reload_encounters`, `debug_lines`), `player_targeting.gd`
  (`locked_entity`, `gather_candidates`, `switch_target`, `sync_lock`, `maintain_lock`,
  `on_any_entity_died`), `camera_rig.gd` (`lock_focus_point`), `telegraph_visuals.gd` (`_ends`),
  `attack_runner.gd` (`run`), `stress_scenario.gd` (`_ready`, `_physics_process`, `start_bot`,
  `result`, `check_failures`), `actor_rig.gd` and `quadruped_rig.gd` (`set_render_lod`, `_process`).
- **The brace's size override lives in the band pulse** (the action's `attacker_height_override_m`,
  else the formation's `brace_height_m`), not in `HitFactory`: decision 21's general override is
  P32's.
- **The target provider flips the NPC's or prop's team** to the player's for the duration of the
  unit's own hit query (`AttackRunner.provider_target`), so the M1 team rule and every hit shape stay
  as they are; nobody else can hit a villager.
- **Join or rout from stage 4**: an outmatched soldier with no formation to join flees and routs at
  stage 4 and above; below, P04's ad-hoc lines are unchanged.
- **Audio data for the new soldiers**: P28 owns the M7–M9 audio additions (W17), but the coverage
  tests ask every enemy for a voice and a step class and every sector attack for its own sweep cue,
  so `data/audio/mix_default.json` gains `human` voices and steps for the three soldiers and
  `data/audio/bank_abilities.json` two sweep cues on existing sounds (`pike_thrust`: the blade swing
  at pitch 0.75; `shield_bash`: the blunt swing at 0.9), as P26 added its props' materials. P28 may
  replace them.
- **The banner's lock threat is 0.55** (its rank's 0.6): with equal threat the banner, one rank
  behind, won the first lock; now the formation does and the banner is a switch away.
- **The crowd's `brace` pose also shows an aware formation standing** (pikes levelled, shields up),
  not only a braced one.
- **A line formation of plain soldiers draws rigs**: the soldier's scene (P04's) has no crowd poses,
  and `CrowdRenderer` draws a member without them by its own rig.
- **"A heavy kills the banner through one rank"** is tested with the banner locked through its rank
  (in sight, the lock resolved to the banner, held) and the stage-4 heavy's event through the real
  resolution: the club's stage-4 heavy smash lands about 3 m in front of the giant, so a swing landing
  on human-sized targets beyond that is P31's stage movesets.
- **The default tier's formation tests use the stage-4 profile directly** (`set_profile`) and a
  shorter brace (`windup_s` 0.2 through `TestFixtures`) for the time budget; the shipped brace is in
  the slow perf test and the stress scenario.
- **The formation test level is 440 m wide** so that its clusters lie 150 m apart and each fits the
  caps; **`tests/support/formation_draw_probe.gd`** (beyond the owned list) is the rendered probe's
  child-process script, as P22's `rig_draw_probe.gd`.
- **The rendered probe loads the game's classes at run time**: a `-s` script compiles before the
  autoloads are registered, so it must not name `Formation` or `CrowdRenderer`.

## M7 gate (wave 18)

Three review findings on the formations, fixed at the gate (regression tests in
`tests/integration/test_m7_gate_fixes.gd` and the slow `tests/slow/test_m7_gate_world.gd`):

- **The leash and formations.** The director leashed a formation's members like any other: the toll
  fort's pikes reached 68.3 m from the fort's centre (slots 35 m out, turned, plus the 30 m advance)
  under a 45 m leash, so the front rank was sent home the moment it advanced and stood in RETURN
  behind its own rear rank, and the pikes never engaged a stage-4 giant on the road. Now:
  - `FormationRules.reach_m(enc, f)` is the farthest a member can stand in its slot (the home pivot
    placed as the director places it, `advance_m` in any direction, slots turned all round when the
    formation faces the threat), and `FormationRules.record_errors` (the encounter schema) refuses, in a
    leashed encounter, a formation whose reach passes the leash and an advancing formation without
    `advance_m`. `Formation.slot_local` uses `FormationRules.slot_offset`, the same geometry.
  - The director never leashes a member of an intact formation (formed, advancing, braced): its
    formation holds it in a slot that lies inside the leash; a broken or routed formation's members
    are leashed as before.
  - The data: the toll fort's leash 45 -> 70 m (reach 68.3 m; 70 m is the plan's most, §5.10) and the
    Raiders' Hollow's 55 -> 65 m (reach 63.2 m) - P29's records.
- **The advance stopped outside the brace distance.** `_decide_state` advanced while the giant was
  more than `brace_m + ADVANCE_SLACK_M` away and braced only within `brace_m`, so a formation closing
  on a still giant stopped in that half metre and stood "formed" (the toll fort's pikes 6.6 m from it,
  `brace_m` 6; the Raiders' wall likewise - the damage seen there was its ogre's and archers'). The
  advance now runs while the giant is beyond `brace_m - ADVANCE_SLACK_M`: it ends inside the brace
  distance and braces.
- **The brace struck where its telegraph was not.** The telegraph was drawn once at the windup and
  stays where it was drawn, but the band that struck was rebuilt on the first active tick from the
  formation's facing, which kept turning after the giant for the 0.8 s windup (29 degrees in the
  reviewer's probe): a giant that stepped out of the drawn band was struck. `EnemyActor` now keeps the
  band its windup telegraphs (with the action's instance id) and `p30_brace_band()` returns it while
  that action runs - the action's `tracking_fraction` 0.

Measured on the real world (the slow suite): a defied toll fort's pikes advance from their home rank
16 m before a stage-4 giant to 5.9 m of it, brace twice and hit twice for 114 damage in about 10 s, with
no member leashed; the Raiders' wall braces twice for 70 of the 246 damage the camp dealt.
