# P62 — Townsfolk, named characters, riders and the rival giants

Plan: `docs/plans/M11_PLAN.md` §3.15 (and §0.3's Part C rows, §2.8-§2.12). Wave W36, lane A, beside P63 and the asset
lane. Built on 21 November 2026 on `pkg/P62` from `426cb08`, on the shared container (4 CPUs, no GPU: llvmpipe), with
the other lane's asset builds running beside it.

The goal: every human scene that was still a box rig - the villagers, the elders, the named characters, Corvane, the
cavalry's riders - is a realistic person in play; the cavalry's horse is the creatures' skinned horse; the rival giant
and Harrok are the player's realistic giant in their own looks. Gameplay is unchanged (the soak, the people's
determinism gate with its two new walks).

## What was built

### 1. The townsfolk and the named characters

- `data/people/role_{villager,elder,notable,rider}.json`: the four roles (P59b's assets), their pools, palettes (the
  villagers' skin, hair and cloth in the country's plain colours; the elders' greys) and the factions' colours.
- `data/npcs/*.json` gain `body`: the villager its role (a pool of five: three men, two women, by the id's hash); each
  named character its own notable variant and colours (Edda, Reeve Tamsin, Sedge, the ferrywoman, the herald, the
  toll captain with a sword, the deserter; Sedge with a staff); `data/enemies/corvane.json` the notable `corvane` at his
  record's 2.0 m with a sword.
- `tools/builders/npcs.gd`: the villager's and the talkers' scenes (`elder.tscn` is every talker's) carry
  `people_models.person_visual()` (PersonRig, the brain's pivots without meshes, 45 m to the far level).
  `tools/builders/big.gd`: Corvane the same.
- `PersonRig`: a civilian's flee (its CivilianBrain in FLEE) plays the flee clip; fleeing with nowhere to run (or
  waiting for its path) for `COWER_AFTER_S` 0.5 s it **cowers** where it stands (cross-faded over 0.35 s); a hidden
  actor (a civilian gone indoors) leaves its level and gives its model back. `CrowdRenderer.civilian_pose()`: the
  loners' crowd shows a civilian's flee, walk (advance) and hold poses. `PersonRig.solve_arm()` is the arm solve as a
  static function (the rider uses it).
- **Elder Maren** (`npc.elder`) is a woman: the elder role's two variants are bearded men, so her record names
  `villager_e` (the hooded woman) with grey hair and a staff. The elder role is the talkers' scene's default and is
  otherwise unused by a record (see "What is left").

### 2. `SkinnedQuadrupedRig` and the rider (`scripts/presentation/skinned_quadruped_rig.gd`)

A QuadrupedRig (the brain poses the box pivots as before, counts footfalls, keeps flashes and tints) that, where the
people are drawn and the record's `body.rig` is not `"box"`, hides the box rig and shows the creatures' tacked horse
(the manifest's scene, 25 bones), driven with no clip:

- **The gait is planned in the world.** Each leg is in stance or swing; a planted hoof stays where it landed (it never
  slides, at any speed or in a turn), a swing carries it on an arc to where its stance will be centred under its
  hip or shoulder. The timing is a clock at `stride_hz(speed)`; the walk (four beats, lateral sequence), the trot
  (diagonal pairs) and the transverse gallop (right lead) have their phase offsets and stance shares blended by speed,
  so no gait pops into the next; a swing ends as its leg's phase turns over; a hoof carried past its reach lifts
  early; standing, the hoof farthest off its place steps back, one at a time.
- **The legs are solved** in the skeleton's space: the pastern as at rest (folded in a swing; breaking over - the heel
  lifting - when the hoof is left behind), the cannon plumb (tilted toward the hip or shoulder only as far as the leg
  must reach; the knee and hock folding in a swing), the upper and lower bones by `TipMatching.two_bone` bending as at
  rest; the shoulder joint slides up to 12 cm with the foreleg (the rig has no scapula bone).
- The body bobs and pitches with the gait and leans into a turn (the wheel); the neck nods, the head follows the
  brain's head pivot (raised in a windup) and stretches at the charge, the jaw opens at the gallop, the tail sways
  from the brain's tail pivot and lifts at speed. Dead, held or thrown the legs hang at rest under the brain's roll.
- **The rider**: a pooled `PersonModel` of the record's `body.role` (rider) on the saddle's seat (`saddle_seat`), the
  `seated` clip; the trunk leans forward with the gallop and the charge; the right arm by `solve_arm` holds the lance
  (gear.glb's spear fitted to 4.2 m, 3.5 m before the hand) carried upright by the right knee, **lowered through the
  charge's windup (its telegraph) and couched at the hip for the run**, thrust in the lance attack; the left fist on
  the reins before the pommel.
- Draw calls: the horse, the rider, the lance (3); the horse and the rider cast shadows. The horse's material is
  `person.gdshader` on the horse's atlas with `strands` on; its coat by a hash of the id (bay, dark bay, chestnut,
  grey, black, dun), the housing in the faction's colour.
- At most `MAX_SKINNED_OTHERS` 8 horses animate every frame (the nearest the camera), the rest every third frame. AI
  LOD tier 2 shows `Visual/LodProxy` as the horse's mesh at rest with the rider's seated pose mesh.
- Headless, with `--no-skin`, or with `body.rig: "box"` the box rig draws exactly as before (the beasts' gate's
  fallback, plan §3.18). `person_role_schema.gd`'s `body` gains `rig` (`skinned` | `box`).

### 3. The rival giant and Harrok (`SkinnedRig`)

`SkinnedRig` no longer assumes the player: `fixed_body_scale` (the scene's k: 10 / 3.6576 and 15 / 3.6576), and on its
first frame (`_setup_own`) an enemy's look from its record's new `appearance` key (`Appearance.to_look` of the npc
preset's record: `preset_npc_rival_giant`, `preset_npc_harrok`) and its axe from its weapon record (`WeaponVisuals`, at
the record's length in world metres). `big.gd`'s `giant_visual()` gives both scenes the player's Visual: the stage-1
pivots and hand markers under a scaled Body, an instance of `giant_model.tscn` (the same Mesh resources as the
player's - checked by the suite - so nothing is duplicated in memory or the pack), and WeaponSocket. Everything else is
the player's code: the clips from the brain, the hands, tip matching on their own weapon data, secondary motion on
their hair and beard, flashes and tints on every surface. SKIN_STATS' look stays the player's rig's.

### 4. The shaders

`person.gdshader` and `person_crowd.gdshader`: `strands` (the albedo's alpha cut by alpha scissor at 0.5: the horse's
mane and tail draw as strands; the people's atlases are opaque and draw as before); plate metal held to roughness 0.38
(P60's 0.46), metallic 0.7 and its albedo x 0.92: the helm keeps a small, tight highlight instead of a blown-out patch.
The crowd shader matches.

### 5. The frames

Not committed (P64 owns the people sheet, `tools/character_sheet.tscn`): a scratch harness rendered the town at the
game camera, the named characters front and head, the riders standing, at the walk, the trot and the gallop and in a
real charge (AI on), the rival giants beside the player, and the helm before and after (llvmpipe, 1280x720, a sun with
shadows). The package's report lists them.

## Measurements

`docs/PERFORMANCE.md`, "The townsfolk, the riders and the rival giants in play (P62)": a rider +3 draw calls, a named
character +1; the six settlements at `low` 99-131 draw calls (Millford 101, Brannock 107, Hollowmere 99, Fort Aldmere
102, Skarn 118, Harrok's Hall 131), the Moot test level 226: **every one within 250**. The people's CPU a frame: 0.1-1.3
ms in the settlements, 1.8-2.1 ms for a town of 15 skinned and 2 horses (the editor binary on llvmpipe; P60's finding
that 0.6 ms native is not met on this machine stands; P64's bench decides).

Tip matching on the rival giants' weapon data (`test_rival_giant_rig`): the axe combo's visible outer half within
0.42-0.49 m (the rival giant) and 0.68 m (Harrok) of the tested swing over its ACTIVE frames, inside the axe's 0.9 m
capsule but not exact as the player's is: the records' weapon pivots (the gameplay's shoulder at 0.73 of the height)
sit below the realistic model's shoulder, so the fist cannot reach the tested grip; recorded as the limit of honest
reach (plan §0.3 Part A's rule).
