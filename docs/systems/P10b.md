## What was built

- **The layout table** `tools/data/valley_layout.json` (tools only, never exported): every element
  of the 400 × 400 m blockout of §4 — districts, ground, cliffs, walls, blockers, water, roads,
  cottages, platforms and ramps, landmarks, decor, the Kingsfall arena, props, shrines, waystones,
  dummies, markers, patrol routes, triggers, signs, the player's start and the scenery trees.
- **The builder** `tools/builders/valley.gd` (already listed in `BUILDERS`), which turns the table
  into
  - `scenes/world/valley/<district>.tscn` for `hollow`, `forest`, `barrow`, `village`, `quarry`,
    `ruins`, `kingsfall` and `hills` (the map edge and the impassable hills): `Collision` (one
    StaticBody3D on WORLD with one CollisionShape3D per element), `Blockers` (one StaticBody3D on
    the blockers layer: the river banks and the bridge rails), `Visual` (one MultiMeshInstance3D
    per material and unit mesh) and `Scenery` (the trees' MultiMeshes, `visibility_range_end`
    220 m, group `valley_scenery`);
  - `assets/nav/valley_{human,large,giant,small_giant}.res`: the four navmeshes (nav layers
    1/2/4/8), baked offline from the same elements as the collision plus the solid shapes of every
    placed prop, shrine, waystone and dummy, saved compressed (15–19 KB each);
  - `scenes/world/valley.tscn`: the level root (next section).
- **Data**: `level.valley` and the three encounters that need only existing archetypes —
  `encounter.forest_wolves_a`, `encounter.forest_hunters`, `encounter.road_patrol`.
- **The Valley can be played from New Game** (title screen, `--mode=valley --new-game`): the giant
  wakes at the Cradle in Giant's Hollow; the Hollow Shrine is the start checkpoint.

### Navigation layers and links

| Region | Layer | Agent radius / height / climb (m) | Cell (m) | Navmesh height above the ground | Polygons | File |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human | 1 | 0.6 / 2.0 / 0.25 | 0.3 | 0.35 | 1,915 | 18.4 KB |
| Large | 2 | 1.5 / 4.0 / 0.5 | 0.5 | 0.15 | 1,659 | 14.5 KB |
| Giant | 4 | 2.0 / 8.5 / 0.75 | 0.5 | 0.0 | 1,677 | 14.8 KB |
| SmallGiant | 8 | 0.9 / 3.75 / 0.5 | 0.3 | 0.25 | 1,991 | 19.3 KB |

Common: cell height 0.25, max slope 35°, `region_min_size` 8, `edge_max_error` 2.0, the bake
bounds fixed to x/z −205..205, y −1..60.

- **Why the regions sit at different heights.** `NavigationServer3D` connects each end of a
  `NavigationLink3D` to the single closest polygon of the whole map, whatever its region's layers
  (tested on 4.6.3: with four overlapping regions, four single-layer links all attached to the
  first region). Recast puts a navmesh two cell heights (0.5 m) above a flat surface; the builder
  measures that, and moves each region down so the layers end up 0.35 / 0.25 / 0.15 / 0.0 m above
  the walking surface. Each blocking prop gets one link per layer, at that layer's height, so it
  attaches to its own region. Agents gain from it: an enemy's waypoint (path_desired_distance 0.6
  in `EnemyActor`) is 0.35 m above its feet instead of 0.5 m.
- **Links.** `NavLink<Region>` children of `valley.village.barricade`, `valley.ruins.practice_gate`
  and `valley.forest.deadfall`: from local (0, height, +half) to (0, height, −half) across the prop
  (half = the prop's half depth + 2 m + about 1 m: 4.2 / 3.4 / 4.4 m), bidirectional, disabled.
  `BreakableProp` enables every `NavLink*` child when it breaks or opens, and on a reload of a
  broken one. The prop scenes' own `NavLinkThrough` (y 0, layers 15) stays and attaches to the
  lowest region (the giant's): a duplicate giant link.
- **What is an obstacle.** Ground tiles are walkable; cliffs, walls, cottages, landmarks,
  blockers, tree trunks and the extracted prop, shrine, waystone, dummy and pillar shapes are
  obstacles (side faces only, plus a projected obstruction over their footprint, so no island
  forms inside or on top of them); platforms are walkable on top (the ground below them is
  removed); ramps are walkable (the ground below them carved away); the river is removed. The
  arena gate is open (its collision is off) and not baked.
- **Islands** that remain on purpose: the watchtower top (an archer's perch: the tower archer
  spawns there and stays), the cairn ledge top (a hurl target). Unreachable pockets (between the
  arena ring and its corner fills, inside the fence pen) may keep small islands; no path reaches
  them and flee points are confirmed with a layered path (P04).

## Decisions and deviations

1. **Doc name** `docs/systems/P10b.md` (the task); §3.11 names `P10b-valley-world.md`.
2. **Encounter files.** `GameData` loads one record per JSON file, so the three encounters are
   `encounter_forest_wolves_a.json`, `encounter_forest_hunters.json` and
   `encounter_road_patrol.json` (the plan's `encounters_forest.json` would hold two records).
3. **Per-layer links at per-layer heights** (above). The layout's `nav_link` is `{half_m}`, not the
   plan's `{from, to, layers}`: the builder makes one link per layer across the prop's local Z.
4. **A path-search limit in Godot 4.6.3.** A path query whose target is unreachable searches its
   whole connected area; when that search pops `path_search_max_polygons` polygons (4096, what
   `map_get_path` and agents use) the engine clears its heap without resetting the entries' heap
   indices, the fallback search fails, and it logs "It's not expect to not find the most reachable
   polygons" (an error) with a useless path. With the first forest (150 trees, 8-sided trunks,
   Recast's default contour) a small-giant query from the Cradle to Kingsfall tripped it. The
   navmeshes were made leaner (80 forest trees, hexagonal trunk outlines, `edge_max_error` 2.0):
   the worst whole-west-side search now needs 2,500–3,000 polygons, and a slow test runs 352 such
   queries with a 3,500-polygon limit. Callers that search the whole map on purpose can set
   `NavigationPathQueryParameters3D.path_search_max_polygons = 0` (no limit) with `query_path`.
   The coarser contour lets a path come up to about 0.6 m (human) / 1.0 m (giant) closer to an
   obstacle than the agent radius; the walks below show the giant still follows its paths.
5. **MultiMesh transforms are written as a buffer.** The headless (dummy) renderer the builders
   run on keeps a MultiMesh buffer assigned as a whole, but drops `set_instance_transform()`, so
   the first build saved no transforms (nothing drew). `multimesh_buffer()` writes the 3 × 4 rows.
6. **Navmeshes are external files.** `ResourceSaver.save(..., FLAG_CHANGE_PATH)` did not make the
   packed scene reference them (they were embedded: a 623 KB scene); the builder now saves each
   `.res` and assigns the copy loaded back from disk.
7. **Ground colours** are `Color` shader parameters: the checker's uniforms are `source_color`
   vec3s, and a `Vector3` was saved as `null` (every district came out the default green).
8. **Agent sizes in whole cell heights**: the small giant's height is 3.75 m (plan 3.7) and the
   climbs are 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.5 / 0.75 m (human, small giant, large, giant): Recast floors other
   values with a warning.
9. **Forest and village.** A ridge (x −40..−30, z −35..100) separates the Old Wood from the fields
   and the village's west side, so the road through the Grove (and the road patrol, which waits for
   `reward.main.grove`) is the way east. The plain round the palisade stays open.
10. **Bridge and river.** The bridge approach is a 12 m channel between stone abutments that the
    barricade fills exactly; the bridge's edges are BLOCKER rails (a 1.5 m parapet could be jumped
    into the river); the strip between the river and the ruins' west wall is solid rampart except
    the bridgehead court, so nothing reaches the ruins or Kingsfall but through the practice gate.
11. **Ruins details.** The west wall is 5 m high beside the practice gate (z −46..5), 10 m elsewhere
    (an archer at 6 m must see out); the gate wall walk is 4 m wide against the wall
    (x 126..130, z −46..−30, ramp to z −62) so the plan's gate soldier at (132, −30) stands on open
    ground; the armory is an alcove without a roof (stage 3 reaches the Giantforge).
12. **Placed objects of the parallel lane** are skipped with a note while their scene or record is
    missing: the two forges (`scenes/world/forge.tscn`, P12), the supply cache and the crown
    (`item.supply_cache`, `item.crown_first_king`, P12), and the crates' `loot_table`
    (`loot.crate_quarry`, `loot.crate_armory`) until `BreakableProp` has that property. The wave
    merge's full rebuild adds them (and re-bakes the navigation with the forges' shapes).
13. **Objective markers are standing spots** (in front of the Deadfall, outside the practice gate,
    before the oak); the cairn's marker is on the cairn (a hurl target, not a destination).
14. **Waystone stones** stand beside the road (the layout's `stone` offset), not behind the marker.
15. **Not built**: the Hollow cottage arch (§4.2's optional 4.5 m stage-1 shortcut; the village has
    its 4 m alleys) — P15 can add it with the dressing (an overhead lintel bakes as a ceiling: side
    and bottom faces, no obstruction).
16. **Extra root node** `Dummies` (not in §2.12's list) for the two practice dummies.
17. **Encounter yaws** are this package's choice (the wolves face the canyon, the hunters the road
    from the north, the road patrol the Grove); the hunters have no commander (§4.4 names none).
18. **Existing tests changed** (`tests/integration/test_session_flow.gd`, P06's): three tests
    encoded "the Valley does not exist yet". `test_the_title_without_the_valley` now hides the
    Valley's scene with a record override so the fallback stays covered; the new
    `test_the_title_with_the_valley` checks New Game; `test_continue_from_the_title...` expects New
    Game enabled; `test_command_line_modes...` checks that `--mode=valley` without a save starts a
    new game in the Valley, and the fallback with the scene hidden.

## Known gaps

- Visuals are checked only by eye: renders under Xvfb during development (overview, the Cradle,
  forest road, Grove, village, bridge, courtyard, quarry, Kingsfall) — no automated image test.
- Draw calls and frame times are not measured here (P14).
- The bridge deck is visual (0.1 m above the ground the giant walks on).
- Small unreachable navmesh islands remain in sealed pockets (arena corners, the fence pen).
- The web build is not run (P18); nothing bakes at runtime and the four navmeshes are 67 KB.
- The Hollow cottage arch (decision 15) and the dressing (barley rows, cosmetic props, extra
  signs, scenery density) are P15's.
