# P45 — The Web build, performance, warm-ups and the Chromium checks

Plan: `docs/plans/M10_PLAN.md` §3.5 (and §0.6 "How answer 1 applies", §2.12). Wave W27, lane A, beside P46.
Built in two sittings: the code, the native captures and a first round of exports on 14 November 2026; the merge
of `wave-26`, the final exports, the five Chromium checks and the final check on 18 November 2026.
The measurements are in `docs/PERFORMANCE.md`, "The M10 Web build and its budgets (P45, W27)"; this file says what
was built and why.

## What was built

### The owner's answer 1: the Web keeps the 1024 px maps, 26 MB from M10

- `export_presets.cfg`: `Web` and `Web (test)` no longer exclude `assets/giant/tex_hi/*`. Both still leave out
  `assets/giant/manifest.json` (the pipeline's record, as the audio's); the desktop presets leave nothing of the
  giant out.
- `tools/build_web.sh`: the milestone's budget for the shipped `index.pck` is 7.5 MB up to M6, 21 MB at M7, 23 MB
  at M8 and M9 and **26 MB from `-m10` on**, still in parts of at most 15 MB. `GIANT_SHARE`'s default cap stays the
  budget less 18.4 MB, so it is **7.6 MB** at 26. The new `--version=V` judges a build as version `V`
  (`0.10.0-m10`) instead of the project's `config/version`; only the budget rule reads it, and a version without
  its `-m<milestone>` is a usage error (exit 2). It exists because the version stays `0.9.0-m9` until P47: the
  tests run the `-m10` rule through it, and every W27 export was built with `--budget-mb=26` (the share's cap
  follows the budget).
- `GiantLook`'s tier "auto" needed no change: it picks "hi" wherever `tex_hi/normal.png` is in the pack, which
  is now every pack. On the release Web export the game reports the tier it built its materials with
  (`GiantLook.built_tier`) through the probe below: `["hi"]` in the creator's preview, in the Valley after Begin
  and in the world after Continue (`creator_check.mjs` fails on anything else).

### The launcher

`web/launcher.html`: "Start in the open world" hands the engine `['--', '--mode=world', '--creator']`, so the
world start too opens in the character creator (P42 parses `--creator`, P44's title opens the creator from
`GameSession.creator_start`). The button's arming, its label and its 6 s lapse are unchanged.

### The UI probe (`scripts/debug/ui_probe.gd`, `class_name UiProbe`)

A passive node that `scripts/main.gd` makes when the command line has `--ui-probe` (nothing of it exists
otherwise). It reads the screen stack, the focus and the clock each frame and prints:

- `UI_PROBE {"t":"state", ...}` whenever what a player would see changes: the mode, the level, `loading`, the top
  screen, the title's `warming`, the shrine's `tab`, the creator's `context`, `category`, `changed` and `history`,
  the focused control's text (and `focus_tab` when it is one of the shrine's tab buttons), the interact prompt,
  whether the mouse is captured, and the texture tiers of the giants in the tree;
- `UI_PROBE {"t":"stall", "mark", "first_ms", "median_ms", "ratio", ...}` once per mark: the longest of the three
  frame intervals after the mark against the median of the forty after them (§2.12: at most 3x; under 25 ms is
  never a stall). The marks are `title` (the worst frame from the start until New Game enables), `creator`,
  `level` (the first frames once the loading screen lets go; the load's own worst frame is reported apart) and
  `shrine_creator` ("Change your look..." at a shrine);
- with `--ui-probe-bench`, `UI_PROBE {"t":"bench", "skin_ms", "giants", ...}` when a mark's frames are done and the
  player's skinned rig is in the tree: `SkinnedRig._drive_tree`, the `AnimationTree`'s advance, the skeleton's
  update and `PoseBrainModifier`'s pass in a tight loop, the best of five batches of 200 (P43's measure, now
  inside the running game, native and Web).

It also carries three small **tours** for native runs, where no browser presses the keys (the keys go through
`Input.parse_input_event`): `--ui-probe=new_game` (the title, Enter, the creator, PageDown, R, Ctrl+Z, R,
Ctrl+Enter, the level), `--ui-probe=shrine` (a level entered with `--continue` at a shrine: E, Up to the tab
buttons, Right to Appearance, Enter, Down to "Change your look...", Enter) and `--ui-probe=level` (any level: the
giant's own draw calls and primitives, the frame's counts with its models shown and hidden). With a tour it also
prints the rendering server's texture and buffer memory, draw calls and primitives at each mark
(`{"t":"skin"}`) and, with `--ui-probe-shots=<dir>`, saves the window. Tour state lives in static variables,
because Begin and Continue reload the main scene and the probe with it.

### `tools/web/creator_check.mjs` and `test_creator_check.mjs`

The fifth Chromium check (headless, the release build, the keyboard only; it follows the probe's lines and never
reaches into the game). Two parts, each in a fresh browser profile:

- **new_game**: Play; the title's worst frame until New Game enables; Enter; the creator (its first frame);
  PageDown changes the category; R randomizes it; Ctrl+Z undoes it; R again; a screenshot; Ctrl+Enter (Begin); the
  Valley loads (its first frame), the pointer lock asked for while the press still counts as the player's gesture
  (`navigator.userActivation.isActive` at the `requestPointerLock` call); the first save holds `level.valley` and
  the look; the tier is "hi"; a third-person screenshot of the giant.
- **shrine**: the world save fixture of `tools/web/world_check.json` (a save from before the creator) put into
  the page's IndexedDB; Play; Enter (Continue); the world's first frame; E at the shrine (a step back first when
  the giant does not face it); Up to the tab buttons, Right to "Appearance (new)", Enter; Down to "Change your
  look...", Enter; the creator over the shrine (its first frame); a screenshot.

It reports each mark's stall, the skin's CPU a frame after each level and with the creator's preview open over
the shrine (two skinned giants in the tree), the page's own frame clock (the main thread's time inside each
animation frame), console errors (the engine's warnings apart) and failed requests. A stall over 3x the median
frame and a skin over 0.35 ms are reported as **MISS** lines and fail only with `--strict`: software WebGL is all
this machine has. Everything else fails the check. Its own test drives it against a stub launcher (no engine):
34 checks over the modes ok, stall, slowskin, noundo, nolock, webtier, nolook, turned, warning and error.

### The other Chromium checks

- `tools/web/audio_check.mjs`: after Enter (New Game) it presses Ctrl+Enter (Begin) once the creator has had four
  seconds to open, and again every 40 s (at most four times) while no zone music has come; the report gains
  `begin_presses`. `test_audio_check.mjs`'s stub starts the Valley's music only on Ctrl+Enter after Enter
  (26 checks).
- `tools/web/world_start_check.mjs`: Play is Enter, then Ctrl+Enter in the creator, and the first save must hold
  `level.valley` and a look; the open-world start must hand the engine `['--', '--mode=world', '--creator']`,
  write no save while the creator shows, and after Ctrl+Enter save `level.world` with a look. It has no test of
  its own (it never had one); `test_giant_web_presets.gd` ties its arguments to the launcher's.
- `world_check.mjs` and `stream_check.mjs` needed no change (Continue, and `--mode=world --new-game` with a
  scenario, do not pass the creator).

## Command line

```
tools/build_web.sh [--version=V] ...                 # judge the budget as version V (tests; W27's exports used --budget-mb=26)
<game> -- --ui-probe [--ui-probe-bench]              # print UI_PROBE lines (the web checks)
<game> -- --ui-probe=new_game|shrine|level [--ui-probe-shots=<dir>]   # the native tours
node tools/web/creator_check.mjs [--build=builds/web] [--size=960x540] [--parts=new_game,shrine]
     [--stall-ratio=3] [--strict] [--args=a,b] [--shots=DIR] [--json=FILE]
node tools/web/test_creator_check.mjs
```

The native stall runs (Xvfb, the Compatibility renderer):

```
xvfb-run -a -s "-screen 0 1280x720x24" tools/gd.sh --path . --rendering-driver opengl3 --resolution 640x360 \
  -- --no-user-settings --no-audio --save-dir=<empty dir> --ui-probe=new_game --ui-probe-shots=<out>
xvfb-run -a -s "-screen 0 1280x720x24" tools/gd.sh --path . --rendering-driver opengl3 --resolution 640x360 \
  -- --no-user-settings --no-audio --save-dir=<dir with the fixture as valley_1.json> --continue \
  --ui-probe=shrine --ui-probe-shots=<out>
```

- **Draw calls** at `low` (the Web's preset): the Valley walk 94 / 139 / 143 / 152 (p50 / p95 / p99 / max), the

- **The giant alone**, the fullest look in the presets (12 meshes): 12 draw calls and 20,678 primitives at `low`
  (<= 13 and <= 55,000).
- **Memory** (the rendering server's monitors): the giant's textures 4.23 MB with the 1024 px maps (2.73 MB at the

- **The five Chromium checks** on the release build: `creator_check.mjs` OK (3 misses reported),

  `audio_check.mjs` OK on its second run (the first failed on one hole of 206 ms the recorder did not place on the

## Decisions and deviations

- **No warm-up was added to `scripts/main.gd`.** §3.5 item 5 asks for one only if the shrine path or Continue
  stalls above 3x the median, and neither does: the world's first frame after Continue is 1.22x the median frame
  natively (257 against 210 ms) and 2.45x on the Web (1,354 against 553 ms), and the first "Change your look..."
  at a shrine 1.53x (267 against 175 ms) and 1.31x (282 against 214 ms). The Valley's first frame after Begin is
  1.66x and 2.67x. P44's title warm-up already compiles the giant's variants before New Game and Continue enable.
  The one path over the rule is the creator's first frame from the title on the Web, 3.50x in the quiet run
  (525 against 150 ms) and 1.14x on the busy machine; the loading screen is not on that path, so a warm-up there
  could not change it. It is recorded as a miss and handed on (`docs/plans/FOLLOWUPS.md`).
- **`scripts/main.gd` gained four lines for the probe** instead of a warm-up: `UiProbe.from_args(...)` and
  `add_child`. §1.5 gives P45 the file "only if measured necessary" for the warm-up; the checks of §3.5 item 2
  (the title's worst frame until its buttons enable, the creator's and the level's first frames, the first
  "Change your look...") need marks from inside the game, and the game printed none. The probe changes nothing
  without `--ui-probe`.
- **The title's worst frame is reported as a miss by design.** P44's title compiles the giant's shaders in a
  hidden viewport behind "Preparing..." and holds New Game and Continue until it is done: the frame §2.12 lists
  as "the title's worst frame until its buttons enable" is that warm-up itself. It is what keeps the creator's
  and the level's first frames within 3x.
- **A stall or a slow skin is a MISS line, not a failure, unless `--strict`**: as `world_check.mjs` treats the
  music's holes on this GPU-less machine. A gate machine with a GPU runs `--strict`.
- **P41's `BudgetTest` was updated, not weakened.** `build_giant.py` reads the last default `build_web.sh`
  assigns, which is now the `-m10` rule's 26; the test's docstring had named this change as P45's. The first
  sitting had not run `check.sh --giant`, so the final check found it.
- **`build_web.sh --version=V`** is new: the task for W27 was to test the `-m10` rule through the script's version
  parsing without editing `project.godot`.
- **The `-m10` runs of `build_web.sh` are in the slow tier** (`test_giant_tooling.gd`, P41's suite of the same
  script): each is a process, and the default tier's cap for this package is 0.1 s.
- **`world_start_check.mjs` got no test of its own**: §3.5 names "their tests where they exist", and it never had
  one. Its constants are tied to the launcher by `test_giant_web_presets.gd`.
- **The creator check runs at 640x360 here** (its default is 960x540): software WebGL draws the creator's preview
  at 0.3-0.5 s a frame at that size.
- **The fullest look has 12 meshes, not 13**: no preset pairs the warrior outfit with long hair and a full beard;
  the Iron Veteran (warrior, cropped hair, short beard) and the Northern Chieftain (chieftain's furs, long hair,
  full beard) both show 12, and the latter was measured.
- **The plan's §2.12 row was not edited** (the orchestrator amended it before W27); `PERFORMANCE.md` records the
  figures against the amended row.

## Known gaps

- **The creator's first frame on the Web is 3.50x the median frame** in the quiet run (the rule: 3x). Natively
  it is 2.55x. What in that frame costs the extra 375 ms was not isolated.
- **Draw calls at `medium`** are over 250 in the Valley walk (395 at most) and in the stress arena (674): the
  sun's shadow passes draw the giant's meshes again in each cascade. The Web runs `low`.
- **Every time here is software rendering's** (llvmpipe natively, SwiftShader in Chromium): no GPU, no real
  browser, no phone. `creator_check.mjs --strict` and the three audio criteria are the owner's on a machine with
  a GPU.
- **The native captures were not repeated after `wave-26` was merged** (it adds `GiantModel.play_idle()`, used by
  the creator's preview only). The exports and the Chromium checks are of the merged branch.
- **The giant's primitives beyond 120 m** (<= 4,000; P41's row in FOLLOWUPS on `Hair_long_back`'s two LODs at
  1080p) were not measured in the running game: the probe's level tour counts the giant at the camera's own
  distance only.
- **The skin bench times the player's rig only**: with the creator's preview open it reports two giants in the
  tree, but the preview's own `AnimationPlayer` idle is outside the loop (W26 measured it: 0.07-0.19 ms).
- **The Web's frames with the realistic giant are heavy on SwiftShader**: 150-550 ms a frame at 640x360 and
  0.77 s at p50 while walking the world at 1280x720, so the music's holes (193 in 60 s in the Valley, 607 in
  470 s in the world) are the main thread's; none of it says how a GPU fares.
- **`audio_check.mjs`'s first run failed** on one hole the recorder did not place on the main thread
  (206.1 ms at 29.1 s, beside 192 placed ones); the second run passed with 269 holes, all placed. The
  default form's verdict depends on where one hole falls on this machine.
- **The Linux pack's listing** is of 14 November (before the merge); the giant's files did not change since.
