# P41 — The giant asset pipeline and the model

Wave 25, lane A (beside P42). Branch `pkg/P41`. Plan: `docs/plans/M10_PLAN.md` §3.1 (design items 1-11),
with §2.5 (the pipeline, the manifest, the index, the import settings), §2.6 (meshes, regions, render
layers, the bone contract), §2.12 (budgets), §1.1 (definition of done), §1.5-§1.6 (shared files) and §1.7
(test caps: default 0.4 s, slow 5 s, gate 0). The goal: every file of `assets/giant/` regenerated by
committed code from vendored CC0 data, bit-exact on the reference toolchain (but for the Cycles bake's
own jitter of a texel), and the model the game
instances (`GiantModel`), so that the later packages only add looks, clips and content.

## What was built

- **The toolchain** (`tools/giant/requirements.txt`, `setup_venv.sh`). Fifteen pins with the sha256 of
  their Linux x86_64 wheels (bpy 5.3.0, numpy 2.5.4, scipy 1.18.1, Pillow 13.0.0, OpenEXR 3.5.4 and their
  dependencies), installed with `pip --require-hashes --no-deps --only-binary=:all:` into `GIANT_VENV`
  outside every worktree (a venv whose `pip freeze` already equals the pins is kept). `GIANT_CACHE`
  holds the bake cache and the audited data wheel. Python 3.13.
- **The CC0 inputs** (`tools/giant/mpfb2/`, 133 files, 6,748,786 bytes, with `FILES.sha256`,
  `SOURCE.md` and an empty `.gdignore`): the hm08 base mesh, the 122 targets of the giant's phenotype,
  the regional modifier table, the "game_engine" rig and its weights, six region mask images and the
  CC0 licence, copied as data from the wheel `anny-0.6.2-py3-none-any.whl`.
  `tools/giant/fetch_mpfb2.py` audits the copy (`AUDIT OK: 133 vendored files ...`): it extracts the
  wheel's data folder to a temporary directory, computes the files the kit reads and compares each with
  the wheel and with `FILES.sha256`; `--extract` rewrites the folder, `--download` fetches the wheel.
  Nothing from the wheel is executed.
- **The kit** (`tools/giant/kit/`), ported from the research run's frozen snapshot: `mh_data`
  (the MakeHuman formats, the phenotype, the bone families), `giant_body` (the morphed body at the
  stage-1 height 3.6576 m on the game_engine rig, the derived masks and landmarks), `giant_skin` (the
  procedural skin material; the beard shadow and the scalp tint left to the shader), `skin_textures`
  (the micro-relief tile), `giant_hair` (strand growth and cards: `Hair_long_back` 170+140 cards,
  `Hair_crop` 400, `Beard_full` 140+100, `Beard_short` 160), `giant_garment` (the braies and belt from
  the base mesh's tights helper; tunic, jerkin, bracers, boots and furs cut from the body by bone-weight
  regions), `node_util`, and the research's scripts as `export_game`, `bake`, `designs` and `pack`, plus
  the new pure-numpy `split.py` (parts, regions, cut loops, caps).
- **The export** (`kit/export_game.py`): one `giant.glb` on one 53-bone skeleton. The body is split into
  `Body_Head` (layer 3), `Body_Torso` (2) and `Body_Limbs` (1) at the neck, the shoulders and the hips:
  five cut loops (50, 28, 28, 34 and 34 vertices), every one capped on both sides by a fan (the rim keeps
  its skin weights, the centre is 100 % the nearest bone). The 16 region ids ride in `COLOR_0.r =
  (id + 0.5) / 16` (BYTE_COLOR, decoded with `int(COLOR.r * 16.0)`). `Eyes` (two 20 x 14 spheres at
  1.02 x the eyeball radius); the four hair and beard meshes (every vertex 100 % `head`, custom normals);
  the seven garments, of which the braies and the tunic are split at the same body boundary into
  `_core` (layer 2) and `_limbs` (layer 1) parts with capped cuts; the belt, jerkin and furs are torso
  parts, the bracers and boots limb parts; the boots decimated to 2,500 triangles. Each garment's
  `covers` (the regions it hides: at least 97 % of a region's body vertices, by rays along their normals
  reaching 12 cm at human scale) and its coverage per region go into the index. The landmarks (shoulders, elbows, wrists, palms, hips, knees, ankles, eye midpoint,
  head, neck, pelvis and the two reaches) and the body's hash go into the manifest.
- **The bakes** (`kit/bake.py`): Cycles, 1024 px, seed 0, `BAKE_THREADS` (2), selected-to-active from
  the subdivided body onto the base mesh's UVs, in two groups: the cheap passes (albedo at a neutral
  tone, roughness, tangent normal, the stubble/scalp/brow/lip masks, the region map from a face
  attribute, the rest position and normal for the designs) and the expensive ones (AO, inside-AO
  thickness). Each group is cached in `GIANT_CACHE/bake/<key>/` under a hash of its inputs.
- **The designs** (`kit/designs.py`): the floor's 17 - 8 tattoos (arm bands, arm chevrons, chest
  spiral, chest knot, back wings, back tree, face lines, face dots), 2 war paints (face stripes,
  shoulder bands), 4 scars (brow, forearm slash, chest claws, knuckles) and 3 ancestry marks (granite
  veins, lightning tree, claw stripes) - drawn as distance fields in 3D on the rest pose from the bake's
  rest position, normal and region maps, packed into four 512 px RGBA layers (`designs.png`, stacked
  vertically) under the packing rule; the scars fill layer 0 (`scar_layer`).
- **The pack** (`kit/pack.py`): `tex/` (albedo, normal, ORM, masks at 512 px), `tex_hi/` (normal and ORM
  at 1024 px; the ORM's R and B hold AO and thickness losslessly), resized one channel at a time; the
  hair atlas (strands drawn in numpy); three 128 px tileable detail maps (`cloth_weave`, `cloth_leather`,
  `cloth_fur`: a normal from a height field in RGB, OpenGL Y+, and an albedo detail in A, 0.75 neutral).
- **The manifest, verify and budget** (`tools/giant/build_giant.py`, `manifest.py`). Steps `--body`,
  `--garments`, `--hair` (one export), `--bake`, `--designs`, `--pack`, `--clips` (P43's `kit/clips.py`
  when it exists), `--all`. `assets/giant/manifest.json` (schema 1) records every output (the step, its
  input hash, sha256, size, whether Cycles pixels reach it), the toolchain (versions and a CPU
  fingerprint), the inputs' hashes (the vendored files, the kit's sources), the bake settings and keys,
  the export and design information, the body's hash and the giant index. `--verify` regenerates every
  file into a temporary folder and compares: bit-exact on the reference toolchain but for the bake's own
  jitter (a Cycles map may differ in at most 64 texels by 1/255: `TOLERATED`), Cycles outputs
  within 2/255 per channel and 0.1/255 on average on another (`TOLERATED`), with `TOOLCHAIN DIFFERS`
  kept apart from `CONTENT DIFFERS` and `SHIPPED FILE` (the committed files against the manifest); it
  never reads the bake cache and refreshes it unless `--no-cache`. `--skip-bake` runs the cheap passes
  only and reads AO and thickness back from the committed `tex_hi/orm.png`, after checking that the
  expensive passes' inputs still hash to the manifest's (else it asks for `--giant-full`). `--budget`
  estimates each shipped file's size from measured rates (see "Decisions as built").
  `ensure_import_files` writes the `.import` settings of §2.5 for every output.
- **The check options** (`tools/check.sh`): `--giant` (the pipeline's tests, `--verify --skip-bake` and
  `--budget` in the shared venv under `nice -n 19`), `--giant-full` (`--verify` with every pass),
  `--require` (a skipped check fails: a missing venv or audio toolchain, any suite's "skipped - " note),
  `--strict-times` (a tier over its budget runs once more and the faster run decides; both are recorded
  in `suite_times.txt`).
- **The model** (`scripts/presentation/giant_model.gd`, `class_name GiantModel`; its builder
  `tools/builders/giant_model.gd`, before `player.gd` in `BUILDERS`; `scenes/player/giant_model.tscn`).
  The scene is a `GiantModel` root holding the glTF instance "Giant" turned 180° about Y (the glTF faces
  +Z, every actor faces -Z) and the exported `index`. `_ready()` joins `giant_models` and applies every
  mesh's render layer and the default visibility from the index (a SceneKit save keeps nothing set
  inside an instance). The glTF is `preload`ed, so it loads once per process.
- **The Web accounting**: both Web presets exclude `assets/giant/manifest.json` and
  `assets/giant/tex_hi/*`; `tools/pck_files.py --giant-share PCK [--verbose]` and `--sizes PCK`;
  `tools/build_web.sh` prints `GIANT_SHARE ... cap_mb=N` after the budget and fails (exit 1) over the
  cap, before the launcher is touched.
- **Provenance and credits**: `assets/giant/PROVENANCE.md`, `CREDITS.md` (new), `tools/giant/README.md`.

## Public APIs

### GiantModel (`scripts/presentation/giant_model.gd`)

| Member | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `const GROUP := "giant_models"` | every model joins it in `_ready()` |
| `const SCENE := preload("res://assets/giant/giant.glb")` | the imported glTF, held for the process |
| `@export var index: Dictionary` | the giant index (below), saved in the scene |
| `apply_defaults()` | every index mesh on its layer (`1 << (layer - 1)`), visible when `defaults.shown` lists it; `_ready()` calls it |
| `skeleton() -> Skeleton3D` | the one skeleton (53 bones, the game_engine names) |
| `mesh(id) -> MeshInstance3D` | an index mesh by id; null for an unknown id |
| `meshes(category) -> Array[MeshInstance3D]` | the meshes of "body", "eyes", "hair", "beard" or "garment", in id order |
| `landmarks() -> Dictionary`, `static landmarks_of(index)` | the landmarks as `Vector3` joints and float reaches, stage 1, this node's frame (+X the giant's right, +Y up, -Z his front) |
| `hand_transform(right) -> Transform3D` | the hand bone's global pose (pending pose changes applied first), for the preview's weapon |
| `pose_relaxed()`, `pose_rest()` | arms 38° down from the A-pose with 12° of elbow, and back |

### The giant index (`GiantModel.index`, from `manifest.json`'s `index`)

The plan's contract (§2.5) plus a few fields the look and the creator use:

```
meshes:    {id: {node, category, layer, dye ("primary"|"secondary"|"none"), covers: [region ids], tris,
                 detail ("weave"|"leather"|"fur", garments), part ("torso"|"limbs", garments),
                 coverage: {region id: share >= 0.5} (garments), atlas ("hair_atlas", hair and beards)}}
garments:  {braies: [Garment_braies_core, Garment_braies_limbs], tunic: [..._core, ..._limbs], belt, jerkin,
            bracers, boots, furs: [one mesh each]}
designs:   {id: {layer, channel 0..3, kind ("tattoo"|"paint"|"scar"|"mark"), regions}}   # 17
regions:   [{id, name}] x 16 (0 scalp ... 14 foot, 15 cap)
design_regions: {face: [1], chest: [3, 4], back: [5, 6], arms: [8, 9]}
landmarks: {shoulder_r: [x, y, z], ..., reach_r, reach_l}
defaults:  {shown: [Body_Head, Body_Torso, Body_Limbs, Eyes, Hair_long_back, Beard_full,
                    Garment_braies_core, Garment_braies_limbs, Garment_belt]}
tiers:     {web: res://assets/giant/tex/, hi: res://assets/giant/tex_hi/}
textures:  {designs, design_layers (4), scar_layer (0), hair_atlas, detail_weave, detail_leather, detail_fur}
body_hash, manifest_sha256 (the builder's: the manifest text's sha256)
```

Numbers that are whole in the contract are ints in the saved scene (the builder converts JSON's floats).
The landmarks reproduce the animation brief's: the right shoulder at (0.414, 3.015, -0.033), the right
reach 1.384983 m.

## Decisions as built

- **The scars are designs.** The research baked its scars into the skin; here the skin has none and the
  four scars are distance fields in the design array's layer 0, so a look can show or hide each.
- **The design values are quantized to the S3TC block's levels** (0, 1/3, 2/3, 1) after a smoothstep
  that steepens the edges (`designs.downsample`): DXT5 then keeps every design within the slow suite's
  0.05 mean error with no ghost in another channel (before: the forearm slash 0.073 and the chest claws
  0.057).
- **The coverage rule.** A garment `covers` a region when at least 97 % of the region's body vertices
  meet it along their normals within 12 cm at human scale (18 cm on the giant); `coverage` keeps every
  share of at least 0.5, for the look's own rules.
- **Garment hems stay open**; only the cuts at the body's part boundaries are capped (a garment is a
  shell over the body, its hems lie on skin).
- **The vendored subset is 133 data files**, not the plan's 136: the default rig and weights (the region
  masks come from the game_engine weights' bone families instead) and the SSS image (the skin has no SSS
  map) are not needed.
- **The detail maps are 128 px** and there are three (a fur map for the furs beside the plan's weave and
  leather); RGB is the normal, A an albedo detail around 0.75.
- **`--verify` never reads the bake cache**: a regeneration that passes has baked every pass it compares.
  It writes the cache afterwards (so a following `--all` is quick) unless `--no-cache`. The skin's
  micro-relief tile is no exception: a run that reads the cache finds it under the hash of its generator
  (`skin_textures.py`), a run that does not makes its own, in a separate interpreter before the bake
  starts, so the bake's process does the same work either way.
- **The bake's own jitter is tolerated on the reference toolchain.** The tangent-space normal bake is not
  quite deterministic: run again on the same inputs and machine it came out one texel different (one
  channel by 1/255, of 1,048,576) in 3 of 14 bakes with the tile made beforehand, and in 3 of 4 with the
  tile made inside the bake's process; every other pass was the same each time. `--verify` therefore
  accepts a Cycles map (`"cycles": true` in the manifest) that differs in at most 64 texels by at most
  1/255 (`manifest.image_jitter`) and reports it as `TOLERATED`; anything more is a content difference.
- **The budget estimate** (`--budget`) counts only what the Web preset ships (it reads the preset's
  `exclude_filter` from `export_presets.cfg`) at rates measured on the asset brief's export (meshes 61 B
  a vertex with LODs and shadow meshes, cards 35 B, textures by their VRAM size and a per-map factor), and
  judges it by build_web.sh's rule: the default budget less 18.4 MiB. The real share is the export's
  `GIANT_SHARE` line.
- **`GIANT_SHARE`'s cap is the budget less 18.4 MB** (4.6 at 23; the owner's 26 MB answer gives 7.6), so
  the budget's change in P45 moves the cap with it; `--giant-share-mb=N` overrides it.

## Deviations

- **The Web presets exclude `tex_hi/`** although §0.6's owner answer 1 is 26 MiB with the Web keeping the
  1024 px maps: the plan's text for W25 (decision 17, §1.6, §2.12, §3.1 item 9) keeps the exclusion and
  gives "lets the Web keep the 1024 maps" to P45 together with the budget's change (plan §7, "Noticed,
  not an M9 change"). P41 followed the W25 text; the follow-up for P45 is in `FOLLOWUPS.md` ("From P41").
  The measured cost of keeping them: 1,814,464 bytes shipped (1.73 MiB, the plan assumed about 1.3).
- **The braies' cut is one loop of 50 vertices** around both thighs (the braies join the legs at the
  crotch, so the two hip loops of the body are one on the garment); one fan caps it. The tunic's cut
  has the body's four loops.
- **The behavioural Web share test runs in the slow tier** (`tests/slow/test_giant_tooling.gd`, 0.31-0.44 s:
  python3 and six script runs): in the default tier it cost 0.73 s, over P41's 0.4 s cap.
- **The hair crop has 400 cards** (the research's 200 doubled, the plan's first option).
- **"Bit-exact on the reference toolchain" allows the bake's own jitter** (above): at most 64 texels of a
  Cycles map off by 1/255. The plan asks for bit-exact; three bakes in fourteen were not, on one texel.

- **P43**: `--clips` runs `kit/clips.py` when it exists and its output joins the manifest like any step;
  `scenes/player/giant_model.tscn` is rebuilt by its builder before `player.gd`.
- **P44**: `scar_brow` is small (30 texels at 1024 px, 8 at 512); the option check at the Face framing
  decides whether it reads.
- **P45**: the owner's 26 MiB (FOLLOWUPS). `GIANT_SHARE` is measured by every Web build.
- **After W25 the body mesh, skeleton, regions and landmarks are frozen** (decision 9): the manifest's
  `body_hash` (`9cd81c08...`) changes only with an orchestrator's decision.
- The `TOLERATED` path of `--verify` (another toolchain's Cycles pixels) is exercised only on synthetic
  images by the pipeline's tests (`ToleranceTest`); this container is the reference toolchain.
- **The tangent-space normal bake jitters by a texel** (above): a regeneration can change one texel of
  the normal maps by 1/255, so `--all` after a verify that refreshed the cache can show the two normal
  maps as changed in git with no change of content. Where it comes from inside Blender (a tie between
  two faces in the selected-to-active ray cast, most likely) was not traced.
