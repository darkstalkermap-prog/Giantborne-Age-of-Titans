## The launcher's notes (as shipped)

> **New in 0.13.0-m13.** The people and beasts around you are realistic now, made the same way as your giant. Soldiers, pikemen, shieldmen, archers, mages, bannermen, siege crews and the iron guard wear mail, leather and helms under a surcoat in their side's colour, so you can tell a formation's side across a field; villagers and elders, men and women, walk the towns and farms; the named characters, Warlord Corvane and the riders on their horses are people too, and no two in a crowd look quite alike. The ogres, the trolls and the whelps are heavy brutes, and the Ogre King and the Troll Matriarch wear their own gear; the wolves and dire wolves have fur; the rival giants and Harrok are giants like yours, in looks of their own. They all move as bodies: they walk, run, flee and brace, and their attacks wind up where you can read them. A pikeman crouches over the planted pike and thrusts it in both hands, a shield bash comes across the chest, an archer kicks, a rival giant lifts its axe in both hands for an overhead chop, a wolf draws back with its jaws wide before it snaps. A soldier you hold lets its weapon hang away from you, one you throw goes down and gets back up, and the dead drop their weapons on the ground and stay where they fell. Nothing about the fighting changed: the same enemies, numbers and warnings as in 0.12.0-m12. Your saves and looks from 0.10.0-m10, 0.11.0-m11 and 0.12.0-m12 carry over unchanged.
>
> **What it is not yet.** Some bodies still look rough up close: the women share the men's face with softer features; the wolf's ears are bare, the horse has no saddle, and the troll's and the ogre's heads show flat facets at the brow and jaw; a hand that holds a pike, a staff or a club with the other hand lies open on the shaft. Some motion is plainer than it should be: a soldier you threw gets up by playing its fall backwards, the dead all lie in the same pose (turned different ways), a rank seen from afar stands in one pose, the horse's gallop is plainer than a real one, a brute's two-handed swing leaves its other hand open, and a dead horse falls with stiff legs. A whelp the Troll Matriarch throws passes through her arm as she lifts it. The rival giants' axes are still simple shapes, and so are the sparring post, the dummies and the siege engines, and the world itself. Hair is still made of shaped strips and layers, not single strands. In first person you do not see your legs or feet. The balance is a bot's: it walks from objective to objective, no person has played the world yet, and some fights are still harder for it than they should be (the Iron Regiment, if you take the crown's oath first at 36 ft). Siege trains do not wander the roads. The browser version runs on one thread, so expect slow frames and music that can stutter on weaker computers; in the browser the creator's grids show names instead of pictures.
>
> **The next build.** This build finishes the plan that made your giant, its wardrobe and the people around it realistic. What comes next has not been chosen yet.

The masthead reads "realistic people and creatures - build 0.13.0-m13"; the download note "About 56 MB" (the
figure the launcher's script writes from the BUILD line: the engine and the pack as shipped, 56,432,680 bytes).

## Decisions and deviations

- **The release's Web build is in `builds/web/`** (the plan's default; P52 and P58 built with `--out=`), so
  `build_web.sh` wrote the BUILD line into `web/launcher.html` itself. The Chromium checks ran on an earlier build
  of the same pack; the folder to publish is the build on the final check's commit (§5.4 of the handoff).
- **The download note says 56 MB, not 57**: the launcher's script rounds the engine and the pack as shipped
  (56.43 MB) and `test_web_presets` requires the static note to be that figure; the whole page is 56.84 MB.
- **No cross-version run**: Part C changed no save or look record (the schema 2, the record `v` 2); the fixtures of
  `0.5` to `0.12` load in the suites. P58's driver is still not in the repository (FOLLOWUPS).
- **No `--strict-times`, no `--audio` and no `--giant-full`**: the check is the one the plan and the brief name
  (`--build --slow --gate --giant --people --people-full --web-tools --require`); Part C changed no audio and no giant
  bake input (P64c's and P65d's checks ran `--giant`).
- **Not run on the export**: the assisted bot runs and the tour under Xvfb; the tour's part C ran from source.
- **No game file changed; three tests changed with the release** (`test_giant_web_presets`' expected branches,
  `test_giant_tools.BudgetTest`'s last default, `test_giant_tooling`'s forced budgets named as 0.12's): each states
  the rule the release moves, none was loosened.
