# P42 — The look: appearance rules, data and saves; the giant's shaders and GiantLook; tip matching; the M10 pre-declarations

Wave 25, lane B, beside P41 (the giant asset pipeline). Branch `pkg/P42`, from `e74520f` (0.9.0-m9 plus the M10
plan). Plan: `docs/plans/M10_PLAN.md` §3.2, with §1.5-§1.7 (pre-declarations, shared edits, test tiers), §2.2
(the record), §2.3 (the data), §2.4 (the look), §2.7 (the code rule), §2.8 (shader parameters), §2.9 (tip
matching), §2.10 (the creator's rules the record serves), §2.11 (events, command line, flags) and §2.13 (test
conventions). The look never touches the simulation: nothing that runs a tick reads it, and nothing in it draws
from `Sim.rng`. No symbol of the partner lane is used: P41's `giant_model.tscn` and its index are read through
`ResourceLoader.exists()` and the index's documented format, and the tests that need them skip with a note.

## What was built

### The record and its rules (`scripts/progression/appearance.gd`, `class_name Appearance`, pure)

The optional `character.appearance` (schema 2 kept; the record carries its own `"v"`, 1 in M10):

```
{"v": 1, "preset": "appearance_preset.wanderer", "skin": "skin.ruddy", "eyes": "eyes.grey_green",
 "hair": {"style": "hair.long_back", "color": "haircolor.chestnut", "grey": 0.12},
 "beard": {"style": "beard.full", "color": "", "grey": 0.2},
 "tattoos": {"arms": "", "chest": "", "back": "", "face": "", "ink": "ink.woad"},
 "paint": {"design": "", "color": "paintcolor.chalk"}, "scars": [], "mark": "auto",
 "outfit": {"set": "outfit.wanderer", "primary": "dye.undyed", "secondary": "dye.dark_leather"}}
```

An option's id is `<set category>.<key>`. Absent means the default look; only the creator writes a record, always
the full record.

- `resolve(character)`: the character's record (sanitized for its line), else the `--appearance=` preset's
  (`Appearance.cli_preset`), else the default preset's (the Wanderer). `default_record()`, `preset_record(id)`
  (short or full id), `preset_ids()`, `migrate(record)` (one pure step per version; none needed in M10).
- `sanitize(record, ancestry_id) -> {record, dropped, notes}` never fails: a record that is not a dictionary or
  whose fields have the wrong types becomes the default with `NOTE_BROKEN` ("Your giant's look could not be read;
  the default look is used."); a record with `v` above 1 is kept untouched with `NOTE_FUTURE`; otherwise every id
  is checked against its set - unknown ids fall back to the default record's and are listed in `dropped`, options
  locked for the character's line are reset and named in `NOTE_LOCKED`, a tattoo in another region than its own
  is removed with `NOTE_MISPLACED`, scars are made unique and capped at four, greys clamped and cast, `v` cast to
  an int, a mark mode other than "auto"/"none" becomes "auto", an unknown preset name is cleared, and keys this
  build does not know are kept (a later build's addition survives). `structure_errors()`, `is_future()`,
  `is_valid()`.
- `to_look(record, ancestry_id)`: the concrete look `GiantLook.apply` draws (§2.4): palettes resolved (hex, sRGB),
  locked options replaced, `mark: "auto"` resolved with the line's mark (`ancestry_mark()`), the beard in the
  hair's colour when its own is "", tattoos as `{design, ink}`, the outfit's garments and its two dyes. A later
  build's record draws as the default.
- `random_record(rng, scope, keep_outfit, ancestry_id, base)`: the creator's Randomize, from the creator's own
  generator only. Scopes `presets`, `skin`, `eyes`, `hair`, `beard`, `tattoos`, `paint`, `scars`, `outfit`, `all`
  (aliases `clothing`, `war_paint`, ...). Natural hair colours 90 % of the time, at most two tattoo regions and
  never one the outfit covers, war paint 30 % of the time and never where the outfit covers, the beard in the
  hair's colour 70 %, two scars at most, the mark's choice kept, "all" keeps the outfit when `keep_outfit`; locked
  options are never picked.
- `summary_lines(record)`: nine lines for the shrine's Appearance tab, e.g. "Hair: long, combed back, chestnut, a
  little grey"; `grey_words()`.
- `options(category, ancestry_id)` (copies with `id`, `locked`, `reason`), `option(id)`, `option_ids()`,
  `set_default()`, `locked_reason(id, ancestry_id)` ("Choose your line at a shrine of the Old Kingdom" before a
  line, "Only for the <line> line" after another), `is_locked()`, `required_ancestry()`.
- `covered_regions(record, index = {})` ("Hidden by your clothing"): without an index the outfit option's `covers`
  (design regions); with the giant's index, a design region is covered when all its body regions are covered by
  the outfit's garments' meshes (`covered_body_regions(garments, index)`, also GiantLook's `hidden_regions`).
- `data_errors(records, record_paths)`: GameData's post-pass at anchor P42 - one set per category and all thirteen
  when any appearance record exists, the default preset uses every set's default, every preset names itself and
  passes `sanitize()` before any line with nothing dropped or noted. A test's temporary data without appearance
  records is not checked.

### Schemas (`scripts/core/schemas/appearance_{set,preset}_schema.gd`)

`appearance_set`: the category one of the thirteen, `id` = `appearance_set.<category>`, options with unique
lowercase keys, exactly one default (none in tattoo, paint, scar and mark), the category's fields (a palette's
`#rrggbb` colour, a design option's design and region, a mark's line, an outfit's garments and `covers`).
`appearance_preset`: `id` prefix, a full record of version 1 without structural errors, an optional `ancestry`.

### Saves (`character_state.gd`, `save_slots.gd`)

`CharacterState.sanitize()` repairs the look for the character's ancestry and passes up its dropped ids and its
notes (`{data, dropped, notes}`); absent stays absent, so every older save loads as it was and whole-character
comparisons are unchanged. `SaveSlots.read()` returns `notes` and joins them into its message beside "Dropped ids
this build does not know"; a broken look is never a reason to refuse a file or to fall back to the backup.

### The giant's component (`scripts/actors/player/player_appearance.gd`, `PlayerAppearance`)

`apply_state`/`capture_state` move the key only when present (a later build's record is written back unchanged);
`record()` (own, else `--appearance=`, else default), `look()`, `has_record()`, `ancestry()`, `set_record(record)`
(the creator's "Keep this look": stores the sanitized record, applies it, emits
`Events.appearance_changed(tick, entity_id)`), `restore_record(previous)` (Cancel; no event), `refresh()` (resolves
the look and calls `player.rig.apply_look(look)` when the rig has it - nothing on the stylized rig of W25). On
`ancestry_chosen` the look is resolved again, so `mark: "auto"` shows the line's mark at once; for a look of the
player's own (a record, or a save from before M10) it then sets `appearance.mark_shown`. No simulation hook.

### The session (`game_session.gd`)

- `change_mode(mode, options)`: `options.appearance` (the creator's record) is stored in the new character of a
  Valley or world New Game (`start_new_valley(appearance)`, `start_new_world(start, appearance)`,
  `start_world_for_tests(start, appearance)`), repaired for the character's line.
- `--appearance=<preset id or key>` sets `Appearance.cli_preset` in every mode (sandbox, bots, soak, command-line
  New Games); an unknown preset warns and is ignored; never written into a save.
- `--creator` with `--mode=world` or `--mode=valley --new-game` leaves the session on the title with
  `creator_start = {"mode", "options"}` (P44's title opens the creator from it); otherwise it warns.
- `appearance_changed` joins the F15 list: a look kept while a shrine's screen (or the creator over it) is open
  re-takes the shrine's checkpoint at the end of the frame; `_only_shrine_screens_open()` counts the creator.
- A Continue of a save whose character has no look sets `appearance.legacy_save` (in WorldState and in the
  checkpoint document, so a death before the next checkpoint keeps it).
- `reset_for_tests()` clears `creator_start` and `Appearance.cli_preset`.

### The shaders (`assets/shaders/giant_{skin,hair,eye,cloth}.gdshader`)

Ported from the asset research's shaders, the uniforms of §2.8: the skin's albedo, normal, ORM and mask maps and
the design array (`sampler2DArray`), `skin_tone` against `bake_tone`, hair and beard colours, stubble, scalp and
beard tints, brows; the region arrays `region_tattoo`, `region_paint`, `region_mark` (16 ints, code = layer x 4 +
channel + 1, 0 = none; the vertex's region from `COLOR.r x 16`), ink, paint and mark colours and the mark's glow,
`scar_layer`/`scar_on`, `hidden_regions` (a bitmask; hidden regions sink by `hide_shrink`), the wrap, transmission
and roughness terms; the hair's atlas, colour, tips and grey (cull disabled, depth pre-pass); the eye's iris colour
and glow; the cloth's detail map, dye, detail strength and the creator's screen-door `fade`. Every shader declares
`tint` (vec4) and `flash` (vec3) for RigPaint. No World style (deferred, §0.5).

### `GiantLook` (`scripts/presentation/giant_look.gd`)

- `build_materials(root, index, tier)`: one `ShaderMaterial` per index mesh and instance (never shared) of its
  category's shader (body: skin, eyes: eye, hair and beard: hair, garment: cloth), the tier's textures from a
  static cache, then the stored look applied. Tiers: "web" (the index's `tiers.web`, 512 px), "hi" (normal and ORM
  from `tiers.hi`, each falling back to the web folder when its file is missing) and "auto" (hi when
  `tex_hi/normal.png` exists); `--giant-tier=web|hi` forces one (`GiantLook.forced_tier`).
- `apply(root, index, look)`: shows the look's hair, beard and garment meshes and hides the others, stores the look
  on the root, and when materials exist sets every uniform (the region arrays always filled). Headless, without
  materials, it only sets visibility and stores the look.
- `validate(root, index, look)`: unknown mesh, garment and design ids, a design of the wrong kind, scars on more
  than one layer, index meshes without their node or with an unknown category, and every uniform name GiantLook
  sets that its shader does not declare (`get_shader_uniform_list()`: a headless material stores a misspelled name
  without complaint). `[]` = clean, never an engine error.
- `materials(root)` (RigPaint's path), `applied_look()`, `built_tier()`, `set_garment_fade()`,
  `hidden_regions_mask()`, `region_codes()`, `scar_uniforms()`, `map_paths()`, `resolve_tier()`, `mesh_node()`,
  `meshes_of()`, `DEFAULT_LOOK` (§2.4's Wanderer; `to_look(default)` equals it within 1/255 a channel).
- File names it and P41 agree on: `<tier>/albedo.png`, `normal.png`, `orm.png` (R AO, G roughness, B thickness),
  `masks.png` (R stubble, G scalp, B brows, A lips); `assets/giant/designs.png`; `assets/giant/hair_atlas.png`;
  `assets/giant/cloth_weave.png` and `cloth_leather.png`. The index may name others (`tiers`, `design_array`, a
  mesh entry's `atlas`, `detail`, `detail_kind`).

### `TipMatching` (`scripts/presentation/tip_matching.gd`, pure)

From the weapon data and the landmarks (`from_index(index)` - the index's `landmarks` when both shoulders are
there, a missing joint taking the default - else `DEFAULT_LANDMARKS`, the animation research's lab body):
`swing_geometry(profile, weapon, stage, action, f, offset)` (the tested segment in Body space, stage-1 metres),
`match_swing(pivot, dir, arm, weapon, side)` -> `{tip, grip, weapon_dir, visible_tip, hand_dir, wrist, elbow,
palm, fist_error, reached, exact}`, `tip_matched_grip()` (the natural grip at 0.93 x reach moved onto the
weapon-length sphere around the tip and projected alternately into reach; when they do not meet, the farthest
reachable point toward the tip), `hand_axis()` (41° off the weapon, toward the arm), `two_bone()` (the analytic
solve, the elbow's pole down, back and out, lifted out across the body), `idle_grip(family, side)` (the creator's
resting grip), `tested_segment()`, `outer_half_deviation()`, `angle_deg()`.

### Pre-declarations (§1.5, §2.11)

`events.gd`: the "appearance (M10)" block with `appearance_changed(tick: int, entity_id: String)` (emitter
PlayerAppearance, listener GameSession) above the anchors P41, P43, P44, P45, P46, P47; `game_data.gd`: anchors
P42 (its own post-pass) and P46; `character_state.gd`: anchor P46. Registries: `ScreenStack.SCREENS["creator"]`,
`ShrineScreen.TABS` Appearance (last), `GiantPlayer.COMPONENTS` appearance and its `appearance` field,
`DataValidator.SCHEMA_PATHS` appearance_set and appearance_preset, `ResetHooks.AFTER_EACH` SkinnedRig
(`reset_for_tests`, before the world index entry, which stays last). `tests/support/anchors_expected.gd` lists the
rows with P42 as their creator.

### Numbers the plan asks for

- **Tip matching** (default landmarks, 1,004 samples over every player weapon arc at stages 1, 3, 4 and 6): median
  7.08° off the tested direction, worst 12.66°; the weapons that fit their stage keep the outer half within 0.85 x
  the capsule's radius and 9.8°; the ones carried past their fit stage miss by up to 4.16 x the radius
  (deviation 3).
- **Randomize**: 0.43 ms a record (sanitize and checks included), so the plan's 1,000 seeds in every scope cost
  4.3 s.
- **The rendered frame** (stand-in, 320x240, llvmpipe, two threads): 9.2-16.1 ms a frame by wall time; the
  viewport's measured render time 14.3 ms CPU and 16.7 ms GPU in one run. **Not the real model's**: P43's caps need
  the W25 merge's run on `giant_model.tscn`.
- **Headless cost of the look**: `apply()` without materials sets visibility and stores a dictionary; the
  synthetic model's whole suite (12 meshes, two instances, materials built) runs in 25 ms.

## Runtime added

- Per giant: one `PlayerAppearance` component - a `to_look()` (a sanitize and a few dozen dictionary lookups, well
  under a millisecond) at setup, on a restore, on `set_record` and on `ancestry_chosen`; nothing per tick.
- Per save read: `Appearance.sanitize` on a character that has a look.
- Per session start: `--appearance=` and `--creator` parsed.
- `GiantLook`: nothing until P43 calls it; then one material per mesh and instance and a static texture cache.
- Data: 23 records in `data/appearance/` and two tutorial prompts (GameData's load and post-pass: no change
  visible in the runner's start-up).

## Decisions and deviations

1. **The session's tests are a new suite** (`tests/integration/test_appearance_session.gd`), not additions to
   `test_session_flow` (P44's in W26), and the shrine case (F15, the mark and its prompt) is a slow suite,
   `tests/slow/test_appearance_shrine.gd`: the shrine's scene and screen cost about 0.25 s, which the default cap of
   0.6 s cannot carry. The default tier keeps the structural half (`test_predeclared_m10`: GameSession listens to
   `appearance_changed`).
2. **`random_record` over 1,000 seeds in every scope is in the slow tier** (`tests/slow/test_appearance_random.gd`,
   one shared check in `tests/support/appearance_random_check.gd`); the default suite runs 6 seeds a scope. At
   about 0.43 ms a record, the plan's 10,000 records cost 4.3 s.
3. **The tip-matching contract holds for the weapons that fit the stage.** A weapon carried past its fit stage (an
   unreforged club, sword or hammer at stage 4 or 6: `PlayerEquipment.too_small_at`) keeps its stage-2 length
   while the arm grows; its tested capsule starts up to 0.28·k beyond the real hand's reach, so no grip can put
   its outer half inside the capsule. For those the test checks the direction (at most 15°: worst 12.7°), the
   reach and the solve, and prints the capsule's miss (up to 4.2 x the radius, the stage-6 sword). Every weapon
   that fits keeps the full contract with a margin (outer half at most 0.85 x the radius, worst angle 9.8°; over
   all 1,004 samples the median is 7.1° and the worst 12.7°). The contract runs at stages 1, 3, 4 and 6 (§2.9's list; §3.2 names 1, 4 and 6). P43 decides how a
   too-small weapon is held (FOLLOWUPS).
4. **The mark's prompt is the player's own**: `appearance.mark_shown` is set only for a look with a record or a
   save from before M10, never for a command-line or bot look, so the bots' runs set no new flag (their
   `flags_hash` stays as it was). `appearance.legacy_save` is set on every Continue of a save without a look -
   which in W25 also means a character a New Game made without the creator (P44 makes every New Game write a
   record).
5. **Covered regions without the index**: an outfit option's optional `covers` (design regions) in the data, so
   Randomize avoids hidden regions before the model exists; with the index the garments' own `covers` decide.
6. **An empty record sanitizes to the default with `preset: ""`** (it was not built from the Wanderer); an unknown
   preset name is cleared the same way.
7. **The rendered probe is a main scene**, `tests/support/giant_shader_probe.tscn`: a `-s` script runs without the
   autoloads, and `Appearance` (which `GiantLook.apply` uses for the hidden regions) reads `GameData`. Its frames
   are drawn by the normal loop, two per capture, and it quits right after (a watchdog quits it on a script error;
   the test runs it inside `timeout`), rather than turning the render loop off between captures.
8. **Edits outside the owned list**, both needed by what the plan adds: `scripts/audio/cue_router.gd` lists
   `appearance_changed` in `SILENT_SIGNALS` (`test_sfx_coverage` requires every signal routed or silent); and
   `tests/integration/test_valley_content.gd` expects the two new prompts and counts their flags' writers
   (GameSession, PlayerAppearance, and P44's tab for `appearance.tab_seen`).
9. **The beard's colour in the summary** is named for a beard with cards or a stubble of at least 0.5 ("Beard:
   none" stays "none" though its option keeps a 0.15 shadow).
10. **`--creator` without a matching mode** warns and starts nothing different; with one, the session stays on the
    title and P44's title opens the creator from `creator_start`.

## Known gaps (for later packages)

- Nothing here has met the real model: `test_giant_look_real`, `test_tip_matching`'s exported landmarks and
  `test_giant_shaders`' model frames (the hair bar's Face and Hair framings) and its per-frame cost skip with a note
  until P41's `scenes/player/giant_model.tscn` exists; the W25 merge runs them. The rendered check here ran on a
  stand-in of spheres and cards.
- `appearance.tab_seen` is written by nothing until P44's Appearance tab; until then the shrine prompt ends on its
  15 s timeout.
- `tools/check.sh` on this branch has no `--require` (P41's), so a rendered check without `xvfb-run` skips with a
  note rather than failing.
