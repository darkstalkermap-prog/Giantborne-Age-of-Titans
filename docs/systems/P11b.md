## What was built

- **The village faction** (`scripts/world/faction_state.gd`, `class_name FactionState`): Thornwick's
  standing toward the giant as WorldState flags (`faction.village.{warned,hostile,stood_down}`),
  static helpers to read them, and one shared hub that turns the player's blows into offences (the
  first warns, a second within 30 s turns the village hostile) and announces every change as
  `Events.faction_changed` with a HUD line, in Sim POST_RESOLVE (order 150).
- **Guards at peace** (EnemyActor and EnemyBrain, below `## @anchor P11b`, plus five one-line hook
  calls - see decision 2): an enemy with a `faction` follows its faction's standing. Neutral (or
  warned) it is team NEUTRAL (not lockable, area effects skip it, it cannot hurt the giant) and HOLDs
  at its post facing the giant with its guard up, never attacking and never aware; stood down it
  walks to its post and lowers its guard (STAND_DOWN); hostile it is team ENEMY with the normal AI
  and is told where the giant is at once. Posts are the formation markers of its own faction beside
  its home (`valley.marker.guard_post_1..4`).
- **Awe** (EnemyBrain, P11b section): the first time a unit perceives a target at least
  `tactics.awe_ratio` times its own height it loses `tactics.awe_morale_loss` morale, once per life
  (`morale_changed` reason "awe", never x1.5). Soldiers, archers and mages: 4.0 and 35 - a stage-3
  giant (7.92 m) awes them, a stage-2 giant (5.49 m) does not.
- **The brace counter** (`ability.soldier_brace`, the soldier's `tactics.hold_attack`): an
  outmatched soldier holding formation (P04's HOLD) thrusts at a giant whose capsule comes within
  3.0 m (`ai_max_range_m` 3.0 + the giant's radius), behind a 0.8 s line telegraph: 35 damage to a
  stage-2 giant (armour 0); blockable and parryable.
- **Civilians** (`scripts/actors/civilian.gd`, `class_name Civilian`; `scripts/ai/civilian_brain.gd`,
  `class_name CivilianBrain`): villagers that flee - when the giant rushes within 8 m (faster than
  3 m/s), when combat starts within 20 m, or when the village turns hostile or stands down - to the
  nearest *reachable* safe marker (snapped to the human navmesh, confirmed by a layer-1 path),
  re-path only after a path failure, and take shelter there (hidden, process disabled). The elder
  (role talker) never flees, refuses every blow (`protected`: IMMUNE, still an offence) and carries
  P13's `NpcTalker` (`dialogue.elder`).
- **The sparring post** (`scripts/actors/sparring_post.gd`, `class_name SparringPost`;
  `scripts/ai/sparring_brain.gd`, `class_name SparringBrain`; `enemy.sparring_post`, archetype
  training): immobile, unkillable (it resets to full health at 1), team ENEMY and lockable (threat
  0.1), no XP, no fury; it swings its padded arms every 3 s while the giant stands within 4 m - a
  circle telegraph (r 3 m) over a 0.8 s windup, then a parryable pulse, so blocking, parrying and
  dodging give BLOCKED, PARRIED and EVADED. It is never aware (it never defers a checkpoint) and it is
  not in the `enemies` group (decision 4). In Giant's Hollow (`encounter.hollow_sparring`, not
  budgeted) and in the sandbox.
- **The `npc` record type** (`scripts/core/schemas/npc_schema.gd`) and the enemy schema's
  `unkillable` and `training` fields (anchor P11b).
- **Data**: `npc.villager`, `npc.elder`, `enemy.sparring_post`, `ability.soldier_brace`,
  `ability.sparring_swing`, `weapon.sparring_arm`; the soldier's brace and awe, the archer's and the
  mage's awe; the encounters `encounter.village_guards`, `encounter.village_folk` and
  `encounter.hollow_sparring` (all from §4.4).
- **Scenes** (`tools/builders/npcs.gd`): `scenes/npcs/villager.tscn`, `scenes/npcs/elder.tscn`,
  `scenes/enemies/sparring_post.tscn`, `scenes/test/village_test.tscn`; the sandbox's sparring post
  (`tools/builders/sandbox.gd`).

## Public APIs

### FactionState (`scripts/world/faction_state.gd`, `class_name FactionState extends RefCounted`)

```gdscript
const HOOK_ORDER := 150; const OFFENCE_WINDOW_S := 30.0; const NO_TICK := -1000000000
const OFFENCE_OUTCOMES := [HIT, BLOCKED, PARRIED, IMMUNE, GUARD_BROKEN]
const ANNOUNCED := ["hostile", "stood_down"]          # flags announced when they are set
const TEXTS := {"village": {"warned", "hostile", "stood_down"}, "": {...}}   # HUD lines
static func flag_id(faction, state) -> String        # "faction.<faction>.<state>"
static func flag_set(faction, state) -> bool; static func is_hostile(f) / is_stood_down(f) / is_warned(f) -> bool
static func standing(faction) -> String               # "hostile" > "stood_down" > "warned" > "neutral"; "" without a faction
static func offended_faction(result: HitResult) -> String   # the faction a hit offends, or ""
static func offence_result(warned, last_tick, tick, window_ticks) -> String   # "warned" | "hostile" (pure)
static func text_for(faction, state) -> String; static func truthy(v) -> bool
static func hub() -> FactionState                     # the shared hub (created and connected on first use)
static func reset_for_tests() -> void                 # forgets the offences in memory
var history: Array                                    # [{"tick","faction","target","outcome","action","result"}]
func last_offence_tick(faction) -> int; func set_last_offence_tick(faction, tick) -> void   # tests
```

- **Offence**: `Events.hit_resolved` with an outcome in `OFFENCE_OUTCOMES`, not an area effect, from a
  PLAYER-team attacker, on a CombatEntity whose `faction` is set and not hostile. One blow (attack
  instance id) counts once, however many members it struck.
- **POST_RESOLVE (150)**: offences in order - `offence_result(is_warned, last offence tick, tick, 30 s)`:
  "warned" → `set_flag(warned, true)`, `faction_changed(tick, f, "warned")`, HUD line (kind "warn");
  "hostile" → `try_set_flag(hostile, true)`. Then announcements: every `flag_changed` that set
  `faction.<f>.hostile` or `.stood_down` truthy (from any writer: the second offence, the barricade's
  `on_destroyed_flags`, P15's intimidation choice) → `faction_changed(tick, f, state)` + HUD line
  ("bad" / "info").
- Never touches `quest.village.outcome` (only the cart barricade sets "force", decision 11).

### EnemyActor (below `## @anchor P11b`; hooks in `_ready` and `is_guarding`)

```gdscript
const FACTION_POST_RADIUS_M := 6.0
var faction_standing := ""               # "" (no faction), "neutral", "warned", "stood_down", "hostile"
func at_peace() -> bool                  # neutral, warned or stood down
func faction_post() -> Dictionary        # brain.post_resolver: {"post": Vector3, "face": Vector3}
```

- `_p11b_ready()` (from `_ready`): a member (`faction != ""`) connects `Events.faction_changed`,
  creates the FactionState hub and applies `FactionState.standing(faction)`; every
  `faction_changed` of its faction re-applies it. Standing → team (NEUTRAL at peace, ENEMY hostile),
  tokens released at peace, `brain.set_faction_mode("neutral" | "stood_down" | "")`; turning hostile in
  the game also reports the giant to the brain (a certain noise).
- `is_guarding()`: at peace, `_guarding_at_peace()` (neutral or warned: guard up while in HOLD, not
  acting, having seen the giant within `ai.memory_s`, with the stamina; stood down: never); otherwise
  the archetype's `_is_guarding()` as before.
- Posts: the nearest free formation marker (group `ai_formation_points`) whose `faction` equals the
  member's, within 6 m of its home, claimed with P04's `formation_holder` meta (so a hostile member's
  formation HOLD keeps it); else the home position. The post faces the way the actor faced when it
  entered the tree.

### EnemyBrain (below `## @anchor P11b`; hooks in `perceive`, `on_noise` and `decide`)

```gdscript
const POST_ARRIVE_M := 0.4; const POST_LEAVE_M := 1.0; const POST_REFRESH_S := 1.0
var faction_mode := ""                   # "", "neutral", "stood_down"
var post_resolver := Callable()          # () -> {"post", "face"}; unset: stays where it made peace
var awed := false                        # awe already cost morale (once per life)
func set_faction_mode(mode: String, tick := -1) -> void
func at_peace() -> bool; func peace_post() -> Dictionary
```

- `perceive()`: awe for every unit (`_check_awe`), then, at peace, it only remembers where the target
  is (to face it) and stays unaware.
- `on_noise()`: at peace, a blow only turns it toward the attacker; nothing makes it aware.
- `decide()`: at peace (after DEAD / STAGGERED and the morale recovery), it walks to its post (0.4 m to
  arrive, 1.0 m to leave), then HOLDs facing the giant while it saw it within `ai.memory_s` (else the
  post's facing) or STANDs DOWN facing the post's way; no attacks, no tokens, never flees.
- `set_faction_mode("")` returns to IDLE (from HOLD / STAND_DOWN); making peace drops the target,
  awareness, tokens, a formation hold and any flight.

- Children: `CombatEntity` (team NEUTRAL), `NavAgent`, `Visual` (ActorRig) with `Body` and `LodProxy`,
  `Anchors/Torso`, `Hurtboxes/{Head,Torso,Legs}`, `BodyShape`; the elder also `Talker` (NpcTalker,
  radius 4 m, priority 40).
- Perception (5 Hz): the giant's position, distance and horizontal speed, and the faction flags (read
  every time). Noise: `ability_started` of an attack (light, heavy, ability, enemy attack, ultimate) by
  anyone, and every landed `hit_resolved`, measured from where it happened.
- Shelter: the candidate safe markers (faction "" or its own), nearest first; each is snapped to the
  region of its nav layer (`region_get_closest_point`, within 2 m of the marker) and accepted when
  `map_get_path(..., nav_layers)` ends within 1 m of that point. The agent gets the target once; no
  progress for 2 s is a path failure: the next marker. At the marker (within 1 m): hidden and
  `process_mode = DISABLED` (Sim skips it; the director's LOD leaves it alone).
- Spawned while its faction is already hostile or stood down, a villager is put straight at its
  shelter once the navigation map answers (no crowd running home after every reload).

## Decisions and deviations

1. **Doc name** `docs/systems/P11b.md` (the task); §3.13 names `P11b-village-factions.md`.
2. **Hooks outside the anchors.** The faction behaviour must act inside functions that already exist,
   so the shared enemy files carry five one-line calls into the P11b sections: `EnemyActor._ready`
   (`_p11b_ready()`), `EnemyActor.is_guarding` (`_guarding_at_peace()` at peace), `EnemyBrain.perceive`
   (`_p11b_perceive`: awe, and no awareness at peace), `EnemyBrain.on_noise` (`_p11b_heard_in_peace`)
   and `EnemyBrain.decide` (`_p11b_decide`). Everything else is below `## @anchor P11b`. No other wave-7
   package edits these files (P14's hotspots are elsewhere), so the merge stays clean; the guards'
   class (SoldierEnemy) is unchanged.
3. **FactionState is a shared hub, not a node.** No package owns `project.godot` (an autoload) or a
   level node this wave, and the hub must exist wherever a faction member does: the first member
   creates it (`FactionState.hub()`), and it lives for the run (a static RefCounted with its Sim hook
   and Events connections). Its only memory is the last offence tick per faction; it counts only while
   the `warned` flag is set, so a new game (or a test's `WorldState.clear()`) starts from a first
   offence. Flags stay WorldState's, as §2.3 says (`set_flag` for warned, `try_set_flag` for hostile).
4. **The sparring post is not in the `enemies` group.** EnemyActor puts every actor there, and the M1
   soak (`Autopilot.pick_soak_target`, P14's file this wave) attacks the nearest living "enemy" first:
   an unkillable post near the sandbox dummies would have held it forever (no fence break, no resets).
   The post joins `sparring_posts` and `training_targets` instead. It is still team ENEMY and lockable,
   and its brain is never aware, so nothing that reads `enemies` (checkpoint deferral, the soak, the
   debug panel) needs it; tools find it by entity id or group.
5. **The sandbox post stands at (−22, 40)**, west of the test wall near the wall dummy, not beside the
   front dummy (0, 32): there it sat on the obstacle route's path (`test_obstacle_route` stuck at
   (−5.9, 26.4)). The new spot is more than 9 m from every leg of that route and about 20 m from the
   soak's paths; the 120 s soak's numbers are unchanged (attacks 41, hits 32, kills 4, fence breaks 2,
   debris 10, loot 2).
6. **The brace reaches 4 m, and its telegraph is 4 m long** (plan: a 3.5 m line). The brace starts when
   the giant's *capsule* is within 3.0 m of the soldier's centre, but the giant's leg hurtbox lies
   inside its capsule (at stage 2 its near face is 0.675 m behind the capsule surface: 3.675 m from the
   soldier; 3.89 m at stage 3), so a 3.5 m strike could never touch a giant at its own trigger
   distance. The pulse (a 50° sector) and its decal both reach 4 m (P11a's rule: the hit area equals
   the telegraph).
7. **"A light swing from 4.5 m does not trigger it"** holds, but the stage-2 lights do not reach 4.5 m
   against a 1.8 m soldier: measured, light 1–3 reach about 4.0–4.2 m from the giant's centre, the
   heavy 5 m and more (the M2 arc tuning, P04/P05; §4.8's "4.8–5.0 m" predates it). With the plan's
   numbers a giant striking with lights from the edge of their reach (≈ 4.1 m) is inside the brace
   reach (4.2 m at stage 2), while the heavy strikes from beyond it unpunished. The test therefore
   checks the light from 4.5 m (no brace) and a heavy from 4.5 m (it lands, no brace). P17b can tune
   `ai_max_range_m` (2.8 would leave the lights' edge outside).
8. **GUARD_BROKEN is an offence too** (plan: HIT, BLOCKED, PARRIED or IMMUNE): a heavy that breaks a
   neutral guard's guard connected as surely as a blocked one. EVADED and IGNORED are not.
9. **One blow, one offence.** A sweep that clips two villagers in the same swing warns once (offences
   are counted per attack instance), so "strike again" means a second blow.
10. **Neutral guards keep their guard up while they watch the giant** (§3.13 says "guard down" only for
    standing down); they never become aware, so they never defer a checkpoint, never alert their
    encounter and are never leashed for watching. A blow only turns them to the attacker.
11. **Hostility wins over standing down** (`standing()`): a giant that intimidates the village and then
    strikes it twice within 30 s fights its guards.
12. **The elder never flees** (role talker: she stays to talk, including the hostile node P15 writes),
    and her `dialogue` is an id, not a checked reference: `dialogue.elder` is P15's, and until it ships
    the talker says "They have nothing to say." (P13's NpcTalker). A misspelt id is P15's content test's
    to catch (§3.17 "content integrity").
13. **Sheltered villagers are hidden** as well as stopped (they went indoors), and a villager spawned
    into a village that is already hostile or stood down goes straight to its shelter.
14. **The npc record carries no faction**: a member's faction comes from its encounter spawn (or the
    scene's default "village"), like the guards'.
15. **Encounter files.** One record per JSON file (as P10a, P10b and P11a): the plan's
    `encounters_village.json` is `encounter_village_guards.json` + `encounter_village_folk.json`. This
    package also ships `encounter_hollow_sparring.json` (§4.4's Hollow post, which needs its archetype),
    and the post's weapon record `weapon.sparring_arm` (an enemy record needs a weapon).
16. **Existing tests changed** (the soldier now has a hold attack, as §3.13 asks):
    `test_enemy_brain_v2.test_formation_hold_slot_reach_and_hold_attack` gives its first soldier no
    hold attack for the "with its own attacks" case (the hold attack is checked further down, and in
    `test_awe_brace`); `test_schemas_v2.test_untelegraphed_enemy_attack_warns` writes the brace record
    beside its copy of the soldier (the copy references it). No assertion was weakened.
17. **The village guards do not count toward the active-combatant budget** (`counts_toward_budget`
    false; §4.4 leaves it at the default). Budgeted, the six neutral guards held half of the director's
    12 slots wherever the giant stood within 150 m of (70, −20) - the quarry, the fields, the bridge,
    the whole ruins and the Kingsfall antechamber - so the courtyard fight (8) could not spawn in full
    and, with the forest and field ogres still active, the Ogre King could not spawn at all. P10b's and
    P11a's Valley suites caught it (`test_valley_walk`: the road patrol stayed dormant;
    `test_valley_boss`: the king did). A neutral garrison is not a combatant; turned hostile, the six
    fight outside the budget (the `force` branch only). The director could count faction members only
    while they are team ENEMY: a change for the director's owner (P14 this wave holds it for tuning
    constants only).
