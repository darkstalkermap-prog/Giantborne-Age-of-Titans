# P64 — Brutes and beasts in the game; the Web tiers and performance for people

Plan: `docs/plans/M11_PLAN.md` §3.17 (and §2.8-§2.12, §0.3's Part C rows). Wave W37, lane A, beside P65 (the wave's
asset writer). Built on 21 November 2026 on `pkg/P64` from `ee99670`, on the shared container (4 CPUs, no GPU:
llvmpipe natively, SwiftShader in Chromium), with the other lane's asset work running beside it.

The goal: the ogre, the troll, the whelp, the Ogre King, the Troll Matriarch, the wolf and the dire wolf are P63's and
P65a's realistic creatures in play instead of their box rigs, the wolves with a fur shell; the Web build measured for
the people and the creatures. Gameplay is unchanged (the soak's AUTOPILOT_RESULT and the determinism gates with the
creatures on and off).

## What was built

### 1. The brutes and the bosses (`scripts/presentation/brute_rig.gd`, `BruteRig`)

A `BruteRig` is an `ActorRig`: the pose brain stays and poses the box rig's pivots (hidden) on the frame clock, counts
the footfalls and keeps flashes and tints. Where the people are drawn (`PersonRig.layers_enabled()`: a display,
`--skin`, `enable_for_tests()`; never `--no-skin`) and the record's `body.rig` is not `"box"`, the box rig's meshes hide
and the creature's glTF shows under `Visual/Body/Model` (turned 180 degrees, scaled from its file's height to the
record's `height_m`: the ogre 1.0, the King 2.5, the troll 1.0, the Matriarch 1.636, the whelp 0.595):

- **The clips on the brain's clock** (P63's `<kind>_clips.glb`, the tracks retargeted to the model's own skeleton so
  the whelp takes the troll's clips): `walk` and `run` (above the mean of the record's walk and run speeds) on the
  distance walked - one turn of the clip a stride of the clip (the manifest's gait stride times the model's scale), so
  a planted foot holds its place (`test_brute_rig`: under 2 cm a frame at touch-down and lift-off while the body moves
  3.7 cm, 0 in between); the smash, the slam and the stomp play `slam` from the action's phase - the windup raises
  both fists over the head (the clip to 0.55 s), the active phase brings them to the ground in front (to 0.8 s), the
  recovery stands up - the box rig's timing exactly; a phase's roar, a pulse of morale and the King's charge's windup
  play `roar` the same way; the sweeps, the combos, the frenzy, the whelp's swipe and the whelp throw stand in `brace`
  with both arms put where the brain's arm poses point by `PersonRig.solve_arm` (the torso's twist on the spine), so
  each wind-up and swing is the box rig's pose on the brain's clock; a block or a guard braces; held in a fist the
  brute braces with its arms pushing at the fist, shaking; dead it plays `death` on its own clock (the clip falls the
  body: the brain's fall is left out, as the people's); else `idle`. Clips cross-fade over 0.2 s.
- **The gear**: the bosses' gear meshes (`ogre_king_gear`: mantle, belt, crown; `troll_matriarch_gear`: hide cloak and
  bone crown) on the same skeleton in the same material; a club in the right fist along the hand's line (a gnarled limb
  made once a process, vertex coloured in the person shader's gear mode; the King's banded in gold) at the box club's
  length (1.6 m at the ogre's 3.4 m).
- **Paint**: `person.gdshader` on the creature's atlas with the record's palette (`body.palette` over the kind's:
  the hide, the moss, the loincloth, the King's mantle); a flash and a status tint (the troll's regeneration and
  burning pulses) reach the body, the gear and the club.
- Draw calls: the body and the club (2), the bosses 3 with their gear; one shadow draw a cascade (the body).

### 2. The wolves (`scripts/presentation/skinned_wolf_rig.gd`, `SkinnedWolfRig`)

A `QuadrupedRig` (the brain poses the box pivots, counts the paw falls, keeps flashes and tints) that shows P63's wolf
(P65a's head) at the variant's size (the wolf 1, the dire wolf 2) and palette (the coat, the marks, the eyes' amber or
ember), driven with no clip. **`skinned_quadruped_rig.gd` was not edited**: the wolf has its own copy of the horse's
gait plan, made for a wolf, so the parallel fix of the horse's gallop does not move it:

- each paw is in stance or swing in the world: a planted paw never slides (`test_wolf_rig`: 0 at 1, 3 and 8 m/s), a
  swing lands it where its stance is centred under its hip or shoulder; the walk (four beats), the trot (diagonal
  pairs) and the rotary gallop blend by speed;
- the wolf is the horse's dynamic similar (every length the horse's times 0.5 x the size, every speed by its square
  root) with a quicker step (`HZ_GAIN` 1.3: a trot of 3 m/s at 3.1 strides a second, a gallop of 8 m/s at about 4) and
  a shoulder blade that slides with the foreleg (up to 7 cm), so every standing leg reaches its paw (within 2 cm);
- the back flexes and extends at the gallop, the body bobs and leans into turns, the neck nods; the bite's windup opens
  the jaw (`wolf_poses.glb`'s `jaw_open`) as the head rises and its active phase snaps it shut; the lunge's dash takes
  the `lunge` pose (forelegs reaching, the jaw open) and the paws plant again where it lands; dead, held by the scruff
  or thrown, the legs hang at rest under the brain's roll.
- **The fur shell** (`assets/shaders/fur_shell.gdshader`): P63's `wolf_shell` (1,800 triangles) as twelve stacked
  copies in one surface made once a process (each copy's layer in UV2), skinned to the wolf's bones: one draw call. A
  layer is pushed out along the normal by its share of the fur's length (`wolf_fur.png`'s B: long on the ruff, the
  back, the thighs and the tail; none on the pads), combed along the map's flow (RG) and sagging a little; a strand
  grows where a hash of its cell says so, thinning to the tip; alpha scissor (no sorting); the colour is the coat's
  atlas and band colour, darker at the roots. Off at the `low` graphics preset (the Web), beyond 30 m x the size, and
  it casts no shadow. Draw calls: the coat and the fur (2; 1 at `low`).

### 3. The slots (`scripts/presentation/creature_slots.gd`, `CreatureSlots`) and the far meshes

Plan §2.10's "others": at most 8 brutes and wolves skinned and animated at once (`--others-cap=N`); the bosses always
hold one; then the striking, the staggered, the falling and the held; then the nearest the camera (a holder keeps its
slot against a newcomer 15 % nearer). Without a slot, or farther from the camera than the record's `render_lod_m`, a
creature shows its far mesh (P63's 300-triangle idle pose; the wolf's 150) unanimated; a corpse that loses its slot
keeps its last pose. AI LOD tier 2 shows the far mesh in `Visual/LodProxy`. The cavalry's horses keep their own
throttle. `PEOPLE_STATS` gains `others` (skinned frames, the most at once, the far frames, the cap).

### 4. The data and the scenes

- `data/enemies/{ogre,ogre_king,troll,troll_whelp,troll_matriarch,wolf,dire_wolf}.json` gain `body`: `creature`
  (ogre, troll, wolf), `variant` (ogre, troll, whelp; wolf, dire_wolf), the bosses' `outfit` (ogre_king,
  troll_matriarch) and palette overrides (the King's darker skin and red mantle, the whelp's paler and the Matriarch's
  darker skin). `scripts/core/schemas/person_role_schema.gd`'s `body` takes `creature` and `outfit`; a body names a
  role or a creature (one of them).
- `tools/builders/heavy.gd` (the ogre's body plan, which `big.gd`'s trolls share), `wolf.gd` and `big.gd`'s dire wolf
  script their Visual with the new rigs; every gameplay node and the box rig stay (the brain and the fallback).

### 5. The Web: the warm-up, the people's check, the tier

- **The warm-up** (`scripts/presentation/people_warm_up.gd`): the first time a loading screen draws its warm-up frames
  (`Main.hold_until_drawn`, with a display only) a skinned soldier, a far mesh, a crowd instance and a wolf with its
  shell stand 5 m before the camera under the level's own lights, so the people's and the creatures' shader variants
  are made behind "Loading...".
- **`tools/web/people_check.mjs`** (and its test `test_people_check.mjs`, run by `check.sh --web-tools` without a
  browser): the `Web (test)` build in Chromium with `--ui-probe --people-probe` (`scripts/debug/people_web_probe.gd`,
  `PeopleWebProbe`): the formation test level, then forty soldiers 25 m before the giant (the mark
  `first_formation`), then five wolves and a dire wolf 12 m before him (`first_wolf`); the village test level as it
  stands. Each view's draw calls (a failure over 250), primitives, texture and buffer memory, the people skinned,
  crowd and far, the creatures skinned and far, the people's CPU a frame (a miss over 2.5 ms); each mark's first
  frames against the median (a miss over 3x); the renderer process's peak (a miss over 1.4 GB); 0 console errors.
- **The people tier**: see "Measurements"; the `low` preset (the Web) draws no fur shell.

### 6. The people sheet (`tools/character_sheet.gd --sheet=c`)

Three pages from the actors themselves (AI off) in a stage of the world's light with a soldier for scale: c1 the
ogre, the troll and the whelp (front, three-quarter, side, the head, walking, an attack's wind-up), c2 the King and
the Matriarch with their gear (front, back, three-quarter, side, the head, a wind-up), c3 the wolf and the dire wolf
with the fur shell and without. The pages are not committed (the report lists them).

**The people's tier**: at `low` (the Web) twelve people are skinned (`PersonRig.LOW_SKINNED`), not sixteen; the shell
is off. The first Web run with sixteen measured 4.95 ms (the machine busy with renders beside it); with twelve and a
quieter machine 1.70 ms. The six-layer shell of the plan's tier was not needed: the brief turns the shell off at `low`.

**Not met**: a creature costs 0.26-0.47 ms a frame natively in the editor binary on a loaded machine, of which about
0.18 ms is the box brain the rig keeps (the box rigs cost the same before) and 0.10 ms the clip's mixer; a pack of six
wolves adds about 1 ms on the Web. The slots cap the creatures at eight; no budget names them.
