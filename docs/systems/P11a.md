Wave 6, lane A (beside P13). Branch `pkg/P11a`. Plan: `docs/plans/M2_M5_PLAN.md` §3.12, with the
contracts in §2.4 (`boss_engaged`, `boss_phase_changed`, `boss_defeated`), §2.6.1 (`force_action`),

## What was built

- **The ogre** (`enemy.ogre`, the fifth role): a 3.4 m brute with two super-armoured strikes behind
  long windups - the smash (a ground pulse all round it, circle telegraph r 3.5 m) and the sweep (a
  120° sector in front, cone telegraph r 4 m) - and long recoveries, the punish window: super armour
  does not cover them and poise taken there counts x3.5, so one stage-1 heavy staggers it
  (35 x 1.08 x 3.5 = 132 >= 120). Slow turns (90°/s), the large nav layer, ignores morale and Fear,
  grabbable with both hands at stage 3. `OgreEnemy`, `OgreBrain`, `scenes/enemies/ogre.tscn`.
- **The Ogre King** (`enemy.ogre_king`, archetype boss): 8.5 m, 1,500 health, armour 30, poise 150
  (x3.5 in its recoveries), the giant nav layer. Phase 1 (above 50 %) strictly alternates the two-hit
  combo (blockable, parryable; combo 1 chains into combo 2) and the slam (circle r 5, unblockable,
  unparryable); at 50 % it announces phase 2 once and is forced into a 1.5 s roar (super armour, a
  circle telegraph, no damage); phase 2 cycles combo, slam, charge. The charge: a 0.9 s windup that
  turns toward the giant at 90°/s, then a committed 15 m/s run without steering for up to 6 s that
  ends against the arena wall or a pillar in a forced 2.5 s wall recovery (the punish window), or on
  its cap in its own 1.4 s recovery; its hit is the `body_sweep` shape. Immune to Grab and Fear,
  ignores morale. `OgreBoss`, `OgreBossBrain`, `scenes/enemies/ogre_king.tscn`.
- **Boss phases and attack sequences, generic** (EnemyBrain and EnemyActor, below `## @anchor P11a`):
  any enemy record with a `boss` block gets phases that only advance, sequence-driven attack choice
  (with range bands, facing rules and a 2 s patience), the phase announcement and forced enter
  action, and `chain_next` follow-ups for enemy attacks. The schema's `boss` block (enemy schema,
  anchor P11a) with its semantic checks.
- **Lock anchors** (spec 5): `CombatEntity.lock_anchors()` / `lock_anchor_position(viewer_height)`;
  the camera's lock framing, the lock's line of sight, its candidates and the HUD's lock marker all
  use it, so at stages 1-2 the camera frames a knee of the 8.5 m king and never looks steeply up.
- **The arena** (`BossArena`, `scripts/world/boss_arena.gd`): a safe exit before engagement; on
  entering `valley.area.arena_engage` the gate closes behind the giant (never on it), intruders are
  removed (through the EncounterDirector), the king is told where the giant is and `boss_engaged` is
  emitted; the king's death opens the gate, sets `world.ogre_king.defeated` and emits
  `boss_defeated` (GameSession's checkpoint trigger). In the Valley at `Kingsfall/BossArena`
  (`tools/builders/valley.gd` + the layout's `arena.boss_arena`), and on the new test level
  `scenes/test/boss_arena_test.tscn`.
- **The boss bar** (`ui/hud/boss_bar.gd`): top-centre; the boss's name, a health bar notched at each
  phase threshold, one pip per phase. `Hud` hides its target frame while the bar shows the locked
  boss.
- **Data**: the two enemies, eight actions, two weapons, three encounters (forest ogre, field ogre,
  the Kingsfall boss); the builder `tools/builders/heavy.gd`.
- **Combat seams**: super armour holds through windup and active only (the recovery is the punish
  window); Titan Surge's +25 % poise applies to the poise a parry deals (the M2-gate follow-up).

### EnemyBrain (below `## @anchor P11a`)

```gdscript
signal boss_phase_entered(phase: int, phase_id: String, enter_action: String)
const SEQUENCE_PATIENCE_S := 2.0; const SEQUENCE_LINE_FACING_DEG := 45.0
const SEQUENCE_BAND_MARGIN_M := 1.0; const SEQUENCE_CHASE_M := 6.0; const SEQUENCE_LOG_SIZE := 64
var boss_phases: Array; var phase_index := 0; var sequence_index := 0
var sequence_log: Array[String]          # consumed entries: the id struck with, or "skip:<id>"
func init_boss_phases(record) -> void    # archetype brains call it from _init
func has_boss_phases() / boss_phase() (1-based) / boss_phase_count() / phase_record()
func phase_attacks() -> Array; func phase_sequence() -> Array
func update_boss_phase(health_frac: float, tick := -1) -> int   # newly entered phase or 0; never back
func current_sequence_entry() -> String; func advance_sequence(performed: bool) -> void
func choose_phase_attack() -> String     # the entry, or a weighted pick without a sequence
func sequence_band(action_id) -> Vector2 # ai_min/max_range_m (from the target's capsule surface)
static func entry_facing_deg(action_id) -> float   # circle telegraph 180, line 45, else 35
func _decide_sequence(...) -> void       # the engaged states of a sequence brain
func sequence_path_failed() -> bool      # a path failure while chasing a band skips the entry
```

- `update_boss_phase` picks the deepest phase whose `health_frac_below` the fraction is at or below;
  on a change the sequence restarts, a pending request is dropped, the brain waits in ATTACK for the
  enter action (when the phase has one) and `boss_phase_entered` is emitted - once per phase.
- `_decide_sequence`: ATTACK waits for the strike (and any chained follow-up or forced action), then
  RECOVER pauses `ai.strike_pause_min_s..max_s`; APPROACH works the current entry: inside its band and
  facing it (per `entry_facing_deg`) it takes a token and strikes; otherwise it closes in (run, to
  just inside the band, never into the target) or backs off (run, to band.x + 1 m). The entry is
  skipped after `SEQUENCE_PATIENCE_S` out of its band - counted only while the target is at most
  `SEQUENCE_CHASE_M` beyond the band (walking up to the giant skips nothing) - or at once on a path
  failure while chasing it.

### EnemyActor (below `## @anchor P11a`)

```gdscript
var phase_changes := 0
func install_boss_hooks() -> void   # connects boss_phase_entered; adds every phase action to the library
func update_boss_phase() -> void    # feeds the brain the health fraction (living actors)
func queue_chain_next() -> void     # in the recovery of an action with chain_next, requests it every tick
```

- A phase entered: `Events.boss_phase_changed(tick, entity_id, phase)`, then
  `controller.force_action(enter_action)`.
- The chain: requested every tick of the action's recovery, the follow-up waits in the controller's
  input buffer and starts on the tick the action ends; a stagger, a death or a forced action (each
  clears the buffer) cancels it.

## Decisions and deviations

1. **Doc name** `docs/systems/P11a.md` (the task and §1.1 item 5); §3.12 names `P11a-ogre-boss.md`.
2. **Encounter files.** `GameData` loads one record per JSON file, so `encounters_ogres.json` is three
   files: `encounter_forest_ogre.json`, `encounter_field_ogre.json`, `encounter_kingsfall_boss.json`.
3. **Super armour ends at the recovery** (`combat_controller.gd`, P01's file, outside this package's
   list; no other W6 package edits it). The plan wants super-armoured ogre strikes **and** a heavy in
   the recovery that staggers the ogre; the M1 controller kept super armour through the whole
   action, so a poise break in the recovery was absorbed. Super armour now covers windup and
   active; the recovery is the punish window. Nothing else used super armour.
4. **Strikes are pulses that match their telegraphs.** The smash and the slam are cylinder pulses
   (r 3.5 / r 5 round the actor), the sweep and the combo sector pulses (120°, r 4 / r 6.5): with no
   animation the decal is the only cue, and the hit area now equals it exactly. Pulses hit once per
   target; the sector filter keeps the sweep and the combo to the front.
5. **The combo chain** runs through the controller's input buffer (`queue_chain_next`), because
   `chain_next` only chains `light` actions in the controller. The brain counts combo 1 + combo 2 as
   one decision.
6. **The charge passes through actors** while it runs (its collision mask drops the actors layer):
   otherwise it stopped on the giant (kinematic bodies do not push each other) and ended on its cap
   instead of against stone. Its one hit shoves the giant aside off the line.
7. **Charge turn rate.** The windup's 90°/s is `OgreBoss.CHARGE_TURN_DEG_S` (the walk turn rate is
   the record's 80); `tracking_fraction` 1.0 makes it track the whole windup. The line telegraph
   follows the facing during the windup (TelegraphVisuals) and stays where the run began.
8. **`charge_min_range_m` 8** is the charge's `ai_min_range_m` 8 (from the giant's capsule surface, like
   every band); the record does not repeat it in `tactics`.
9. **Sequence decisions.** A decision is a consumed entry: struck with, or skipped. The patience
   counts only while the giant is at most 6 m beyond the entry's band (a long walk-up must not burn
   entries: the first live fight opened with a slam because the combo had been skipped on the way
   in); a path failure while chasing skips at once. The facing an entry needs comes from its
   telegraph shape (the slam hits all round; the charge turns during its windup).
10. **The roar** is an `enemy_attack` with `hit_shape: none`, a 1.0 s windup and a 0.5 s active phase
    (1.5 s in all) under super armour, with a circle telegraph (r 9), so the phase change is never an
    unseen event; it has no recovery, so no punish window. The wall recovery (windup 0, active 0.05 s,
    recovery 2.45 s) is all recovery, so the x3.5 applies through it; neither has a hit.
11. **The arena's gate closes behind the giant, never on it.** The threshold trigger is entered while
    the giant's capsule still overlaps the gateway; closing the gate then would have overlapped it
    (and could have pushed it out). Engagement therefore waits until the capsule is clear of the
    gateway on the arena side; stepping back out instead calls it off (the safe exit). The intruders
    are removed at that moment, so `boss_defeated` is emitted at the king's death ("after the arena
    is clear"). The arena bounds are the ring (inner radius + 2 m) and the antechamber rectangle.
12. **The king faces the entrance** (yaw 0; §4.4's table says 180, which faces away from the gate in
    the layout's Godot yaw convention). It does not see the antechamber (sight 30 m, the gate 55 m
    away); the engagement tells it where the giant is.
13. **Ogre morale.** §4.4's "Ignores fear" is both `ignores_morale` and the Fear status immunity. Its
    stagger is 1.2 s, 2.0 s against a giant at least twice its height (the stage-3 grab window); the
    king's is 1.5 s with a 3 s immunity (no stun-lock).
14. **Combo 2's poise is 30** (the plan gives none): two combo hits (46 + 46 x 1.55 scale) stay below
    the stage-2 giant's 100, so the combo alone never staggers it.
15. **Lock anchors.** `player_targeting.gd`'s anchor is at the end of the file; the four call sites
    above it (`is_visible`, `gather_candidates`, `switch_target`, `maintain_lock`) now call
    `lock_point()`, which lives below the anchor. The plan's "target line" is read as the HUD's lock
    marker; projectiles still aim at the torso (`player_attack.gd` is not this package's file).
16. **The test arena** (`boss_arena_test.tscn`) is a plain Node3D root (no LevelRoot, no director): the
    king is placed in the scene, so its `BossArena.encounter_id` is "" and intruders are freed; the
    director path is covered in the Valley (slow suite).
17. **Ogre, data:** the ogre's strikes keep the record's `uses_weapon` (multiplier 1.0); the king's
    charge does not use the weapon (a body blow).
18. **Unused anchors:** AttackRunner's and the action schema's P11a anchors (the body sweep is registered
    on the boss's own runner, as §2.7.2 says; no action field was needed).
19. **No existing test changed.** Check counts that follow what exists: `test_anchors` (compiles the
    new scripts), `test_scene_kit` (+1: the heavy builder), `test_schemas_v2` (iterates the records).

  recovery a reel; no audio.
- The king has no grapple window (deferred, §0.4); the knees are lock anchors only (no limb poise).
