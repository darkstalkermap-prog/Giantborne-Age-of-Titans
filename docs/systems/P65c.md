# P65c — The third look pass: the wolf's mouth, the bosses' cloaks, the whelp, the women, the mail, the grips

Plan: `docs/plans/M11_PLAN.md` §3.18 (P65, of which this is the third part), with P64's list of asset changes. Built on
21 November 2026 on `pkg/P65c` from `66826f1`, on the shared container (4 CPUs, no GPU: llvmpipe), beside the code
lane. The owner's bar: people and creatures "look and feel realistic and not clunky", natural proportions.

Every judgement below was made from renders before and after with the game's actors (SkinnedWolfRig with its fur
shell, BruteRig with the bosses' gear, PersonModel for the people) in the formation test level's light, llvmpipe. The
frames are not committed (the package's report lists them).

## 1. The wolf's head (`kit/quadruped.py`: `wolf_head`, `wolf_flews`, `wolf_mouth_cut`, `wolf_jaw_share`, `wolf_teeth`, `wolf_coat`, `wolf_fur_length`)

| Defect (reproduced) | State |
| --- | --- |
| No teeth; the bite's open jaw stretched the closed skin into a web from the lip to the chin | **done**: the lower jaw is its own body inside a gap (`WOLF_GAP_M` 9 mm) that runs round its top and sides from the front to the corner of the mouth (`WOLF_CORNER_U`, under the eye), tapering shut there; every point under the gap's middle is the jaw bone's, every point over it the head's (`weights`' `jaw_share` hook, the wolf's only), so `jaw_open` opens a real mouth: the gums, the palate and the tongue in a dark mouth colour (`MOUTH_RGB`, no band), the lips black. A teeth piece (180 triangles, its own tile) on the head and the jaw: three incisors, the canine, two premolars, the carnassial and a molar above; three incisors, the canine and three cheek teeth below. Shut, every tooth lies inside the body or the gap (`TestLookPassC`) |
| The mouth's line read as a smile | **done**: the upper lip's fullness (`wolf_flews`) is a cone each side whose lower edge runs straight and a little lower toward the corner (P65a's ellipsoid rose toward it) |
| The fur shell spiked on the ears | **done**: the fur map's length is 0.02 on the ears, the muzzle (from the stop forward), the lips and the mouth, and the shins (under 36 cm); the shell has no triangle over the mouth's gap (a bridging triangle would hide the open mouth): 1,685 triangles (1,800) |

Kept: the 25 bones and their rest, the poses (`jaw_open` volume 1.000, the lowest point 1 mm), the palettes, the dire
wolf as the same mesh at x 2. **Triangles**: `wolf` 4,772 (body 4,500, eyes 60, fur 32, teeth 180) of 6,000.
**Not reached**: seen from the side with the jaw open the upper teeth are mostly behind the hanging upper lip (only the
canine's tip shows below it; the lower teeth, the tongue and the open gape read); raising the lip line to show them
let the closed mouth show a toothy slit at rest (tried and dropped).

## 2. The bosses' cloaks (`kit/brute.py`: `MANTLE`, `mantle_pieces`, `collar_piece`)

- From behind both mantles read as boxes: `hang` pushed each column of the back out to the hunch above it, up to 30 cm.
  Now the back's cloth spans the spine's groove and the muscles' hollows (`hull_fill`, `bridge` 40 neighbour averages)
  and hangs at most `hang_most` (9 cm the King's, 10 the Matriarch's) off the back: it falls close from the shoulders.
- **The Troll Matriarch**: the hide reaches below the waist (`back_low` -7 cm) with a raggeder hem; the rolled sausage of
  hide round the shoulders is replaced by a flat fold lying on the shoulders behind the neck (`collar_flat` 0.5,
  `collar_back_only`: the fold thins to nothing toward the front, so the chest is bare under it). 2,152 triangles.
- **The Ogre King**: the ivory collar a lower, softer roll (`collar_flat` 0.7); the red mantle reaches the belt.
- **Left**: a small bright fold on the mantle's middle low on the back (both bosses, about the size of a hand at 3 m; it
  was there before as a crease); from the side the Matriarch's fold still shows over the shoulder's top.

## 3. The whelp's crotch (`kit/brute.py`: `crotch_of`, `kit/people_outfit.py`: `cull(protect=)`)

The gap was a hole: the troll's skin culled under its loincloth, carried onto the whelp's body, where the flaps no longer
covered it (the ground seen through the crotch). The skin of the crotch and the inner thighs (`CROTCH_KEEP_M`) is never
culled for the brutes. **Done** (front, back and side).

## 4. The younger women (`kit/people_roles.py`: `FACES`, the villagers; `kit/people_outfit.py`: `hair_piece`)

- `fair`, `fair_lean`, `fair_round` reweighted from the vendored targets only (no file added; the added targets stay at
  3.87 MB of 4): the jaw drop, the chin's width and prominence smaller, the eyes larger, the nose smaller, the lips
  fuller with a stronger cupid's bow, the head's age lower, the neck slimmer. A narrower head and a higher forehead were
  tried and dropped: the notables' shared mesh, decimated on Edda, took a crease down the nose and a peak in the hairline.
- `villager_d` and `villager_e` (the villagers' women) take `fair` and `fair_round` (they wore the men's `soft` and
  `round`).
- Long hair (`LONG_HAIR_CROWN_M`, `LONG_HAIR_WIDTH_M`): up to 9 mm fuller toward the crown and wider down the back to
  the shoulder blades, so from the front its mass shows beside the neck; a parting tried down the middle read as a bald
  wedge and was dropped.
- The lips' albedo rosier (0.80, 0.57, 0.60; every role, the men's too, a little).
- **Not reached**: the brows and the lashes stay as they were: the atlas is the role's, shared with its men (the notables'
  three, the villagers' three), so painting a woman's brows or lashes there would paint them on the men.

## 5. The mail (`kit/people_outfit.py`: `mail_over`, `unwrap_atlas`)

- **The red mark low on the back**: a decimated tunic triangle whose centre fell outside a slice's hull of the mail came
  through it; below the hips every cloth triangle wholly above the mail's hem and within its outline goes. **Done.**
- **The rings' break at the cleft**: a UV seam down the back's middle inherited from MakeHuman's halves (the high mesh's
  seams); the mail's only seams now are its front/back cut. **Done**: the rows run on across the cleft.
- The spanned back smoothed (`MAIL_SMOOTH_ITERS` 4) where the decimated mail kept a fold down the cleft: the dark crease
  is now a soft vertical fold; at 1 m its rings are stretched a little in the fold. **Mostly done.**

## 6. The brutes' grip landmark (`kit/brute.py`, `build_people.py`)

Each brute variant records, as the people's, the grip landmark in its hand bone's own frame (the file's metres: the
ogre's 3.4 m, the troll's 5.5 m and the whelp's 5.88 m before their game scale): **`grip_local_r`, `grip_axis_r`,
`grip_local_l`, `grip_axis_l`** — where a handle passes through the rest hand's curled fingers and along which line
(`people_body.grip_landmark`) — in `assets/creatures/manifest.json`, both under `<kind>.variants.<name>` and in the
index the game reads, `index.<kind>.variants.<name>`. The rig code is unchanged (BruteRig still uses its own fist
point and `CLUB_AXIS`); `PersonRig.grips_of` shows how the people's code reads the same keys.

## 7. Facets (`kit/brute.py`: `HEAD_DETAIL`)

The brutes' heads keep more of their triangles in the collapse (the whole head at 0.92, the scalp no longer reduced;
P65a 0.7 less a fifth on the scalp). **Partly**: the ogre's skull is a little rounder against the sky; the troll's cheeks
and nose barely change. The facets are the low mesh's silhouette within the 9,000 triangles (ogre 8,930, troll 8,935,
whelp 8,938); a real fix needs more triangles there or a smoothing of the low head after the collapse.

## How it looks against the bar

- **The wolf**: at the game camera a wolf, the bite now opens a red mouth with white teeth; at 3 m the closed muzzle reads
  natural (a straight dark lip line, no grin), the ears are smooth with dark tips, the open mouth shows the tongue, the
  lower teeth and the canines; the upper cheek teeth stay hidden behind the lip.
- **The Ogre King**: at the game camera fine; at 3 m the mantle hangs from the shoulders to the belt instead of a crate
  standing off the back, still broad because the hunch is.
- **The Troll Matriarch**: a hide cloak to below the waist with a fold of a collar, no roll; at 3 m from the side the fold
  shows over the shoulder.
- **The whelp**: no gap.
- **The women**: at the game camera they read as women; at 3 m somewhat softer and fuller-haired than before, still
  plain; the brows remain the shared atlas's.
- **The mail**: no red mark, rows continuous; a soft fold at the cleft.
- **The ogre's and the troll's heads**: little changed.
