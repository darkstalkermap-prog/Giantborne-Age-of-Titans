# P65a — The early look pass: the wolf, the brutes, the saddle, the people's faces

Plan: `docs/plans/M11_PLAN.md` §3.18 (P65, of which this is the early half), §2.8, §2.12. Built on 21 November 2026 on
`pkg/P65a` from `426cb08`, on the shared container (4 CPUs, no GPU: llvmpipe), beside P62's lane. The owner's bar:
natural, not clunky, judged at 3 m (the stage-1 giant is 6 foot and stands beside these) and from the game camera.

Every judgement below was made from renders with the game's `person.gdshader` (two lines of alpha scissor added in the
scratch harness for the strands' cards, as P63's frames did) in the formation test level's light: close views (front,
side, back, three-quarter, the head) and the game camera beside the giant at stages 1 and 4, before and after. The
frames are not committed (the package's report lists them).

## 1. The wolf (`kit/quadruped.py`: `WOLF`, `wolf_head`, `wolf_trunk`, `wolf_leg`, `paw`, `wolf_tail`, `fur_cards`,
`wolf_coat`)

P63's wolf failed at 3 m: a greyhound's head, a toothy grin, the ruff's cards as scattered dark flakes, a flat fan of a
tail, stick legs, thin spiked ears. Rebuilt in the same signed distance field:

- **The head**: a broad cranium (15.8 cm across) and wide cheekbones, brows over the eyes and a clear stop in front of
  them, the muzzle from the stop to the nose 13 cm of the head's 30 (about 43 %), deep and blunt (the upper muzzle and
  the upper lip's fullness over a smaller lower jaw tucked under it), the black nose pad over it. The `muzzle` and
  `chin` landmarks (the head's and the jaw's bone ends) moved with it; the bones and their names are P63's.
- **The mouth is closed**: no groove is cut; the lips are a soft dark line in the coat's own band where the upper
  muzzle's and the lower jaw's surfaces meet (`wolf_upper_muzzle`, `wolf_lower_jaw`), never across the front. No
  teeth are drawn (the jaw-open pose opens the skin; teeth remain for P65).
- **The ears**: rounded triangles 9 cm tall with thickness (a round cone flattened front to back, not side to side as
  P63's, which drew a fin), turned a little out, the hollow in front; dark backs and tips shading into the coat.
- **The ruff is volume**: a thicker neck, a mane over the nape and the withers, the throat's and the cheeks' ruff
  standing out behind the jaw, all the body's own surface. The ruff's, cheeks', breeches' and belly's cards are gone.
- **Legs and paws**: forearms, gaskins, wrists, hocks and the metapodials about 20 % thicker; larger paws (the hind
  a tenth smaller).
- **The tail**: a bushy brush of volume widest past its middle, and two crossed cards (32 triangles) a little wider
  than it, their strands' colour **the coat's under each texel** (`under_coat` in `wolf_bake`: the nearest point of
  the dense surface), so they blend. The tail's dark tip and top are the coat's band darkened (a band's edge on the
  256 px map is a saw-tooth).
- **The coat**: pale cheeks and upper lips under the eyes, a darker bridge to the nose, the eye rims lighter than
  P63's dark patches, the nose's roughness 0.72.
- Kept: the 25 bones, the palette bands (the cloth band the eyes' colour), the dire wolf as the same mesh at x 2,
  `wolf_shell` (1,800) and `wolf_fur.png`, `wolf_far` (150). **Triangles**: `wolf` 4,592 (body 4,500, eyes 60, fur
  32). Withers 0.807 m, length 1.437 m. Poses: volumes 0.973-1.016, the lowest point -1.8 cm (the trot), the skin's
  99th-percentile stretch at most 1.75 (the lunge's shoulder).

The horse's tail kept its own constants: the wolf's cards are `WOLF_TAIL_CARDS` (a first draft named them
`TAIL_CARDS` and cut the horse's tail to two cards; the test `TestLookPass` pins the horse's eight).

## 2. The brutes (`kit/brute.py`)

- **The legs' outline** (`leg_fill`): MakeHuman's muscle target at its most on the shortened legs bulged the thighs
  and the calves and pinched the knees between them (a diamond, front and back). Each leg's vertices are eased toward
  the leg's own smoothed outline (per sector of angle round the hip-knee-ankle line, the radius against the distance
  along it smoothed over 6 cm): a bulge keeps 45 % of its depth over the outline, a pinch 25 %, from a third of the
  thigh down to two thirds of the calf; the joints' helper cubes do not move.
- **The skull's top** keeps more triangles in the collapse (the scalp's detail weight); the silhouette is rounder but
  still shows a few facets from above.
- **The ear's dark blocks**: the decimated ear is far from the high one, so its texels read the ear's folds; round
  the ears (`EAR_SOFT_M`) the bake's normal is eased toward the low mesh's and its occlusion lightened.
- **The loincloth's "hole"**: it was the occlusion of the buttocks' cleft under the back flap, baked as a dark tear;
  the garments' occlusion is floored at 0.82 (`GARMENT_AO_FLOOR`).
- **The troll's face** (`TROLL_FACE`, `TROLL_SHAPE`): a wider mouth, a stronger and wider chin, a heavier brow ridge
  (3.4 cm, a wider band), the ears drawn out into long points (MPFB's ear mask: P63's rule pushed the head's widest
  vertices, which on the troll's broad head missed the ears). **The moss**: darker, in patches with clumps (sharper
  edges), with relief in the normal map, on the shoulders, the back and the scalp, never the face or the front's
  lower half.
- **The bosses' mantles** (`MANTLE`, `mantle_pieces`, `collar_piece`): a cloak over the shoulders built in the cape's
  slot (the shoulders' 4.6 cm stand-off made P63's puffy), hanging straight from the shoulder blades to the waist
  (`hang`: not drawn into the small of the back), shallow in front, its hem a little uneven; a thick collar roll swept
  along the mantle's neck opening. The King's mantle is his red with an ivory fur collar; the Matriarch's is hide with
  a hide collar. Skinned by the nearest body vertex's weights to the 23 bones, as the rest of the gear.
- **The King's belt**: a domed oval buckle plate at its front (`buckle_piece`), the belt's plates kept by a larger
  budget (560).
- **The crowns**: P63's loop named the points' tube radii `rr`, overwriting the head ring's radii, so every point after
  the first stood inside the head; now the gold crown has eight points, tall and short in turn, the front tallest, and
  the bone crown its nine.
- Kept: the 23 bones, the clips' interface (the troll's clips are re-keyed on its slightly changed rest skeleton),
  the variants. **Triangles**: ogre 8,927, troll 8,935, whelp 8,932 (at most 9,000); far meshes 297-300; gear: the
  King 2,656, the Matriarch 1,824 (`GEAR_TRIS_MAX` 3,000, P63 2,400). **Reach bands**: ogre 1.016, King 1.016, troll
  1.014, Matriarch 1.025, whelp 1.012 (within 5 %).

## 3. The horse (`kit/quadruped.py`: `saddle_sdf`, `dense_normal`)

- **The saddle**: a dished seat rising toward a raised pommel arching over the withers (7.5 cm) and a high curved
  cantle (7.8 cm), rolled bars along the seat's edges, knee rolls at the flaps' front, thinner flaps, a rear skirt
  under the cantle. Every added term is nought at the seat's point, so **`saddle_seat` stays spine_01 at 1.608 m** and
  the stirrups are where they were (`TestHorseCloseUp`, `TestLookPass`). 400 triangles (320); the body 4,670 (4,750)
  so the tacked horse stays at 5,990 of 6,000.
- **The normal bake** did have P59's fault in a milder form: each texel took the inverse-distance mean of the four
  nearest dense vertices (12 mm apart), which can read a surface facing the other way where two lie close (an ear's
  hollow, the lips, between the legs). `dense_normal` now reads the nearest point on the dense mesh's triangles facing
  the texel's way (`people_bake._nearest_on`), for the horse and the wolf. The cheek's remaining facets are the low
  mesh's silhouette, not the map: still visible at 1 m.

## How it looks against the bar (10 m from the game camera; 3 m close)

- **The wolf - passes at 10 m; at 3 m it now reads as a grey wolf**, not a borzoi: the broad head with its stop, the
  blunt muzzle with the closed mouth, the triangular ears, the ruff and mane, the brush. Still off close up: the legs
  are slender for a wolf seen from the front, the dark saddle's edge on the neck is a little blotchy, the nose takes a
  bright highlight, and without the fur shell the coat is smooth. The dire wolf reads as a big dark wolf.
- **The ogre - passes at 10 m; at 3 m better**: the legs no longer pinch at the knees; the skull's top a little faceted.
- **The troll - passes at 10 m; at 3 m it reads as a troll** (pointed ears, heavy brow, long nose, mossy back), no
  longer an old man; the moss could be a touch less dark from behind.
- **The Ogre King - passes at 10 m and at 3 m**: a red mantle to the waist with an ivory fur collar, a buckled gold
  belt, a crown of eight points.
- **The Troll Matriarch - passes at 10 m; at 3 m acceptable**: a hide cloak with a rolled collar and the bone crown;
  between the collar and the cloak's top a band of mossy skin shows on her back.
- **The horse - passes at 10 m; at 3 m better**: the saddle has a pommel and a cantle; its flaps still plain leather.
- **The people**: faces smoother on every variant; the iron guard's tabard whole but for one notch.
