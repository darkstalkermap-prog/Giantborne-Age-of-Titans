# P25 — World builder and geography: the 2 × 2 km world, regions, the skyline, POIs, shrines, waystones, caches, cairns, light content, the map picture and the index

Wave 16, lane A, beside P30 (formations). Branch `pkg/P25`. Plan: `docs/plans/M6_M9_PLAN.md` §3.8,
with §2.7 (world data), §5.1–§5.5 (the map, the chunk grid, the terrain, the regions and POIs, the
routes and size gates), §5.8 (the equipment budget), §5.10 (the light content and the M7 content
rule) and decisions 8 (world data ≤ 5.0 MB), 12–14 (the streamed world, the skyline, the index).
Built on P24a (ChunkData, the streamer, the skyline, HeightField, ChunkWriter, SkylineWriter), P24b
(NavBake, NavMaps, NavQuery) and P20 (WorldIndex, the region and POI schemas, LevelExit).

## What was built

- **The layout tables** (`tools/data/world/*.json`, not exported): `world.json` holds the frame (an
  8 × 8 grid of 256 m chunks from (−1024, −1024), 4 m height spacing, the interior ±896 m), the noise,
  the five regions (rectangles tiling the interior, each with its base height, noise, zone low/high,
  surface and skyline tint), the highland wall (a 16 m cliff face between the lowlands and the
  Stonehold's plateau), the rim (rising to ~160 m beyond ±896 m) with its three notches (the Kingsgate
  shelf, the Broken Stair, the Wend's gap), the ordered terrain features (a tilt, hills, mounds,
  ridges, the Troll Den's bowl, the Hidden Grove's ring ridge with the Needle's slot and the rockfall
  cut, the Raiders' Hollow trench, ramps — the Ironway, the Giant's Stair, the shelf's ramp — pads,
  the Sunken Steps' terraces, basins — the Mere, the bell pool, Hollowmere's pond — the deep channel,
  the causeway, the Wend with its bridge deck), 20 roads (carved, or uncarved: the shelf ramp, the
  Ironway ramp, the Giant's Stair and four Greywood "rides"), the road rules (12 % grade), the beacons
  and the index's extra edges (the Needle, `direct`, max stage 4; the Grove's rockfall route, min
  stage 5). One table per region (`crownmarch`, `greywood`, `drowned_steps`, `aldmere`, `stonehold`)
  holds its `elements` (landmarks and light structures), `shrines`, `waystones`, `checkpoints`,
  `exits`, `size_gates`, `secrets` (the seals of sealed places and the two ledges), `caches`,
  `cairns`, `markers` (safe markers included), `areas`, `pines` and `scenery` rules.
- **The terrain recipe** (`tools/world/features.gd`, `WorldFeatures`): the base (the zone's regions
  blended over 120 m, noise, the highland wall), every feature in table order, the roads (each carved
  road's profile smoothed over ±40 m, then grade-limited to 12 % by averaging a cut-only and a
  fill-only envelope; never filling water except bridge decks), water (the Mere, the pools, the Wend:
  flat planes as 8 m rectangles), and the queries the builder and the tests use (`walkable`,
  `region_index`, `water_surface_at`, `surface_kind`, `gate_at` — the Wend's bed 32, the deep
  channel 64, the Giant's Stair and the Moot's gate 128 —, `slope_deg`, `chunk_id_of`).
- **The border rule** (`WorldFeatures.soften_borders`, then `HeightField.relax_borders` and a
  `border_report` after quantising): no walkable face within 8 m of a chunk border steeper than 35°.
  P24a's Laplacian relaxation cannot bend a planar 35–45° slope (a hill's flank, a river bank, a
  cliff's shoulder), so the world first runs a slope-limiting projection: each walkable band cell's
  triangles are projected onto 34.8° with the least change of their free samples, the height moved
  into a 36 m transition beside the band (held under 44.6°, so nothing walkable becomes a cliff);
  cliffs (> 45°), the rim and everything beyond the transition are pinned. A band cell still over the
  rule beside a cliff (a sliver sharing the cliff's steep edge) is pushed into the cliff (> 45.8°),
  and the last few are settled by greedy 0.25–2 m nudges kept only when they move the band cells
  away from the rule. On today's world: 1,070 samples moved, 9 cells pushed, 46 nudges; the steepest
  walkable border face 34.96°; `relax_borders` then finds nothing to do.
- **Element kinds** (`tools/world/kinds/*.gd`: house, hall, wall, tower, palisade, gate_prop, bridge,
  ruin, column, stone_circle, bones, tent, field, pier, beacon, statue, stair_visual, cache, cairn,
  green, rock, falls): `parts()` (boxes, cylinders, cones, frustums, roofs, pyramids, spheres,
  wedges with palette colours), `solids()` (collision and navigation obstacles), `build()` for the
  scripted kinds (cache, cairn, gate_prop). `tools/world/layout.gd` (`WorldLayout`) loads and merges
  the tables (unique ids in the id form, known kinds), frames elements on the ground, meshes parts
  (one vertex-coloured surface) and gives their plan footprints for the map.
- **The world builder** (`tools/builders/world.gd`, run by `tools/build_scenes.tscn -- --only=world`):
  the field (soften, relax, quantise to 16 bits), the no-scenery and road grids, per chunk its
  ChunkData (heights with the apron, 16 m surface and 64 m region cells, water rectangles), its
  scenery (Poisson-disk trees per region with 1.0 m pine / 1.2 m broadleaf trunk solids so a
  stage-6 capsule passes trees 9 m apart; bushes, heather, reeds by water, rocks), the static
  elements merged into one vertex-coloured structures mesh per chunk (a far-scenery key drawn to the
  terrain's 700 m) with their solids, and the scripted places in props sub-scenes of at most 150 nodes
  (shrines, waystones, checkpoints, exits, size gates, seals with a closed NavigationLink3D per nav
  layer, caches, cairns, great pines, markers, areas, flag-shown elements). Then navigation per chunk
  (NavBake: human, large, titan and the titan gate regions, with the chunk's and its eight
  neighbours' obstacles; size gates are never obstacles), the seals' and gates' link ends snapped onto
  the baked meshes (`snap_links`: each end moves outward along the link until it stands over one of
  its layer's polygons — a titan link may end on a gate region —, so a rockfall on a barrow's flank
  or a vault's door over the Mere's channel still joins), the skyline (the coarse terrain coloured
  by the regions' skyline tints, the merged silhouettes of the `skyline: true` landmarks, the great
  pines as one pine shape), the map picture, the index and `scenes/world/world.tscn` (WorldRoot,
  environment, sun, streamer, skyline with the two beacon columns, bounds walls along the walkable
  edge with the shelf's and the Broken Stair's notches walled round, spawns, the giant at the shelf,
  the encounter director, the debris pool). `--chunks=c2_6,c3_6` rebuilds those chunks and their
  eight neighbours (`expand_chunks`) and writes only theirs; the skyline, index, map and root scene
  are always written. Two builds write the same bytes.
- **The index** (`tools/world/index_writer.gd`, `WorldIndexWriter`, `assets/world/index_world.res`):
  every shrine, waystone and checkpoint (display names, kind, chunk, region, the layout's max stage;
  the plain checkpoint `world.cp.broken_stair` without fast travel), markers, areas, exits and
  encounters with their chunks, props (scripted props with world ids, every static element with an
  id — indestructible by construction —, the cairns; each with `landmark`, `quest_target`,
  `quest_critical`, `indestructible`, `kind`), the POIs of the `poi` records, the staged road graph
  (a node every 40 m and at every vertex, loose ends joined within 26 m, connectors from every POI,
  checkpoint, exit and green to the nearest node, the extra edges), the region grid (64 m cells) and
  the map (`map_image`, `map_bounds`).
- **The map picture** (`tools/world/map_render.gd`, `WorldMapRender`, `assets/world/map_world.png`):
  1,024 × 1,024 (drawn at 512 and scaled), a hillshade in nine bands lit from the north-west over the
  regions' map tints, the rim, water (deep water darker), roads and rides, the elements' plan
  footprints in their own colours (low parts first, roofs over them) and marks for shrines,
  waystones, cairns, seals and great pines. 52 KB.
- **Records**: `data/world/level_world.json` (plan §2.7: the world block, the render presets with
  `terrain_visibility_end_m` 700 and `camera_far_m` 2,400, `nav_query`, `regions`,
  `max_formation_members` 40 and 20 on the web), five `region_*.json`, 48 `poi_*.json`, five
  `data/shrines/shrine_world_*.json` (Kingsgate: max stage 4 and growth on `world.kingsgate.lit`;
  the Greywood, the Steps and Aldmere 5; the Standing Stones 6), 30 `data/loot/cache_*.json`, eight
  light encounters (`encounter_{greywood,drowned,aldmere,stonehold}_*.json`) and seven cairn
  dialogues (`dialogue_cairn_*.json`: one attribute point once, `reward.cairn.<name>`).
- **Runtime scripts**: `scripts/world/inscription.gd` (`Inscription`, an NpcTalker whose speaker is
  the stone: `read_flag()`, `is_read()`, `gift_flag()`; group `inscriptions`) and
  `scripts/world/flag_visibility.gd` (`FlagVisibility`: an element shown or hidden by a world flag,
  its collision with it, on load and on `flag_changed`).
- **Schema and GameData**: `level_schema.gd` (anchor P25: `regions`, `max_formation_members`,
  `web_overrides.max_formation_members`; a region listed once, the web budget no bigger);
  `game_data.gd` (anchor P25: every POI's level and region exist, the region is the POI's level's
  and holds its position within 64 m of its bounds, the POI's landmark stands in the level's index;
  a level's regions are its own; every prop an index marks quest-critical is indestructible).
- **Tools**: `tools/world/seam_check.gd` (the seams and the border rule on chunk files; check.sh runs
  it after a build that ran the world builder) and `tests/support/world_draw_probe.gd` (the static
  world's draw calls at the shelf and the six settlements under Xvfb). `tools/check.sh`:
  `--build-args=` (e.g. `--build-args=--chunks=c2_6`), the seam check after a world build (on the
  rebuilt chunks with `--chunks=`), the build limit raised to 1,500 s.

## Command line

```
tools/gd.sh --headless --path . res://tools/build_scenes.tscn -- --only=world               # the whole world (~40 s)
tools/gd.sh --headless --path . res://tools/build_scenes.tscn -- --only=world --chunks=c2_6  # c2_6 and its neighbours (~18 s)
tools/check.sh --only=world --build-args=--chunks=c2_6                                       # the same, checked, with the seam check
tools/gd.sh --headless --path . -s res://tools/world/seam_check.gd -- [--chunks=c2_6]
tools/gd.sh --headless --path . --fixed-fps 60 -- --no-user-settings --mode=world --new-game \
  --autoplay=200 --scenario=world_walk "--route=-300:915;-300:780;-220:712;60:528" --walk-speed=16
```

## Decisions and deviations

- **The border rule needs more than relaxation.** HeightField.relax_borders (P24a) cannot bend a
  planar slope; the world's hills, banks and cliff shoulders cross 14 border lines. `soften_borders`
  (above) runs first; `relax_borders` stays the plan's step and finds nothing left. The builder fails
  naming the place if a border face is still over the rule after quantising.
- **Coordinates moved from §5.4** (each because the plan's point fell on water, a gate region or
  outside its landmark's reach): the Wend's course (out of the Mere at (30, 96)–(30, 152), bending at
  Brannock, its bridge at (60, 343) and `poi.brannock_bridge` at (60, 345)); the Wend gated for stages
  under 4 from 120 m along its course (its head is a ford of the Mere Road); `world.cp.mere_south`
  (78, 182); `poi.causeway` (22, 66) r 45 and the sunken vault at (22, 40) behind its rockfall at
  (22, 28); `poi.giants_stair` at the stair's foot (0, −420) r 60 (the plan's (0, −475) is inside the
  stage-6 gate region); `poi.wend_falls` (865, 588) r 70; `poi.broken_stair` (−40, 905) r 45 (the
  tunnel mouth is at the notch's end); `poi.brannock` r 100 (its waystone at (60, 546));
  `poi.the_needle` r 50 (its mouth's cache); `poi.mere_of_kings` (−20, 20) (the plan's (0, −40) is in
  the deep channel); the Titan's Bones turned 90° (clear of the Mere Road); the Ironway's high road
  passes south of the Standing Stones.
- **Titan-sized places.** NavBake's lean settings drop titan regions under 64 m² (region_min_size 8
  at 1 m cells) and erode 4 m: the three vaults are 22 × 19 m chambers with 15 m doors (`door_m`)
  that their rockfalls close; the Moot's gate is 28 m wide; Ballista Ridge's flanks are about 35° so
  its emplacements and the eyrie's foot are walked to; Hollowmere's pad keeps off the Hidden Grove's
  ring (a pad reaching its wall would ramp onto the ring's top). The Greywood's titans move on four
  uncarved, tree-free rides (index road edges) and every POI keeps its road connector.
- **Size gates gate what reaches 6 m into them.** NavBake (P24b) gates every titan polygon that
  touches gated ground anywhere; at the Giant's Stair's foot Recast's long lowland triangles touched
  the stair's rectangle with slivers under 5 m deep, and 3,700 m² of open ground (the stair's cache
  with it) was closed to stages 4 and 5. The builder draws each size gate's rectangle in by
  `GATE_GRAZE_M` (6 m; a narrow gate keeps its middle 4 m) for the split: grazing polygons stay
  open, every polygon on the gated ground (the ramp, the Moot's gateway) reaches far deeper and
  stays gated, so a stage-4 path can end at most 6 m past a gate's line, behind its wall, and never
  cross it (the gate suite: a stage-5 giant goes round by the Ironway, 1,505 m against the stair's
  131 m; the Stone Throne stays out of reach at stage 5). The water gates (the Wend, the Mere's
  channel) keep P24b's rule.
- **The reachability check is the gate suite's.** §3.8 makes it a builder check (every talker's
  green and every interactable reachable on the titan layer from its nearest road node at the stage
  that needs it). The builder bakes offline, without the navigation server's maps, so
  `tests/gate/test_world_reachability.gd` runs it, from the start checkpoint (a place reached from
  the shelf is reached from every road node the shelf reaches): every checkpoint, exit, green and POI
  centre and every interactable of the region tables (the caches, cairns, seals and great pines) at
  the stage its POI or its own entry needs, 119 places. The builder fails on the border rule after
  quantising, and check.sh runs the seam check after every world build.
- **The rim** (§5.3: "+160 m over 128 m (slopes > 55°)") rises on a smoothstep to 160 m (± 14 m of
  noise) across the 128 m beyond ±896 m, 72–158 m above the interior's edge (median 127 m): its
  middle is steep (a fifth of its faces over 55°, the steepest 79°) and its foot and crest gentler
  (a tenth under 16°). No giant climbs it all the same: nothing beyond ±896 m but the shelf's and the
  Broken Stair's notches has navmesh, and the world scene's Bounds walls (layer 6, 400 m tall) stand
  along the walkable edge and round the two notches.
- **The Hidden Grove's second key** is the north ridge's rockfall at (−504.7, −70.1) (heave, stage 5;
  the index's `grove_rockfall` edge, min stage 5); the Needle's slot (5 m) has no titan navmesh and
  is the index's `direct` edge with max stage 4.
- **Shrine POIs give no discovery XP** (§5.4's table: "30 / 0"); the content-rule test exempts them
  from "every POI gives XP" (the shrine is the reward).
- **`world.kingsgate.lit`** is read by the Kingsgate shrine and the skyline's beacon column and
  written by P29's beacon; until then the shrine's growth stays locked (by plan).
- **The static draw calls** are measured with the giant hidden (the static world); with the giant in
  view the views cost 8 more (P24a's test-world numbers included it).
- **Beyond the owned list**: `scripts/world/flag_visibility.gd` (the runtime of `visible_when_flag` /
  `hidden_when_flag`, plan §2.7), `tools/world/seam_check.gd` (the seam check check.sh runs) and
  `tests/support/world_draw_probe.gd`.
- **Existing tests changed** (the world now exists): `test_world_start`, `test_title_checkpoint_names`
  and `test_campaign_travel` simulate a build without the world by overriding `level.world`'s scene
  with a missing path; `test_valley_content` no longer reads the world's shrine records as the
  Valley's (their flags are the world packages'; `test_world_data` checks the Kingsgate flag).
- **Fixed on the way**: the builder's `--chunks=` and the layout loader's error list appended to
  copies of packed arrays (GDScript passes PackedStringArray by value) and never took effect.
