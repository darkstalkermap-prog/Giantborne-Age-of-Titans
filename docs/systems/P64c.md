# P64c - Gate C's code lane: the poses keyed on the action, the dead, the held and the thrown

The M13 gate review (Gate C, M11 part C: the skinned people and creatures) found that the realistic bodies still
took many attack poses from the old box rig's generic mapping - the box rig's sector-pulse "T-pose" put onto real
arms, the rival giants' chop with no pose at all - and that the dead dropped their gear badly. The owner's bar: "it
needs to look and feel realistic and not clunky". This lane follows P64b's approach for the brutes' sweeps: poses
keyed on the action's own clock (its phase and phase fraction, the box rig's easing), never on the frame clock, so
the timings, the hit shapes, the random streams and the gameplay are untouched (the soak, the determinism gates and
the readability gate are the controls). Presentation, tests, debug tools and docs only: no asset, no data record, no
gameplay script changed.

## 1. The rival giants' chop and strokes (`actor_rig.gd`, `pose_brain_modifier.gd`, `skinned_rig.gd`)

- **The overhead chop** (`ability.giant_axe_chop`, `ability.harrok_chop`: a line pulse with the weapon) had no case
  in `ActorRig.ability_pose` and the giants stood idle through its 1.4 s windup and its hit. The brain now has an
  overhead pose for it (`ActorRig.is_weapon_chop`: a line pulse that uses the weapon; the player's Earthquake, a
  line pulse without one, keeps its pose), and the skinned giant keys it (`PoseBrainModifier.CHOP_KEYS`,
  `chop_key`): the axe lifted and raised over the head across the windup (the fists over the head, the head of the
  axe behind it), brought down in the first 45 % of the active phase (through "over" to "hit": the fists low, the
  axe's head on the line ahead, the spine bent 30 degrees into it) and held while the crack runs, then the recovery
  lets go. During the stroke the arm bones are not held to Gate A's ARM_STEP_DEG_S (the stroke's own clock moves
  them, as a tested swing's).
- **The strokes** (the combos, Harrok's frenzy: weapon arcs) were one rigid arm held out sideways. The windup now
  draws the axe back over the shoulder of the stroke (`ARC_KEYS`: the fist at the shoulder, the haft back and up;
  the backhand mirrored); the active phase keeps tip matching exactly (the tested swing: `test_rival_giant_rig`'s
  0.42 m and 0.68 m worst misses are unchanged).
- **Two hands**: in every phase of both the off fist holds the haft `TWO_HAND_GAP` (17 %) of the axe's length above
  the weapon fist (`two_w`, the left hand's grip clip closed on it), the shoulder brought forward by the clavicle
  when the haft is beyond the arm's reach. The blade's edge leads the stroke (the socket's up: right for the chop,
  down for a stroke from the right, up from the left). `SkinnedRig.two_handed()`: an enemy carrying an axe (the
  player's giant is unchanged).

## 2. The people's strikes and braces (`person_rig.gd`)

`PersonRig.STRIKE_KEYS`, keyed on the action id (not the hit shape), mixed on the action's clock (`strike_key`):
"ready" to "top" over the windup (eased out), "top" to "hit" over the first half of the active phase, back over the
recovery; a brace is lowered into over its windup and held. A key: the weapon fist's direction and reach, the
weapon's direction, the off fist on the haft (sliding down it toward the shoulder when the keyed point is out of
reach, never nearer than `HAFT_MIN_M`) or on its own direction with an elbow pole, the torso's twist, the spine's
bend, and a kick.

- **The pike's thrust**: drawn back at the hip in the windup (the torso turned away), thrust level at the target in
  both hands.
- **The shield's bash**: the shield drawn in, then punched forward across the chest with the shoulder in (the torso
  turned to lead with it), the sword held back.
- **The archer's kick**: a real leg kick - the knee chambered in the windup, the leg extended forward in the active
  phase (`_kick`: the hip flexed, the knee bent), the body leaning back.
- **The mage's staff**: drawn back over the right shoulder in two hands; the active phase stays the tested swing
  (tip matching) with the off hand on the staff, letting go over the recovery.
- **The pike's brace**: crouched over it, the pike angled up ahead at the giant in both hands, its butt on the
  ground: the pike slides back through the fist until the butt reaches the ground (`BUTT_*`: at most 1 m).
- **The shield's brace**: the shield squared before the body (the forearm across the chest).
- **The lance** (`skinned_quadruped_rig.gd`): the lance attack lowers the lance to the couch over its windup and draws
  it back (`THRUST_DRAW_M`), thrusts, and raises it over the recovery.

## 3. The dead (`person_rig.gd`, `crowd_renderer.gd`, `brute_rig.gd`)

- A dead person's weapon no longer stands upright out of the fist: over the fall's second half it falls out of the
  hand and lies flat on the ground beside it (`corpse_gear_weight`, `corpse_gear`: along the arm's way out from the
  body, turned by up to 40 degrees by a hash of the id).
- **No pop at the corpse switch**: the corpse crowd instance draws the same weapon at the same place (the corpse
  pose's frame: `CrowdRenderer.corpse_gear_transform`) and the shield and the quiver on the corpse pose's arm and
  back. Chosen over fading the gear out: the gear stays where it fell (the least visible option is no change at
  all). `test_person_strikes`: the corpse instance's sword within 2 cm and 2 degrees of where the skinned one lay.
- **Corpse variety**: the falling body turns to its corpse's yaw (`corpse_yaw`: seven ways 21 degrees apart by a
  hash of the entity id, no random stream read), and the corpse instance lies the same way. Limb variants are not
  possible without more corpse meshes (FOLLOWUPS).
- A dead ogre's club arm (the death clip left it raised) is laid on the ground with the club flat (`_lay_dead_arm`).

## 4. Held, thrown, the wolf's bite

- **Held**: the weapon hangs blade down and away from the giant who holds the soldier (`HELD_GEAR_DOWN`), not
  through his thigh.
- **Thrown**: a body that lands alive is knocked down and gets up within its stagger (`knockdown_time`: the fall clip
  down over 35 % of the stagger, lying to 55 %, played back up), not upright in a T and in guard a moment later.
- **The wolf's bite** (`skinned_wolf_rig.gd`, `bite_pose`): the head drawn back (the neck up, the head kept on its
  mark) and a crouch in the windup, the jaw fully open by 80 % of it; the head snapped forward and down with the body
  behind it in the active phase, the jaw shutting at 40 % of it.

## 5. The tools

- **`scripts/debug/capture_m11.gd --part=c`**: an attack is filmed by its subject's own phase (`_strike_beat`:
  `<beat>_top` at 93 % of the windup, `<beat>_hit` at the active phase's middle). P64b's clock took the brutes'
  "windup's end" 0.2-0.3 s late, inside the active phase. New beats: every human role's strike (the soldier's slash
  and chop, the archer's shot and kick, the mage's bolt and staff, the pike's thrust, the shield's bash, the lance),
  the pike's and the shield's braces, the rival giant's chop and stroke, a soldier held and thrown, a field of the
  dead. Headless 264 frames, 0 errors, no note, about 22 s.
- **`tools/character_sheet.gd --sheet=c`**: page c4 (every human role and named character - soldier, pikeman,
  shieldman, archer, mage, siege crew, bannerman, Corvane, cavalry, villager, elder - front, side and three-quarter,
  and beside the giant at stages 1 and 4) and c5 (the rival giant and Harrok beside the stage-1 giant and a soldier,
  facing the camera; forty soldiers from the front at 25 m and at 60 m for three factions, levelled as the game
  levels them).

## How it looks against the bar

See the lane's report for the frames. In short:

- **The rival giants' chop**: at the game camera a clear two-handed overhead chop - raised, brought down onto the
  line, held - where there was nothing. At about 4 m the raise reads naturally from the side and three-quarters; the
  legs keep the stance (no knee bend at the impact) and the off hand's grip is a solve, not a clip.
- **The strokes**: two-handed - the axe drawn back over the shoulder, both hands on the haft through the swing and
  the follow-through. At 4 m the windup's off forearm crosses the chest close to the face.
- **The people**: the pike thrust and brace, the shield bash and brace, the kick and the staff read as what they
  are at the game camera; at 3-4 m the pike's off hand lies on the shaft open (no left grip clip), and the brace
  crouches by the spine more than by the legs.
- **The dead**: the gear lies on the ground and stays there through the switch; the corpses lie several ways, all
  in the one spread-eagled pose.
- **Held, thrown, the bite**: the held soldier's blade hangs down and away; the thrown one goes down and gets up
  (quick: 0.6 s); the wolf draws back with its jaw wide and snaps forward.
