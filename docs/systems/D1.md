# D1 — The Frostbound Titan, on the Rimeglass over the Hoarwatch

Milestone D's first package, on `pkg/d1` from the shipped 0.16.0-m16. The version stays **0.16.0-m16** (development
toward 0.17). The fifth signature titan: the old kings' watcher, frozen for a thousand winters into the Hoarcrown's ice,
come down to kneel in a frozen tarn above the Colossus Steps. Decisions D1-1..D1-12 are in `docs/DECISIONS.md` §2.7;
the duels are BALANCE_LOG "D1"; what is left is FOLLOWUPS "From D1".

## 1. Where he is: the Rimeglass

A new land, `rimeglass` (`tools/data/world/world.json`): a glacier shelf of 260 x 240 m east of the Hoarwatch, at
[500..760] x [-1700..-1460], reached only across the Hoarwatch's shelf (the land's `joins`: no bounds wall in the 46 m
where the two meet; elsewhere its west face stands 40-60 m sheer over the Hoarfell, walled). The Hoarwatch is above the
Colossus Steps, whose gate wants a stage-7 giant: a stage-6 giant cannot walk to him. The land is a `zone` of the
Hoarfell (its region, its weather, its music) with four features: the shelf (a hill topped at 262 m), a moraine, the
tarn's hollow (flattened to 252 m, 72 m round) and the glacier's tongue running down into it.

On it: the **tarn's ice** (an `ice_sheet` element, 66 m round, 9 m cells), eight **seracs** and the old kings' **split
cairn** (the landmark, at the glacier's foot), a warning post at the shelf's edge, and - once Maera has sent them - the
Hearth's **watchers**: two balefires on the shore, smoke, two hide tents and a woodpile. **Lesser life** of the kinds
the Hoarfell already has: two rimkin on the shelf, three trolls among the seracs, five dire wolves on the walk
from the Steps, and the watchers (friendly, after the lead). The Rimeglass is a POI (`poi.rimeglass`, discovered on the
tarn's edge).

## 2. How the fight plays

He kneels frozen in the ice 22 m north of the tarn's middle. A giant at the shelf's edge does not wake him; walking out
onto the ice, or striking him, does (`TitanTerritory`, `wake_on_sight` off): his **intro** (`frostbound_wake`: he
tears free of the ice, rises and roars - 3.5 s with no blow, telegraphed as a 20 m circle), his voice (the Hoarfell
matriarch's calls pitched down an octave, `voice.frostbound`), the boss bar, the region's boss music (Harrok's, reused).

**The cold (rime).** The giant has a new pool, `rime` (0-100, `PlayerRime`), shown as the HUD's frost meter over the
status row while there is any. His breath, his blows (each ability's `rime`), his phase aura and open water add it,
scaled by the giant's stage (a stage-6 body takes 1.5 x, stage 7 0.85 x, stage 8 0.55 x: a bigger body keeps its heat)
and by the Frostbound set. It slows him (45 % of his speed at full) and at 100 he is **frozen fast** for 2.2 s (no
dodge, jump or sprint; the rig tinted white; the HUD says "FROZEN - heavy blow!"); a **heavy blow shatters it**
(-12 rime each heavy, and frees him at once). It falls away by itself after 2.5 s without more (4 a second) and fast by a
**fire** (30 a second within a balefire's warmth: `WarmthSource`). After a freeze it drops to 55.

**The ice.** The tarn's ice has cells whole, cracked or open. His fist's shock line, the hurled serac and his nova crack
it; the giant's stamps and Heavy Footfall crack it too. Cracked ice under his next step gives (open water). **Stepping
into open water takes him through to the knee**: a 4.5 s down (his topple's kneel), his blows' bonus, then not again for
20 s. A giant wading in open water is slowed (0.65) and rimes (3.5 a second). Open water freezes over again thin
(cracked) after 45 s. **So the fight's lever is the ice**: break the ice where he walks, bait him across it, and he goes
down where you can reach his crown and heart.

**His moves** (power as data; each telegraphed): the claw-rake (60, a 120-degree cone), the ice fist (85, a 34 x 9 m
shock line that cracks the ice), the breath (a 43 m, 80-degree cone of rime: 13 a tick on whoever is in it, little
damage), the stamp (a giant hugging his legs), the fist-sweep (72, 230 degrees). **Phase 2** at 65 %: the cold roar
(rime on everyone within 45 m), a 30 m cold aura (3 rime a second; not while he is down), the serac torn from his back
and hurled (95, it cracks the ice where it lands), strikes 15 % faster. **Phase 3** at 28 %: the nova (75; rime 45 within
24 m, it breaks the ice around him), a 36 m aura, strikes 30 % faster.

**His plates**: the cairn-crown (head, weak x2.2: a capstone), the pauldrons, the serac crest on his back (broken, it
unbalances him: poise x1.4), the heart-ice in his breast (weak x2.6), the ice gauntlet on his right fist, the greaves
(broken, he goes down on a knee for 5 s). Health 14,000, poise 700.

**His death** drops the Heart-ice, seven hoarfrost shards and two cairn capstones (and each broken plate's own); the
tarn thaws when its chunk next loads (open water with floes, no cold left in it), the watchers' banner stands over it
(`world.rimeglass.thawed`), and Maera's dialogue closes the quest.

## 3. Him as an asset

`tools/giant/kit/frostbound.py` (body) and `frostbound_moves.py` (17 clips) build him in the people kit
(`build_people.py`, group `frostbound`): a gaunt, stooped, long-armed giant 28.0 m tall (92 ft: over a stage-7 giant's
23.8 m, under a stage-8's 29.3 m), blue-grey frost-bitten skin, a forward-thrust head, long beard of icicles, a crown
of the cairn's capstones grown through with ice, ice pauldrons, a serac crest along his spine, a heart-ice in his
breast that glows (`ice_plate.gdshader`: rime, cracks and an inner light that brightens as he draws breath), an ice
fist on the right hand. His own face (not the Bastion's: a long gaunt head, sunken eyes) and his own voice. Distinct
silhouette: the stoop and the crest against the Bastion's upright wall and the Rootwarden's tree.

Shipped: `frostbound.glb` 0.85 MB, `frostbound_clips.glb` 0.30 MB, three 512 textures 0.28 MB (people share 17.39 of
18.0 MiB). `FrostboundModel` / `FrostboundRig` (`scripts/presentation/`) drive the plates' cracks, the heart's pulse, the
breath's frost and the hurled shard.

## 4. The reward

- **The Frostbound set** (medium; seven pieces; `data/armour/armour_frostbound_*.json`, `set_frostbound.json`): Rime
  Crown, Glacier Pauldrons, Heart-ice Cuirass, Hoarfrost Bracers, Rimed Girdle, Ice Greaves, the Heart-ice (trinket).
  2 pieces: rime takes 25 % less hold; 4: armour +5 and elemental resistance +10 %; 7: rime a further 40 % less, poise
  +10. Made at Last Hearth's titan forges (the Hearth's forge and the Colossus Anvil; `works: frost_ice`, forge tier
  `titan`) once he has fallen; reforged there as every set. Drawn on the giant (`giant_garment.py`'s rigid ice pieces).
- **The Rime-maul** (a hammer, x1.6, fits stages 7-8): its heavies **chill** whatever they strike (slowed 35 % for 3 s;
  the weapon perk `chill`).
- What he drops pays for the set and the maul from one kill (a test holds it).

## 5. The quest and the world's answer

`quest.frostbound`: from Maera's lead (C4's `lead.frostbound.hearth`) or from finding the tarn - cross onto the
Rimeglass, bring him down, tell Maera. With the lead the watchers go up and light the shore's balefires (the fight's
fires); after his fall Maera's dialogue (`frost_fallen`) sets `world.last_hearth.frost_told`: the Heart-ice stands on a
plinth below Last Hearth's beacon, the watchers stay on the thawed tarn, and Maera tells what the beacon's watchers see
now - a storm that has not moved in nine days over the Drowned Steps (`quest.stormcaller_lead`, the lead toward the
Stormcaller; nothing of him is built). The Character menu's Titans tab names him (fifth) with where he is said to be.

**No stage 9.** The growth track is unchanged: stage 8's condition stays C1's.

## 8. What is weak

- **The duels are a bot's**, on the test arena's flat tarn (70 m of ice, two balefires); a person may fight the ice
  very differently (more or fewer falls). Stage 7 is two wins in three by small margins either way.
- **His look is the kit's**: faceted ice slabs and a MakeHuman-derived body; the ice is a shader on hard shapes. The
  breath is particles; no frost creeping on the giant's skin (a tint).
- **The tarn's ice is a disc of cells** (9 m): breaks are square-ish under a ragged edge; open water is a flat dark
  plane, no ripples, no floating chunks but the thawed floes.
- **The fall through the ice is a kneel**: he does not sink, the water does not splash beyond a dust puff.
- **The thaw comes on the chunk's next load**: a giant who stays at the tarn after the kill sees the ice until he
  walks away and back.
- **His voice and sounds are re-pitched reuse** (the world's SFX cap holds 0.79 s of room).
- **Last Hearth's stone ring is still not solid** (D1-11).
