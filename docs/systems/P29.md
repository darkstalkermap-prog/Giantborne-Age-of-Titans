# P29 — The Crownmarch: the Kingsgate shelf and beacon, Brannock, Millford, the oaths opening, settlements and factions by data, the Crownmarch's formation fights

Plan: `docs/plans/M6_M9_PLAN.md` §3.12 (design §5.4, §5.7–§5.10, §2.12, decisions 17, 26, 31, 32).
Wave 18, lane A, beside P31. Milestone M7: from the crown to Brannock's oath, every path.

The first region with full content. Leaving the Valley through the Kings' Door, the giant stands on
the Kingsgate shelf with the beacon below held by toll-rider scouts; relighting it opens the Kingsgate
shrine (stage 4) and names the three powers of the Old Kingdom; at stage 4 the main thread's `oaths`
stage shows three leads at once (Brannock's green, Hollowmere's green, the war camp's edge) and
Brannock's oath starts. Off the thread: the King's Road toll (pay, cow or breach), the Barrow Fields,
the millrace, the Shepherd's Watch beacon, the sealed Citadel - and two towns that react to the giant.

## What was built

### Hotspot code (anchors P29)

- **`near` quest condition** (`scripts/quests/quest_conditions.gd`): `{"type": "near", "target", "radius_m"}`
  (default 150 m), horizontal distance from the giant to the target. Pure: the context gives the
  target's position (`ctx.positions[id]`, else `ctx.position_of`, a `Callable(id) -> Vector3`).
  `Quests.condition_context()` adds `position_of` = `Quests.objective_position` (a live node, else the
  level's world index). Helpers `QuestConditions.target_position(ctx, id)`, `near_holds(ctx, id, r)`.
- **The world's main quest** (`scripts/quests/quests.gd`): `quest.world_main` becomes
  `Quests.main_quest_id` once it has started while `quest.main` is not active (after the crown, or from
  world_start where `quest.main` never ran) - `p29_main_for(valley_state, world_state)` is the pure rule,
  `_p29_follow_main()` runs at the start of every evaluation (so the switch lands on the evaluation after
  the world's quest starts, at most `EVALUATE_EVERY_TICKS` later). A main quest a test chose is kept.
  The compass's leads (P27) follow it: the `oaths` stage's three markers.
- **`stand_down_when`, provocation, talkers on greens** (`scripts/world/encounter_director.gd`): while an
  encounter's `stand_down_when` holds (a quest condition), every faction member of it stands down
  (`EnemyActor._apply_standing("stood_down")`: NEUTRAL, at its post) whatever its faction's standing;
  when it stops holding they take their faction's standing again, alerted. A connecting blow of the giant
  on a member standing down sets `encounter.<short>.provoked` (saved) and ends the stand-down for good.
  Talkers (npc role `talker`) whose spawn lies in a settlement POI with `max_inner_stage` and `green_m`
  stand on the green while the giant's stage is above `max_inner_stage` (placed when they spawn, moved
  when the giant grows). Public: `provoked_flag(id)`, `p29_stands_down(id)`, `p29_standing_down(id)`,
  `p29_update_stand_downs()`, `p29_green_for(member)`, `p29_on_green`.
- **FactionState** (`scripts/world/faction_state.gd`, the W18 owner): reconciliation by dialogue - a
  choice whose outcome is `reconcile_<short>` calls `reconcile(<short>)` every time it is chosen (a town
  that turns hostile again can be reconciled again); the respawn blocker `respawn_refusal(checkpoint)`
  (registered with `GameSession.register_respawn_blocker` by each level's director): refused while a
  faction whose record has `respawn_guard_m` is hostile and one of its armed members (an enemy spawn of
  an uncleared encounter of the level, not killed) stands within that distance; `armed_members_near()`,
  `respawn_guard_m()`, `reconcile_outcome_faction()`. Fixed on the way: `prop_tags()` reads the
  `prop_tags` meta only when it exists (`get_meta` with a null default still errors - a faction's
  gatehouse carries its faction but no tags; the force walkthrough found it).
- **Schemas**: `faction.respawn_guard_m` (0-1000 m; a warning for a reconciliation without a cost).
- **GameData post-pass** (`scripts/quests/oath_rules.gd`, class `OathRules`): every oath quest present
  (`quest.oath_<key>`) has a resolution that can be won as the first oath at stage 4 (decision 26): a
  value some record writes to `oath.<key>.outcome` (dialogue choice `set_flags`, quest stage
  `set_flags`, prop `on_destroyed_flags`) whose guarding conditions can hold then - no stage above 4, no
  other oath's flag or quest, every flag they read itself writable under such conditions (a flag no
  record writes is the engine's: reachable); its start conditions and every stage's `complete_when`
  too. And every faction's `reconcile` is offered: some dialogue choice with outcome
  `reconcile_<short>` taking exactly its cost. `first_oath_resolutions(quest, records)` is public (P35,
  P36 use it).

### New runtime scripts

- `scripts/world/beacon_fire.gd` (`BeaconFire`, group `beacons`): the relight interaction at a beacon's
  foot. Lit once every `requires_flags` flag holds (the hill's encounter cleared): sets `lit_flag`, pays
  its cache once (`Loot.award_once` on its world id), reveals the places within `reveal_m`
  (`Discovery.reveal_around`), says `lit_message`, plays `scene_dialogue`; its `Fire` child shows while
  the flag is set, from any writer.
- `scripts/world/town_forge.gd` (`TownForge` extends `Forge`): Brannock's smithy - shut while its
  `faction` is hostile (`hostile_message`), open once reconciled; otherwise a Forge (its flags,
  its reforge). It overrides `is_open()` and `blocked_reason()` only.

### World builder kinds (`tools/world/kinds/`)

- `breakable_site`: a breakable or structural prop built in code (BreakableProp contract: CombatEntity on
  Anchors/Center, Block* shapes, Hurtboxes, Intact/Broken visuals, a gate's NavigationLink3D per nav
  layer). Styles `house` (`prop.house_town`, faction house: offences), `gatehouse`
  (`prop.gatehouse_brannock`: an open gateway between two solid towers), `gate` (`prop.gate_toll`), `dam`
  (`prop.millrace_dam`). `record_of(el)` is public (tests).
- `forge_site`: the shared forge scene; style `town` makes it a TownForge with its faction.
- `beacon_fire`: a BeaconFire with its flames at `fire_y`.
- `dressing`: banners, the tribute pile, the toll bar, stalls - flag-shown with `visible_when_flag`.

## Decisions and deviations

- **Toll fort garrison and captain are two encounters** (`toll_fort`, `toll_post`), so the fort can be
  cleared without killing the talker; the garrison stands down by `stand_down_when` rather than by a
  faction standing (the toll-riders are hostile everywhere else).
- **Edda's help path's "free reinforcement"** is paid as 2 iron (plus `world.brannock.free_refit` for a
  later forge rule): P31 edits `forge.gd` in parallel, so the forge's price was not touched.
- **12 individuals per cluster** is checked over sets of encounters pairwise within 150 m (cliques):
  read as "every encounter within 150 m of its centre", the plan's own positions (scouts, road wolves,
  raiders) would sum to 17 at stage 3 although no two of the outer ones are within 150 m.
- **The Brannock archers stand about 8 m inside the south wall**, not on it: on the wall they started outside
  the 60 m guard leash (the leash would loop on them).
- **Unavoidable discoveries** for the XP budget count the POIs covering the arrival checkpoint, the
  objectives' markers and the talker a stage-4 giant must reach (Edda inside the gate) - the plan's
  marker rule alone would miss the shelf and Brannock that its table counts.
- **The `oaths` stage cannot complete in M7** (two of three oaths; only Brannock's ships): by design
  (plan §5.7); the shrine of the Standing Stones likewise waits.

## M7 gate (wave 18)

- **The toll fort's and the Raiders' Hollow's leashes hold their formations.** The pikes reached
  68.3 m from the fort's centre (their slots 35 m out, turned, plus their 30 m advance) under a 45 m
  leash: the director leashed the front rank as soon as it advanced, and the fight on the road never
  threatened a stage-4 giant. The leashes are now 70 m (the toll fort; the plan's most, §5.10) and
  65 m (the Hollow; its shield wall reaches 63.2 m). The encounter schema now refuses a formation that
  reaches past its encounter's leash (`FormationRules.reach_m`; `docs/systems/P30.md`, "M7 gate"), and
  the director leaves an intact formation's members to their formation. With the garrison behind its
  closed gate the larger leash changes little for the soldiers; once the gate is breached they may
  follow the giant 25 m further down the road. The advance's half-metre stop outside the brace
  distance (P30) is fixed too: a defied fort's pikes now come down the road to a stage-4 giant 16 m
  before their home rank and brace (`tests/slow/test_m7_gate_world.gd`).
- **Fast travel and the respawn blocker.** A hostile town's waystone (Brannock's, its guards within
  `respawn_guard_m`) was offered by the map, and the reload then woke the giant at the stone it had
  left, without a word. `GameSession.travel_refusal` now refuses fast travel to a stone its respawn
  blockers refuse, with their reason ("Brannock's guards hold the ground there."), which the map shows
  with Travel off; the blockers judge the destination's level (`respawn_blocker(id, level)`,
  `respawn_blocker_level()`, read by `FactionState.respawn_refusal`), so a fast travel from the Valley
  is judged in the world. An exit is a door and is not refused this way. A load whose saved respawn a
  blocker moves (a death reload, a Continue) now says why and where the giant wakes, on the HUD and in
  `last_load_message`. Tests: `test_fast_travel`, `test_m7_gate_fixes`.
- Not changed, recorded for P36 (`docs/plans/FOLLOWUPS.md`, "From the M7 gate review"): the toll fort's
  two tower archers leave the watchtower for a target on the road (their 12-20 m band) and stand
  behind the closed gate, aware and never firing.
