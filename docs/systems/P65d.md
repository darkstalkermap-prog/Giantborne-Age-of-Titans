Plan: `docs/plans/M11_PLAN.md` §3.18 (P65; design 3: "faction palettes checked in the world's light so a formation's
side reads at 60 m"), §2.8, §2.12, and the Gate C review (lens 8, the look). Built on 21 November 2026 on
`pkg/gateC-assets` from `wave-39`, on the shared container (4 CPUs, no GPU: llvmpipe), beside other lanes.

Every judgement below was made from renders before and after through the game's code: the formation test level
through `scenes/main.tscn` with `--skin`, blocks of 40 spawned from their scenes and records (a row of shieldmen, a row
of pikemen, two rows of soldiers, a row of archers) at 30 m and 60 m in front of the stage-1 giant, seen through the
game camera, the rival giant and Harrok spawned from their records, the bosses from theirs; `tools/character_sheet.gd`
`--sheet=c --pages=c2` for the bosses. The frames are not committed (the gate's report lists them).

## 1. A formation's side at 60 m (`kit/people_roles.py`, `kit/people_outfit.py`: `far_mesh`)

**What was wrong.** The faction band was on the tunic, under the soldier's mail hauberk, and on the hoods and shields;
from the front a soldier, a pikeman, a shieldman or a bannerman showed grey mail and an iron helm, the shield's face
was held edge-on. At 60 m the formation draws the far mesh (150 triangles) and the crowd poses: the faction band was
about 1 % of a soldier's front (between the thighs and the shoulders) at every level, and nine factions' blocks had
the same mean colour within 3/255.

**What changed.**

- Every soldier variant (`soldier_a`, `soldier_b`, `soldier_c`; the iron guard had one) wears the tabard (P65b's
  straight-sided panel, front and back joined over the shoulders, to just under the hips) over the mail, in the
  faction band: a surcoat in the side's colour. The crowd poses and the far mesh are `soldier_a`'s, so they wear it.
  No new file, no atlas change (the tabard uses the faction tile); the soldiers' shared mesh keeps its budget (the iron
  guard's pieces were already the worst), the variants 3,944 to 4,133 triangles (5,000).
- The far mesh colours each collapsed triangle from the pose's **outermost** layer over it (a ray from
  `FAR_LAYER_REACH`, 12 design cm, outside it along its normal, inward; the nearest point as before where the ray
  misses). The nearest point to the collapsed centre (inside the body) had been the mail under the surcoat: the first
  regeneration with the surcoat still drew grey far soldiers. Every role's far mesh is recoloured this way (the others'
  geometry unchanged).

## 3. The rival giants (`kit/designs.py`, `data/appearance/preset_npc_harrok.json`)

Rendered with the looks' parts removed one at a time (scars, garments, accessories, each slot):

- **The rival's pink blob on the chest** was the `scar.chest_claws` design, not the garments: its three strokes lay
  3.2 cm apart and, at the 4 cm rule, ran together into one blob, and they ran from the shoulder down toward the
  breastbone, along the war harness's strap, which hid all of it but the blob at its edge. The claws now run down and
  out across the left breast, three strokes 7.5 cm apart (`CLAW_SPACING`), each at 0.8 of the rule (`CLAW_HALF`), so
  they cross a harness's strap and read as three healed marks. `designs.png` regenerated (the design is the player's
  "Chest claws" too). **Done.**
- **Harrok's skin flap over the kilt at the right hip** was his right thigh's skin through the kilt: present with every
  garment but the kilt removed, absent at build (0, 0) and at girth alone, small at brawn 0.9 alone, large at his brawn
  0.9 and girth 0.5. The build sliders push each vertex along its normal by its build weights; the kilt's hanging
  skirt carries the hip's weights (its front sides near zero or below) while the thigh's are about 0.5, so the thigh
  grew through the skirt. His preset's build is now brawn 0.5, girth 0.2 (the skirt clears the thigh by 1.1 cm at stage
  1 in the rest pose; he stays heavy-set and is 15 m tall). **Done for Harrok.** **Left**: the kilt's skirt rows take
  the hip's build weights for anyone (the player at a high brawn and girth in the kilt would show the same); a fix
  there is the giant export's (`export_game.py`: a skirt row's build weights from the skin it hangs over), not taken
  in this lane (the giant's parts and the freeze).

## 4. The bosses' mantles (`kit/brute.py`: `span_spine`)

The "pale diamond" in the middle of the Ogre King's and the Troll Matriarch's mantles from behind (P65c's "small
bright fold") was not a seam or culling: offset out from the brutes' deep, narrow spine groove the shell's two sides
crossed over its bottom, and the collapse kept folded slivers there whose normals turned inward. The collapsed mantle
is now spanned across the groove: each horizontal slice (2 cm) of the back within 16 cm of the spine is raised to its
upper hull within 7 cm of it, then that strip smoothed (20 Taubin passes, the open edge held). **Done**: no groove, no
diamond; left, the Matriarch's cloak keeps a short crease where its two tiers meet low on the back, and the King's
back under the fur collar takes a soft shadow where the raised cloth meets it.
