# A1 — Stoneback, the quarry titan, as an asset (no fight yet)

The open world's first titan, so the owner can see him in the engine before his fight is built. Built on
`pkg/a1` from `0.13.0-m13`. **No AI, no encounter, no gameplay change.** A1b (below) refined his look and opened the
expansion's working version, `0.14.0-m14`, with the owner's raised Web budget.

## What he is

Stoneback is an original brute-giant **16.8 m (55 ft)** tall who lives in a quarry beside a village: a heavy man's
believable body (legs, torso and shoulders in proportion, a slight hunch, a small head between broad trapezius, big
hands and feet), a weathered umber hide (no green: not an ogre or a troll), a hide kilt and belt, iron shackles on the
wrists and ankles with broken chain lengths, an uprooted tree trunk for a club (its root ball the head) and thick
quarried stone plates on the shoulders, the upper back, the brow and skull, the forearms and the shins.

## The asset (`tools/giant/kit/stoneback.py`, `build_people.py --stoneback`)

- **The body**: the brutes' code (`brute.body_data`, `brute.reshape`) with his own macro, face, proportions and shape
  tables; the kilt and belt are the giant's garment shells; one shared mesh of 8,935 triangles, one 512 px atlas
  (`people_bake`), the skin band repainted (`stoneback.repaint`: the hide, quarry dust on the feet, shins and
  forearms, **pale scarred skin under every plate**, so a plate taken off shows what it covered; the kilt's hide).
- **The plates**: eight separate skinned meshes on the same 23 bones (`plate_shoulder_l`, `plate_shoulder_r`,
  `plate_back`, `plate_brow` over the weak points `WEAK_POINTS` names; `plate_forearm_l/_r`, `plate_shin_l/_r`).
  Each is the body's own surface under its footprint (a chipped outline), subdivided, pushed out into a slab and
  smoothed, pressed onto flat tilted facets (Voronoi cells), bevelled, its rim running under the skin (no gap, no
  float), its weights the skin's stiffened a little; 280-680 triangles, sharp edges at the facets.
- **The shackles** (1,560): four cuffs and their chains (the wrists' hang straight down in the idle), rigid to the
  forearms and calves, on the atlas's iron tile. **The club** (2,102): a bark trunk 9 m from its broken top to the root
  ball, its handle through the right fist's grip landmark (rigid to `hand_r`), a torn root plate with roots; its head
  in the hand's frame is the manifest's `pieces.club.head_local`. **The boulder** (320): a chiselled rock on the left
  palm (`hand_l`), shown in the throw only. The far mesh: 300.
- **The clips** (`stoneback_clips.glb`): idle, walk and run (the brutes' heavy gaits at his 7.4 m step, no slide),
  roar, and the wind-ups **overhead** (the trunk raised two-handed over and behind the head, both fists on it),
  **charge** (the shoulder charge's lean, the left shoulder leading, the trunk trailing) and **throw** (the boulder
  drawn back over the left shoulder), each from the stance to its peak (its last key). The held arms are solved
  (`Holder.hold`: a two-bone solve to the wrist and the hand turned onto the trunk's line or a given palm).
- **The detail textures**: `stoneback_stone_*` and `stoneback_bark_*` (256 px albedo and normal, tileable, the
  project's own noise).

Regenerated bit-exact by `build_people.py --verify` (`check.sh --people`); the creatures' manifest has his group, its
index entry and the facts; `PROVENANCE.md` the rows. The giant's manifest hashes the new kit module.

## In the engine

`StonebackRig` (`scripts/presentation/stoneback_rig.gd`; `scenes/creatures/stoneback.tscn`, from the `people_models`
builder) loads him with no actor: `pose(clip, t)`, `pose_windup(clip)`, `set_plate_visible(plate, on)`,
`set_plates(on)`, `club_head()`. The body and the shackles use `person.gdshader`; the plates, the boulder and the club
use `assets/shaders/stone_plate.gdshader` (the tileable texture in metres, a macro variation, RigPaint's tint and
flash). A later package puts him under an actor (as `BruteRig` does the ogre).

## The sheet (`tools/character_sheet.tscn -- --sheet=d`)

`LP_NUM_THREADS=3 xvfb-run -a -s "-screen 0 1920x1080x24" tools/gd.sh --path . --rendering-driver opengl3
--resolution 1920x1080 --audio-driver Dummy res://tools/character_sheet.tscn -- --sheet=d --out=<dir>
--pages=d1,d2,d3 --jpg` (about a minute a page on llvmpipe): **d1** the turnaround; **d2** the scale line-up (a soldier
at 6 ft, the player giant at 26 ft and at 62 ft, Stoneback at 55 ft) and close-ups of the head, plates, shackles and
club; **d3** the plates hidden (all on, shoulders off, back off, brow off, all off; front and back) and the three
wind-ups from two sides. The frames are not committed.

## What still looks weak (honestly)

- The plates read as rough stone lumps more than as cut slabs: the shoulders' are large rounded mounds, the back's a
  hump; the facets show in close-ups, less from afar. The stone texture is procedural noise and looks a little like
  plaster close up.
- The face is the brutes' pushed human face: heavy-browed but plain; the brow plate reads as a stone cap.
- The root ball's roots are tubes and read a little spiky; the trunk is plain beyond its bark.
- The hands keep the people's relaxed rest curl: the left hand hangs half open, the boulder sits on the curled fingers.
- The kilt is a plain knee-length wrap, the cuffs plain bands.
- The throw's right arm is held out for balance and reads a little stiff from the front.

## A1b — the look refined; the expansion's working version

The owner's review of the first pass (plates as glued-on boulders, a rock hat, a plain bald face, salmon skin under the
plates and plastic skin overall, a box kilt, a stiff open hand, spiky roots, plaster stone, no walk on the sheet) and
what changed, all in `tools/giant/kit/stoneback.py` unless named:

1. **The plates are quarried slabs strapped on.** Each slab is the body's patch under its footprint cut to a chipped
   outline of straight edges (`chip_cuts`: planes across the outline; `snap_patch` moves the outline onto them), its
   top the intersection of a few flat cut faces (`cut_faces`, `slab_height`: ridges, flat faces, walls of clear
   thickness, a narrow chamfer; no saw-toothed rims). The shoulders and the back are **two slabs**, the smaller laid
   over the larger (`PLATE_FORM["upper"]`); the back is one great slab with a second over its top, its edge cracked
   across. Iron bands hold them (`plate_bands`, `band_ring`, in each plate's own mesh, so a plate taken off takes its
   bands): two bandoliers crossed over the back slab and the chest, a band round each upper arm, two round each forearm
   and shin. The shoulder slabs now lie over the top of the shoulder, not down the arm.
2. **The brow plate is a cracked stone half-helm** low over the brow, over the crown, its back edge rising over the
   occiput, with an iron chin strap down in front of the ears and under the jaw.
3. **The face**: the head aged, a heavier dropped and slightly crooked jaw, the brow low and forward over deep-set eyes
   with the cheekbones up, a broken nose (the vendored nose asymmetry target), the mouth's corners down, one brow pulled;
   painted on the atlas (`hide`): cropped dark hair over the scalp (seen with the helm off), stubble over the jaw and
   lip, heavy brows, a lined forehead and crow's feet, four old scars (`face_scars`: the right cheekbone, the nose's
   bridge, the lip to the chin, the left brow).
4. **The skin**: under the plates the same skin a little paler and less red with faint welts (no more salmon); the whole
   hide weathered (broad grime and sun patches, darker hands and forearms), quarry dust on the feet, shins, forearms and
   hands and in patches over the chest, thighs and shoulders; the skin's roughness raised to 0.80 (dry, not oiled).
5. **The kilt** (`kilt_panels`): eleven torn hide panels, each its own slanted length (70-100 % of the hang), slits
   between them from the hem up, every other one a little out; a darker hide's front and back flaps over it (the
   giant's loincloth shell); a heavier belt (`BELT`: 0.11 m tall at the human scale, twice as thick). The panels' rows
   follow the thigh behind them, so no thigh comes through in the wind-ups.
6. **The hands**: the free (left) hand a loose, heavy fist with the thumb laid along the index (`LEFT_CURLS`,
   `LEFT_THUMB_DEG`; the people's graded curl had left the thumb standing out: it read as pointing); the boulder is
   set where the curled fingers' pads press its faces (`BOULDER_SINK`). In the idle the club hand turns, key by key,
   until the root ball's lowest point is 2 cm over the ground (`club_at_rest`): it rests on it in every key.
7. **The root ball**: six thick, kinked, knotted roots with blunt broken ends, six clods of earth caught in them (the
   vertex colour's matte earth), the trunk 7.5 m.
8. **The stone texture**: six bedding planes a repeat (wavy dark lines, each bed its own shade and a little proud),
   a partly open crack network and a finer one, crusty grey-green and some ochre lichen, ochre stains.
9. **Sheet D**: page d1 has the walk from his left side at two phases half a stride apart.

`stone_plate.gdshader` reads the vertex colour (white where a mesh has none): alpha 1 the detail texture tinted, 0 iron
(dark, a little metallic), 0.5 a matte colour (the club's earth). The plates' test measures a two-slab plate against
both slabs' thickness (`plate_depth_m`); new checks: the bands and the earth by vertex colour, the idle's root ball 0 to
10 cm over the ground, sheet D's walk tiles.

### The Web: 0.14.0-m14

The owner raised the download cap for the expansion: `project.godot`'s version is **0.14.0-m14** (a development build
of the expansion; it plays as 0.13.0-m13), and `tools/build_web.sh` gives -m14 a **46 MB** pack budget and a **14.0
MiB** people's share, so the giant's derived share stays 46 - 18.4 - 14.0 = 13.6 (-m13's branch now says `-eq 13`; the
last default, which `build_giant.py`'s estimate reads, is -m14's 46; `--print-caps` for -m6 to -m13 prints what it
printed before). Trimmed first, at no visible cost: his scene imports without the importer's LODs and shadow meshes
(`build_people.py` `IMPORT_SETTINGS["stoneback.glb"]`); the plates' budgets were cut to 260-640 triangles.

### What still looks weak (A1b, honestly)

- The shoulder slabs still read a little as rounded rocks from the front: their outline follows the deltoid's round;
  the flat faces and the cracks show from the side and close up. The helm is bulky for a fitted plate.
- The bands are rings fitted round the body by angle: they bridge hollows as hoops do, and the bandoliers pass through
  the arms' roots when the arms come down.
- The face's character is mostly paint on a 512 px atlas: soft in a close-up.
- The boulder sits on the curled fingers more than in them (one rest curl for the fist and the throw).
- The stone is still procedural; its lichen and stains read at a close-up more than from afar.
