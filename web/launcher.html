GiantbornAge of Titans

You are a 12-foot juvenile giant in a 120 × 120 m test arena. Break the fence, learn your club on the practice dummy, then go north and fight the soldier who patrols there.

About 12 MB to download. Play on a computer with a keyboard and mouse; a gamepad may also work.

About this build

      
What it is. The first playable milestone of Giantborn: Age of Titans, built with Godot 4.6.3: one arena, one giant at stage 1 of 10, one enemy, a dummy and a breakable fence.

      
What it is not yet. The art is placeholder boxes and there is no sound. Growth stages, grabs, more abilities and the valley come in later milestones.

      
If something goes wrong. Esc opens the pause menu, where Restart sandbox resets everything. Settings are saved in this browser. Note what you did, what you expected and what happened.
