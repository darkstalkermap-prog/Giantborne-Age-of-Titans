GiantbornAge of Titans

You wake as a 12-foot juvenile giant in Giant's Hollow. The Valley of Broken Kings is your tutorial: wolves and an ogre in the Old Wood, a village you can help, cow or force, and the Ogre King at Kingsfall, growing to 18 and then 26 feet at its shrines (about 20–30 minutes). Then the Kings' Door opens onto the Old Kingdom, an open world of five regions, two kilometres across, where you grow to 36, 48 and 62 feet, towns react to your size, pikes, shield walls and siege engines hold the roads, and oaths are won by help, fear or force. Win two of the three oaths, grow to 62 feet at the Standing Stones, enter the Moot, face Harrok the Unbowed and take the Stone Throne.

About 30 MB to download. Play on a computer with a keyboard and mouse; a gamepad may also work.

Skips the Valley: a new game on the Kingsgate shelf as the 26-foot giant who took the crown. It replaces a game saved in this browser.

Gamepad: sticks move and look, RB light, RT heavy, LB block, B dodge, A jump, X use. Abilities are LT + A/B/X/Y and LT + d-pad, Titan Surge is LT + RT, Back opens the inventory and Start pauses. On its own the d-pad opens the map (down) and the journal (right) and tracks the next quest (left).

    


    

      
What to try

      
    
        
  1. Choose New Game. The quest tracker (top right) and the diamond marker show the way. In Giant's Hollow, try both cameras (V) and spar at the post to learn block, parry and dodge.
    2. 
        
  2. In the Old Wood, wolves circle and lunge: dodge, then punish. Stagger a soldier with a heavy attack, grab it with E and throw it.
    3. 
        
  3. Grow to 18 ft at the Grove Shrine, choose how to deal with Elder Maren at Thornwick, break into the ruins and face the Ogre King at Kingsfall. Below half health it charges: make it hit a pillar, then punish.
    4. 
        
  4. Take the crown and walk through the Kings' Door: the Old Kingdom opens below the Kingsgate shelf. Start in the open world begins here.
    5. 
        
  5. Drive the scouts off the beacon hill and relight the Kingsgate beacon. The Kingsgate shrine then grows you to 36 ft, and Heavy Footfall and Boulder Hurl become Titan Stomp and Crag Hurl. At every shrine, the Talents tab spends the point each level brings in three trees (new tiers open at levels 6, 8, 9, 10 and 12), and in the Old Kingdom the Ancestry tab lets you choose, once, Stoneblood, Stormborn or Wildheart.
    6. 
        
  6. Open the map (M): it fills in as you explore, the waystones and shrines you have visited are fast-travel points, and P pins a spot for the compass. The journal (J) lists your quests; T tracks the next one.
    7. 
        
  7. In Brannock, swear the march oath by helping its people, cowing them or breaking the gatehouse; the town remembers which. Pikes at the King's Road toll fort and a shield wall in the Raiders' Hollow brace together as you close: step out of the band drawn on the ground, then go for the banner.
    8. 
        
  8. In the Greywood, trolls close their wounds unless they burn: smash a bonfire or a brazier beside one, or throw it into the flames. Sedge at Hollowmere asks for the Troll Matriarch: slay her in her den, or burn her three nests and drive her out. Trees between the camera and you (or the target you lock) fade to a ghost, so the pines no longer hide the fight.
    9. 
        
  9. At the war camp, Aldmere's herald names the crown's three roads: break Fort Aldmere's gate and fight the Iron Regiment until Warlord Corvane yields (hold Enter, the mouse button or pad A on Spare or Strike); wreck the seven war engines of the camp and Ballista Ridge; or show her a token (Brannock's banner, the deserters' word, Stoneblood, or your 48 ft) and sound the horn at the fort's gate for a parley.
    10. 
        
  10. Siege engines warn before they shoot: a ballista's bolt follows its aim line, a catapult's stone falls in its circle, and riders charge down a line: step aside. At 62 ft, seize a staggered or uncrewed engine with E and throw it.
    11. 
        
  11. Your first oath lets the shrines of the Greywood, the Sunken Steps and Aldmere grow you to 48 ft; two oaths wake the Shrine of the Standing Stones, which grows you to 62 ft. Below 62 ft the Giant's Stair turns you back: go round by the Ironway, up from Aldmere's road. Then climb the Stair, enter the Moot, settle the clans' champion, Harrok, in its ring, and take the Stone Throne.
    12. 
        
  12. Off the road: pay, cow or breach the King's Road toll, the ogre pair in the Barrow Fields, Millford's millrace and the Shepherd's Watch beacon; the Needle and the great pines in the Greywood; Wenna's five sunken bells and the Stoneknuckles, the sunken vault and the Titan Forge at the Titan's Bones; the deserters at the burned mill; Skarn and its siege in the Stonehold; and back in the Valley, the sealed Citadel that only a 36-foot giant can breach.
    13. 
        
  13. The world moves without you: raiders fall on Millford, an army column marches, and bands wander the roads. Walk out of a boss's arena and the fight starts over (the screen warns you first), except in Fort Aldmere's bailey, whose portcullis drops behind you until the Iron Regiment is beaten.
    14. 
        
  14. Death returns you to the last shrine or waystone. Progress is saved in this browser, so Continue picks up where you left off. Sandbox is still there for practice.
    15. 
      

    


    

      
About this build

      
What it is. Giantborn: Age of Titans (Godot 4.6.3): the Valley of Broken Kings as the tutorial, then the Old Kingdom, a streamed open world of five regions with a map, a compass, discovery and fast travel. Growth to 62 ft, abilities that evolve as you grow, talents and an ancestry, formations with banners, siege engines and cavalry, trolls, dire wolves and rival giants, three bosses (the Troll Matriarch, the Iron Regiment and Harrok the Unbowed), towns that react to your size, three oaths each won more than one way, and the campaign's end on the Stone Throne.

      
Sound on. The Valley, every region and every town have their own score and ambience: music that follows the place and the fight, and threats you can hear coming. Browsers start a page's sound only after a click or a key press, so if you hear nothing, click the game once. The volumes are in Settings, and the sound mutes while the tab is hidden.

      
What it is not yet. The art is placeholder boxes. Every region is filled in and the main quest ends, but the world's balance is not tuned yet: bots have played the Valley and scripted runs finish the campaign through every pair of oaths in either order, yet no bot or new player has played the world from end to end, so expect rough edges and fights that are too hard or too easy. Siege trains do not wander the roads yet, and the world is not yet tuned for browsers: the browser version runs on one thread, so expect slow frames on weaker computers.

      
The next build. 0.9.0-m9, the last of this plan: a world bot that plays the whole campaign, peacefully, by conquest and in between, in every order of the oaths; a 60-minute stability run; the balance pass and a report on how the world plays; then the finished docs, exports and play link.

      
If something goes wrong. Esc opens the pause menu, where you can load the last checkpoint or quit to the title. Settings and your saved game stay in this browser. Note what you did, what you expected and what happened.
